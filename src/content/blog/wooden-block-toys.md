Yes, Melissa & Doug wooden blocks are FSC certified and made from non-toxic materials. They meet strict safety standards, ensuring no choking hazards. These blocks are designed with smooth edges to keep toddlers safe during playtime.



### What Educational Benefits Do Shape Sorting Cubes Offer?

### What Storage Options Come With Wooden Block Sets?



Many wooden block sets include storage trays, pouches, or containers with shape sorter lids. These storage solutions keep blocks organized and easy to access. They also promote tidiness and responsibility in young children after playtime.
