## Step-by-step Crafting Guide

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=creating-a-handmade-cardboard-toy-laptop-is-a-fun-and-simple-project.-you-only-need-cardboard,-scissors,-glue,-and-some-markers.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Creating a handmade cardboard toy laptop is a fun and simple project. You only need cardboard, scissors, glue, and some markers.

This guide will help you make a cute toy laptop with clear steps. Follow along to build your own craft.

### Cutting The Cardboard

Start by choosing a sturdy piece of cardboard. Use a ruler to measure parts for the laptop body.

Cut two rectangles. One will be the screen, and the other will be the keyboard base.

* Screen piece: about 8 inches by 6 inches
* Keyboard base: about 8 inches by 5 inches
* Extra small pieces for keys and screen details

### Assembling The Laptop Shape

Fold the keyboard base and screen piece to form an open laptop shape. Use glue to hold the edges.

Make sure the pieces are aligned so the laptop can open and close like a real one.

* Apply glue along one edge of the keyboard base
* Attach the screen piece carefully
* Let the glue dry for 10 minutes

### Adding Keys And Screen Details

Cut small squares or rectangles from cardboard for the keys. Glue them evenly on the keyboard base.

Draw screen details with markers or colored pencils. Add buttons or logos for a realistic look.

* Make keys about 0.5 inches square
* Arrange keys in rows
* Draw a simple screen image or icons

## Safety Tips For Crafting

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-handmade-cardboard-toy-laptops-is-fun-and-creative.-safety-is-important-to-keep-everyone-happy-and-free-from-harm.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making handmade cardboard toy laptops is fun and creative. Safety is important to keep everyone happy and free from harm.

Follow simple rules to protect yourself and others while crafting. These tips help you enjoy your project safely.

### Handling Sharp Tools

Sharp tools like scissors and craft knives need careful use. Always cut away from your body and keep fingers clear of blades.

1. Use tools with safety handles for a better grip.
2. Cut on a flat, stable surface to avoid slips.
3. Store sharp tools out of reach of children.
4. Wear protective gloves if possible.
5. Take breaks to avoid tired hands and mistakes.

### Using Non-toxic Materials

Choose non-toxic glue, paint, and markers. These materials are safe for skin and lungs. Check labels before use to avoid harmful chemicals.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Material | Safety Tip |
| Glue | Use water-based, non-toxic glue only |
| Paint | Choose child-safe, non-toxic paint |
| Markers | Select washable and non-toxic markers |

### Supervision Guidelines

Children should always have adult supervision during crafting. Adults help with difficult tasks and keep the work area safe.

**Supervision Tips:**

* Keep sharp tools away from unsupervised children.
* Help with cutting and gluing steps.
* Watch for signs of tiredness or frustration.
* Encourage safe and clean work habits.

### What Materials Are Used For Handmade Cardboard Toy Laptops?

### How Durable Are Handmade Cardboard Toy Laptops?
