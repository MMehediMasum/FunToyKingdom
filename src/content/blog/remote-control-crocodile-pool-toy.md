Clean the toy after every use to remove dirt and pool chemicals. This prevents damage and keeps it looking good.

Store the crocodile pool toy in a cool, dry place. Keep it away from direct sunlight to avoid fading and damage.

Remove batteries before storing for a long time. This stops battery leaks that can harm the toy.

* Choose a shaded, dry storage spot
* Keep the toy away from heat sources
* Remove batteries if not used for weeks
* Store the remote control separately

If the toy stops working, check the batteries first. Replace old batteries with fresh ones to restore power.

Clean the remote control and toy contacts if they look dirty. Moisture can also cause problems, so dry everything well.

* Replace old or weak batteries
* Clean battery contacts with a dry cloth
* Make sure the toy is dry before use
* Check remote control signal and range
* Reset the toy by turning it off and on
