The 1:18 scale means each toy car is about 18 times smaller than the real one. This size allows for fine details like doors that open, realistic paint, and authentic logos. Brands like Maisto produce popular models, including classic Mustangs, Lamborghinis, and Chevrolet Chevelles.



Some models feature lights and sounds, making playtime more fun. These cars suit collectors and children, combining beauty and durability. Whether displayed on a shelf or raced across the floor, 1:18 scale cars bring joy and fascination. They create a connection to real cars while fitting perfectly in your hands or collection.
