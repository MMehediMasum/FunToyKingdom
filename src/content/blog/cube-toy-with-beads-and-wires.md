### Cube Toy Vs Traditional Bead Mazes

Cube toys have beads that slide along wires in many directions. Traditional bead mazes usually have flat bases with simple wire paths.

The cube shape allows movement on all sides. Traditional mazes limit movement to one plane. This difference helps develop hand-eye coordination in new ways.

* Cube toy offers 3D movement and more challenge
* Traditional bead maze is easier for young children
* Both improve fine motor skills and focus

### Benefits Over Electronic Toys

Cube toys do not use batteries or screens. They encourage hands-on play and reduce screen time for kids.

Electronic toys often distract with sounds and lights. Cube toys focus on touch and movement, which support sensory development better.

* Enhances tactile and visual senses
* Improves concentration without distractions
* Safe and durable for long-term use

### Choosing The Right Sensory Toy

Select a toy that matches the child's age and skill level. The toy should be fun but not too hard to use.

Consider what senses the toy stimulates. Toys that engage touch, sight, and movement help children learn better.

* Pick toys with safe, non-toxic materials
* Choose toys that encourage creativity and focus
* Look for toys that grow with the child’s skills
