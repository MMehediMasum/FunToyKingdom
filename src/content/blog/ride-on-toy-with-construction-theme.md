## Maintenance And Care

Keeping your ride-on toy with a construction theme in good shape is important. Regular care helps the toy last longer and work well.

Simple steps like cleaning, battery care, and proper storage protect the toy from damage and wear.

### Cleaning Tips

Clean the ride-on toy often to keep dirt and dust away. Use a soft cloth and mild soap for the best results.

Avoid harsh chemicals that can harm the paint or plastic parts. Dry the toy well to stop rust and mold.

* Wipe dirt with a damp cloth
* Use mild soap and water
* Dry all parts after cleaning
* Check wheels for debris
* Clean battery terminals carefully

### Battery Care

Take care of the battery to keep the toy running smoothly. Charge it fully before use, but do not overcharge.

Check the battery regularly for leaks or damage. Replace old batteries to avoid problems and keep the toy safe.

* Charge battery before first use
* Do not overcharge or leave plugged in
* Store battery in a cool, dry place
* Inspect battery for damage often
* Replace battery if it shows wear

### Storage Solutions

Store the ride-on toy in a dry, shaded area to protect it from sun and rain. Keep it off the ground if possible.

Cover the toy with a cloth or plastic sheet to stop dust and dirt from settling on it during storage.

* Keep toy indoors or in a covered space
* Use a protective cover when stored
* Store away from direct sunlight
* Place toy on a raised surface if possible
* Remove batteries if storing long-term
