## Setting Up The Ride

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-up-the-outdoor-eagle-inflatable-ride-on-toy-is-simple-and-fun.-proper-setup-ensures-a-safe-and-enjoyable-experience-for-kids.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting up the Outdoor Eagle Inflatable Ride on Toy is simple and fun. Proper setup ensures a safe and enjoyable experience for kids.

Follow easy steps to inflate, place, and maintain the ride. This guide helps you prepare the toy quickly and safely.

### Inflation Tips

Use an electric pump for faster inflation. Make sure the valve is fully open before starting. Inflate slowly to avoid damage.

Check for firm but not over-stretched air pressure. The ride should feel stable and soft. Close the valve tightly after inflating.

* Use a clean, dry pump nozzle
* Inflate on a flat surface
* Avoid sharp objects near the valve
* Do not overinflate to prevent tears

### Safe Placement

Place the ride on a flat, clean surface. Avoid rough or uneven ground to keep it stable. Make sure there is enough space around the ride.

Keep the ride away from sharp objects, heat sources, and water. Check the area for rocks, sticks, or anything that can cause damage.

* Choose a shaded area if possible
* Clear debris from the ground
* Keep away from pools or open water
* Ensure adult supervision nearby

### Maintenance Advice

Clean the ride after each use with mild soap and water. Dry it completely before storing to prevent mold and mildew. Check for holes or tears regularly.

Store the ride in a cool, dry place. Avoid direct sunlight during long storage. Repair small damages with a patch kit to extend the ride’s life.

* Inspect seams and valves often
* Use gentle cleaning tools
* Do not fold while wet
* Store away from pets and rodents
