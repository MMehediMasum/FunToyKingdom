Black and white toys are perfect for newborns. They help babies see shapes and patterns clearly.

Newborns see high contrast colors better than soft colors. Black and white toys make it easier to spot details.

* Help babies focus on shapes and edges
* Improve eye tracking skills
* Support the growth of the optic nerve

## Choosing Safe And Durable Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-toys-for-newborns-is-very-important.-safe-and-durable-toys-help-babies-play-without-harm.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right toys for newborns is very important. Safe and durable toys help babies play without harm.

Parents and caregivers should pick toys that last and keep babies safe from small parts or harmful materials.

### Material Considerations

Toys made from strong and safe materials protect babies during play. Look for toys with soft but tough surfaces.

* Choose natural fabrics or BPA-free plastics
* Avoid toys with sharp edges or loose parts
* Check for easy-to-clean surfaces

### Size And Shape Safety

The size and shape of toys matter for newborns. Toys should not fit fully in a baby’s mouth to avoid choking.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Feature | Safe Toy Size |
| Length | More than 4 inches |
| Width | More than 1.75 inches |
| Shape | No small detachable parts |

### Non-toxic Certification

Non-toxic toys keep babies safe from harmful chemicals. Look for certifications from trusted safety organizations.

Common non-toxic labels include:

* ASTM F963
* EN71
* CPSIA compliant
