Gi Joe first appeared in 1964 as a military-themed toy. It was named after the common American soldier. The line grew to include many figures with different roles. The toys inspired stories of bravery and teamwork.

Cobra is the enemy force in the Gi Joe universe. It was introduced in the 1980s as a secret terrorist group. Their goal was to take over the world. Cobra’s rise made the Gi Joe stories exciting and full of action.
