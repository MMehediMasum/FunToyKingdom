Toy Story Blocks let kids build their own stories and scenes. This encourages imagination and new ideas.

Handling the blocks improves hand-eye coordination. Kids learn to control their fingers and hands better.

Toy Story Blocks challenge children to think and solve problems. They figure out how to fit pieces together.
