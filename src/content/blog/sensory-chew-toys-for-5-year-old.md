## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Is Suitable For Sensory Chew Toys?



Sensory chew toys are ideal for babies aged 6 months and older. They support teething relief and sensory development. For 5-year-olds, choose toys with varied textures and features to enhance fine motor skills and engagement.



### Are Silicone Teething Toys Safe For Toddlers?



Yes, silicone teething toys are made from food-grade, non-toxic materials. They are BPA-free, durable, and gentle on gums. These toys provide safe chewing options for toddlers, reducing choking hazards and promoting healthy oral development.



### How Do Musical Rattles Aid Sensory Development?



Musical rattles stimulate hearing, sight, and touch through sound and light. They encourage sensory exploration and improve fine motor skills. These toys keep toddlers engaged while supporting cognitive and sensory growth.



### Can Chew Toys Improve Fine Motor Skills?



Yes, chew toys with varied textures and shapes enhance grip and hand coordination. They promote fine motor skill development by encouraging grasping, squeezing, and manipulating. Sensory chew toys support both oral and motor skill growth.



### What Features To Look For In Baby Chew Toys?



Look for food-grade silicone, easy-to-clean surfaces, varied textures, and bright colors. Toys with music or light features add sensory stimulation. Choose age-appropriate sizes to ensure safety and effective teething relief for toddlers.
