## Top Anime Figure Types

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=anime-action-figures-come-in-many-styles-and-designs.-collectors-enjoy-different-types-based-on-their-preferences-and-budgets.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Anime action figures come in many styles and designs. Collectors enjoy different types based on their preferences and budgets.

This guide covers the top anime figure types. Learn about scale figures, nendoroids, and articulated figures.

### Scale Figures

Scale figures are detailed models made to a specific size ratio. They show characters in realistic poses with fine details.

Common scales include 1/8, 1/7, and 1/6. Larger scales offer more detail but cost more and need more space.

### Nendoroids And Chibis

Nendoroids and chibis are small, cute figures with big heads and small bodies. They often have interchangeable parts.

* Height usually around 10 cm (4 inches)
* Multiple face plates for different expressions
* Posable arms, legs, and accessories
* Popular for their adorable style
* Easy to display in small spaces

### Figma And Articulated Figures

Figma and other articulated figures focus on poseability. They have many joints for dynamic action poses.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Feature | Figma | Other Articulated Figures |
| Height | About 13-16 cm | Varies |
| Joints | Multiple for flexible posing | Varies by brand |
| Accessories | Interchangeable hands and faces | Depends on figure |
| Material | Plastic with metal parts | Plastic or PVC |

* Budget options use simpler molds and fewer accessories

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Drives The Growth Of The Anime Action Figure Market?

The market grows due to rising anime popularity worldwide. Collectors seek limited editions and detailed craftsmanship. Online platforms and conventions boost sales. New anime releases often spike demand rapidly. Licensing deals with popular series also fuel growth.

### How To Identify High-quality Anime Action Figures?

Check for detailed sculpting and accurate paintwork. Look for official licensing and brand reputation. Materials like PVC and ABS ensure durability. Packaging should be intact and authentic. Reviews and unboxing videos help verify quality before purchase.

### Where Can Collectors Find Rare Anime Action Figures?

Rare figures appear at conventions, auctions, and specialty stores. Online marketplaces like eBay and dedicated collector sites are useful. Joining collector communities offers trade and alerts. Limited edition releases from manufacturers are highly sought after.

### What Factors Influence Anime Action Figure Pricing?

Rarity, character popularity, and production quantity affect price. Condition and packaging also play key roles. Exclusive or limited-run figures command higher prices. Market demand fluctuates with anime trends. Authenticity ensures better resale value.
