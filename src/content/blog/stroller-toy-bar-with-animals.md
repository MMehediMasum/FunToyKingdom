## Choosing Safe Animal Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=stroller-toy-bars-with-animals-are-fun-for-babies.-they-keep-little-hands-busy-and-eyes-curious.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Stroller toy bars with animals are fun for babies. They keep little hands busy and eyes curious.

Choosing safe toys is very important to protect your child. Safety means less worry for parents.

### Non-toxic Materials

Always pick toys made from non-toxic materials. Babies often put toys in their mouths. Non-toxic means no harmful chemicals or paints.

* Look for BPA-free plastics
* Choose natural wood with safe finishes
* Avoid toys with lead-based paint
* Check for certification labels on the packaging

### Secure Attachments

Animal toys should attach firmly to the bar. Loose parts can fall off and cause accidents. Check all clips and fasteners before use.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Attachment Type | Safety Tip |
| Plastic Clip | Test strength before each use |
| Velcro Strap | Make sure it holds tight |
| Elastic Band | Check for wear or tearing |
| Button Snap | Confirm it stays closed firmly |

### Choking Hazard Prevention

Small parts can be choking hazards. Choose toys without tiny detachable pieces. Inspect toys regularly for damage.

Here are tips to prevent choking:

1. Do not buy toys with parts smaller than 1.25 inches
2. Check for loose buttons or beads
3. Supervise your baby while playing
4. Replace toys if they break or parts come off
