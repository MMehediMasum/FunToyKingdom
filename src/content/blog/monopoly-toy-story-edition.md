Players can explore a board full of Toy Story themes. The tokens, cards, and board all show scenes and characters from the films. This edition makes the game feel like a Toy Story adventure.

### Unique Game Tokens

The game tokens are small figures of popular Toy Story characters. Each player picks a token that represents a character from the movies. These tokens add personality to the game.

Tokens include Woody, Buzz Lightyear, Jessie, and more. They are detailed and colorful. These tokens replace the usual Monopoly pieces like the car or top hat.

The board shows locations from the Toy Story movies. Instead of streets, players buy places like Andy’s Room and Pizza Planet. The design uses bright colors and cartoon art.

### Setting Up For Play

Place the board on a flat surface. Each player picks a character token from Toy Story.

Shuffle the cards, place money and tokens nearby. Give each player the starting money.

* Each player gets $1500 in Monopoly money
* Shuffle and place Chance and Community Chest cards on the board
* Put houses and hotels in reach of all players
* Players place their token on the 'Go' space

### Basic Rules And Objectives

Roll the dice to move your token around the board. Buy properties you land on if unowned.

Collect rent when other players land on your properties. The goal is to bankrupt others by owning properties.

* Roll two dice and move your token forward
* Buy properties or pay rent when landing on owned spaces
* Collect money when other players land on your properties
* Build houses and hotels to increase rent
* Trade properties with other players to complete sets

### Special Toy Story Edition Rules

This edition includes unique tokens and places from the Toy Story movies. Some cards have special actions based on the story.

Use the Toy Story tokens and enjoy themed Chance and Community Chest cards that add fun twists.

* Character tokens include Woody, Buzz, and others
* Properties are locations from the Toy Story world
* Chance and Community Chest cards have Toy Story events
* Special rules may affect how you pay or collect rent
