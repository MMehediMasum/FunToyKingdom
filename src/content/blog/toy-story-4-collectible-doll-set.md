## Care And Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=toy-story-4-collectible-doll-sets-are-treasures-for-fans.-proper-care-ensures-they-stay-in-great-condition.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Toy Story 4 collectible doll sets are treasures for fans. Proper care ensures they stay in great condition.

Follow these tips to keep your dolls looking new. Cleaning, storage, and handling are key aspects.

### Cleaning Techniques

Regular cleaning prevents dust build-up. Use gentle methods to avoid damage.

Soft cloths and mild soap work well. Avoid harsh chemicals on your dolls.

* Use a soft brush for detailed areas
* Dry dolls thoroughly after cleaning
* Test cleaning products on small areas first

### Storage Solutions

Proper storage protects your dolls from dust and sunlight. Keep them in a cool, dry place.

Display cases are great for showcasing and protecting your collection. Avoid direct sunlight exposure.

* Use acid-free boxes for long-term storage
* Keep dolls away from heat sources
* Regularly check for any signs of wear

### Handling Guidelines

Handle your dolls with care to prevent wear. Clean hands before touching to avoid stains.

Gently move any parts to avoid breakage. Encourage others to handle them carefully too.

* Support dolls from the base when lifting
* Avoid using too much force on movable parts
* Supervise children when they play with the dolls
