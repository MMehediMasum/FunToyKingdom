## Care And Maintenance

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-your-monster-high-ghoulia-yelps-doll-keeps-it-looking-great.-proper-care-helps-the-doll-last-longer.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of your Monster High Ghoulia Yelps Doll keeps it looking great. Proper care helps the doll last longer.

Simple steps for cleaning, storing, and preserving will protect your doll's details and colors.

### Cleaning Techniques

Clean the doll gently with a soft cloth to remove dust. Use a damp cloth if needed, but avoid soaking it.

For hair, use a small, soft brush to remove tangles. Do not use harsh chemicals or strong water pressure.

* Wipe plastic parts with a dry or slightly damp cloth
* Brush hair carefully to avoid pulling or damage
* Avoid using soap or cleaning products on painted areas
* Let the doll air dry after cleaning

### Storage Recommendations

Store the doll in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Sunlight can fade colors and damage plastic.

Use a box or display case to protect from dust and accidental damage. Keep the doll away from pets and small children.

* Avoid places with high heat or humidity
* Place the doll on a soft surface when stored
* Keep accessories and clothes in separate labeled containers
* Check the doll regularly for any signs of damage

### Preserving Doll Condition

Handle the doll with clean hands to avoid transferring oils or dirt. Avoid bending joints too much to prevent breaks.

Keep the doll’s clothes clean and dry. Fix small damages early to stop further wear.

* Wash hands before touching the doll
* Limit direct handling to reduce wear
* Repair loose joints or accessories quickly
* Keep the doll away from water and sharp objects
