## How To Play Outdoor Curling

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=outdoor-curling-is-a-fun-and-active-game-you-can-play-on-grass-or-smooth-surfaces.-it-uses-curling-stones-and-a-target-area-to-score-points.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Outdoor curling is a fun and active game you can play on grass or smooth surfaces. It uses curling stones and a target area to score points.

This game is great for friends and family. It helps improve aim and teamwork while enjoying the outdoors.



### Basic Rules And Objectives

The main goal is to slide your stones closer to the target than your opponent. The target is a circle called the house.

Players take turns sliding stones toward the house. The team with stones closest to the center scores points.

* Each player or team gets an equal number of stones.
* Stones must be slid, not thrown or tossed.
* Points are counted after all stones are played in a round.
* The team with the highest score after set rounds wins.

### Setting Up The Game Area

Find a flat, smooth outdoor surface like a driveway or lawn. Mark a target circle, called the house, about 12 feet from the throwing line.

Use chalk, tape, or cones to mark the house and the throwing line. Make sure there is enough space for players to slide stones safely.

* Draw a circle about 6 feet in diameter for the house.
* Mark a throwing line 12 feet away from the house center.
* Clear the area to avoid obstacles.
* Provide enough space for players to stand and slide stones.

### Tips For Beginners

Start by practicing sliding the stones smoothly. Aim for the center of the house and control your speed.

Work with your team to plan shots. Watch where stones land and adjust your throw next turn.

* Keep your hand steady when sliding stones.
* Try to slide stones straight without spinning them.
* Focus on speed; too fast or slow can miss the target.
* Have fun and learn from each round.

## Best Surfaces For Outdoor Play

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-surface-for-your-outdoor-curling-game-set-helps-improve-play.-the-surface-affects-how-the-stones-slide-and-the-overall-experience.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right surface for your outdoor curling game set helps improve play. The surface affects how the stones slide and the overall experience. Some surfaces work better than others for outdoor use.

This guide covers three common surfaces. Learn their benefits and what to expect from each one.

### Grass And Turf Options

Grass and turf give a natural feel for outdoor curling. Soft surfaces may slow the stones, but they are easy on the body if you fall. Turf is more even than grass and can offer a smoother slide.

* Grass may vary in smoothness and height.
* Turf provides a consistent, flat playing area.
* Both surfaces need regular maintenance to stay playable.
* Wet grass or turf can affect stone movement.

### Concrete And Pavement

Concrete and pavement offer a very firm and flat surface. Stones slide well on these surfaces, making gameplay faster. These areas are easy to clean and maintain.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surface Type | Slide Quality | Maintenance |
| Concrete | Good | Low |
| Pavement | Good | Low |
| Grass | Moderate | High |
| Turf | Moderate | Medium |

### Using Indoor Floors Outdoors

Some players use indoor flooring mats outdoors. These mats can simulate ice and allow smooth stone slides. They are easy to set up and remove but need a flat base underneath.

**Tips for indoor floors outdoors:**

* Place mats on flat concrete or pavement.
* Secure edges to prevent slipping.
* Keep mats clean and dry.
* Store mats indoors when not in use.
