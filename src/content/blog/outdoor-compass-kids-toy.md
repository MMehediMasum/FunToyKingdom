## Teaching Kids Compass Basics

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=teaching-children-how-to-use-a-compass-is-a-great-way-to-start-outdoor-learning.-it-helps-them-understand-directions-and-maps.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using an outdoor compass toy makes learning fun and simple. Kids can try real compass skills during hikes or backyard adventures.

### How To Read A Compass

A compass has a needle that points north. The needle moves to line up with Earth’s magnetic field. The compass face shows four main directions: north, south, east, and west.

* Hold the compass flat in your hand.
* Turn your body until the needle points north.
* Look at the direction you want to go on the compass face.
* Keep the needle lined up while moving.

### Understanding Directions

Directions help us find places and stay safe outdoors. North is usually at the top on maps. East is to the right, south is down, and west is to the left.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direction | Position on Compass | Position on Map |
| North | Top of needle | Top |
| East | Right side | Right |
| South | Opposite North | Bottom |
| West | Left side | Left |

### Using A Compass With Maps

Maps and compasses work together to help find places. You can match the compass directions with map directions to find your way.

Here is a simple way to use both:

1. Lay the map flat on a table.
2. Place the compass on the map with the arrow pointing north.
3. Turn the map and compass together until the compass needle points north.
4. Find your location on the map.
5. Follow the compass to reach your destination.

## Maintaining And Caring For Compass Toys

Compass toys are fun and educational for kids. Keeping them in good condition ensures longer enjoyment. Here are some tips on how to maintain and care for them.

Proper cleaning and storage, addressing common issues, and extending the toy's lifespan are key aspects of caring for compass toys.

### Cleaning And Storage

Cleaning your compass toy helps keep it working well. Use a soft cloth to wipe away dust and dirt.

Store the compass in a dry place to prevent rust. Avoid placing it in direct sunlight for long periods.

* Use a gentle cleaner for tough spots
* Keep the compass in a protective case
* Check storage area for moisture

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

Sometimes compass toys may not point correctly. This could be due to interference from magnetic objects nearby.

If the needle sticks, gently tap the compass to free it. Ensure the toy is on a level surface for accurate readings.

* Keep away from electronic devices
* Remove metallic items from the surrounding area
* Check if the needle is bent or damaged

### Extending Toy Lifespan

To make your compass toy last longer, handle it with care. Teach kids the importance of gentle use.

Regular maintenance checks can help spot potential problems early. This keeps the toy in good working condition.

* Encourage careful handling during play
* Inspect the toy for wear and tear monthly
* Replace damaged parts promptly
