## Tips For Safe Play

Kids love ride on toys with horn sounds. These toys bring joy and fun. Safety is very important during playtime.

### Supervision Guidelines

Always watch children when they use ride on toys. Stay close to help if needed. Supervision lowers the chance of accidents.

Check the toy before play to ensure it works well. Make sure the horn sounds are not too loud for kids’ ears.

* Stay within arm’s reach of young children
* Watch for obstacles or hazards nearby
* Teach kids how to use the horn safely

### Protective Gear

Wear protective gear to prevent injuries. Helmets protect the head from bumps and falls. Knee and elbow pads protect joints.

Choose gear that fits well and is comfortable. Protective gear helps kids play without getting hurt.

* Use a properly fitted helmet
* Wear knee and elbow pads
* Choose bright colors for visibility

### Safe Play Areas

Pick safe places for riding toys. Avoid streets and busy areas. Flat, smooth surfaces are best for riding.

Remove objects that may cause falls. Keep the play area clear and well lit to prevent accidents.

* Use driveways or playgrounds with soft ground
* Keep away from traffic and water bodies
* Check the area for sharp or hard objects
