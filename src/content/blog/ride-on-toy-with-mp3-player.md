### Supervision Recommendations

Always supervise children when they are riding their toys. This ensures immediate help if needed.

### Protective Gear

Wearing the right gear can prevent injuries. It is essential to equip your child properly.

* Helmet: A must for head protection.
* Knee pads: To guard against scrapes.
* Elbow pads: Prevents bruising during falls.
* Closed-toe shoes: Protects feet from injuries.
