## Game Basics

Kan Jam is a fun outdoor frisbee game for all ages. It is easy to learn and quick to set up. The goal is to score points by throwing and deflecting the frisbee into the cans.

This game works best with two teams of two players each. It encourages teamwork and skill while being active outside.

### What You Need

You need a few simple items to play Kan Jam. The main equipment includes the official cans and a frisbee.

* Two Kan Jam cans (also called goals)
* One official Kan Jam frisbee
* An open playing area, such as a backyard or park
* Two teams of two players each

### Setting Up The Field

Set up the playing field by placing the two cans 50 feet apart. This distance is standard but can be adjusted for younger players.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Item | Details |
| Distance between cans | 50 feet (15.24 meters) |
| Playing surface | Flat grass or open space |
| Player positions | One player throws, one deflects per team |

### How To Play

Players take turns throwing the frisbee toward the opposite can. Their partner tries to guide or deflect the frisbee to score points.

1. The throwing player aims to hit or enter the can.
2. The partner tries to deflect the frisbee without touching it with their hands.
3. If the frisbee hits the can, the team scores points.
4. If the frisbee goes inside the can with help, more points are scored.
5. The game continues until a team reaches exactly 21 points.

### Point System

Points in Kan Jam are scored by throwing the frisbee and hitting or entering the goal. Different actions give different points.

* 1 point: The frisbee hits the front of the goal.
* 2 points: A teammate deflects the frisbee to hit the goal.
* 3 points: The frisbee goes through the slot on the front of the goal without help.
* Instant win: The frisbee goes into the goal without any deflection.

### Fouls And Penalties

Players must follow rules to keep the game fair. Fouls result in lost points or turns.

* Stepping past the throwing line causes a foul.
* Throwing out of turn is not allowed.
* Deliberately distracting opponents results in a penalty.
* Physical contact with the opponent’s goal is forbidden.

If a foul occurs, the team loses the chance to score on that turn.

### Winning The Game

The game ends when a team reaches exactly 21 points. Scoring more than 21 points means the team’s score goes back to 17.

An instant win happens if a player throws the frisbee into the goal without help. This ends the game immediately.
