## Installation Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=installing-rubber-floor-mats-on-a-ride-on-toy-helps-protect-surfaces-and-improve-safety.-proper-installation-keeps-the-mats-in-place-and-extends-their-life.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Installing rubber floor mats on a ride on toy helps protect surfaces and improve safety. Proper installation keeps the mats in place and extends their life.

This guide covers how to prepare the toy surface, place the mats correctly, and keep them clean and maintained.

### Preparing The Ride On Toy Surface

Clean the toy surface before installing the rubber mats. Dirt and dust can prevent mats from sticking well.

* Wipe the surface with a damp cloth
* Remove any grease or sticky residue
* Allow the surface to dry completely
* Check for rough spots and smooth them if needed

### Proper Placement Techniques

Place the rubber mats carefully to cover key areas. Avoid folding or stretching the mats during installation.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Area | Placement Advice |
| Seat | Center the mat flat on the seat surface |
| Footrest | Cover the entire footrest area without wrinkles |
| Floor | Align edges with the toy’s base shape |

### Maintenance And Cleaning

Keep the mats clean to avoid wear and tear. Regular cleaning also keeps the toy looking neat.

Follow these tips for cleaning and maintenance:

* Use mild soap and water for washing
* Wipe mats dry after cleaning
* Check mats weekly for damage or loose edges
* Replace mats if cracks or tears appear




## Safety Precautions

Inspect the ride on toy and rubber mats before each use. Look for tears, cracks, or loose parts.
