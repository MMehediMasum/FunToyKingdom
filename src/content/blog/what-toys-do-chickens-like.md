**Are you wondering how to keep your chickens happy and entertained? Just like pets, chickens enjoy having toys to play with.**

But what toys do chickens like the most? Knowing what grabs their attention can make a big difference in their mood and health. You’ll discover simple, fun toys that your chickens will love. Keep reading to find out how to boost your flock’s happiness with the right playthings!




## Benefits Of Toys For Chickens

Toys offer many benefits for chickens beyond just fun. They keep chickens active and healthy. Toys also help chickens stay calm and happy. They encourage natural behaviors that chickens need. Providing toys improves chickens’ quality of life. It creates a more interesting and stimulating environment for them.

### Enhancing Physical Activity

Toys make chickens move more throughout the day. They help chickens stay fit and strong. More activity reduces the risk of health problems. Toys like hanging balls or pecking blocks promote movement. Active chickens have better muscle tone and bone health.

### Reducing Stress And Boredom

Chickens get bored without things to do. Boredom can cause stress and bad behaviors. Toys keep chickens busy and mentally engaged. This lowers stress and reduces pecking fights. Calm chickens lay more eggs and live happier lives.

### Encouraging Natural Behaviors

Toys help chickens act like they would in nature. Pecking, scratching, and exploring are natural chicken actions. Toys that move or contain treats encourage these behaviors. This keeps chickens mentally sharp and satisfied. Natural behavior is important for their well-being.

## Popular Toy Types

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=chickens-enjoy-toys-that-keep-them-busy-and-happy.-toys-help-reduce-boredom-and-stop-bad-habits.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Chickens enjoy toys that keep them busy and happy. Toys help reduce boredom and stop bad habits. Different types of toys suit different chicken personalities. Here are some popular toy types that chickens like a lot.

### Pecking And Foraging Toys

Chickens love to peck and find food. Pecking toys hold treats or seeds inside. Chickens peck to get the food out. This activity keeps their minds sharp and bodies active. Foraging toys mimic natural searching behavior. They help chickens feel calm and entertained.

### Swinging And Hanging Toys

Swings and hanging toys catch chicken attention. These toys move when touched or pushed. Chickens enjoy the gentle motion and change of view. Hanging toys also encourage exercise and curiosity. They add fun to the chicken coop environment.

### Mirrors And Reflective Objects

Mirrors and shiny objects fascinate chickens. They see their reflection and get curious. Some chickens talk or peck at their image. Mirrors can help lonely chickens feel less alone. Always watch for signs of stress from mirrors.

### Balls And Rolling Toys

Rolling toys like balls are fun for chickens. They push and chase the toys around. This activity helps chickens stay active and engaged. Balls with bells or textures add extra interest. Rolling toys also improve coordination and playfulness.

## Diy Chicken Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=diy-chicken-toys-are-a-great-way-to-keep-your-birds-happy-and-active.-making-toys-at-home-saves-money-and-lets-you-use-what-you-already-have.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

DIY chicken toys are a great way to keep your birds happy and active. Making toys at home saves money and lets you use what you already have. Chickens love toys that move, make noise, or hide treats. Creating simple, safe toys can add fun to their day.

These toys also help reduce boredom and stop bad behaviors like pecking each other. You do not need fancy materials or skills. Just a little time and some creativity.

### Using Household Items

Many household items work well as chicken toys. Empty plastic bottles can become treat dispensers. Old CDs or keys tied to a string make fun noise makers. Cardboard boxes give chickens places to explore and hide. Even kitchen scraps like corn cobs can entertain them.

### Safe Materials To Use

Choose materials that do not harm chickens. Avoid sharp edges or toxic substances. Use untreated wood, natural fibers, and food-safe plastics. Clean any item before giving it to your birds. Make sure toys are sturdy to prevent choking risks. Safety is the top priority in any DIY project.

### Easy Craft Ideas

Try simple crafts like hanging pine cones coated in peanut butter. Tie a bunch of old keys or small bells to a rope. Make a foraging board with holes to hide treats. Use a small mirror for chickens to peck at their reflection. These easy toys keep chickens curious and active.




## Choosing Safe Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-safe-toys-for-chickens-is-very-important.-chickens-like-to-peck,-scratch,-and-play.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing safe toys for chickens is very important. Chickens like to peck, scratch, and play. Unsafe toys can hurt them or make them sick. It is best to pick toys that keep your birds safe and happy.

Safe toys also last longer and stay clean. This helps your chickens enjoy their playtime without any risks. Knowing what materials and sizes to pick can make a big difference.

### Avoiding Toxic Materials

Many plastics and paints can be harmful to chickens. Avoid toys with chemicals or sharp edges. Natural wood and untreated materials work best. Check labels to make sure toys have no toxic parts. Safe toys protect your chickens from poisoning and injuries.

### Size And Durability Considerations

Choose toys that fit your chickens’ size. Small pieces can cause choking hazards. Strong toys last longer even with rough play. Avoid breakable items that can splinter or crack. Durable toys save you money and keep chickens safe.

### Cleaning And Maintenance Tips

Dirty toys can spread germs and diseases. Clean toys regularly with mild soap and water. Rinse well and dry before giving them back. Remove broken or damaged toys immediately. Keeping toys clean helps chickens stay healthy.
