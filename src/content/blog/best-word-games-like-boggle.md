### Scrabble And Its Variants

Scrabble is a classic board game where players form words using letter tiles. It focuses on word knowledge and strategy to score points.

* Players draw seven tiles and build words on the board.
* Each letter has a point value based on its rarity.
* Special squares on the board multiply letter or word scores.
* Variants include Speed Scrabble and Super Scrabble with different rules and board sizes.

### Wordament Challenges

Wordament is an online word search game similar to Boggle. Players find as many words as possible in a grid of letters.

### Upwords Strategy

Upwords is a word game like Scrabble but with stacking tiles. You can place letters on top of existing words to create new ones.

* Focus on building tall stacks for higher points.
* Watch for opportunities to change letters and form new words.
* Control the center of the board to have more options.
* Plan moves ahead to block your opponent’s chances.

### Letterpress Gameplay

Letterpress is a word game where players take turns to make words from a grid of letters. Each letter used turns the tile to your color.

* Players try to control more tiles than their opponent.
* Words must be valid and connected to the letters on the board.
* Strategy includes blocking your opponent's moves.
* The game ends when all tiles are claimed or no words remain.

### Alphabear Mechanics

Alphabear uses letter tiles to create words on a grid. When you use letters, bears grow in size.

* Form words using the available letters before time runs out.
* Letters used become bears that cover tiles and score points.
* Bears grow bigger when you use letters near each other.
* Longer words earn more points and bigger bears.
* Try to use rare letters for bonus points.

### Spelltower Strategies

SpellTower is about clearing letters by making words from a tower of letters. The goal is to avoid the tower reaching the top.

1. Look for longer words to clear more letters at once.
2. Focus on letters near the bottom to prevent tower growth.
3. Use common prefixes and suffixes to form words quickly.
4. Save rare letters for high scoring words later.
5. Plan your moves to avoid getting stuck with bad letters.
