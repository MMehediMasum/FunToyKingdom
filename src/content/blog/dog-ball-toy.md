## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Dog Ball Toy Is Best For Medium Breeds?



Medium-sized dog ball toys, around 6 cm diameter, suit medium breeds well. They offer easy grip and safe play. Products like Chuckit Fetch Medley and Chuckit! Air Fetch Ball are perfect choices for dogs weighing 9 to 27 kg.



### Are Squeaky Dog Ball Toys Safe For Puppies?



Yes, squeaky dog ball toys designed for puppies are generally safe. Look for gentle materials like KONG Squeakair Balls. These toys are soft on teeth and provide engaging sounds to keep puppies entertained without harming their gums.



### How Do Rope Dog Toys Benefit Chewing Puppies?



Rope dog toys help puppies satisfy their chewing urges while promoting dental health. They clean teeth and massage gums gently. Sets like the 10-piece rope chew toys keep puppies engaged and reduce destructive chewing behavior effectively.



### Can Dog Ball Toys Improve Fetch Game Experience?



Absolutely! Dog ball toys like Chuckit Fetch Medley enhance fetch games with durable, bouncy materials. Different textures and sounds boost your dog’s excitement and exercise, making playtime more enjoyable and interactive for both owner and dog.



### What Makes Glow-in-the-dark Dog Balls Useful?



Glow-in-the-dark dog balls, such as Max Glow balls, allow nighttime play. They improve visibility in low light, keeping dogs safe and active after sunset. These balls are ideal for evening fetch sessions or indoor fun in dim areas.



### Are Puzzle Ball Toys Good For Dog Mental Stimulation?



Yes, puzzle ball toys like Zeta ball puzzles challenge dogs mentally. They encourage problem-solving and keep dogs entertained for longer periods. These toys reduce boredom and destructive behavior by stimulating your dog’s brain during playtime.
