## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Glue For Preserving Puzzles?



Mod Podge Puzzle Saver is ideal for preserving puzzles. It dries clear, seals pieces firmly, and prevents damage. Its non-toxic formula is safe for all ages, making it perfect for long-lasting puzzle displays and crafting projects.



### How Do Mod Podge Glues Differ For Puzzles?



Mod Podge offers matte and gloss finishes for puzzles. Matte gives a non-shiny look, while gloss adds shine. Both are quick-drying, easy to clean, and seal puzzles effectively, protecting them from dust and wear.



### Can Diamond Painting Glue Work On Puzzles?



Yes, diamond painting glue like LANBEIDE provides a permanent hold and shine. It dries clear and protects puzzles, making it a great alternative for sealing and preserving completed puzzles.



### Is Puzzle Glue Safe For Children?



PHILODOGS Puzzle Glue is non-toxic and safe for kids. It dries quickly and preserves puzzles without harmful chemicals. Always supervise children during use to ensure safe handling.



### How Much Glue Do I Need For A Large Puzzle?



For large puzzles, use around 8-16 ounces of glue like Mod Podge. This amount covers a typical 1000-piece puzzle with an even, protective layer.



### What Tools Help Apply Puzzle Glue Evenly?



Using a silicone brush, such as the one included with LANBEIDE glue, helps apply glue smoothly. It prevents clumps and ensures an even coat for better sealing and shine.
