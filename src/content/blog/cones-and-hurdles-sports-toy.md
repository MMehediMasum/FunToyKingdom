## Safety Tips For Kids

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=playing-with-cones-and-hurdles-is-fun-and-helps-kids-stay-active.-safety-is-important-to-prevent-injuries.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Playing with cones and hurdles is fun and helps kids stay active. Safety is important to prevent injuries.

Parents and teachers should follow some simple rules to keep kids safe while they play.

### Proper Setup

Place cones and hurdles on flat, even ground. Avoid hard or slippery surfaces like concrete or wet grass.

Make sure the space is clear of obstacles like rocks or sticks. This helps prevent trips and falls.

* Use soft, grassy areas when possible
* Set hurdles at a safe height for children
* Keep enough distance between cones and hurdles

### Supervised Play

Always have an adult watch kids while they play with cones and hurdles. Supervision helps stop accidents quickly.

Adults can remind children to take turns and play safely. They also help if a child needs assistance.

* Keep a close eye on all players
* Stop play if children get too rough
* Help kids understand safety rules

### Appropriate Footwear

Children should wear shoes that fit well and have good grip. This reduces slipping and protects their feet.

Avoid flip-flops, sandals, or shoes without support. Sneakers or sports shoes work best for active play.

* Shoes with non-slip soles
* Closed-toe shoes for foot safety
* Proper fit to prevent blisters
