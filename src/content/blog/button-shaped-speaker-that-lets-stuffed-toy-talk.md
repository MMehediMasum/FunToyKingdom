## Magic Behind Button Speakers

Button-shaped speakers are small devices that let stuffed toys talk. They turn sound into tiny vibrations. These vibrations create sound waves we can hear.

Inside these tiny speakers, smart parts work together. They make sure the sound is clear and loud enough. This lets your toy say words or play music.

### How Button Speakers Work

The button speaker has a small diaphragm that moves fast. This movement pushes air to create sound waves. The speaker changes electrical signals into sounds.

* Electric signals enter the speaker coil.
* The coil creates a magnetic field.
* The magnetic field moves the diaphragm.
* Moving diaphragm pushes air to make sound.
* Sound comes out as speech or music.
