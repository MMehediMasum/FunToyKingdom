### Can These Smart Balls Be Used For Kittens And Adult Cats?



Yes, these toys are designed for all cat ages. They have adjustable speeds and interactive features suited for kittens’ curiosity and adult cats’ energy levels. This versatility makes them perfect for households with cats at different life stages.



### How Long Does The Rechargeable Battery Typically Last?



Rechargeable cat balls usually offer 1 to 2 hours of continuous playtime. Charging time varies but generally takes 1 to 2 hours. This ensures plenty of active play while minimizing downtime between uses.



### Do These Cat Balls Require Supervision During Play?
