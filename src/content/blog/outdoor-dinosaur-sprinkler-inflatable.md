## Setting Up Your Inflatable Sprinkler

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-up-your-outdoor-dinosaur-sprinkler-inflatable-is-simple.-follow-easy-steps-to-get-it-ready-for-fun.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting up your outdoor dinosaur sprinkler inflatable is simple. Follow easy steps to get it ready for fun.

This guide helps you pick the best spot, inflate it correctly, and keep it working well.

### Location Tips

Choose a flat area free from sharp objects like rocks or sticks. This helps protect the inflatable from damage.

Pick a spot near a water source and with enough space around the sprinkler. This prevents water from hitting walls or furniture.

* Flat ground to keep the sprinkler stable
* Away from trees that may drop leaves or branches
* Near a hose connection for easy water supply
* Open space to allow water spray without obstacles

### Inflation And Water Connection

Unfold the inflatable fully before inflating. This helps it fill evenly and keeps the shape correct.

Use an air pump designed for inflatables to fill it with air. Connect the hose to the sprinkler’s water inlet securely.

* Check all air valves are closed tightly after inflation
* Attach the garden hose firmly to prevent leaks
* Turn on water slowly to avoid sudden pressure
* Make sure water sprays evenly from all sprinkler points

### Maintenance During Use

Watch the inflatable while it runs to catch any leaks or deflation early. Fix small holes quickly with repair patches.

Keep the water pressure steady and avoid dragging the sprinkler on rough surfaces. This extends its life and keeps it safe.

* Check air pressure daily and add air if needed
* Turn off water and air when not in use
* Remove debris from sprinkler holes to keep water flow smooth
* Store in a dry place after use to prevent mold and damage

## Care And Storage Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-good-care-of-your-outdoor-dinosaur-sprinkler-inflatable-will-help-it-last-longer.-proper-cleaning-and-storage-keep-it-safe-from-damage.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking good care of your outdoor dinosaur sprinkler inflatable will help it last longer. Proper cleaning and storage keep it safe from damage.

Follow simple steps to clean, deflate, and store your inflatable after each use. This protects it from dirt, tears, and mold.

### Cleaning After Use

Rinse the inflatable with clean water to remove dirt and grass. Use a soft cloth or sponge for gentle cleaning.

Avoid harsh soaps or chemicals that can damage the material. Let it dry completely before storing to stop mold growth.

* Use mild soap if needed
* Rinse thoroughly with water
* Wipe with a soft cloth
* Dry fully in shade

### Proper Deflation Techniques

Turn off the sprinkler and unplug any power source. Open the air valves to let the air escape naturally.

Press gently to push air out. Avoid folding or rolling while the inflatable still has air inside.

* Switch off water and power
* Open valves fully
* Gently press to release air
* Wait until fully deflated

### Storage Solutions

Store the inflatable in a cool, dry place. Keep it away from sharp objects and direct sunlight to avoid damage.

Fold it loosely to prevent creases. Use a storage bag or box to protect it from dust and pests.

* Choose a dry, shaded area
* Fold without tight creases
* Use a protective bag or container
* Keep away from sharp items
