## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Sensory Toys For Infants 0-6 Months?



Best sensory toys for 0-6 months include soft crinkle toys, hanging plush rattles, and high-contrast black-and-white books. These stimulate sight, touch, and hearing, aiding early development and sensory exploration safely.



### Which Teething Toys Are Safest For Babies Under 12 Months?



Safe teething toys for babies under 12 months are BPA-free, made from food-grade silicone or natural wood. They offer easy grip and soothe gums effectively without harmful chemicals or choking hazards.



### How Do Montessori Toys Benefit Infant Development?



Montessori toys promote hands-on learning, fine motor skills, and sensory exploration. They encourage independent play and cognitive growth by offering simple, natural materials suited for infants aged 0-12 months.



### What Makes A Good Baby Rattle For Newborns?



A good baby rattle is lightweight, easy to grasp, and produces gentle sounds. It aids auditory development and hand-eye coordination for newborns and infants up to 12 months.



### Are Stacking Toys Suitable For Babies 6 Months And Older?



Yes, stacking toys are ideal for babies 6+ months. They enhance fine motor skills, spatial awareness, and problem-solving abilities through colorful, safe, and easy-to-handle pieces.
