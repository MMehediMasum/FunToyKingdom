## Choosing Safe Dolls

Picking dolls for a 3 year old needs care. Safety is important to keep children safe while playing.

Look for dolls that do not have harmful parts or materials. This helps avoid accidents and health risks.

### Materials To Avoid

Some materials can be unsafe for young children. Avoid dolls made from hard plastics that break easily.

Stay away from dolls with small detachable parts. These can be swallowed or cause injury.

* Hard plastics that crack or splinter
* Metal parts that can rust or pinch
* Paints with harmful chemicals
* Stuffing that can come loose easily

### Non-toxic Paints And Fabrics

Choose dolls painted with non-toxic paints. These are safe if a child chews or licks the doll.

Fabrics should be free from harmful dyes and chemicals. Soft, natural fabrics are better for children.

* Look for labels stating "non-toxic" or "safe for children"
* Natural cotton or wool fabrics reduce allergy risks
* Avoid dolls with strong smells from chemicals

### Size And Choking Hazards

Dolls should be large enough to avoid choking risks. Small parts can block a child’s airway.

Check if the doll has buttons, eyes, or accessories that may come off. These parts can be dangerous.

* Choose dolls bigger than 1.25 inches in diameter
* Avoid dolls with small removable pieces
* Check for secure stitching and glued parts

## Tips For Doll Care

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-dolls-helps-keep-them-clean-and-safe-for-children.-proper-care-also-makes-dolls-last-longer.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of dolls helps keep them clean and safe for children. Proper care also makes dolls last longer.

Learn simple ways to clean, store, and fix dolls. These tips work well for 3-year-olds' favorite toys.

### Cleaning And Maintenance

Clean dolls regularly to remove dirt and germs. Use a soft cloth with mild soap and water for plastic parts.

For dolls with fabric clothes, wash gently by hand or spot clean. Avoid harsh chemicals to protect colors.

* Wipe plastic dolls with a damp cloth
* Spot clean fabric clothes with mild detergent
* Air dry dolls completely before storing
* Keep dolls away from direct sunlight to prevent fading

### Storage Ideas

Store dolls in a clean, dry place to avoid dust and damage. Using containers helps keep them organized.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Option | Benefits |
| Plastic bins with lids | Protects from dust and moisture |
| Fabric bags | Breathable, good for fabric dolls |
| Shelves or cubbies | Easy access and display |
| Drawer organizers | Keeps dolls separated and neat |

### Repairing Minor Damages

Fix small tears or loose parts quickly to keep dolls safe. Use child-safe glue for plastic or fabric repairs.

**Quick Repair Tips:**

* Sew small holes with a needle and thread
* Use fabric glue for minor rips on doll clothes
* Attach loose eyes or buttons with non-toxic glue
* Trim frayed hair gently with scissors
