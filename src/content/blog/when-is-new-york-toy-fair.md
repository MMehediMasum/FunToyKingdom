## New York Toy Fair 2026 Dates

The New York Toy Fair 2026 is one of the most awaited events in the toy industry. It brings together toy makers, retailers, and fans. Knowing the exact dates helps everyone plan ahead. This year, the event will take place in February, as usual. It offers a chance to see new toys and trends early. Here are the important dates and details for 2026.

### Official Event Schedule

The New York Toy Fair 2026 runs from February 17 to February 20. It starts on a Monday and ends on a Thursday. Each day has special activities and showcases. Exhibitors set up their booths before the opening. The event features product launches and demonstrations. Visitors can explore the latest toys and meet creators.

### Key Dates For Exhibitors

Exhibitors must arrive by February 15 to set up. Setup days are February 15 and 16. Booths must be ready before the event opens. Exhibitors will also attend special meetings and workshops. The last day for exhibitors to pack up is February 20 after closing.

### Visitor Access Days

Visitors can enter the fair from February 17 to 20. Each day offers new displays and activities. Tickets are required for entry and should be purchased early. The fair is open to the public and industry professionals. Visitors can meet toy makers and see new products first hand.
