Start by unpacking your inflatable on a flat, clean surface. Remove any sharp objects nearby that could cause damage.

### Water Connection Options

Your dragon sprinkler connects easily to most garden hoses. Choose the best water source near your setup area.

Use a hose with a good water flow to keep the sprinkler spraying well. Check the hose for leaks before connecting.

* Attach hose to the sprinkler’s water inlet
* Use a hose splitter for multiple sprinklers
* Turn water on slowly to avoid pressure shock
* Check water spray pattern after turning on

Place your inflatable on soft grass or a smooth surface. Avoid rough or rocky ground to protect the material.

## Maintenance And Storage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-outdoor-dragon-sprinkler-inflatable-clean-and-well-stored-helps-it-last-longer.-proper-care-prevents-damage-and-keeps-it-ready-for-fun.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Outdoor Dragon Sprinkler Inflatable clean and well-stored helps it last longer. Proper care prevents damage and keeps it ready for fun.

Follow simple steps for cleaning, deflating, and storing your inflatable safely after each use.

### Cleaning After Use

Rinse the inflatable with clean water to remove dirt and grass. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the material.

* Use a soft cloth or sponge to wipe the surface gently.
* Check for stuck debris in seams and corners.
* Let it air dry completely before packing.

### Deflating And Packing

Deflate the sprinkler slowly to avoid damage. Press the air out evenly, starting from the far end.

1. Close all air valves tightly after deflation.
2. Fold the inflatable carefully, avoiding sharp creases.
3. Roll it up gently to save space.
4. Place it in the original storage bag or a dry container.

### Seasonal Storage Tips

Store your inflatable in a dry, cool place during off-season months. Protect it from sunlight and moisture.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Storage Condition** | **Reason** |
| Cool temperature (below 77°F / 25°C) | Prevents material from weakening or melting |
| Dry and ventilated area | Stops mold and mildew growth |
| Away from direct sunlight | Reduces color fading and material damage |
| Off the ground if possible | Avoids pests and moisture buildup |
