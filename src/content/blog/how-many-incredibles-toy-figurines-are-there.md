Understanding the materials and sizes helps you pick the right figurine for display or play. Below, we explore the key types you will find.

### Plastic Vs. Vinyl Figures

Incredibles figurines are mainly made from plastic or vinyl. Each material has its own qualities and uses in toy making.

* **Plastic figures**are hard and durable. They hold fine details well and often have movable parts.
* **Vinyl figures**are softer and lighter. They usually come in larger sizes and have a smooth finish.
* Plastic is better for action figures that require poseability.
* Vinyl is popular for collectible display models that focus on appearance.

### Miniature Vs. Action Figure Sizes

Incredibles toys vary from small miniatures to larger action figures. Size affects how you can use or display the figurines.

| Size Type | Height Range | Common Features |
