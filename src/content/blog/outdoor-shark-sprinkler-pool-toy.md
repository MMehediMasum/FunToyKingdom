## Maintenance And Storage

Taking care of your Outdoor Shark Sprinkler Pool Toy helps it last longer. Proper maintenance keeps it clean and safe for kids to play with.

Storing the toy correctly prevents damage and makes it easy to use again next season. Follow these tips for cleaning, winterizing, and storage.

### Cleaning Tips

Clean the toy after each use to remove dirt and prevent mold. Use mild soap and water for best results.

* Rinse the toy with clean water to remove debris.

* Check the sprinkler holes and clear any blockages.

### Winterizing The Toy

Protect the toy from freezing temperatures by draining all water before cold weather arrives. This prevents cracks and damage.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Action | Reason |
| Drain all water | Prevents freezing damage |
| Dry thoroughly | Stops mold and mildew growth |
| Store indoors | Keeps toy safe from weather |

### Storage Solutions

Store the toy in a dry, shaded place to avoid sun damage and dirt buildup. Keep it away from sharp objects.

**Storage Tips:**

* Use a large storage bin with a lid to keep the toy clean.
* Keep the toy folded or rolled to save space.
* Place silica gel packs nearby to absorb moisture.
* Check the toy monthly during storage for any damage.
