## Choosing The Right Cardboard

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-handmade-cardboard-toy-animals-starts-with-picking-the-right-cardboard.-the-type-you-choose-affects-how-strong-and-easy-your-toy-will-be.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making handmade cardboard toy animals starts with picking the right cardboard. The type you choose affects how strong and easy your toy will be.

Not all cardboard is the same. Some kinds are thick and sturdy, while others are thin and flexible. Knowing the differences helps you create better toys.

### Types Of Cardboard

Cardboard comes in many types. Each type suits different toy projects. Here are some common kinds:

* **Corrugated cardboard:**Has three layers with a wavy middle. It is strong and good for big toys.
* **Chipboard:**A single thick layer. It is smooth and easy to cut for detailed parts.
* **Paperboard:**Thin and light. Best for small or delicate toy pieces.
* **Recycled cardboard:**Made from used cardboard. Eco-friendly but sometimes less sturdy.

### Recycling And Upcycling Ideas

Using recycled cardboard is good for the planet and your wallet. You can turn old boxes into fun toy animals. Here are ideas to reuse cardboard:

1. Collect clean cardboard from food or shipping boxes.
2. Cut shapes and parts carefully for your toy design.
3. Paint or decorate with scraps of paper or fabric.
4. Glue pieces together to build your animal.
5. Use leftover cardboard for extra details or accessories.

| Cardboard Type | Best Use | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corrugated | Large, sturdy toys | High |
| Chipboard | Detailed parts | Medium |
| Paperboard | Small, light pieces | Low |
| Recycled | Eco-friendly projects | Varies |

## Step-by-step Toy Animal Ideas

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=creating-toy-animals-from-cardboard-is-fun-and-easy.-you-can-make-many-animals-using-simple-shapes.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Creating toy animals from cardboard is fun and easy. You can make many animals using simple shapes.

This guide shows how to build a cardboard elephant, a lion with a mane, and a colorful bird. Each project uses common materials.

### Making A Cardboard Elephant

Start by cutting large pieces of cardboard for the elephant’s body. Use smaller pieces for the trunk and ears. Glue the parts together firmly.

1. Cut two large rectangles for the body.
2. Shape and attach a long trunk piece.
3. Make big floppy ears from thin cardboard.
4. Draw eyes and details with markers.
5. Let the glue dry before playing.

### Crafting A Lion With Mane

The lion needs a round head and a thick mane. Use strips of cardboard or paper for the mane. Attach them around the head shape.

| Material | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Cardboard | Body and head |
| Paper strips | Mane |
| Glue | Stick parts together |
| Markers | Draw face details |

Glue mane strips carefully to look full. Add eyes and nose with markers for a friendly face.

### Building A Colorful Bird

Use bright colored paper or paint to decorate the bird’s body. Cut wings and tail feathers from cardboard. Glue them in place.

* Shape the body with folded cardboard.
* Attach wings on each side.
* Make tail feathers with different colors.
* Draw eyes and beak clearly.
* Let everything dry well.
