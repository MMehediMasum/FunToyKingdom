### How Do Toy Story Toys Support Sensory Play?



Toy Story toys like the Fidget Toy and crinkle books offer tactile, auditory, and visual stimulation. These features help develop sensory processing skills in babies and toddlers. Toys with music, flashing lights, and different textures engage young children effectively.
