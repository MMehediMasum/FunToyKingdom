## Preservation Techniques

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collecting-star-wars-toy-figurines-is-a-fun-hobby.-keeping-them-in-good-shape-keeps-their-value-and-look.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collecting Star Wars toy figurines is a fun hobby. Keeping them in good shape keeps their value and look.

Using the right preservation techniques helps protect your figurines from damage over time.

### Storage Best Practices

Store figurines in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Light can fade colors and damage plastic.

Use display cases or shelves with dust covers. Keep them upright and avoid stacking to prevent warping.

* Keep away from heat sources like radiators
* Use acid-free boxes or containers for long-term storage
* Keep figurines separated using soft padding
* Store in a room with stable temperature and humidity

### Cleaning And Maintenance

Clean your figurines gently using a soft brush or cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage paint.

For tough dirt, use mild soap with water on a damp cloth. Dry figurines fully before storing again.

* Remove dust regularly to prevent buildup
* Handle figurines with clean hands or gloves
* Check for loose parts and tighten gently
* Avoid soaking figurines in water

### Avoiding Common Damages

Keep figurines away from direct sunlight to prevent fading and cracking. Heat can also warp plastic parts.

Do not drop or apply pressure on your figurines. Fragile parts like weapons and joints break easily.

* Store away from pets and small children
* Avoid exposure to moisture to stop mold and rust
* Use proper supports to prevent falling
* Keep figurines in original packaging if possible
