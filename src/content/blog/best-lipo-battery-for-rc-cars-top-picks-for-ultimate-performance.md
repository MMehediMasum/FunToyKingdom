## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Lipo Battery Capacity For Rc Cars?



A 5200mAh capacity like Zeee or TEKKPOW batteries offers long run time. It suits 1/8 to 1/10 scale RC cars well. Higher capacity means longer play but more weight.



### How Do Battery Connectors Affect Rc Car Compatibility?



Connectors like T, XT60, Dean-style, or EC5 ensure proper fit. Choose batteries with connectors matching your RC car model for safe use and optimal power transfer.



### Are 2s Or 3s Lipo Batteries Better For Rc Cars?



2S (7. 4V) batteries suit beginners and smaller RC cars. 3S (11. 1V) deliver higher speed and power for advanced users and larger models. Match battery voltage with your car’s requirements.



### How Important Is The Discharge Rate (c Rating) In Lipo Batteries?



Higher C rating means faster current delivery. Batteries with 50C to 80C like OVONIC or TEKKPOW are ideal for high-performance RC cars. It ensures better acceleration and power without damage.



### Can I Use Li-ion Batteries Instead Of Lipo In Rc Cars?



Li-ion batteries like Tosiicop 7. 4V 1000mAh offer good capacity but lower discharge rates. Lipo batteries provide better power and lighter weight, preferred for racing and high-speed RC cars.



### What Chargers Are Compatible With Lipo Batteries For Rc Cars?



USB chargers with 1 to 2 port options, like those bundled with Tosiicop or VICMILE batteries, are convenient. Always use chargers designed for your battery type to avoid damage or fire hazards.
