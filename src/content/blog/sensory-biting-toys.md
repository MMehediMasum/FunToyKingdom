## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Sensory Chew Toys For?



Sensory chew toys help children with autism, ADHD, and SPD. They provide oral stimulation, reduce anxiety, and improve focus. Chewing can also aid in calming and self-regulation. These toys are made of safe, durable materials and are designed to withstand continuous use.



### How Do Chew Necklaces Benefit Children?



Chew necklaces offer a discreet way to manage sensory needs. They reduce stress and anxiety through oral stimulation. These necklaces are portable and easy to wear, making them perfect for on-the-go relief. They help children focus and decrease unwanted biting behaviors.



### Are Silicone Chew Toys Safe For Kids?



Yes, silicone chew toys are safe for kids. They are made from food-grade, non-toxic silicone. These toys are free from harmful chemicals like BPA, phthalates, and lead. Always supervise children during use to ensure safety and proper use of the toys.



### Can Adults Use Sensory Chew Toys?



Yes, adults can use sensory chew toys. They help reduce anxiety and improve focus. Many adults find them useful for managing stress and sensory needs. They are designed to be discreet and effective for individuals of all ages.



### How Do Chew Toys Support Oral Motor Skills?



Chew toys enhance oral motor skills by providing resistance and strengthening jaw muscles. They improve biting and chewing abilities. Regular use can aid in speech development and coordination. These toys offer a safe outlet for oral sensory exploration.



### What Features To Look For In Chew Toys?



Look for non-toxic, durable materials like food-grade silicone. Ensure the toy is easy to clean and dishwasher-safe. Consider the design for portability and discreet use. Choose varying textures for different sensory experiences, and ensure it's suitable for your child’s chewing needs.
