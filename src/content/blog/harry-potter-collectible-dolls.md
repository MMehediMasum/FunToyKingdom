Authentic dolls come from official manufacturers. Checking authenticity helps avoid fake or copied dolls.

Look for official logos, quality marks, and certificates that prove the doll is real.

* Official Harry Potter or Warner Bros. logos
* Manufacturer’s name and serial numbers
* High-quality materials and paint
* Original packaging and certificates

## Caring For Dolls

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=harry-potter-collectible-dolls-are-special-and-need-careful-care.-proper-care-helps-keep-them-nice-for-a-long-time.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Harry Potter collectible dolls are special and need careful care. Proper care helps keep them nice for a long time.

Taking good care of your dolls keeps their colors bright and parts strong. This guide shows easy ways to care for them.

### Storage Tips

Store your dolls in a clean, dry place away from direct sunlight. Sunlight can fade their colors and damage materials.

Use boxes or display cases to protect dolls from dust and dirt. Keep them standing or lying flat to avoid damage.

* Keep dolls away from heat and moisture
* Use soft cloth or tissue paper to wrap dolls
* Store dolls separately to avoid scratches
* Keep away from pets and small children

### Cleaning Methods

Clean your dolls gently to avoid damage. Use a soft brush or cloth to remove dust from the surface.

For plastic parts, use a damp cloth with mild soap. Do not soak dolls or use harsh chemicals on them.

* Brush dust off regularly
* Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap
* Dry dolls fully before storing
* Avoid water on cloth parts or hair

### Repairing Damages

Fix small damages quickly to keep dolls in good shape. Use glue made for plastic if parts come loose.

For loose hair or fabric tears, gently sew or glue with fabric-safe glue. Avoid using too much glue to keep it neat.

* Use plastic glue for broken parts
* Sew or glue fabric tears carefully
* Handle repairs gently to avoid more damage
* Ask a professional for big repairs
