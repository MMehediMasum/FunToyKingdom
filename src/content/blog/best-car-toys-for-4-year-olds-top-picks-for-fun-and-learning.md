Yes, pull-back cars are safe if made from non-toxic materials and without small detachable parts. They are easy to use and help develop fine motor skills. Always check age recommendations on packaging for safety.



### How Do Remote Control Cars Benefit 4 Year Olds?



Remote control cars improve hand-eye coordination and focus. They encourage outdoor play and active movement. Models designed for ages 4 to 7, like 4WD RC stunt cars, are durable and simple to operate.
