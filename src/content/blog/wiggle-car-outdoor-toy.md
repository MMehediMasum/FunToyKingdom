## What Is A Wiggle Car

The Wiggle Car is a fun outdoor toy for kids. It lets children move without pedals or batteries. Kids use their body to make the car wiggle forward.

This toy helps improve balance and coordination. It is easy to use and works on smooth surfaces like pavement or floors.

### Design And Features

The Wiggle Car has a simple but clever design. It usually has a seat and a steering wheel. The body is made from strong plastic for safety.

* Lightweight but sturdy frame
* Non-slip seat for comfort
* Easy-to-grip steering wheel
* Four wheels for stability
* Bright colors and fun shapes

### How It Moves

The car moves when the rider wiggles the steering wheel side to side. This motion causes the wheels to turn and the car to roll forward.
