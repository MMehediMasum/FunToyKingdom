Setting up your Outdoor Turtle Sprinkler Inflatable is quick and easy. This guide helps you get it ready to use in no time.

Follow simple steps to inflate the turtle and connect it to your water source safely.

### Easy Inflation Steps

Inflating your turtle is simple. Use the air valve and a pump or your breath to fill it with air.

1. Find the air valve on the turtle’s body.
2. Open the valve cap carefully.
3. Use a hand pump or electric pump to inflate the turtle.
4. Fill the turtle until it is firm but not overinflated.
5. Close the valve cap tightly to prevent air leaks.

### Connecting To Water Source

Attach the sprinkler hose to your garden hose or faucet. This lets water flow through the turtle’s sprinkler system.

### How Do I Set Up The Turtle Sprinkler Inflatable?

Setup is simple. Connect the inflatable’s hose attachment to your garden hose. Inflate the turtle using a pump or air compressor. Turn on the water supply, and the sprinkler will spray water from the turtle’s shell or head automatically.

### Is The Turtle Sprinkler Inflatable Safe For Kids?

Yes, it is designed with child safety in mind. The inflatable is made from non-toxic, durable materials. It sprays gentle water streams, avoiding high pressure that could harm children. Always supervise kids during water play for added safety.

### Can The Turtle Sprinkler Inflatable Be Used Indoors?

No, it is intended for outdoor use only. The sprinkler sprays water, which can cause indoor flooding and damage. Use it in your garden, backyard, or any outdoor space where water drainage is possible.
