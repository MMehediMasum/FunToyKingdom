## Maintenance And Care

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-good-care-of-your-outdoor-school-bus-sprinkler-toy-helps-it-last-longer.-regular-maintenance-keeps-it-working-well-and-safe-for-children.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking good care of your Outdoor School Bus Sprinkler Toy helps it last longer. Regular maintenance keeps it working well and safe for children.

Simple cleaning and proper storage prevent damage. Fix small problems early to avoid bigger issues later.

### Cleaning Guidelines

Clean the sprinkler toy after each use to remove dirt and debris. Use mild soap and water to avoid damage.

Rinse it well to remove all soap. Let it dry completely before storing to stop mold and mildew growth.

* Use a soft cloth or sponge for cleaning
* Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive brushes
* Check all water holes for blockages and clear them gently

### Storage Recommendations

Store the sprinkler toy in a cool, dry place when not in use. Avoid direct sunlight to prevent fading and cracking.

Drain all water from the toy before storing. Keep it away from sharp objects that could puncture the plastic.

* Choose indoor storage to protect from weather
* Keep away from pets and rodents
* Use a storage bin or bag to keep it clean

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the sprinkler stops working, first check the water supply and hose connection. Make sure water flows freely.

Look for cracks or leaks in the toy. Small holes can be patched with waterproof tape or sealant made for outdoor toys.

* Clear clogged sprinkler holes with a toothpick or pin
* Replace damaged parts if possible
* Contact a professional for serious repairs
