Rechargeable and automatic options offer hours of entertainment without constant human involvement. Different speeds and sounds can attract cats’ attention and suit various play styles. Choosing the right interactive cat toy ball supports your cat’s physical exercise and mental well-being. It creates a lively environment, especially for cats that spend most of their time indoors.
