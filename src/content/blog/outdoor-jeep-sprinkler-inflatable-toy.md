## Maintenance And Storage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=proper-care-helps-your-outdoor-jeep-sprinkler-inflatable-last-longer.-cleaning,-drying,-and-storing-it-right-keeps-it-safe-and-ready-to-use.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Proper care helps your outdoor jeep sprinkler inflatable last longer. Cleaning, drying, and storing it right keeps it safe and ready to use.

Follow simple steps after each use to protect the material and avoid damage.

### Cleaning After Use

Remove dirt, grass, and debris from the inflatable surface. Use a soft cloth and mild soap with water to clean it gently. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the fabric.

* Turn off the water supply before cleaning.
* Wipe all surfaces with soap and a soft cloth.
* Rinse thoroughly to remove soap residue.
* Check all seams and valves for dirt.

### Proper Drying Techniques

Dry the inflatable completely before storage. Moisture can cause mold and mildew, which weaken the material and cause bad smells.

1. Deflate the toy slowly to release air.
2. Spread it out flat in a shaded area.
3. Use towels to absorb excess water.
4. Let it air dry for several hours.
5. Check for damp spots and dry again if needed.

### Safe Storage Practices

Store the jeep sprinkler in a dry, cool place away from direct sunlight. Proper storage protects the inflatable from tears and fading.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Tip | Reason |
| Keep away from sharp objects | Prevents punctures and tears |
| Store in a breathable bag | Allows air circulation to prevent mold |
| Avoid extreme temperatures | Prevents material from cracking or warping |
| Do not fold tightly | Reduces stress on seams and fabric |
