## Setting Up The Sprinkler

Setting up your outdoor goose sprinkler inflatable toy is easy and fun. Follow simple steps to get it ready for play.

### Connecting To Water Source

Find a garden hose or outdoor faucet to connect your sprinkler. Use the right adapter to fit the hose nozzle.

Secure the hose tightly to avoid water leaks. Turn on the water slowly to check the flow and pressure.

* Use a standard garden hose for best results
* Check for leaks at connection points
* Adjust water pressure to control spray intensity

### Inflation Tips

Use an electric air pump or a manual pump to inflate the goose sprinkler. Avoid overinflating to prevent damage.

Check all air valves to make sure they are closed tightly after inflation. This keeps the toy firm and stable.

* Inflate on a flat surface free of sharp objects
* Do not use high pressure air pumps
* Close all valves firmly after inflating

### Placement For Maximum Fun

Place the inflatable goose on a soft, flat area like grass. This helps keep it steady during use.

Make sure the sprinkler spray reaches an open space. Avoid placing near delicate plants or electrical equipment.

* Choose a shady spot to keep the toy cool
