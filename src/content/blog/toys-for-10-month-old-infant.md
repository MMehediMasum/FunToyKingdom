## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Toys Are Best For 10-month-old Infants?



Stacking toys, sensory Montessori sets, and interactive keys are ideal. They promote motor skills and sensory development for 10-month-olds.



### How Do Montessori Toys Benefit 10-month Babies?



Montessori toys encourage exploration and fine motor skills. They help infants learn shapes, textures, and cause-effect relationships naturally.



### Are Teething Toys Safe For 10-month-old Infants?



Yes, pull-string silicone teething toys are safe and soothing. They relieve discomfort while supporting oral development and grasping skills.



### Which Toys Improve Hand-eye Coordination In Infants?



Stacking rings, shape sorters, and grasping hammers enhance hand-eye coordination. These toys foster precision and spatial awareness in babies.



### What Interactive Toys Engage 10-month-old Infants?



Toy car keys with lights and sounds capture attention. They stimulate auditory and visual senses, encouraging pretend play and curiosity.



### Can Activity Centers Support Infant Development At 10 Months?



Yes, sturdy activity centers offer multi-sensory learning. They build motor skills, problem-solving, and keep babies entertained safely.
