## Caring For Your Collection

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collecting-marvel-superhero-figurines-is-fun-and-rewarding.-keeping-them-in-good-shape-keeps-their-value-and-charm.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collecting Marvel superhero figurines is fun and rewarding. Keeping them in good shape keeps their value and charm.

Proper care helps your toys look their best for a long time. Small habits make a big difference.

### Proper Display Techniques

Show your figurines in places with low sunlight. Too much light can fade their colors.

Use shelves or cases to keep toys safe from dust and damage. Keep them away from heat sources.

* Place figurines on sturdy shelves
* Use glass display cases to reduce dust
* Avoid direct sunlight to prevent fading
* Keep away from heaters and vents

### Cleaning And Maintenance Tips

Clean your figurines regularly with a soft brush or cloth. This removes dust without scratching.

Use mild soap and water for tougher dirt. Dry toys fully before putting them back on display.

* Use a soft-bristle brush for dust
* Wipe with a damp cloth for sticky spots
* Avoid harsh chemicals or cleaners
* Dry completely to avoid moisture damage

### Storage Solutions For Protection

Store figurines in boxes with padding to stop scratches. Use bubble wrap or foam for extra safety.

Keep storage areas cool and dry. Avoid places with high humidity to prevent mold and warping.

* Use individual padded boxes for each figurine
* Store in a cool, dry place
* Keep away from areas prone to moisture
* Label boxes for easy finding
