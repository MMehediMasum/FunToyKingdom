## Display And Storage Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=captain-america-toy-soldier-style-action-figures-look-great-on-display.-proper-display-and-storage-keep-them-safe-and-neat.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Captain America toy soldier style action figures look great on display. Proper display and storage keep them safe and neat.

Use smart ways to show off your collection and keep the figures in good shape for a long time.

### Showcasing Your Collection

Place your Captain America figures where light is soft and not direct. This stops colors from fading.

Use shelves with glass doors to protect from dust. Keep figures on stands to avoid falling over.

* Choose a spot with low sunlight
* Use display cases with clear doors
* Arrange figures by size or pose
* Keep stands or mounts for stability
* Leave space between figures for viewing

### Preserving Condition

Store action figures away from heat and moisture. These can cause damage or warping over time.

Wrap figures in soft cloth or bubble wrap when not on display. Use airtight containers to avoid dust and bugs.

* Avoid placing near heaters or windows
* Keep in a dry room with stable temperature
* Use soft materials to wrap figures
* Store in plastic boxes with lids
* Check figures regularly for damage
