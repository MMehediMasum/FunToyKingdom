## Maintenance Tips For Longevity

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-outdoor-monster-truck-sprinkler-toy-in-good-shape-helps-it-last-longer.-regular-care-stops-damage-and-keeps-it-working-well.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Outdoor Monster Truck Sprinkler Toy in good shape helps it last longer. Regular care stops damage and keeps it working well.

Simple steps like cleaning and proper storage protect your toy from wear and tear. Follow these tips to enjoy it for many seasons.

### Cleaning And Storage Advice

After playing, clean the toy to remove dirt and grass. Use a soft cloth and mild soap to wipe it down. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the plastic.

Dry the toy completely before storing it. Moisture can cause mold and weaken parts. Store the toy in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

* Rinse off dirt and debris
* Use gentle soap and water
* Dry fully before storing
* Keep it indoors or in a shaded area

### Preventing Damage In Harsh Weather

Strong sun, wind, and rain can harm the sprinkler toy. Protect it by bringing it inside during storms or extreme heat.

If you leave it outside, cover the toy with a waterproof tarp. This keeps water and dirt from causing damage. Check it regularly for cracks or leaks.

* Bring the toy indoors during bad weather
* Use a waterproof cover when outside
* Inspect for damage after storms
* Fix small cracks quickly to prevent bigger problems
