## Tips For Maintaining Wooden Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=wooden-toys-are-strong-and-safe-for-children.-they-last-a-long-time-if-you-care-for-them-well.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Wooden toys are strong and safe for children. They last a long time if you care for them well.

Good maintenance keeps toys clean and looking nice. It also helps avoid damage and wear.



### Cleaning And Care

Clean wooden toys regularly to remove dirt and germs. Use a soft cloth with mild soap and water.

Do not soak wooden toys in water. This can cause the wood to crack or warp over time.

* Wipe toys with a damp cloth, then dry quickly.
* Use natural oils like mineral oil to keep wood smooth.
* Avoid harsh cleaners that remove the wood’s finish.

### Storage Suggestions

Store wooden toys in a dry place away from direct sunlight. Sunlight can fade the colors and dry out the wood.

Keep toys off the floor to avoid moisture and dust buildup. Use shelves or storage bins with lids.

* Store toys in a cool, dry room.
* Use breathable fabric bags or wooden boxes.
* Do not keep toys in plastic bags for long periods.

### Repairing Minor Damages

Small cracks or chips can be fixed easily. Use child-safe wood glue for repairs.

Sand rough spots gently with fine sandpaper. This keeps toys smooth and safe to handle.

* Apply wood glue to cracks and clamp until dry.
* Sand damaged areas to remove splinters.
* Reapply natural oil to protect repaired spots.
