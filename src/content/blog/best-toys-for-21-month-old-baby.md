## Choosing Safe Toys

Choosing safe toys is important for a 21 month old baby. Parents need to pick toys that protect babies from harm.

Safe toys help babies learn and play without risks. Always check the toy’s material, size, and parts.

### Material And Durability

Toys for young babies should be strong and made from safe materials. Durable toys last longer and do not break easily.

* Choose toys made from solid wood or thick plastic.
* Avoid toys with sharp edges or small parts that can break off.
* Check if the toy’s paint is lead-free and non-toxic.
* Look for toys that can be cleaned easily to keep them hygienic.

### Size And Choking Hazards

Small toys or parts can cause choking. It is important to pick toys that fit the baby’s age and mouth size.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Size | Safe for 21 Month Old |
| Toy length | At least 4 inches |
| Small parts | None smaller than 1.25 inches in diameter |
| Loose pieces | Should be avoided |

### Non-toxic Components

Toys should have no harmful chemicals. Babies often put toys in their mouths, so non-toxic parts are a must.

Look for these labels on toy packaging:

* “BPA-free”
* “Phthalate-free”
* “Lead-free paint”
* “Meets safety standards”

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Are The Best Toys For 21-month-old Babies?

The best toys encourage sensory exploration, fine motor skills, and creativity. Look for stacking blocks, shape sorters, and interactive plush toys. These help toddlers develop coordination and cognitive skills while keeping them engaged and entertained safely.

### How Do Toys Help 21-month-olds Develop Skills?

Toys at this age promote hand-eye coordination, problem-solving, and language development. Interactive and cause-effect toys boost curiosity and learning. They also support social skills through pretend play and sharing, helping toddlers grow in multiple areas simultaneously.

### Are Educational Toys Suitable For 21-month-old Babies?

Yes, educational toys are ideal for this age. They stimulate cognitive growth and early learning. Choose toys with bright colors, numbers, letters, or simple puzzles. These engage toddlers and prepare them for future learning milestones effectively.

### What Safety Features Should Toys Have For 21-month-olds?

Toys should be non-toxic, free of small parts, and sturdy. Avoid sharp edges and choking hazards. Look for toys labeled safe for toddlers, ensuring they meet safety standards and provide worry-free playtime for both parents and babies.
