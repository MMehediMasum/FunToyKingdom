## Safety Tips For Outdoor Play

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=playing-with-a-foam-rocket-launcher-is-fun-but-needs-care.-safety-keeps-the-play-safe-and-happy.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Playing with a foam rocket launcher is fun but needs care. Safety keeps the play safe and happy. Follow these tips to enjoy outdoor play safely.

Understanding safe areas, handling, and supervision helps prevent accidents. Use these safety tips every time you play.

### Choosing A Safe Launch Area

Pick a wide open space for launching foam rockets. Avoid places near roads, windows, or breakable objects. Clear the area of obstacles before play.

* Launch in parks, backyards, or open fields
* Stay away from trees, power lines, and cars
* Keep at least 10 feet away from other people
* Check the ground for rocks or holes
* Never launch indoors or near fragile items

### Proper Handling Techniques

Handle the foam rocket launcher carefully to avoid injury. Follow these simple rules every time you use it.

| **Do** | **Don't** |
| --- | --- |
| Keep fingers clear of the launch tube | Point launcher at people or animals |
| Use soft foam rockets only | Modify launcher or rockets |
| Launch rockets one at a time | Fire near faces or eyes |
| Store launcher safely after use | Leave launcher unattended with children |

### Supervision Guidelines

Adults should watch children while they play. Supervision helps stop unsafe actions and keeps play fun.

1. Always have an adult nearby during play
2. Explain safety rules before starting
3. Stop play if someone uses the launcher unsafely
4. Check that all players understand how to use the toy
5. Keep a first aid kit close by just in case
