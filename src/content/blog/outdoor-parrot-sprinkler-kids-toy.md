## Safety Tips For Water Play

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=water-play-is-fun-and-helps-kids-stay-active-during-warm-days.-using-the-outdoor-parrot-sprinkler-kids-toy-can-add-joy-to-outdoor-time.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Water play is fun and helps kids stay active during warm days. Using the Outdoor Parrot Sprinkler Kids Toy can add joy to outdoor time.

It is important to keep safety in mind to prevent accidents and keep children healthy. Follow these tips for safe water play.

### Supervision Guidelines

Always watch children closely while they play with water toys. Never leave them unattended, even for a short time. Adults should stay near and be ready to help if needed.

### Slip And Fall Prevention

Wet surfaces can be slippery and cause falls. Use these tips to keep kids safe around water play areas.

* Place the sprinkler on flat, soft ground like grass.
* Remove any sharp or hard objects nearby.
* Encourage children to walk, not run, near the sprinkler.
* Check shoes for good grip or let kids play barefoot on soft surfaces.

### Hydration And Sun Protection

Kids can get thirsty and sunburned quickly during water play. Keep them safe with proper hydration and sun care.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Tip | Details |
| Hydration | Give water breaks every 20 minutes or more often. |
| Sun Protection | Apply sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher before play. |
| Shade | Set up a shaded area for rest and cooling off. |
| Clothing | Use hats and light, long sleeves to protect skin. |
