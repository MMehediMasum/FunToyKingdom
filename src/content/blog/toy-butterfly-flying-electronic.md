## How To Use Toy Butterfly Flyers

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=toy-butterfly-flyers-are-fun-electronic-toys-that-mimic-real-butterflies.-they-fly-around-using-small-propellers-hidden-in-their-wings.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Toy butterfly flyers are fun electronic toys that mimic real butterflies. They fly around using small propellers hidden in their wings.

Using these toys is simple if you follow some basic steps. This guide will help you charge, fly, and care for your butterfly flyer.

### Charging And Setup

Before flying, fully charge the butterfly flyer using the included USB cable. Charging usually takes about 30 minutes.

After charging, gently attach the butterfly to the launcher or your finger. Make sure the wings can move freely.

* Connect USB cable to a power source
* Charge until indicator light shows full
* Attach butterfly securely to launcher or finger
* Check wings for any blockages

### Flying Tips And Tricks

Hold the launcher or finger steady when you release the butterfly. A smooth release helps the flyer take off well.

Fly indoors or in calm weather outdoors. Wind can affect the butterfly’s flight and make it hard to control.

* Release butterfly with a gentle push
* Fly in open spaces free of obstacles
* Avoid flying near fans or open windows
* Practice timing your releases for longer flight

### Maintenance And Care

Keep the butterfly flyer clean and dry to protect its electronics. Wipe the wings with a soft cloth after use.

Store the toy in a safe place to avoid damage to the wings or motor. Avoid dropping or squeezing the flyer.

* Clean wings with a dry, soft cloth
* Do not use water or cleaning sprays
* Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sun
* Handle the butterfly gently to avoid breaking parts
