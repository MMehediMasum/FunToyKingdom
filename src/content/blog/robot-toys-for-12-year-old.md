Robot toys should match the child’s age to keep them safe and interested. Toys made for younger kids might be too simple.

At 12 years old, kids can handle more detailed and challenging robots. Look for toys labeled for ages 10 and up.

* Find robots that encourage creativity and learning

### Skill Level And Complexity

Consider how much skill the child has with robot toys. Some robots need coding or building skills.

Start with simple robots if the child is new to these toys. More complex kits suit kids who like challenges.

* Simple remote-controlled robots for beginners
* Robots with coding functions for tech-savvy kids
* **[Building kits](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=robot+building+kits+for+kids&tag=gearcookware-20)** for hands-on learners

### Budget Considerations

Robot toys come in many price ranges. Set a budget before shopping to narrow your choices.

Expensive robots may have more features. Cheaper options can still be fun and educational.

* Decide how much you want to spend
* Look for good value, not just the lowest price
* Check for extra costs like batteries or apps
