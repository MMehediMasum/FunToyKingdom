### Setting Up A Creative Play Space

Choose a quiet and well-lit area for Lego play. Keep all blocks and pieces organized in containers or drawers. A clean space helps kids focus and find what they need quickly.

* Provide good lighting to avoid eye strain

* Include a comfortable chair for longer play
* Display completed projects to inspire

### Integrating Lego Challenges Into Daily Activities

Adding small Lego challenges during the day keeps kids curious and thinking. These tasks improve problem-solving and creativity.

1. Build a bridge that holds weight
