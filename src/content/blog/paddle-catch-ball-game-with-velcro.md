## Game Setup

Setting up the Paddle Catch Ball game with Velcro is easy and fun. The right materials and preparation make the game enjoyable for everyone.

This guide helps you get ready by focusing on materials, paddle preparation, and ball choice.

### Required Materials

* Two paddles with Velcro surfaces
* One lightweight ball that sticks to Velcro
* Flat playing area free of obstacles
* Optional: extra Velcro strips for paddle repairs

### Preparing The Velcro Paddles

Prepare the paddles by checking the Velcro surface. It must be clean and sticky to catch the ball well.

Follow these tips to prepare your paddles:

1. Remove any dirt or lint from the Velcro using a fine comb or brush.
2. Make sure the Velcro is firmly attached to the paddle base.
3. Replace worn-out Velcro strips with new ones if needed.
4. Test the paddle by catching a ball before playing.

Paddle Catch Ball with Velcro is a fun outdoor activity. Players use paddles to catch and throw a ball covered in Velcro.

Understanding the basic rules helps players enjoy the game fairly and safely.

### Starting The Game

The game begins with players standing opposite each other. Each player holds a paddle with Velcro on one side.

* A coin toss decides who throws first.
* The first player throws the ball toward the opponent’s paddle.
* Players must stay within the play area during the game.

### Scoring System

Points are scored when the ball sticks to the opponent’s paddle or when the opponent misses the catch.

| Action | Points Awarded |
| --- | --- |
| Ball sticks to paddle | 1 point |
| Opponent misses catch | 1 point |
| Ball hits ground before catch | 0 points |

### Winning Criteria

The game ends when a player reaches a set number of points or after a timed round.

1. The first player to reach 10 points wins.
2. If time runs out, the player with the highest score wins.
3. Ties can be broken by a sudden-death round.

## Maintenance And Care

Taking good care of your Paddle Catch Ball Game with Velcro will keep it fun to play. Proper maintenance helps the paddles last longer and work well.

Follow simple steps to clean, store, and replace parts when needed. This will keep the game in top shape for many uses.

### Cleaning Velcro Paddles

Clean the Velcro paddles often to remove dirt and dust. This keeps the Velcro sticky and ready to catch the ball.

* Use a soft brush to pull out loose dirt from the Velcro hooks.
* Press sticky tape lightly on the Velcro to lift small particles.
* Avoid washing the paddles with water to keep Velcro strong.
* Let paddles air dry if they get wet.

### Storage Tips

Store paddles and balls in a dry place away from heat. Proper storage stops damage and keeps parts ready to use.

| Storage Tip | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Keep paddles flat | Prevents bending and warping |
| Store balls separately | Stops balls from getting dirty or squashed |
| Avoid direct sunlight | Protects Velcro and materials from fading |
| Use a box or bag | Keeps all parts together and clean |

### Replacing Worn Parts

Check paddles and balls regularly for wear. Replace parts to keep the game safe and fun.

1. Inspect Velcro on paddles for loss of stickiness.
2. Look for cracks or tears on paddle handles.
3. Replace balls if they become soft or damaged.
4. Buy new Velcro strips or paddles if needed.
