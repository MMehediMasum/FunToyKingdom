## Assembly Process

### Step-by-step Building Guide

Follow these steps in order to build your robotic dinosaur correctly. Take your time and check each step before moving on.

1. Open the box and organize all parts on a clean surface.
2. Read the instruction manual carefully before starting.
3. Attach the main body parts using the provided screws.
4. Connect the motor units to the body following the diagrams.
5. Install the battery pack and secure it in place.
6. Attach the legs and tail for balance and movement.
7. Connect all wiring to the control board as shown.
8. Test the connections by powering on the robot.
9. Make any needed adjustments for smooth movement.
10. Close the body panels and tighten all screws.

### Tips For Beginners

New builders may find some parts tricky. These tips will help you avoid common mistakes and enjoy the process.

* Work in a well-lit and quiet area to see small parts clearly.
* Use the right tools, like a small screwdriver and tweezers.
* Keep all parts organized in separate containers.
* Follow the manual images closely for correct placement.
* Do not force parts; check if they fit smoothly first.
* Ask for help if you find a step confusing or hard.
* Take breaks to avoid frustration and mistakes.

### Common Challenges And Solutions

Some parts may not fit or work as expected. Use this table to find solutions to common issues during assembly.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem | Cause | Solution |
| Parts do not fit together | Wrong orientation | Check diagrams and rotate parts |
| Motor does not run | Loose wiring | Reconnect wires firmly |
| Robot wobbles when moving | Uneven leg attachment | Tighten screws and align legs |
| Battery pack won’t fit | Incorrect placement | Follow manual slot instructions |
| Control board not responding | Power issue | Check battery charge and connections |

## Maintenance And Care

Keeping your robotic dinosaur building kit in good shape helps it last longer. Regular care prevents damage and ensures smooth operation.

Follow these tips to clean, store, and fix common problems with your kit. Simple steps make a big difference.

### Cleaning Tips

Dust and dirt can harm the electronic parts and joints of your robotic dinosaur. Clean it gently and regularly to avoid issues.

* Use a soft, dry cloth to wipe the surface.
* For stubborn dirt, slightly dampen the cloth with water only.
* Avoid harsh chemicals or sprays that can damage plastic or electronics.
* Clean small crevices with a soft brush or cotton swab.
* Do not immerse any part in water.

### Storage Recommendations

Proper storage protects your robotic dinosaur from dust, moisture, and physical damage. Store it in a safe, dry place.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Tip | Reason |
| Keep away from direct sunlight | Prevents color fading and heat damage |
| Store in a closed box or container | Keeps out dust and dirt |
| Place on a flat, stable surface | Prevents falls and broken parts |
| Remove batteries if unused for a long time | Prevents battery leaks and corrosion |

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

Some problems can happen with your robotic dinosaur. Try these fixes before seeking help.

1. Check if batteries are inserted correctly and have power.
2. Make sure all parts are tightly connected and not loose.
3. Reset the robot by turning it off and on again.
4. Clean sensors gently if they are not responding.
5. Refer to the manual for error codes or unusual sounds.
