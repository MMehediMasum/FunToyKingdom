## Choosing Safe Toys

Choosing safe toys for a 19-month-old baby is very important. Toys help babies learn and grow. They also keep babies busy and happy.

Parents must check each toy carefully. Safety protects babies from harm and keeps playtime fun.



### Material Safety

Toys must be made from safe, strong materials. Avoid toys with small parts that can break off. Look for materials that are BPA-free and allergy-safe.

* Choose toys made from wood, hard plastic, or fabric
* Avoid cheap plastics that may contain harmful chemicals
* Check for labels that show the toy passed safety tests
* Do not buy toys with sharp edges or points

### Size And Shape Considerations

Size and shape matter for babies who put toys in their mouths. Toys must be large enough to prevent choking. Rounded edges are safer than sharp corners.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Feature** | **Recommended** | **Not Recommended** |
| Size | Larger than 1.25 inches diameter | Small pieces under 1.25 inches |
| Shape | Rounded edges and smooth surfaces | Sharp points or edges |
| Weight | Lightweight for easy handling | Heavy toys that can hurt |

### Non-toxic Finishes

Paint and finishes on toys must be safe if babies chew on them. Non-toxic finishes protect babies from harmful chemicals.

Look for these signs on toy packaging:

* Water-based paint
* Lead-free certification
* Safe for children under 3 years
* Non-toxic label

## Toy Maintenance Tips

Keeping your 19-month-old's toys clean and safe is crucial. Regular maintenance ensures your child plays in a healthy environment. Learn how to clean, store, and inspect toys effectively.



### Cleaning And Sanitizing

Regular cleaning keeps germs at bay. Use mild soap and water for most toys. For electronic toys, wipe with a damp cloth.

1. Wash plush toys in the washing machine.
2. Sanitize plastic toys with a vinegar solution.
3. Disinfect bath toys with bleach diluted in water.

### Storing Toys Safely

Proper storage prevents damage and keeps toys organized. Use [clear bins or baskets](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=clear+toy+storage+bins&tag=gearcookware-20) for easy access. Label containers to sort toys by type.

Consider using **child-proof latches**on storage areas to ensure safety.

### Inspecting For Wear And Tear

Regular inspection prevents accidents. Check for broken parts or sharp edges. Repair or discard damaged toys immediately.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Type of Toy | Inspection Frequency |
| Plush Toys | Monthly |
| Plastic Toys | Bi-weekly |
| Wooden Toys | Weekly |
