## Selecting Age-appropriate Toys

Choosing toys for 12-year-olds can be tricky. It's important to find the right balance between fun and safety. Kids at this age have diverse interests and skill levels.

Parents should consider toys that match their child's development. This ensures the toys are both engaging and appropriate.

### Safety Considerations

Safety is a top priority when selecting toys. It is crucial to ensure the toys are free from harmful materials and small parts.

* Check for non-toxic materials
* Avoid toys with sharp edges
* Ensure the toy is durable and sturdy
* Verify age recommendations on the packaging

### Skill Level Matching

Match the toy with the child's skill level. Toys that are too easy might get boring quickly, while those too difficult can cause frustration.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Skill Level | Recommended Toy Type |
| Beginner | Puzzles with large pieces |
| Intermediate | Construction sets |
| Advanced | Robotics kits |

### Interest And Preferences

Understanding what your child likes can help in picking the right toys. Kids are more likely to engage with toys that match their interests.

Consider toys that align with hobbies:

* For creative kids: art supplies and crafts
* For tech enthusiasts: beginner coding kits
* For active kids: indoor sports games
