## Preparing The Box

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=preparing-the-box-is-the-first-step-to-make-a-fun-toy-puppet-box.-you-need-a-box-that-is-easy-to-work-with-and-safe-for-kids.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Preparing the box is the first step to make a fun toy puppet box. You need a box that is easy to work with and safe for kids.

This guide helps you choose the right box and cut the puppet opening correctly. These steps make your craft look great.

### Choosing The Right Box

Pick a box that is strong and the right size for your puppet. A medium-sized box works well for most puppets.

Cardboard boxes are best because they are easy to cut and decorate. Avoid plastic or metal boxes as they are hard to work with.

* Choose a box big enough for your hand to fit inside
* Make sure the box has no sharp edges or damage
* Flat surfaces on the box make decorating easier
* Lightweight boxes are easier to carry and play with

### Cutting The Puppet Opening

Cut an opening for the puppet to come out. The opening should be big enough for your hand to move freely.

Use a pencil to draw the opening shape before cutting. A rectangle or oval shape works well for puppet openings.

* Measure your hand to get the right size
* Mark the shape on one side of the box
* Use scissors or a craft knife to cut carefully
* Smooth the edges with sandpaper or tape for safety

## Assembling The Puppet

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=assembling-your-diy-toy-puppet-box-is-fun-and-easy.-it-brings-your-puppet-to-life-for-play-and-shows.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Assembling your DIY toy puppet box is fun and easy. It brings your puppet to life for play and shows.

Follow simple steps to attach the puppet and add control parts. This helps you move the puppet smoothly.

### Attaching Puppets To Box

First, decide where to place your puppet on the box. The puppet should be secure but able to move.

Use glue or tape to fix the puppet’s base to the box. Make sure it holds well and does not fall off.

* Pick a flat spot on the box for the puppet
* Use strong glue or double-sided tape
* Press firmly and let it dry well
* Check that the puppet can move without falling

### Adding Strings Or Rods

Strings or rods help you control the puppet’s movements. Choose what works best for your puppet style.

Attach strings to the puppet’s limbs or rods under the box. Make sure the strings or rods are easy to pull or push.

* Use strong thread or thin sticks for strings or rods
* Tie strings to arms, legs, or head of the puppet
* Secure rods under the box with tape or glue
* Test movement to see if it is smooth and easy

## Tips For Safe Crafting

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-a-diy-toy-puppet-box-is-fun-and-creative.-safety-is-important-to-enjoy-crafting-without-accidents.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making a DIY toy puppet box is fun and creative. Safety is important to enjoy crafting without accidents.

Simple safety steps help protect you and children during the craft. Follow these tips to stay safe.

### Handling Tools Carefully

Use scissors, glue guns, and knives with care. Always hold tools properly and cut away from your body.

Keep your workspace clean to avoid accidents. Turn off electrical tools after use and store them safely.

* Use child-safe scissors for kids
* Keep sharp tools out of children’s reach
* Wear protective gloves if needed
* Work on a flat, stable surface
* Never run with scissors or sharp tools

### Supervising Kids

Always watch children while they craft. Help them use tools safely and guide their actions.

Set rules before starting. Explain what is safe and what to avoid during the craft activity.

* Stay close to children at all times
* Explain tool dangers simply
* Encourage asking for help
* Limit use of sharp or hot tools
* Make sure kids take breaks
