## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Is Best For Motor Skill Toys Like Stacking Blocks?



Motor skill toys like stacking blocks are ideal for infants aged 6 months and older. They help develop hand-eye coordination and fine motor skills during early growth stages. These toys engage babies in sorting, grasping, and stacking activities, enhancing cognitive and sensory development effectively.



### How Do Sensory Toys Support Infant Motor Skill Development?



Sensory toys stimulate infants’ senses, encouraging exploration and movement. They improve fine motor skills by promoting grasping, squeezing, and manipulation. Bright colors and varied textures engage babies, fostering hand dexterity and coordination, which are essential for motor skill progression in infants aged 6 to 18 months.



### Are Teething Toys Beneficial For Motor Skills In Infants?



Yes, teething toys like silicone rings help infants develop fine motor skills. They encourage grasping and hand-mouth coordination while soothing sore gums. Teethers also promote sensory exploration, making them perfect for infants aged 0 to 24 months who are developing their motor abilities.



### What Makes Pull-string Toys Good For Toddler Development?



Pull-string toys encourage toddlers to use their hands and arms actively. They develop gross motor skills through pulling and walking motions. These toys also enhance coordination and balance, making them excellent for infants aged 8 to 18 months learning to move independently.



### Why Are Shape Sorter Toys Recommended For Infants?



Shape sorter toys boost problem-solving and hand-eye coordination in infants. They teach shape recognition and improve fine motor skills through grasping and fitting shapes. These toys are suitable for babies aged 12 months and above, aiding cognitive and physical development simultaneously.
