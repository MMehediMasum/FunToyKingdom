Trivial Pursuit has been a favorite board game for decades. Its classic editions bring fun and challenge to families and friends. These editions focus on general knowledge and test players in many categories.

Understanding the classic editions helps appreciate why the game remains popular. They offer a mix of history, entertainment, and learning that many enjoy.

### Original Game Highlights

The first Trivial Pursuit game appeared in 1981. It quickly gained fans worldwide. The game board has six colored wedges. Each color stands for a different category of questions.

* Geography (Blue)
* Entertainment (Pink)
* History (Yellow)
* Arts & Literature (Brown)
* Science & Nature (Green)
* Sports & Leisure (Orange)

Players answer questions to earn wedges. The goal is to collect all six wedges. Then they move to the center space to win.

### Popular Vintage Versions
