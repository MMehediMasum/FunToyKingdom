## Care And Maintenance

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-your-daenerys-targaryen-collectible-doll-keeps-it-in-great-shape.-proper-care-helps-keep-its-details-and-colors-bright.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of your Daenerys Targaryen collectible doll keeps it in great shape. Proper care helps keep its details and colors bright.

Follow simple steps to clean, store, and handle your doll safely. This will protect your collectible for years to come.

### Cleaning Tips

Use a soft, dry cloth to gently dust the doll. Avoid water and cleaning chemicals that may damage paint and fabric.

* Lightly brush hair with a soft brush designed for dolls.
* Spot clean fabric parts using a damp cloth, if needed.
* Do not soak or submerge the doll in water.
* Keep away from direct sunlight to prevent fading.

### Storage Recommendations

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Condition | Recommended Practice |
| Temperature | Keep in a cool, dry place |
| Light | Avoid direct sunlight |
| Humidity | Store in low humidity environment |
| Protection | Use a dust cover or display case |

Store the doll away from pets and children to prevent accidental damage. Use acid-free tissue paper if you wrap the doll.

### Handling Precautions

Handle the doll with clean hands to avoid dirt and oils. Support the doll carefully by its body and avoid pulling on clothes or hair.

* Do not bend or twist fragile parts.
* Avoid dropping the doll or placing it in unstable areas.
* Keep the doll away from food and drinks.
* Limit handling to reduce wear over time.
