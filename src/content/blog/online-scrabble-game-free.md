### Basic Rules And Gameplay

Online Scrabble follows the same rules as the board game. Players take turns making words on a grid using letter tiles.

Each letter has a point value. Your goal is to make words that earn the highest score by using special squares on the board.

* Form words horizontally or vertically
* Use all seven tiles for a bonus
* Score points based on letter values
* Use double or triple letter and word squares
* Take turns until tiles run out or no moves remain

## Tips To Improve Your Scrabble Skills

Playing Scrabble well takes both skill and strategy. You can get better by learning new words and planning your moves.

This guide shares simple tips to help you improve your Scrabble skills and enjoy the game more.

### Expanding Your Vocabulary

Knowing more words lets you score higher and use tricky letters. Try to learn short words and unusual letter combinations.

* Read books, articles, and word lists regularly
* Practice with word games and puzzles
* Memorize two- and three-letter words
* Learn common prefixes and suffixes
* Use a dictionary to check new words

### Strategic Tile Placement

Place tiles to build multiple words at once. Plan ahead to keep good letters for later turns.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Tip | Reason |
| Save high-value letters | Use them on bonus squares for extra points |
| Build parallel words | Create multiple words in one move |
| Avoid blocking yourself | Keep space open for future plays |
| Use common letter pairs | Make longer words easily |

### Using Bonus Squares Effectively

Bonus squares can double or triple your score. Use them wisely to get the most points.

* Place high-value letters on double or triple letter squares
* Try to cover double or triple word squares with long words
* Block your opponent from using bonus squares
* Plan moves that use multiple bonus squares at once
