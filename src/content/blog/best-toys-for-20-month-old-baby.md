## Choosing Safe Toys

Choosing safe toys is important for a 20 month old baby. Safe toys help prevent accidents and injuries.

Parents and caregivers should know what materials and shapes to avoid. This keeps playtime fun and secure.

### Materials To Avoid

Some materials can be harmful to babies. Avoid toys made with toxic paint or lead. Also, stay away from toys with small parts that can break off easily.

* Plastic with BPA or phthalates
* Paint containing lead or heavy metals
* Cheap metals that can rust or break
* Latex if the baby has allergies
* Wood with splinters or rough edges

### Size And Shape Considerations

Toys must be big enough to avoid choking. Small parts can block airways and cause harm.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Shape | Safety Tip |
| Small pieces | Avoid to prevent choking |
| Sharp edges | Choose rounded or soft edges |
| Long strings | Keep short to avoid strangulation |
| Heavy parts | Pick lightweight toys for easy handling |

## Boosting Cognitive Development

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=toys-help-babies-learn-about-the-world-around-them.-they-improve-thinking-skills-and-problem-solving.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Toys help babies learn about the world around them. They improve thinking skills and problem-solving.

Choosing the right toys encourages brain growth and builds early learning skills. Simple toys work best for 20 month olds.

### Shape Sorters

Shape sorters teach babies how to match shapes and fit them into holes. This activity improves hand-eye coordination.

Babies learn to recognize shapes and colors. Sorting shapes also helps with fine motor skills and patience.

* Identify basic shapes like circles, squares, and triangles
* Practice placing shapes in the correct slots
* Develop spatial awareness and problem-solving

### Simple Puzzles

Simple puzzles with large pieces are perfect for 20 month olds. They help babies recognize images and shapes.

Babies learn to fit pieces together, which builds memory and logic skills. Puzzles also improve focus and hand skills.

* Use puzzles with 2 to 4 large pieces
* Choose puzzles with familiar images like animals or fruits
* Encourage matching shapes and colors

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Types Of Toys Are Best For 20 Month Old Babies?

Best toys for 20 month olds include stacking blocks, push toys, and interactive books. These support motor skills and cognitive growth while keeping babies engaged and entertained.

### How Do Toys Help 20 Month Old Baby Development?

Toys enhance fine motor skills, hand-eye coordination, and language learning. They also encourage problem-solving and social interaction, crucial for a 20 month old’s growth.

### Are Educational Toys Suitable For 20 Month Old Babies?

Yes, educational toys like shape sorters and puzzles help develop cognitive skills. They stimulate curiosity and encourage early learning through play.

Toys must be non-toxic, large enough to avoid choking, and sturdy. Always check for smooth edges and avoid small detachable parts for safety.
