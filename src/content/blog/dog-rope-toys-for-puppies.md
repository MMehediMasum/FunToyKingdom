## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Rope Toys For Puppy Teething?



The Mammoth Flossy Chews and KONG Puppy Goodie Bone are top choices. They clean teeth and soothe gums effectively. These toys are durable, safe, and designed specifically for puppies’ teething needs, making them ideal for small and medium breeds.



### How Do Rope Toys Help Puppies With Teething?



Rope toys massage gums and reduce discomfort during teething. Chewing on cotton ropes also cleans teeth and prevents plaque buildup. They keep puppies engaged and distracted from destructive chewing, promoting healthy dental habits from an early age.



### Are Cotton Rope Toys Safe For Aggressive Chewers?



Yes, premium cotton-poly rope toys like Mammoth Flossy Chews are durable for aggressive chewers. They withstand strong bites and tugging while remaining safe. Always supervise playtime to prevent accidental swallowing of loose fibers.



### Can Rope Toys Improve My Puppy’s Dental Health?



Absolutely. Rope toys like KONG Goodie Bone and Depets 4PCs help remove plaque and tartar. Chewing these toys stimulates saliva production, which naturally cleans teeth and freshens breath. Regular use supports healthier gums and teeth.



### What Size Rope Toy Suits Small Breed Puppies?



Choose smaller, lightweight rope toys such as 5 Pack Cotton Puppy Dog Rope Toy. They fit comfortably in small mouths and are easy to chew. Avoid oversized toys to ensure safety and effective teething relief for small breeds.



### How Do Interactive Rope Toys Benefit Puppies?



Interactive rope toys promote bonding and mental stimulation through tug-of-war games. They help burn excess energy and reduce boredom. Toys like Mammoth Flossy Chews keep puppies physically active and mentally sharp, supporting overall development and well-being.
