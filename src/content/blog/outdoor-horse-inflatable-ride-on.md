## Cleaning And Storage Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-outdoor-horse-inflatable-ride-clean-and-well-stored-ensures-it-lasts-longer.-proper-care-prevents-damage-and-maintains-safety.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your outdoor horse inflatable ride clean and well-stored ensures it lasts longer. Proper care prevents damage and maintains safety.

Follow these tips to keep your inflatable ride in top condition through different seasons.

### Effective Cleaning Methods

Regular cleaning prevents dirt build-up and keeps the inflatable looking new. Use gentle methods to avoid damage.

**Cleaning Steps:**

1. Use a soft brush to remove loose dirt and debris.
2. Prepare a mild soap solution with water.
3. Gently scrub the surface with a sponge.
4. Rinse thoroughly with clean water.
5. Allow it to air dry completely before storing.

### Preventing Damage During Storage

Proper storage is key to preventing damage. Avoid sharp objects and exposure to sunlight when storing your inflatable ride.

* Deflate the inflatable completely to save space.
* Fold it neatly and avoid sharp creases.
* Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
* Use a storage bag to protect it from dust and pests.

### Seasonal Care Advice

Different seasons require different care. Adjust your maintenance routine to match the weather conditions.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Season | Care Tips |
| Spring | Inspect for damage from winter storage and clean before use. |
| Summer | Store in shade when not in use to prevent UV damage. |
| Autumn | Clean and dry thoroughly before winter storage. |
| Winter | Store indoors to protect from freezing temperatures. |
