Yes, LED lights improve visibility during low light or evening play. They add excitement and make the boats look attractive in water. LED-lit boats also help track movement easily, making them safer and more enjoyable for kids and adults.

Yes, remote control shark toys are waterproof and designed for pools and bathtubs. They offer realistic shark shapes with LED lights and water spray features. These toys provide unique fun and excitement for kids aged 6 and above.
