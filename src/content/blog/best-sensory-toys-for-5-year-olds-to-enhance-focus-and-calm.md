## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Sensory Toys For 5 Year Olds?



The best sensory toys include silicone fidget toys, textured activity boards, and calming sand art. These toys help improve focus and reduce anxiety in kids aged 5 and up.



### How Do Silicone Fidget Toys Help Kids With Adhd?



Silicone fidget toys provide quiet, tactile stimulation that calms restless hands. They help improve concentration and reduce stress in children with ADHD and autism.



### Are Sensory Activity Boards Suitable For Travel?



Yes, sensory activity boards come with portable travel bags. They are quiet, mess-free, and perfect for calming kids on the go.



### What Textures Are Included In Sensory Fidget Toy Sets?



Sensory fidget sets feature diverse textures like strings, bumps, and soft silicone. These variations enhance tactile exploration and sensory development.



### Can Sensory Toys Help With Anxiety And Stress?



Absolutely, sensory toys like fidget cubes and pop toys promote relaxation. They help kids and adults manage anxiety through repetitive, soothing movements.



### Are Sensory Toys Safe And Easy To Clean?



Most sensory toys are made from food-grade silicone or washable materials. They are safe, durable, and easy to clean for regular use.
