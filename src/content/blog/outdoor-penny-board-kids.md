## Choosing The Right Penny Board

Picking a penny board for kids needs care. It must fit their size and style. Safety and fun both matter.

We will look at size, weight, materials, and designs kids enjoy. This helps you find the best board.

### Size And Weight Considerations

Kids need a board that is easy to carry and control. A board too big or heavy can be hard to ride.

1. Choose a board length between 22 and 27 inches for most kids.
2. Pick a lightweight board under 6 pounds for easy handling.
3. Check the width; 6 to 7 inches is best for small feet.

### Material And Durability

Penny boards are made from plastic or wood. Plastic boards are light and resist water. Wood boards feel sturdy but weigh more.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material | Weight | Durability |
| Plastic | Light | Good for wet areas |
| Wood | Heavier | Strong but can warp |

* Glow-in-the-dark decks for night rides
* Cartoon or animal prints for younger kids
* Bold stripes or geometric shapes for older kids
* Customizable boards with stickers or paint

## Essential Safety Gear

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=riding-an-outdoor-penny-board-is-fun-for-kids,-but-safety-must-come-first.-using-the-right-gear-can-prevent-injuries-and-build-confidence.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Riding an outdoor penny board is fun for kids, but safety must come first. Using the right gear can prevent injuries and build confidence.

Each piece of safety equipment plays a key role. Let’s look at helmets, pads, and shoes for young riders.

### Helmets That Fit Perfectly

A helmet protects the head from serious injuries. It must fit well and stay secure during rides. Choose one with adjustable straps and padding inside.

Check the helmet size by measuring the child’s head. The helmet should sit level and not move when shaken gently.

### Protective Pads For Elbows And Knees

Elbow and knee pads cushion falls and reduce scrapes. Look for pads with soft foam inside and a tough outer shell. Straps should fit snugly but not cut off circulation.

* Choose pads made for kids
* Make sure pads cover the joint fully
* Check for adjustable straps
* Test pads for comfort before riding

### Choosing The Right Shoes

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Feature | Importance |
| Closed toes | Protect feet from scrapes |
| Good grip soles | Prevent slipping on the board |
| Supportive fit | Keep feet steady during riding |
| Durable material | Last through outdoor use |

Shoes should be laced or fastened tightly. Avoid sandals or flip-flops because they offer little protection.

## Safe Riding Spots

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=kids-need-safe-places-to-ride-their-outdoor-penny-boards.-choosing-the-right-spot-helps-prevent-accidents.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Kids need safe places to ride their outdoor penny boards. Choosing the right spot helps prevent accidents. It also makes riding more fun and relaxing.

Parents and guardians should look for areas with little traffic and smooth surfaces. This keeps children safe while they enjoy their rides.

### Parks And Smooth Pavements

Parks often have wide paths that are great for penny boards. These paths usually have smooth pavements, making it easier to ride and control the board.

Smooth surfaces help reduce falls and keep the ride steady. Look for places with few cracks or bumps in the pavement.

* Wide park paths allow for safe turning and stopping
* Smooth pavements reduce risk of tripping or falling
* Open spaces help kids avoid obstacles

### Avoiding Busy Streets

Busy streets are dangerous for kids on penny boards. Cars and trucks move fast and may not see children riding.

It is best to choose quiet neighborhoods or bike lanes instead. These places have less traffic and more space for safe riding.

* Stay away from streets with heavy traffic
* Use bike paths or sidewalks where allowed
* Always watch for vehicles and obey traffic rules

### Supervised Play Areas

Supervised play areas offer safety for kids on penny boards. Adults can watch for any danger and help if needed.

These areas often have clear rules and limits to keep everyone safe. Riding with friends under supervision also teaches good habits.

* Adult supervision reduces risk of injury
* Rules help keep riding safe and fun
* Group riding encourages sharing and respect




## Basic Riding Techniques

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=riding-an-outdoor-penny-board-is-fun-for-kids-and-helps-build-balance.-learning-simple-techniques-makes-riding-safer-and-more-enjoyable.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Riding an outdoor penny board is fun for kids and helps build balance. Learning simple techniques makes riding safer and more enjoyable.

Start with easy skills and practice often. This guide covers balancing, stopping, turning, and safe falling.

### Balancing And Stopping

Keep your feet shoulder-width apart on the board for good balance. Look straight ahead and keep your knees slightly bent.

To stop, press your back foot gently on the ground or slow down by dragging your foot behind you.

* Keep your weight centered on the board
* Bend knees to stay flexible
* Use your back foot to slow down or stop
* Practice stopping in a safe open space

### Turning And Steering Tips

Turn by leaning your body in the direction you want to go. Use your shoulders to guide the board’s movement.

Start with gentle turns and increase the angle as you get comfortable. Keep your weight balanced during turns to avoid falls.

* Lean into turns slowly
* Use your shoulders and head to guide direction
* Shift weight to front foot for sharper turns
* Practice in a clear space with smooth ground

### Practicing Falls Safely

Falling is part of learning to ride. Practice falling safely to reduce injury risk.

Try to roll on your side or use your forearms to break the fall. Avoid landing on your hands or wrists straight away.

* Wear protective gear like a helmet and pads
* Practice falling on soft grass or mats
* Keep your arms bent to absorb impact
* Try to roll instead of stopping suddenly

## Maintenance And Care

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-your-outdoor-penny-board-for-kids-keeps-it-safe-and-fun-to-ride.-proper-maintenance-helps-the-board-last-longer-and-perform-well.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of your outdoor penny board for kids keeps it safe and fun to ride. Proper maintenance helps the board last longer and perform well.

Regular checks and simple cleaning steps protect the board from damage and wear over time.

### Cleaning And Storage

Clean the penny board after rides to remove dirt and dust. Use a damp cloth for the deck and wipe the wheels carefully.

* Avoid soaking the board in water
* Dry the board fully before storing
* Store the board indoors in a cool, dry place
* Keep it away from direct sunlight to prevent fading

### Checking Wheels And Bearings

Check wheels and bearings often to ensure smooth rides. Spin each wheel to feel if it moves freely and listen for grinding sounds.

| Part | Check for | Action if Problem Found |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wheels | Cracks, flat spots, or worn edges | Replace damaged wheels |
| Bearings | Noise, rust, or slow rotation | Clean or replace bearings |

### When To Replace Parts

Parts wear out with use and should be replaced to keep the board safe and fun.

1. Replace wheels if they crack or lose shape.
2. Change bearings if they make noise or stop spinning well.
3. Check the deck for cracks or damage and replace if unsafe.
4. Tighten or replace loose hardware regularly.

### What Age Group Is Suitable For Outdoor Penny Boards For Kids?

Outdoor penny boards for kids are ideal for ages 5 to 12. They offer a fun and safe skating experience. The boards are lightweight and sized for smaller riders, making them easy to handle for young children.

### Are Outdoor Penny Boards Safe For Beginner Kids?

### How To Choose The Right Penny Board Size For Kids?

Choose a penny board size based on the child's height and foot size. Smaller boards around 22 inches suit younger kids. Larger boards, 27 inches or more, fit older children and provide more stability.
