## Setting Up The Sprinkler

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-up-the-outdoor-star-inflatable-sprinkler-toy-is-easy.-follow-simple-steps-to-get-it-ready-for-fun.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting up the Outdoor Star Inflatable Sprinkler Toy is easy. Follow simple steps to get it ready for fun.

Make sure you have everything nearby before you start. This helps you set it up quickly and safely.

### Connecting To Water Source

Find a garden hose with a standard connector to attach to the sprinkler. Make sure the hose is not leaking.

Attach the hose firmly to the sprinkler’s water inlet. Tighten it by hand to avoid leaks during use.

### Inflation Tips

Use an air pump to inflate the sprinkler quickly. Avoid using your mouth to prevent damage or germs.

Inflate the toy until it feels firm but not too hard. Overinflation can cause tears or leaks.

* Check all valves are closed before inflating
* Use a pump with the right nozzle size
* Stop inflating when the shape is full and firm

### Placement For Best Coverage

Place the sprinkler on flat ground free of sharp objects. This keeps it stable and prevents damage.

Set the sprinkler where water can reach the whole play area. Avoid placing it near walls or fences that block spray.

* Pick a sunny spot for quick drying
* Leave space around the sprinkler for safe play
* Ensure water drains away to avoid puddles

## Maintenance And Storage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-your-outdoor-star-inflatable-sprinkler-toy-keeps-it-safe-to-use.-proper-maintenance-helps-it-last-longer.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of your Outdoor Star Inflatable Sprinkler Toy keeps it safe to use. Proper maintenance helps it last longer.

Storing the toy correctly protects it from damage. Follow simple steps after each use and during the off-season.

### Cleaning After Use

Clean the sprinkler toy after every use. Dirt and grass can cause holes or weaken the material.

Use mild soap and water to wash the surface. Avoid harsh chemicals that may damage the plastic.

* Rinse with clean water to remove dirt
* Use a soft cloth or sponge with mild soap
* Let it dry completely before storing

### Deflating And Storing

Deflate the toy carefully to avoid tears. Open all air valves and press out the air gently.

Fold the toy neatly without sharp creases. Store it in a dry, cool place away from sunlight.

* Keep away from sharp objects
* Use a storage bag or box for protection
* Store off the ground to prevent moisture

### Seasonal Care Tips

Do not leave the sprinkler toy outside during cold weather. Freezing can damage the material and valves.

Before winter, clean and dry the toy fully. Store it indoors until spring to keep it safe.

* Inspect for damage before and after each season
* Repair small holes with patch kits
* Keep away from heat sources to avoid melting
