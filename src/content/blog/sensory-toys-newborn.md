### How Do Montessori Sensory Toys Support Infant Development?



Montessori sensory toys promote hands-on learning and exploration. They enhance fine motor skills, sensory processing, and problem-solving. These toys encourage independent play and curiosity, supporting babies from 0-12 months in reaching developmental milestones naturally and confidently.



### When Should I Introduce Teething Toys To My Baby?



Teething toys are ideal for babies around 3-6 months. They soothe sore gums during teething and encourage healthy chewing habits. These toys also improve hand-eye coordination and tactile exploration, making them a perfect sensory tool for infant development.



### Are Sensory Toys Good Gifts For Newborn Baby Showers?



Yes, sensory toys make excellent newborn gifts. They combine fun with developmental benefits, aiding visual, tactile, and auditory senses. Gifts like high contrast books, rattles, and teething toys are thoughtful and practical for babies 0-12 months.



### What Features Make A Sensory Toy Safe For Newborns?



Safe sensory toys are made from non-toxic, BPA-free materials. They have no small parts that pose choking hazards. Soft textures and smooth edges ensure gentle interaction. These features protect babies while promoting safe exploration and sensory stimulation.
