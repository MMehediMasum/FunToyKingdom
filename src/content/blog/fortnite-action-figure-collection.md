## Collecting Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collecting-fortnite-action-figures-is-a-fun-hobby.-it-lets-you-enjoy-your-favorite-characters-in-a-new-way.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collecting Fortnite action figures is a fun hobby. It lets you enjoy your favorite characters in a new way.

To start, learn where to buy, how to check if they are real, and the best ways to store and show them.

### Where To Buy

Look for Fortnite action figures in toy stores and online shops. Many stores have new and rare figures.

Check out secondhand markets and collector groups too. Sometimes you find rare items there at good prices.

* Official toy stores
* Online retailers like Amazon or eBay
* Collector forums and Facebook groups
* Garage sales and flea markets

### Authenticity Checks

Fake figures can look very real. Check details carefully to avoid buying fakes.

Look for official logos, quality of paint, and packaging. Real figures have clear and sharp printing.

* Check the packaging for official Fortnite and manufacturer logos
* Look for smooth paint and clean details on the figure
* Compare with pictures of authentic figures online
* Avoid items with misspelled words or blurry images

### Storage And Display Ideas

Store your figures where they stay safe from dust and sun. This keeps them looking new.

Show your collection on shelves or in glass cases. Arrange figures by size or character for a neat look.

* Use closed display cases to keep dust out
* Place shelves away from direct sunlight
* Arrange figures by themes or favorite characters
* Use stands or holders to keep figures stable
