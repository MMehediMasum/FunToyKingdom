## History Of Funko Pop

Funko Pop collectible dolls are popular worldwide. They feature small, stylized vinyl figures.

This article looks at the history of Funko Pop, from its start to famous releases.

### Origins And Growth

Funko started in 1998, founded by Mike Becker. It made nostalgic toys at first.

In 2010, Funko launched the Pop! line. These figures had large heads and cute designs.

* Started with bobbleheads and vinyl toys
* Focused on pop culture characters
* Grew fast due to wide appeal
* Expanded to TV, movies, comics, and games

### Iconic Releases

## Types Of Funko Pop Figures

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=funko-pop-figures-come-in-many-styles-and-editions.-each-type-offers-something-unique-for-collectors.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Funko Pop figures come in many styles and editions. Each type offers something unique for collectors.

Understanding the different kinds helps you find the perfect addition to your collection.

### Standard Pops

Standard Pops are the most common type. They feature popular characters from movies, TV shows, comics, and games. These figures have a simple design with a big head and small body.

### Exclusive Editions

Exclusive Editions are special versions made for certain stores or events. They often have unique colors or accessories. These editions are limited, making them more valuable to collectors.

* Retail Store Exclusives
* Convention Exclusives
* Online Shop Exclusives

### Chase Variants

Chase Variants are rare versions of Standard Pops. They might have a different pose, color, or accessory. These figures appear randomly in shipments, making them harder to find.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Feature** | **Standard Pop** | **Chase Variant** |
| Availability | Common | Rare |
| Design | Basic | Unique details |
| Value | Lower | Higher |

### Specialty Series

Specialty Series are themed collections. They include figures with special paint jobs or materials. These series celebrate holidays, anniversaries, or unique styles.

Examples of Specialty Series:

* Glow-in-the-Dark Pops
* Metallic Finish Pops
* Holiday Themed Pops
* Black and White Series

## Collecting Tips And Tricks

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collecting-funko-pop-dolls-and-figures-can-be-a-fun-hobby-for-fans.-it-takes-patience-and-knowledge-to-build-a-great-collection.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collecting Funko Pop dolls and figures can be a fun hobby for fans. It takes patience and knowledge to build a great collection. This guide helps you with tips to buy, identify, and keep your Pops safe and nice.

Use these simple ideas to enjoy your collecting journey. Learn where to find Pops, how to spot rare ones, and ways to show them off well.

### Where To Buy

You can find Funko Pops in many places. Look in stores, online shops, or at special events. Each place offers different chances to find your favorite figures.

* Visit toy stores and comic shops
* Check online marketplaces like eBay or Amazon
* Join collector groups on social media
* Attend conventions and pop culture fairs
* Watch for limited edition releases

### Identifying Rare Pops

Knowing which Pops are rare helps you find valuable pieces. Look for special features and details that make them unique. Some pops are rare because of their production or design.

| Feature | What to Check | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Limited Edition | Number made, special sticker | Less supply means higher value |
| Exclusive Releases | Store or event logos | Only sold in certain places |
| Misprints | Wrong colors or shapes | Rare mistakes can increase worth |
| Chase Variants | Different pose or color | Hard to find special versions |

### Preserving And Displaying

Keep your Funko Pops in good condition by storing and showing them right. Avoid damage from sunlight, dust, and moisture. Use cases and shelves to organize your collection well.

#### Tips to Preserve and Display

* Keep Pops in original boxes if possible
* Use clear acrylic display cases
* Store away from direct sunlight
* Dust regularly with a soft cloth
* Place on stable shelves to avoid falls
