## Donation Guidelines For Used Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=donating-used-toys-helps-children-and-families-in-need.-many-places-in-los-angeles-accept-toy-donations.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Donating used toys helps children and families in need. Many places in Los Angeles accept toy donations. Following clear donation guidelines makes sure toys are safe and useful. These rules protect kids and keep donations organized. Learn what to check before donating toys in Los Angeles.

### Toy Condition Requirements

Toys must be clean and in good shape. Broken or missing parts are not accepted. Remove dirt, stains, and sticky residue. Batteries should be removed or working properly. Toys with sharp edges or cracks should be avoided. Check toys carefully before donation.

### Safety Standards

All toys must meet safety rules. Avoid toys with small parts for young children. Toys should not have loose pieces that cause choking. Avoid toys with toxic paint or harmful chemicals. Make sure toys pass safety tests. Safety keeps children healthy and happy.

### Age-appropriate Donations

Donate toys suitable for the child’s age. Label the toy with the correct age range. Avoid toys meant for older children if donating to toddlers. Choose toys that match the child’s skill level. Age-appropriate toys encourage learning and fun. Check age labels before giving toys away.
