**Choosing the right toys for older infants supports their growth and keeps them happy. Toys that encourage movement, senses, and learning work best.**



Older infants, aged 6 to 18 months, explore the world through touch, sight, and sound. Toys like Montessori sensory sets, teething rings, and push cars help develop motor skills and coordination. Bright colors and different textures catch their attention and stimulate their senses.



Musical toys and stacking blocks introduce cause and effect and problem-solving. Soft, safe materials suit delicate gums and hands during teething stages. Busy boards and pull-string toys keep toddlers busy and engaged. Selecting age-appropriate toys encourages curiosity and early learning. These toys also make great gifts for birthdays or holidays. Overall, the right toy supports an infant’s physical and mental growth in fun ways.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Sensory Toys For Older Infants?



Sensory toys like Montessori cubes and crinkle tissue boxes engage older infants. They help develop touch, sight, and hearing skills. These toys stimulate curiosity and promote cognitive growth effectively during 6-18 months.



### How Do Teething Toys Benefit Infants Aged 0-18 Months?



Teething toys soothe sore gums and encourage healthy chewing habits. They improve hand coordination and provide comfort during teething phases. Safe silicone or rubber materials are ideal for babies from 0 to 18 months.



### Which Toys Support Infant Motor Skill Development?



Push and pull cars, stacking blocks, and friction toys enhance motor skills. They encourage crawling, grasping, and coordination. These toys are perfect for infants aged 6 months to 2 years.



### Are Musical Toys Good For Infant Learning?



Yes, musical toys with lights and sounds boost sensory and cognitive growth. They help infants recognize cause and effect. Suitable for babies 6 months and older, they promote early learning and entertainment.



### What Makes Montessori Toys Ideal For Toddlers?



Montessori toys focus on hands-on learning and independent play. They support problem-solving and sensory exploration. These toys are designed for toddlers aged 1 to 3 years, fostering creativity and concentration.



### Can Travel Toys Keep Infants Engaged On The Go?



Travel toys like busy boards and push cars provide distraction and learning during trips. They are compact, durable, and educational. Ideal for infants and toddlers from 6 months to 2 years.
