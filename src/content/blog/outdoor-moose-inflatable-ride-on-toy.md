## Maintaining Your Inflatable Ride

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-outdoor-moose-inflatable-ride-clean-and-in-good-shape-is-important.-proper-care-helps-it-last-longer-and-stay-safe-for-play.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your outdoor moose inflatable ride clean and in good shape is important. Proper care helps it last longer and stay safe for play.

Follow simple steps to clean, repair, and store your inflatable. These actions protect it from damage and wear.

### Cleaning Tips

Regular cleaning removes dirt and prevents mold. Use mild soap and water. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the material.

* Use a soft cloth or sponge
* Rinse thoroughly with clean water
* Let it air dry completely before packing
* Clean after each use, especially if outside

### Repair And Patch Advice

Fix small holes or tears quickly to stop leaks. Use repair kits made for inflatables. Clean the area before patching.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Damage Type | Repair Method | Dry Time |
| Small hole (less than 1 inch) | Apply patch with adhesive | 24 hours |
| Large tear | Use heavy-duty patch or seek professional repair | 48 hours |
| Seam separation | Seal with flexible glue | 24 hours |

### Proper Storage Methods

Store your inflatable in a dry, cool place away from sunlight. Make sure it is dry to prevent mold and mildew.

**Storage Tips:**

* Fold gently without sharp creases
* Use a breathable storage bag
* Keep away from sharp objects and pests
* Check inflatable before use after storage
