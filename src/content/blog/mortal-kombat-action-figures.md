## Collecting Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collecting-mortal-kombat-action-figures-can-be-fun-and-rewarding.-knowing-the-right-tips-helps-you-build-a-great-collection.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collecting Mortal Kombat action figures can be fun and rewarding. Knowing the right tips helps you build a great collection.

This guide covers how to check authenticity, store your figures, and trade wisely with others.

### Authenticity Checks

Always verify your action figures are real to keep your collection valuable. Look for official logos and quality details.

* Check the packaging for official licensing marks.
* Inspect paint jobs for clean lines and consistent colors.
* Feel the figure’s material; fake ones often feel lighter or cheaper.
* Compare your figure with photos from trusted sources online.
* Watch out for unusual smells or loose parts, which may mean a fake.

### Storage And Preservation

Proper storage keeps your Mortal Kombat figures in top shape. Avoid damage from dust, sunlight, and moisture.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Tip | Reason |
| Use display cases | Protects from dust and dirt |
| Keep out of direct sunlight | Prevents color fading |
| Store in a dry place | Stops mold and rust |
| Keep figures upright | Maintains shape and joints |
| Wrap loose parts | Prevents loss and damage |

### Buying And Trading

Buying and trading require patience and good judgment. Know the market and value of your figures before deals.

1. Research current prices on trusted collector sites.
2. Meet sellers or traders in safe, public places.
3. Ask for clear photos and condition details before buying.
4. Keep records of trades for future reference.
5. Be honest about your figure’s condition when trading.
