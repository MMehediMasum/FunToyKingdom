### Authenticity Checks

Look for official Monster High logos on the box and doll. These marks show the doll is real, not a fake.

Check the doll's face paint and body details. Authentic dolls have clear colors and smooth finishes.

* Box has “Mattel” logo and copyright year
* Doll has detailed facial paint and hair style
* Check for correct accessories and tags

### Condition Grading

Check if the doll and box are clean and undamaged. Dolls in better condition are worth more.

Look for scratches, paint chips, or loose parts. The box should have no tears or stains.

* Mint: New, no damage, in original box
* Near Mint: Slight wear, no major defects
* Good: Some wear, small paint chips
* Fair: Noticeable damage or missing parts

### Sourcing And Pricing

Buy from trusted sellers to avoid fakes. Check prices from different places to find fair deals.

Prices vary by condition, rarity, and demand. Dolls in mint condition cost more than used ones.
