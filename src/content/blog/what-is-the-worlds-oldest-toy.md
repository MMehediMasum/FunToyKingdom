## Ancient Playthings

Toys have been part of human life for thousands of years. Ancient playthings tell us about early cultures and childhood fun. These toys were simple but brought joy to children long ago. Archaeologists find these toys in old sites, showing us how kids played in the past.

### Early Toys From Archaeological Finds

Many early toys come from ancient digs around the world. Small clay figures and carved stones often serve as toys. Some toys look like animals or people. Dolls made from bone or wood also appear in old sites. Toys like spinning tops and miniature tools were common. These finds prove children played and learned through toys.

### Materials Used In Ancient Toys

Ancient toys were made from natural materials. Clay was easy to shape and bake into hard toys. Wood was carved into figures and small objects. Bone and ivory were also used for detailed toys. Sometimes, fabric or leather made soft dolls. These materials lasted long enough to reach us today.
