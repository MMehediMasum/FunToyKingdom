### Exploring Attraction And Repulsion

Magnets have two poles: north and south. Opposite poles attract, while the same poles repel each other.

Use the kit to test how magnets push and pull. Kids learn how forces work without seeing them directly.

* Bring two magnets close with opposite poles
* Feel them pull toward each other
* Turn one magnet to match the same poles
* Notice how they push away
* Try moving magnets at different angles and distances

### Building Simple Machines

Magnets can help make simple machines like levers and pulleys. Use magnets to create moving parts that stick or slide.

Kids build models to see how magnets add force or hold objects in place. This shows basic engineering ideas.

* Create a lever with a ruler and a small block
* Use a magnet to hold the lever’s end
* Attach iron filings or metal pieces to parts that move
* Try lifting or pulling objects with magnetic force
* Experiment with pulleys by adding magnets to wheels
