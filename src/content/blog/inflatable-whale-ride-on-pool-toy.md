## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=inflatable-whale-ride-pool-toys-bring-fun-to-water-play.-sometimes,-issues-may-occur-that-need-fixing.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Inflatable whale ride pool toys bring fun to water play. Sometimes, issues may occur that need fixing.

Knowing how to solve common problems helps keep your pool toy safe and enjoyable.

### Fixing Leaks And Punctures

Leaks cause air to escape and make the toy sink. Small holes can be patched easily to stop leaks.

Use a patch kit made for inflatables. Clean and dry the area before applying the patch firmly.

* Find the leak by listening for hissing or using soapy water.
* Mark the hole with a waterproof marker.
* Apply the patch smoothly without air bubbles.
* Let the patch dry fully before inflating the toy again.

### Handling Inflation Problems

Inflation problems can cause the whale to be too soft or too hard. This affects safety and comfort.

Check the air valve for dirt or damage. Make sure the pump fits the valve well for proper inflation.

* Inflate slowly and stop when the toy is firm but not overfilled.
* If air leaks from the valve, clean or replace the valve cap.
* Use a manual or electric pump that matches the valve type.

### Dealing With Wear And Tear

Wear and tear happen with regular use. Rough surfaces and sun exposure can damage the toy.

Store the whale in a cool, dry place when not in use. Avoid dragging it on rough ground.

* Inspect the toy regularly for weak spots and small tears.
* Keep it out of direct sunlight for long times to prevent material damage.
* Clean the toy with mild soap and water after use.
