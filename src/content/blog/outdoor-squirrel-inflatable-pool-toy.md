Keeping your outdoor squirrel inflatable pool toy in good shape helps it last longer. Simple care steps prevent damage and wear.

### Cleaning And Storage

Clean your inflatable toy after each use. Remove dirt and debris with mild soap and water. Dry it completely before storing.

* Use a soft cloth to wipe the surface
* Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the material
* Store in a cool, dry place away from sharp objects
* Keep it folded loosely to prevent creases

Small holes can cause leaks. Fix them quickly to keep the toy safe and fun.

### Avoiding Sun Damage

Sunlight can weaken the inflatable’s material. Protect it to keep its color and strength.

* Set up the toy in shaded areas when possible
* Use a UV-protective spray made for plastics
* Do not leave it in direct sun for long times
* Store it indoors when not in use

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is An Outdoor Squirrel Inflatable Pool Toy?

An outdoor squirrel inflatable pool toy is a fun, inflatable float shaped like a squirrel. It is designed for pool use, offering entertainment and relaxation. It’s lightweight, easy to inflate, and ideal for kids and adults alike during summer.

### How Do You Inflate The Squirrel Pool Toy Properly?

Use an air pump or manual inflator to fill the pool toy. Avoid over-inflating to prevent damage. Inflate until firm but soft enough for comfortable use. Check for leaks before use to ensure safety and durability.

### Is The Squirrel Pool Toy Safe For Children?

Yes, most squirrel inflatable pool toys are child-safe when used properly. Always supervise children during pool play. Check for non-toxic, durable materials and avoid sharp objects nearby. Follow manufacturer age and weight recommendations for safe use.

### Can The Inflatable Squirrel Be Used Outdoors Only?

No, the inflatable squirrel pool toy can be used indoors or outdoors. It’s ideal for outdoor pools, lakes, or calm water bodies. Indoors, it can be used in bathtubs or small pools. Always ensure a safe, flat surface for inflation.
