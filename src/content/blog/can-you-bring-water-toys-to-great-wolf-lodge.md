## Water Toy Policy At Great Wolf Lodge

Great Wolf Lodge has clear rules about bringing water toys into the park. These rules help keep everyone safe and make sure all guests enjoy their visit. Knowing what you can bring helps you pack smart and avoid surprises at the entrance.

### Allowed Water Toys

Small, soft water toys are usually allowed. Inflatable arm bands and small pool noodles often pass the check. Kids’ floatation devices, like life jackets approved by safety standards, are welcome. Simple water guns or squirt toys might be okay if they are small and do not disturb others.

### Prohibited Items

Large inflatable rafts and tubes are not allowed. Hard plastic toys and sharp objects are banned. Toys that can cause harm or block water slides are forbidden. Glass bottles and any sharp or heavy items are also prohibited for safety reasons.

### Safety Regulations

All toys must be clean and safe to use in water. Staff may inspect toys at the entrance. Any toy that looks unsafe will be asked to stay outside. Following these rules helps protect kids and keeps the water park fun for all.

Great Wolf Lodge generally does not allow outside water toys for safety and hygiene reasons. Guests should use the park's provided equipment. Check the specific lodge's policy before packing to avoid surprises.

### Are Inflatable Water Toys Permitted At Great Wolf Lodge?

Inflatable water toys are typically not allowed inside the water park. The lodge provides safe and suitable toys for all guests. Confirm with the lodge directly for any exceptions.

### Can Kids Bring Floaties To Great Wolf Lodge Pools?

### Why Does Great Wolf Lodge Restrict Outside Water Toys?
