## Caring For Your Silicone Rattle Teether

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=silicone-baby-rattle-teethers-need-regular-care-to-keep-them-safe-and-clean.-proper-care-helps-your-baby-enjoy-the-teether-without-any-health-risks.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Silicone baby rattle teethers need regular care to keep them safe and clean. Proper care helps your baby enjoy the teether without any health risks.

Simple steps can keep the teether in good shape for a long time. Learn how to clean, store, and check your teether safely.

### Cleaning Tips And Tricks

Clean your silicone rattle teether often to remove dirt and germs. Use warm water and mild soap for best results.

Rinse the teether well to remove all soap. Let it air dry on a clean towel before giving it back to your baby.

* Wash before first use and after each use
* Use a soft brush to clean small areas
* Avoid harsh chemicals or bleach
* Do not use the dishwasher unless the product says it is safe

### Storage Suggestions

Store the silicone rattle teether in a clean, dry place. Keep it away from direct sunlight and heat.

Use a sealed container or a clean cloth bag to protect the teether from dust and germs when not in use.

* Choose a cool, dry spot for storage
* Avoid areas near pets or dirty floors
* Keep it separate from other toys to avoid damage

### Inspecting For Wear And Safety

Check the silicone rattle teether regularly for signs of wear or damage. Cracks, tears, or loose parts can be dangerous.

Replace the teether if you see any damage. Always ensure it is safe before giving it to your baby.

* Look for cracks or tears in the silicone
* Check if any parts are loose or broken
* Make sure the rattle sound is not too loud or broken
* Replace the teether if it shows damage
