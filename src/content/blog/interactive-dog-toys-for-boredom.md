## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Interactive Dog Toys?



Interactive dog toys are designed to engage your pet's mind and body. They help reduce boredom and provide mental stimulation. These toys encourage problem-solving and can improve your dog's cognitive skills. They often include elements like puzzles, moving parts, or sounds to captivate your dog's interest.



### How Do Motion-activated Dog Toys Work?



Motion-activated dog toys use sensors to detect movement and react accordingly. They may roll, vibrate, or produce sounds when your dog interacts with them. This unpredictability keeps dogs engaged and entertained. They're a great way to stimulate your dog's natural instincts and keep them active.



### Are Dog Puzzle Toys Suitable For All Breeds?



Yes, dog puzzle toys are suitable for all breeds and sizes. They come in various difficulty levels to match different skill sets. Whether you have a puppy or an adult dog, these toys can enhance mental stimulation and provide hours of entertainment.



Always choose an appropriate size for your dog's safety.



### Can Interactive Toys Help With Dog Anxiety?



Interactive toys can help alleviate dog anxiety by providing mental stimulation and distraction. These toys engage your dog's mind, reducing stress and preventing destructive behavior. They offer a constructive outlet for your dog's energy, helping them feel calm and content, especially during stressful situations.



### How Do I Choose The Right Dog Toy?



To choose the right dog toy, consider your dog's size, age, and play style. Select toys made from durable materials for aggressive chewers. Look for toys that offer mental stimulation, such as puzzles or motion-activated features. Always ensure the toy is safe and non-toxic for your pet.
