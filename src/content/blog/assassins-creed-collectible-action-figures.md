### Tips For Authenticity Verification

Fake figures can look very close to the real ones. Use these tips to check the authenticity of your Assassin’s Creed collectibles before buying.

* Look for official Ubisoft logos and trademarks on packaging.
* Compare packaging details with photos from official retailers.
* Check the weight and material quality of the figure.
* Ask for close-up photos if buying online.
* Buy from trusted sellers with positive reviews.
* Research common fake markers for the specific figure.
