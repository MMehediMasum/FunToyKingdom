## Maintenance And Safety Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=outdoor-fishing-game-magnetic-toys-are-fun-and-safe-when-cared-for-properly.-regular-maintenance-keeps-the-toy-working-well.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Outdoor fishing game magnetic toys are fun and safe when cared for properly. Regular maintenance keeps the toy working well. Safety tips help avoid accidents during play.

Follow these simple guidelines to keep the toy clean, safe, and ready for use every time.

### Cleaning And Storage

Clean the magnetic fishing toy after every use. Dirt and moisture can damage the magnets and pieces. Use a soft cloth with mild soap and water. Avoid soaking the toy to protect its parts.

Store the toy in a dry place. Keep all parts together in a box or bag. This prevents losing small pieces and keeps the magnets strong.

* Wipe with a damp cloth
* Dry completely before storing
* Keep away from extreme heat or cold
* Store in a sealed container

### Supervision And Safe Play

Always supervise young children during play. The toy has small parts that can be a choking hazard. Teach kids not to put pieces in their mouths.

Encourage gentle play to avoid breaking the toy. Use the fishing rod and magnets as intended. Safe play prevents injury and damage.

* Watch children under 3 years old closely
* Explain safe play rules clearly
* Keep small parts out of reach of babies
* Stop play if pieces break or come loose

### Inspecting For Wear And Tear

Check the fishing game regularly for damage. Look for loose magnets, cracks, or broken parts. Damaged pieces can be unsafe and reduce fun.

Replace or repair parts as needed. Do not use the toy if it has sharp edges or missing magnets. Regular inspections keep the toy safe and durable.

* Examine magnets for cracks or chips
* Check the fishing rod for damage
* Look for loose or missing pieces
* Fix or discard damaged parts immediately
