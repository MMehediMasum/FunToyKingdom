### Making A Wooden Car

Start with a small block of wood to shape the car’s body. Sand it smooth to avoid sharp edges. Drill holes for the axles to hold the wheels in place.

1. Cut the wood block to a car shape using a saw.
2. Sand all surfaces until smooth.
3. Drill two holes parallel to each other for the axles.
4. Attach wooden wheels to the axles.
5. Paint or decorate the car with child-safe paint.

### Creating Stacking Rings

Use wooden rings of different sizes to make this toy. Stack the rings on a wooden peg fixed to a base. This helps children learn about size and order.

| Wooden ring | Large | Red |

### Designing A Shape Sorter

The shape sorter helps kids recognize different shapes. Cut holes in a wooden box matching the shapes of the blocks. Each block fits only in its matching hole.

Basic shapes to cut and create:

* Circle
* Square
* Triangle
* Star

Cut corresponding blocks from wood. Sand edges smooth. Paint each block a different color to attract children’s attention.

## Safety Tips For Crafting

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-wooden-toys-with-preschoolers-is-fun-and-creative.-safety-is-very-important-during-crafting.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making wooden toys with preschoolers is fun and creative. Safety is very important during crafting.

Following safety tips helps prevent accidents and keeps kids happy while they make their toys.

### Handling Tools Carefully

Teach children how to use tools like scissors and hammers with care. Only use tools made for kids.

Keep sharp tools out of reach when not in use. Always supervise children during crafting.

* Use child-safe scissors with blunt tips
* Provide small, light hammers for little hands
* Store tools in a safe box after use
* Show children how to hold and use tools properly

### Choosing Safe Materials

Select wood that is smooth and free from splinters. Avoid painted or treated wood that may be harmful.

Use non-toxic glue and paints made for children. Check all materials for safety labels before using.

* Pick natural, untreated wood pieces
* Sand rough edges to prevent splinters
* Choose water-based, non-toxic paints and glue
* Check for small parts that can be a choking hazard

### Supervising Preschoolers

Always watch children closely while they work on crafts. Help them if they feel unsure or need support.

Set clear rules for how to behave with tools and materials. Remind kids to work slowly and carefully.

* Stay near children at all times during crafting
* Give simple, clear instructions
* Encourage asking for help when needed
* Keep the work area clean and organized
