## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Is Ideal For Using Baby Rattle Toys?



Baby rattle toys are ideal from newborn to 12 months. They support sensory development and hand-eye coordination during this period. Different rattles suit various ages, ensuring safety and engagement as babies grow.



### How Do Rattles Help Infant Development?



Rattles stimulate auditory, visual, and motor skills. They encourage grasping, shaking, and cause-and-effect learning. This aids brain development and fine motor control, essential for early childhood growth.



### Are Bpa-free Rattles Safer For Babies?



Yes, BPA-free rattles are safer. They prevent harmful chemical exposure during mouthing. Many infant rattles, like Bright Starts Oball, use BPA-free materials for healthy play.



### Can Rattles Double As Teething Toys?



Many rattles, like Baby Einstein’s Opus, also function as teethers. They soothe sore gums while providing sensory stimulation and grip practice, making them versatile for infants.



### What Features Make A Rattle Easy For Babies To Hold?



Lightweight, textured, and easy-grip designs help babies hold rattles. Soft materials and small sizes, like those in Sassy My First Rattles set, support infant grasping skills effectively.



### Which Rattles Are Best As Newborn Gift Sets?



Gift sets like iPlay, iLearn 10pcs and Infinno Wrist Rattles offer variety and developmental benefits. They include sensory, teether, and grasp toys ideal for newborns and infants.
