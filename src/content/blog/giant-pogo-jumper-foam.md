## Safety Tips And Usage Guidelines

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=giant-pogo-jumper-foam-is-a-fun-outdoor-activity.-it-offers-exercise-and-excitement-for-kids.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Giant Pogo Jumper Foam is a fun outdoor activity. It offers exercise and excitement for kids. Safety is crucial for a great experience.

Following guidelines ensures safe and enjoyable jumping. Here are some essential tips to keep in mind.

### Proper Supervision

Always watch children while they play. An adult should be present to guide and assist. Supervision prevents accidents and ensures safe play.

Ensure that the child understands the rules. Explain how to use the pogo stick safely. This helps avoid injuries during play.

### Safe Jumping Surfaces

Choose a soft, level surface for jumping. Grass or padded areas are ideal. Avoid hard surfaces like concrete or asphalt.

Clear the area of any obstacles. Ensure there is enough space for safe jumping. This reduces the risk of tripping or falling.

### Maintenance And Care

Regularly check the pogo stick for damage. Inspect the foam and other parts. Repair or replace any broken parts immediately.

Store the pogo stick in a dry place. Keep it away from harsh weather. Proper care extends the life of the pogo stick.

* Inspect the pogo stick before each use
* Store in a cool, dry place
* Replace worn out parts promptly
