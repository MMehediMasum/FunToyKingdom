## Care And Maintenance

The Horizon Zero Dawn Alloy collectible doll is a special item. Proper care keeps it looking great for years. Follow simple steps to protect your doll from damage.

Cleaning, storing, and preserving the doll helps keep its value. Taking small actions can prevent wear and tear.

### Cleaning Instructions

Use a soft, dry cloth to clean the doll. Avoid water or cleaning chemicals. These can harm the paint and materials.

If dust builds up, use a soft brush to remove it gently. Do not scrub hard or use abrasive materials.

* Use a microfiber cloth or soft brush
* Do not use water or cleaning sprays
* Clean gently to avoid scratches
* Keep away from direct sunlight while cleaning

### Storage Recommendations

Store the doll in a dry, cool place away from sunlight. Heat and light can cause colors to fade. Avoid damp areas to stop mold growth.

Use a display case or box with soft padding. This prevents dust and protects the doll from accidental damage.

* Keep away from direct sunlight and heat
* Store in a sealed display case or box
* Use soft padding inside the storage container
* Keep in a room with stable temperature and low humidity

### Preserving Collectible Value

Keep the doll in its original packaging if possible. Packaging helps maintain its value over time. Avoid removing tags or stickers.

Handle the doll with clean hands. Oils and dirt from skin can damage the paint. Show the doll carefully and avoid dropping it.

* Keep original packaging and tags
* Handle with clean, dry hands
* Do not remove stickers or labels
* Avoid dropping or rough handling
