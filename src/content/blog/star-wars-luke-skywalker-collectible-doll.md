## Display And Preservation Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-star-wars-luke-skywalker-collectible-doll-in-great-shape-needs-care.-displaying-it-right-helps-show-off-its-details.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Star Wars Luke Skywalker collectible doll in great shape needs care. Displaying it right helps show off its details.

Preserving the doll keeps it looking new for a long time. Follow simple tips to protect your collectible.

### Best Practices For Display

Pick a safe spot with little sunlight. Avoid places with heat or moisture. Use stands or cases to keep the doll steady.

* Place the doll away from direct sunlight to prevent fading.
* Use a dust cover or glass case to reduce dust build-up.
* Keep the doll on a stable shelf or stand to avoid falls.
* Keep away from heat sources like radiators or vents.
* Choose a dry room to stop moisture damage.

### Maintaining Doll Condition

Regular care keeps your doll looking its best. Clean gently and check for damage often.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Care Action | Details |
| Dusting | Use a soft brush or cloth weekly |
| Handling | Hold by the body, not limbs or accessories |
| Cleaning | Wipe with a damp cloth, no chemicals |
| Storage | Store in original box or protective case if not displayed |
