## Seasonal Care Tips

Outdoor mud kitchen playsets need regular care to last through the seasons. Proper care helps keep the playset safe and fun for kids.

Follow simple steps to protect your playset from weather damage and dirt buildup. Each season requires different attention.

### Weatherproofing The Playset

Protect your mud kitchen from rain and sun to avoid damage. Use weatherproofing products that suit wood or plastic materials.

Apply a waterproof sealant on wooden parts at least once a year. Cover the playset with a tarp during heavy rain or strong sun.

* Check for cracks or loose parts before applying sealant
* Use UV-resistant spray to prevent sun fading
* Remove covers during playtime to keep it dry inside

### Winter Storage Solutions

Winter weather can harm your mud kitchen if left outside. Store it properly to avoid freezing and moisture damage.

Move the playset to a garage or shed if possible. If not, cover it tightly with a waterproof cover and clear snow regularly.

* Empty all containers and remove loose dirt
* Disassemble small parts for indoor storage
* Elevate the playset off the ground to prevent water damage

### Spring Cleaning Guide

Spring is the best time to clean and refresh your mud kitchen playset. Clean dirt and check for needed repairs.

Use mild soap and water to wash all surfaces. Rinse well and let it dry completely before use.

* Scrub off mold or mildew with a soft brush
* Sand rough wooden edges to keep them smooth
* Replace any broken or missing parts before kids play
