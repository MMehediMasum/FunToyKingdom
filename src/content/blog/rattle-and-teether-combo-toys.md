## Safety Features To Look For

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-a-rattle-and-teether-combo-toy-requires-careful-attention-to-safety.-these-toys-help-babies-explore,-so-they-must-be-safe-to-use.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing a rattle and teether combo toy requires careful attention to safety. These toys help babies explore, so they must be safe to use.

Parents should check specific safety features. This helps prevent accidents and keeps babies healthy.



### Non-toxic Materials

Always select toys made from non-toxic materials. Babies often put toys in their mouths, so chemicals can be harmful.

Look for BPA-free, phthalate-free, and lead-free labels. These ensure the toy is safe for chewing and rattling.

* Use food-grade silicone or natural rubber
* Avoid paints or dyes with harmful chemicals
* Check for certifications like ASTM or EN71

### Choking Hazard Prevention

Check that the toy has no small parts that can break off. Small pieces can block a baby’s airway and cause choking.

The toy’s size should be large enough so babies cannot swallow it. Avoid loose attachments or decorations.

* Choose toys with securely attached parts
* Avoid toys with small buttons or beads
* Ensure all pieces are solid and unbreakable

### Easy To Clean

Toys must be easy to clean to prevent germs. Babies’ immune systems are still developing, so cleanliness matters.

Look for toys that can be washed with soap and water. Some can even be sterilized or put in the dishwasher.

* Choose non-porous materials that dry quickly
* Avoid toys with hard-to-reach crevices
* Check if the toy is dishwasher safe

Choose toys made for your baby's age to ensure safety and usefulness. Toys too advanced may frustrate, and those too simple may bore your child.

### Color And Sound Preferences

Bright colors attract babies and help with vision development. Gentle sounds can soothe or entertain, but loud noises might scare them.

* Choose toys with contrasting colors for better attention
* Look for rattles with soft jingles or gentle bells
* Avoid toys with very loud or sudden sounds
