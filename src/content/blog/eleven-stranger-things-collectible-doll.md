Caring for your Eleven Stranger Things collectible doll keeps it looking its best. Proper maintenance helps protect its value and appearance.

Follow simple care tips to ensure your doll stays clean and well-preserved for years to come.

### Cleaning Tips

Clean your doll gently to avoid damage. Use soft cloths and mild products only.

* Use a dry, soft brush to remove dust from hair and clothes.
* Dampen a cloth slightly with water to wipe the doll’s face and hands.
* Avoid harsh chemicals or soaking the doll in water.
* Let the doll air dry naturally after cleaning.

### Storage Recommendations

Store your doll in a safe, clean place away from sunlight and humidity. Proper storage prevents fading and mold.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Condition | Recommendation |
| Light | Keep out of direct sunlight |
| Temperature | Store in a cool, dry place |
| Humidity | Maintain low humidity levels |
| Position | Keep doll upright or lying flat |
| Cover | Use breathable fabric covers |

### Preserving Doll Condition

Handle your doll with clean hands to avoid oils and dirt. Avoid excessive bending or pressure on parts.

**Additional Tips:**

* Do not expose the doll to extreme heat or cold.
* Keep the doll away from pets and small children.
* Regularly inspect for loose parts or signs of wear.
* Consider using acid-free tissue paper for extra protection.
