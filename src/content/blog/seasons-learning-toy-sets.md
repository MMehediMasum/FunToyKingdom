Choose a toy set that matches the child’s age. Toys made for younger kids are simpler and safer.

* Rounded edges and smooth surfaces
* Durable and easy to clean materials
* Certified safety standards met

## Parental Tips For Maximizing Learning

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=seasons-learning-toy-sets-offer-great-chances-for-kids-to-explore-and-learn.-parents-play-a-key-role-in-guiding-this-learning-journey.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Seasons Learning Toy Sets offer great chances for kids to explore and learn. Parents play a key role in guiding this learning journey.

With simple actions, parents can help children understand new ideas and enjoy playtime more fully.

### Guided Play Techniques

Join your child during play to help focus their attention. Ask questions that make them think about what they see and do.

Use the toy sets to tell stories or create scenarios that match the seasons. This makes learning fun and meaningful.

* Show how to use the pieces, then let your child try.
* Ask “What do you notice?” or “What happens next?”
* Praise efforts, not just correct answers.

### Encouraging Curiosity

Let your child explore the toy sets freely. Curiosity drives learning and helps kids ask their own questions.

Talk about the changes each season brings and encourage your child to share what they observe outside.

* Encourage your child to describe colors, shapes, and textures.
* Ask “Why do you think this happens in this season?”
* Use real-life examples to connect toys to nature.

### Balancing Screen Time

Limit screen time to keep playtime active and hands-on. Physical toys help build fine motor skills and imagination.

Set clear rules for screen use and plan toy play sessions to fill free time with learning activities.

* Choose specific times for screens and for toy play.
* Offer toy sets as fun alternatives to screens.
* Join your child during play to make it social and engaging.
