## Basic Throwing Techniques

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=playing-with-a-kids-boomerang-outdoor-toy-is-fun-and-helps-develop-hand-eye-coordination.-learning-the-right-throwing-techniques-makes-the-boomerang-fly-correctly-and-return-to-you.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Playing with a kids boomerang outdoor toy is fun and helps develop hand-eye coordination. Learning the right throwing techniques makes the boomerang fly correctly and return to you.

Here are some simple tips on grip, stance, throwing motion, and catching to get you started.



### Grip And Stance

Hold the boomerang firmly but not too tight. Use your thumb on top and fingers underneath. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and face sideways to your target.

* Grip the boomerang near the end of one arm
* Keep your wrist straight
* Stand with your non-throwing shoulder toward your target
* Bend your knees slightly for balance

### Throwing Motion

Throw the boomerang with a smooth motion. Flick your wrist at release for better spin. Aim slightly above the horizon and release at about a 20-degree angle.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Throwing Step** | **Tip** |
| Hold steady | Keep your arm relaxed |
| Bring arm back | Keep elbow at shoulder height |
| Throw forward | Use wrist flick for spin |
| Release angle | About 20 degrees above horizontal |

### Catching Tips

Watch the boomerang as it comes back. Use both hands to catch it safely. Keep your fingers together and palms facing the boomerang.

Try these tips for catching:

* Keep your eyes on the boomerang at all times
* Reach out with both hands
* Clap your hands gently around it
* Step back if it comes too fast

## Maintenance And Storage Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-kids-boomerang-outdoor-toy-clean-and-safe-helps-it-last-longer.-proper-care-avoids-damage-and-keeps-it-fun-to-use.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your kids boomerang outdoor toy clean and safe helps it last longer. Proper care avoids damage and keeps it fun to use.

Follow simple steps to clean, store, and fix minor issues. These tips make sure the boomerang stays in good shape.



### Cleaning Methods

Clean the boomerang after each use to remove dirt and dust. Use mild soap and water for the best results.

A soft cloth or sponge works well to avoid scratching the surface. Dry it fully before storing to stop mold or mildew.

* Use warm water and mild soap
* Wipe gently with a soft cloth or sponge
* Rinse with clean water
* Dry completely with a towel

### Proper Storage

Store the boomerang in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Heat and sun can warp or fade the material.

Keep it flat or hang it to avoid bending. Avoid stacking heavy objects on top to protect its shape.

* Choose a dry, shaded storage area
* Lay the boomerang flat or hang it
* Keep away from heat and sunlight
* Do not place heavy items on top

### Repairing Minor Damages

Small scratches or cracks can be fixed with simple tools. Repairing early stops damage from getting worse.

Use sandpaper to smooth rough edges. For cracks, apply a safe adhesive and let it dry fully before use.

* Sand rough or sharp edges gently
* Use [non-toxic glue](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=non-toxic+glue+for+plastic+and+wood&tag=gearcookware-20) for cracks
* Allow glue to dry completely
* Inspect regularly for new damage
