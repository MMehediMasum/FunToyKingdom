### How Do Motion-activated Dog Toys Work?



Motion-activated dog toys like the QGI and PetDroid balls respond to your dog's movement. They roll, jump, or wiggle automatically to grab your dog's attention. This encourages active play and keeps dogs engaged without constant human interaction, making them perfect for solo playtime.



### Are Interactive Dog Toys Suitable For All Dog Sizes?



Yes, many interactive dog toys come in sizes for small, medium, and large dogs. Brands like Lollyx and JR JUMIAR offer options tailored to different breeds and sizes. Choosing the right size ensures safety and maximizes playtime enjoyment for your dog.



### Can Interactive Toys Help With Puppy Training And Iq?



Interactive dog puzzle toys provide mental challenges that boost IQ and training. They encourage problem-solving and improve focus in puppies. These toys are excellent for teaching commands and rewarding good behavior while keeping puppies entertained and mentally active.



### How Do Rechargeable Interactive Dog Toys Work?



Rechargeable dog toys like SilveWillo and JR JUMIAR use USB charging for convenience. They offer longer playtime without the need for constant battery replacement. These toys often feature automatic movement and sound to keep dogs engaged and active indoors or outdoors.
