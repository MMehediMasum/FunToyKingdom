## Authenticating And Grading Figurines

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=vintage-mcdonald’s-toy-figurines-are-collectible-items-that-many-people-want-to-keep-or-sell.-knowing-how-to-tell-if-a-figurine-is-real-helps-collectors-avoid-fakes.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Vintage McDonald’s toy figurines are collectible items that many people want to keep or sell. Knowing how to tell if a figurine is real helps collectors avoid fakes. Grading the condition of the figurine also affects its value.

This guide explains how to identify genuine pieces, use professional grading services, and spot common signs of wear on vintage McDonald’s toy figurines.

### Identifying Genuine Pieces

Authentic McDonald’s figurines have specific marks and features. Check the base of the toy for the McDonald’s logo and copyright year. These marks show the toy is official.

Look at the colors and details. Real figurines have clear paint and smooth edges. Poor paint or rough plastic may mean the toy is fake.

* Check for the McDonald’s logo on the base
* Look for a copyright year stamp
* Inspect paint quality and details
* Examine the plastic for smoothness

### Professional Grading Services

Professional grading services evaluate the condition and authenticity of your figurine. They give a grade based on factors like paint, shape, and damage. This grade helps buyers know the toy’s quality.

Graded figurines often come with a certificate or label. Using a grading service can increase the toy’s value if it is in good condition.

* Submit the figurine to a trusted grading company
* Receive an official grade and certificate
* Use the grade to prove authenticity and condition
* Better grades can raise the toy’s market value

### Common Signs Of Wear

Wear reduces the value of vintage McDonald’s figurines. Look for paint chips, scratches, and cracks. These signs show the toy has been used or damaged.

Faded colors and loose parts also lower the grade. Clean the toy gently but avoid harsh chemicals that may cause more damage.

* Paint chips and scratches
* Cracks or broken parts
* Faded or dull colors
* Loose or missing pieces
* Surface dirt or grime

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Affects The Value Of Vintage Mcdonald's Toys?

Condition, rarity, and demand significantly affect their value. Mint condition and limited editions usually fetch higher prices. Packaging presence also boosts value. Popular characters and vintage series are highly sought by collectors, increasing their worth.

### How Can I Identify Authentic Vintage Mcdonald's Figurines?

Online marketplaces like eBay, Etsy, and collector forums are ideal. Local toy fairs and auction houses also attract buyers. Listing detailed descriptions and clear photos increases selling chances. Research prices beforehand to set competitive offers.

### Are Vintage Mcdonald's Toys Valuable Investments?
