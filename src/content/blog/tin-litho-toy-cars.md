### Are Tin Litho Toy Cars Safe For Toddlers?



Yes, most tin litho toy cars are safe for toddlers aged 3 and above. They feature smooth edges and non-toxic paint. However, adult supervision is recommended to avoid small parts hazards for younger children under 3 years.

Tin litho toy cars are excellent for both collecting and active play. Their detailed design appeals to collectors. Meanwhile, their sturdy build and fun features make them ideal for everyday play by kids.



### How Portable Are Tin Litho Toy Car Sets?



Most tin litho toy car sets are compact and lightweight, making them highly portable. They easily fit into backpacks or toy bags, perfect for travel or outdoor play. This portability adds convenience for kids and parents on the go.
