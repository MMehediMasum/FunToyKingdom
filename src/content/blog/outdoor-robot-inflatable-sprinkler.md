## Maintenance And Care Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-outdoor-robot-inflatable-sprinkler-in-good-shape-helps-it-last-longer.-regular-care-prevents-damage-and-ensures-smooth-operation-every-season.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your outdoor robot inflatable sprinkler in good shape helps it last longer. Regular care prevents damage and ensures smooth operation every season.

Follow simple steps after each use. Proper cleaning, storing, and fixing small issues keep your sprinkler ready for fun outdoor play.

### Cleaning After Use

Remove dirt and debris by rinsing the inflatable with clean water. Avoid harsh soaps or chemicals that can harm the material.

Let it air dry completely before storing to stop mold and mildew growth.

### Storage Suggestions

* Deflate the sprinkler fully to save space.
* Fold it gently without sharp creases.
* Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
* Keep it away from sharp objects or pets.
* Use a storage bag or box to protect from dust.
