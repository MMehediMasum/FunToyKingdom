## Maintenance And Storage Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-your-outdoor-owl-inflatable-pool-toy-helps-it-last-longer.-proper-maintenance-keeps-it-clean-and-safe-to-use.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of your Outdoor Owl Inflatable Pool Toy helps it last longer. Proper maintenance keeps it clean and safe to use.

Good storage prevents damage and keeps the toy ready for the next fun day in the pool.

### Cleaning After Use

Clean the inflatable after every use to remove dirt and chemicals. Use mild soap and warm water for washing.

Rinse well to avoid soap residue, which can damage the material over time.

* Wipe with a soft cloth or sponge
* Focus on seams and crevices
* Let it air dry completely before storage

### Proper Deflation And Folding

Deflate the owl toy slowly to avoid damage. Press out the air gently from all parts.

Fold the inflatable carefully to keep its shape and avoid creases that can cause leaks.

* Start folding from the smaller sections
* Avoid sharp bends or tight folds
* Keep it flat and smooth before storing

### Safe Storage Practices

Store the inflatable in a cool, dry place. Avoid direct sunlight and extreme temperatures.

Use a storage bag or box to protect it from dust and pests.

* Keep away from sharp objects
* Do not store while damp or wet
* Check the toy regularly for damage
