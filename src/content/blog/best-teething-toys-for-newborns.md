**Teething can be tough—not just for your baby but for you too. You want to ease your little one’s discomfort quickly and safely.**

That’s where the right teething toys come in. Choosing the best teething toys for newborns can make a big difference in calming your baby’s sore gums and keeping them happy. You’ll discover top picks that are gentle, safe, and effective.

Keep reading to find the perfect toy to soothe your baby and bring you both some much-needed relief.




## Why Teething Toys Matter

Teething is a natural process for newborns as their teeth begin to grow. It can cause discomfort and fussiness.

Teething toys help babies cope with this discomfort. They provide relief and keep babies engaged safely.

### Benefits For Newborns

Teething toys help babies develop their mouth muscles. They also encourage hand-eye coordination and motor skills.

Chewing on toys helps babies explore different textures. This exploration supports sensory development early on.

### Soothing Effects On Gums

Teething toys apply gentle pressure on sore gums. This helps reduce pain and swelling for the baby.

Many toys are designed to be chilled. Cold toys provide extra relief by numbing the gums slightly.

* Pressure eases gum pain
* Cold temperature lowers inflammation
* Chewing distracts from discomfort

### Safety Considerations

Choose teething toys made from non-toxic materials. Avoid small parts that could break off and cause choking.

Look for toys that are easy to clean. Keeping toys clean prevents germs and infections in babies.

* Non-toxic and BPA-free materials
* Durable and without small detachable parts
* Easy to wash and sanitize

## Tips For Using Teething Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=teething-toys-help-soothe-your-baby's-sore-gums.-they-offer-safe-relief-during-this-uncomfortable-stage.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Teething toys help soothe your baby's sore gums. They offer safe relief during this uncomfortable stage.

Using teething toys correctly keeps your baby safe and happy. Follow simple tips for best results.

### Cleaning And Maintenance

Clean teething toys regularly to keep germs away. Use warm water and mild soap for washing.

Check toys for damage before and after cleaning. Remove any broken or loose parts immediately.

* Wash toys after each use or daily
* Use a soft brush to remove dirt
* Rinse well to remove soap
* Air dry completely before giving to baby

### Supervision Guidelines

Always watch your baby while they use teething toys. Supervision prevents choking or other accidents.

Do not leave your baby alone with teething toys. Stay close and ready to help if needed.

* Hold the toy if needed
* Check for loose pieces during play
* Remove toy if baby bites too hard
* Keep small toys away from younger siblings

### When To Replace Toys

Replace teething toys if they show wear or damage. Cracks or tears can be unsafe for babies.

Old toys may harbor bacteria even after cleaning. Using new toys helps keep your baby healthy.

* Replace if plastic is cracked or sticky
* Change if fabric is torn or stained
* Discard toys with missing parts
* Follow manufacturer’s age recommendations




## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Are The Best Materials For Teething Toys?

Safe materials include silicone, rubber, and BPA-free plastic. These are non-toxic, durable, and gentle on babies' gums. Avoid toys with small parts or harmful chemicals to ensure safety and comfort during teething.

### When Should Babies Start Using Teething Toys?

Babies typically start using teething toys around 3 to 6 months. This is when their first teeth begin to emerge, and chewing helps soothe gum pain. Always supervise to ensure safe use.

### How Do Teething Toys Soothe Newborns?

Teething toys provide gentle pressure on sore gums, reducing discomfort. They stimulate saliva production, which helps heal irritated areas. Chilling toys can enhance the soothing effect for babies.

### Are Teething Toys Safe For Newborns?

Yes, teething toys designed for newborns are safe when made from non-toxic materials. Always check for age recommendations and avoid small parts to prevent choking hazards. Regular cleaning is essential for hygiene.
