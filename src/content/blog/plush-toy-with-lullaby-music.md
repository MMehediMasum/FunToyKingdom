## Care And Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-your-plush-toy-with-lullaby-music-helps-it-last-longer.-proper-cleaning-and-handling-keep-it-safe-for-children.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of your plush toy with lullaby music helps it last longer. Proper cleaning and handling keep it safe for children.

This guide covers cleaning, handling electronic parts, and storage tips. Follow these to keep the toy soft and working well.

### Cleaning And Washing Guidelines

Always check the label before washing. Remove batteries or electronic parts first. Use gentle cleaning methods to protect the fabric.

* Spot clean with a damp cloth and mild soap
* Hand wash only if the label allows
* Avoid soaking the toy fully to protect music components
* Air dry completely before use

### Handling Electronic Components

The lullaby music part is sensitive. Keep it dry and avoid dropping the toy. Remove batteries if not in use for a long time.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Do | Don't |
| Remove batteries before cleaning | Use water on the speaker area |
| Keep the toy away from sharp objects | Drop or throw the toy |
| Check battery compartment regularly | Use harsh chemicals on electronic parts |

### Storage Recommendations

Store the toy in a cool, dry place. Avoid direct sunlight to keep colors bright and fabric soft.

Use the following tips for storage:

* Keep the toy away from dust by using a sealed bag or box
* Remove batteries to prevent leaks
* Do not compress the toy under heavy items
* Check the toy regularly for damage or dirt
