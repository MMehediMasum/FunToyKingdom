## Features Of Toy Cat Robots

Toy cat robots are small machines made to look and act like real cats. They have many smart parts that help them move and respond to touch.

These robots use sensors and sounds to make playtime fun and interactive for children of all ages.

### Sensor Types And Functions

Toy cat robots use several sensors to detect their environment and react properly. Common sensors include touch, light, and sound sensors.

* **Touch Sensors:**Detect when the robot is petted or tapped.
* **Light Sensors:**Help the robot react to changes in lighting.
* **Sound Sensors:**Allow the robot to hear and respond to voices or noises.

### Movement And Sound Capabilities

These toy cats move smoothly and make sounds to mimic real cat behaviors. Movements include walking, tail wagging, and head turning.
