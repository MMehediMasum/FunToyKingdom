Role-playing makes playtime more interesting. Kids can take turns being the shopkeeper and the customer. This helps with communication skills.

* Shopkeeper greets the customer
* Customer picks items and asks prices
* Shopkeeper scans or presses buttons
* Customer pays with play money
* Shopkeeper gives change and a receipt

Turn playtime into a learning opportunity. Use the cash register toy to practice math and money skills. This makes learning fun and active.

Games can include counting coins, adding prices, or making exact change. You can also teach simple budgeting or saving ideas.

* Count the total price of items
* Practice giving correct change
* Sort coins by size and value
* Create a budget for shopping
* Role-play saving money for bigger purchases
