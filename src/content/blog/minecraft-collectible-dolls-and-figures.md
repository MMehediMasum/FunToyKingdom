## Caring For Your Collection

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-minecraft-collectible-dolls-and-figures-in-great-shape-takes-some-care.-proper-maintenance-helps-them-look-new-and-last-longer.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Minecraft collectible dolls and figures in great shape takes some care. Proper maintenance helps them look new and last longer.

Learn simple ways to clean, store, and display your items safely. These tips keep your collection safe from dust, damage, and fading.

### Cleaning Tips

Dust and dirt can harm your collectibles if left too long. Clean them gently to avoid scratches or paint loss.

* Use a soft brush to remove dust from small crevices.
* Wipe surfaces with a dry microfiber cloth.
* If needed, lightly dampen the cloth with water only—no chemicals.
* Dry the figure fully before putting it back on display.

### Storage Solutions

Safe storage protects your dolls and figures from damage and fading. Avoid places with high heat or humidity.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Storage Option | Benefits | Notes |
| Display case | Keeps dust away | Use UV-protected glass to prevent fading |
| Plastic containers | Protects from moisture and pests | Choose acid-free liners inside |
| Soft fabric pouches | Prevents scratches | Good for moving or travel |

### Display Ideas

A well-thought display shows off your collection and keeps items safe. Use shelves or stands that fit your space and style.

**Display setup tips:**

* Place figures on stable shelves away from direct sunlight.
* Use clear risers to create levels and show each piece.
* Keep space between dolls to avoid scratches.
* Add LED lights for soft, even lighting.
