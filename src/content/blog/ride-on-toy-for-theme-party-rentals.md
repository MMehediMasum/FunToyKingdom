## Safety And Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=ride-on-toys-add-fun-to-theme-party-rentals.-keeping-them-safe-is-very-important.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Ride on toys add fun to theme party rentals. Keeping them safe is very important. Proper care helps avoid accidents and keeps toys working well.

Follow safety and maintenance tips to protect kids and make parties enjoyable. Regular checks and cleaning are key parts.

### Regular Inspections

Check ride on toys before and after each use. Look for broken parts or loose screws. Fix problems quickly to keep toys safe.

* Inspect wheels and steering for damage
* Check batteries and electrical parts if any
* Look for cracks or sharp edges
* Tighten all nuts and bolts
* Replace worn or broken parts immediately

### Sanitization Practices

Clean ride on toys often to stop germs from spreading. Use safe cleaning products that do not harm plastic or paint.

* Wipe down surfaces with disinfectant wipes
* Clean wheels and handles carefully
* Allow toys to dry before use
* Sanitize after each party or rental

### Supervision Guidelines

Always watch children while they use ride on toys. Supervision helps prevent accidents and ensures rules are followed.

* Limit the number of kids using a toy at once
* Set clear rules for safe riding
* Keep the play area free of obstacles
* Stop use if a child rides unsafely
* Make sure age and weight limits are followed
