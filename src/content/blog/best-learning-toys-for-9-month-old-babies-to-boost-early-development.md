Pull-string and cause-and-effect toys help babies understand actions and reactions. Soft teething toys soothe sore gums while engaging their senses. Selecting toys that fit a baby’s age and interests keeps them curious and happy. These learning toys combine fun with important skills, making playtime both enjoyable and educational.
