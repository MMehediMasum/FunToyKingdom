### Preparing The Play Area

Choose a flat and clean surface to play the game. This helps keep the dominoes stable and prevents them from falling too early.

Make sure there is enough space for all players to sit around and reach the dominoes easily.

* Clear a table or floor area

### Basic Rules To Start

Shuffle the domino tiles face down on the play surface. Each player draws seven dominoes to start the game.

The player with the double domino tile begins the game. If no one has a double, redraw the tiles.

* Shuffle all dominoes face down
* Each player picks seven tiles
* Find the player with a double tile to start
* If no doubles, reshuffle and redraw




## How To Play

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=toy-story-dominoes-is-a-fun-way-to-match-characters-and-numbers.-players-use-domino-pieces-featuring-toy-story-characters-and-numbers.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Toy Story Dominoes is a fun way to match characters and numbers. Players use domino pieces featuring Toy Story characters and numbers. The goal is to connect tiles by matching either the character or the number on each end.

Players take turns placing dominoes to build a chain. Scoring points depends on matching tiles correctly. The game ends when a player uses all tiles or no moves remain.

### Matching Characters And Numbers

Each domino piece shows a Toy Story character and a number. You can place a domino next to another if the character or number matches. For example, match Buzz Lightyear with Buzz Lightyear or a tile with number 5 to another with number 5.

* Look for matching characters on the domino ends.
* Check if the numbers on the tiles are the same.
* Place your tile next to a matching end on the board.
* If you cannot match, you must skip your turn.

### Taking Turns And Scoring

Players take turns adding one domino to the chain. You score points based on the tiles you place. Each correct match earns points equal to the number on the matching side.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Action | Points Earned |
| Match character | 10 points |
| Match number | Points equal to number |
| Match both character and number | 20 points |
| Skip turn (no match) | 0 points |

### Winning The Game

The game ends when a player places all their dominoes or no more moves are possible. The player with the most points wins. Keep track of your points to know who is ahead.

**Tips to win:**

1. Use your high-value tiles early to score more points.
2. Block opponents by limiting their matching options.
3. Remember which tiles have been played.
