Keeping your Ant Man collectible doll figure in top shape requires regular care. Proper maintenance helps preserve its details and value.

### Cleaning Tips

Use a soft brush or cloth to remove dust gently. Avoid water on painted areas to prevent damage.

* Use a microfiber cloth for wiping
* Clean small crevices with a soft brush
* Avoid harsh chemicals or solvents
* Do not soak the figure in water

### Storage Solutions

Proper storage keeps the figure safe from dust, sunlight, and physical damage. Use a cool, dry place.

* Handle repairs in a clean, well-lit area
