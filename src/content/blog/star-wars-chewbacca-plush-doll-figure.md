Read these tips to learn easy ways to clean and store your Chewbacca plush doll safely. These steps protect its soft fur and shape.

### Cleaning Techniques

Use gentle cleaning methods to keep your plush soft and fluffy. Avoid harsh chemicals or rough scrubbing that can damage the fabric.

* Spot clean stains with a damp cloth and mild soap.
* Use a soft brush to remove dust from fur.
* Air dry the plush after cleaning; do not use a dryer.
* For deep cleaning, hand wash gently in cold water.
* Do not soak the doll for long periods to protect stuffing.

### Storage Recommendations

Proper storage helps keep your Chewbacca plush safe from damage and dust. Avoid places with moisture or direct sunlight to keep colors bright.
