## Condition And Authenticity

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=gi-joe-vehicles-and-accessories-hold-special-value-for-collectors.-the-condition-and-authenticity-of-these-items-matter-a-lot.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Gi Joe vehicles and accessories hold special value for collectors. The condition and authenticity of these items matter a lot. Knowing how to check them helps you find the real ones.

This guide covers identifying original parts, assessing vehicle wear, and checking packaging and documentation. Each part helps confirm if your Gi Joe set is genuine.

### Identifying Original Parts

Original parts have unique marks and designs. These details tell if a part is real. Fake parts often miss these small touches.

* Look for the Hasbro logo on plastic parts.
* Check for mold numbers under the vehicle or accessory.
* Examine paint colors and styles against known originals.
* Compare metal pieces for weight and finish quality.

### Assessing Vehicle Wear

Wear on vehicles shows how much they were used. Some wear is normal for [old toys](https://funtoykingdom.com/best-toys-for-2-5-year-old/). Too much wear lowers value and can hide damage.

| Wear Type | Signs | Effect on Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paint Chips | Missing paint spots on edges | Reduces value moderately |
| Scratches | Surface marks on body or accessories | Depends on severity |
| Broken Parts | Missing wheels or broken guns | Significantly lowers value |
| Rust or Corrosion | Discoloration on metal parts | Very negative impact |

### Packaging And Documentation

Original packaging adds to a set’s authenticity. Look for sealed boxes and official labels. Paperwork can prove the item's history.

Check these packaging and documentation points:

* Box art matches the vehicle inside.
* Hasbro copyright and year printed clearly.
* Instruction sheets included and unmarked.
* Certificates of authenticity if available.
