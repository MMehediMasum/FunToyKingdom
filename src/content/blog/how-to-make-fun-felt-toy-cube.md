## Materials Needed

Making a fun felt toy cube is a simple and enjoyable craft. You will need some basic materials to get started.

Choosing the right materials helps your toy look great and last longer. Let’s explore what you need.

### Types Of Felt

Felt comes in many colors and thicknesses. Pick the type that suits your toy’s design and feel.

Wool felt is soft and strong. It is good for toys that need durability.

* Wool felt: thick, soft, and lasts long
* Acrylic felt: cheaper, easy to cut
* Blended felt: mix of wool and synthetic fibers
* Craft felt: thin and ideal for small details

### Additional Supplies

You need more than felt to make your toy cube. Gather these extra supplies to help with assembly and decoration.

* Scissors for cutting felt pieces
* Needle and thread for sewing parts together
* Stuffing material like polyester fiberfill
* Fabric glue for quick fixes
* Markers or fabric paint for adding faces or patterns
* Buttons or beads for eyes or other details

## Preparing The Felt Pieces

Making a fun felt toy cube starts with preparing the felt pieces carefully. You need to cut the felt squares evenly and pick colors that look nice together.

This step helps your cube look neat and colorful. It also makes the sewing part easier and faster.

### Measuring And Cutting

First, measure the size of each side of your cube. Use a ruler or measuring tape for accuracy.

Draw squares on the felt sheets with a fabric marker or pencil. Make sure all squares are the same size.

* Use sharp scissors to cut the felt pieces.
* Cut slowly to keep straight edges.
* Count the number of squares needed before cutting.

### Choosing Colors And Patterns

Select colors that look fun and bright. Pick different colors for each side to make the cube interesting.

You can also use felt with simple patterns or shapes to add more fun details to your toy cube.

* Choose at least six different colors for each cube side.
* Use soft colors for a calm look or bright colors for a playful feel.
* Combine plain and patterned felt for variety.

## Sewing The Cube

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=sewing-a-felt-toy-cube-is-a-fun-and-easy-project.-it-requires-some-basic-stitching-skills-and-patience.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Sewing a felt toy cube is a fun and easy project. It requires some basic stitching skills and patience. You will join six felt squares to make a soft cube toy.

This guide covers simple stitching techniques and how to join the sides neatly. Follow along to create your own felt cube toy.

### Basic Stitching Techniques

Use these stitches to sew the felt pieces together. They are strong and easy to do by hand. Practice on scrap felt before starting your cube.

* **Running Stitch:**A simple in-and-out stitch for light seams.
* **Backstitch:**Stronger stitch that creates a solid line of thread.
* **Whip Stitch:**Stitches around the edge to close seams nicely.

### Joining The Sides

Sewing the six sides together forms the cube shape. Use pins to hold edges before stitching. Stitch carefully along the edges for a neat finish.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Step | Action | Tip |
| 1 | Pin two squares right sides together | Align edges carefully |
| 2 | Sew along one edge with backstitch | Keep stitches close |
| 3 | Repeat to attach all sides except one | Leave one side open for stuffing |
| 4 | Turn cube right side out | Push out corners gently |
| 5 | Stuff with cotton or fiberfill | Fill firmly but not too tight |
| 6 | Sew last side closed with whip stitch | Hide stitches inside |

## Stuffing The Cube

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=stuffing-the-felt-toy-cube-is-a-key-step-to-make-it-soft-and-fun.-proper-stuffing-helps-the-cube-keep-its-shape-and-feel-good-to-touch.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Stuffing the felt toy cube is a key step to make it soft and fun. Proper stuffing helps the cube keep its shape and feel good to touch.

Use the right stuffing and fill carefully to make your toy look neat and last longer.

### Choosing The Right Stuffing

Pick stuffing that is soft, lightweight, and safe for children. Polyester fiberfill is a common choice. It is clean and easy to use.

You can also use cotton or wool stuffing. Avoid heavy or hard materials that can make the cube misshapen.

* Polyester fiberfill is light and fluffy
* Cotton stuffing feels soft and natural
* Wool stuffing adds firmness but stays soft
* Avoid beads or beans for young kids

### Filling Tips For Shape

Fill the cube evenly to keep it looking square. Push stuffing gently into corners and edges first.

Do not overfill. Too much stuffing makes the felt stretch and lose shape. Add stuffing bit by bit for a nice, firm feel.

* Start with small amounts of stuffing
* Use a pencil or stick to push stuffing into corners
* Check the cube shape as you fill
* Close the cube when it feels firm but soft

## Safety Tips For Kids

Making a felt toy cube is fun and safe if you follow some easy tips. Kids can enjoy crafting while staying safe and happy.

Here are some important safety tips to keep in mind when creating a felt toy cube. These tips ensure a fun and secure crafting experience for your child.

### Handling Needles And Scissors

Needles and scissors can be dangerous if not used carefully. Teach kids the right way to handle them.

* Use round-tip scissors for safety.
* Keep fingers away from the cutting edge.
* Store needles in a safe place when not in use.

### Safe Materials Selection

Choosing the right materials is key to making safe toys. Ensure all materials are non-toxic and kid-friendly.
