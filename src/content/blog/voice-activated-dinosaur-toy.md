## Voice Activation Features

The voice activated dinosaur toy listens to your voice commands. It reacts and moves based on what you say. This makes playtime more fun and hands-free.

Kids can control the toy by speaking simple words. The toy understands these and responds with sounds and actions. It helps children learn cause and effect.

### How Voice Commands Work

The toy uses a built-in microphone to hear your voice. It then processes the sound to find commands it knows. This happens fast so the toy can react quickly.

Common commands include words like "Roar," "Walk," or "Dance." The toy is programmed to respond only to these specific words to avoid confusion.

* Microphone captures your voice
* Processor matches words with commands
* Toy performs the matching action

### Interactive Responses

The dinosaur toy makes sounds and moves its parts when it hears commands. It can roar, walk, nod, or swing its tail. This creates a lively play experience.

It also reacts differently to certain tones. For example, a loud command might make it roar louder. This interaction keeps kids engaged and entertained.

* Roars and growls in response
* Moves head and tail
* Walks forward or backward
* Changes reaction based on voice tone

### Customization Options

Some voice activated dinosaur toys let you customize commands. You can teach it new words or change existing ones. This makes the toy more personal and fun.

Other options include changing sound volume and response speed. You can adjust these settings to match your child’s preferences and play style.

* Add new voice commands
* Adjust sound volume
* Set how fast the toy reacts
* Choose different movement patterns
