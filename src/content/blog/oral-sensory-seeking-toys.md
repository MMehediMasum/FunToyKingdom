## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Oral Sensory Seeking Toys?



Oral sensory seeking toys are chewable items designed to provide oral stimulation. They help reduce anxiety, improve focus, and support sensory needs in children and adults with autism, ADHD, or SPD. These toys aid in calming and oral motor development through safe chewing.



### How Do Chew Necklaces Help Kids With Autism?



Chew necklaces offer safe oral stimulation that helps reduce stress and fidgeting in autistic children. They promote self-regulation by providing a sensory outlet. These necklaces are durable, non-toxic, and wearable, making them accessible sensory tools for daily use.



### Are Sensory Chew Toys Safe For Toddlers?



Yes, sensory chew toys made from food-grade silicone are safe for toddlers. They are free from harmful chemicals like BPA and phthalates. These toys support oral motor skills and teething relief while being easy to clean and durable for everyday use.



### Can Chew Toys Reduce Anxiety In Adhd Children?



Chew toys help ADHD children by providing calming oral stimulation. They reduce restlessness and improve concentration by channeling sensory needs safely. Using chew toys can lower anxiety and support better emotional regulation for kids with ADHD.



### How To Choose The Best Sensory Chew Toy?



Choose sensory chew toys made from non-toxic, durable silicone. Consider texture variety, size, and wearability like necklaces or bracelets. Ensure they meet safety standards and suit the child's sensory preferences and age for effective oral motor support.



### What Benefits Do Fidget Bracelets Offer For Sensory Needs?



Fidget bracelets provide tactile stimulation and promote calmness. They help sensory kids and adults focus by reducing stress and restlessness. These stretchy bracelets are discreet, easy to wear, and can be used anytime for sensory regulation and anxiety relief.
