### Supervision And Safety Gear

Adult supervision is key when children play on the Shark Ride on Float. An adult should always be nearby to help if needed. Kids should never be left alone in or near water.

Safety gear like life jackets adds an extra layer of protection. Make sure the life jacket fits well and is approved for water use. Sunglasses and hats can protect kids from sun glare and heat.

* Always watch children closely while they float
* Use properly fitting life jackets for all kids
* Keep water safety rules clear and simple
* Have a first aid kit nearby for emergencies

### Proper Float Maintenance

Check the Shark Ride on Float before each use. Look for holes, leaks, or weak spots that could cause problems. Fix any damage right away or do not use the float.

Keep the float clean and dry when not in use. Store it in a cool, shaded place to avoid sun damage. Proper care helps the float last longer and stay safe.

* Inspect the float for damage before every use
* Patch small holes with repair kits made for floats
* Clean the float with mild soap and water
* Store the float away from direct sunlight and sharp objects
