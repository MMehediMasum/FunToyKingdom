## How To Set Up And Maintain

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-up-your-outdoor-skunk-inflatable-pool-toy-is-easy-with-the-right-steps.-proper-care-helps-it-last-longer-and-stay-fun.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting up your outdoor skunk inflatable pool toy is easy with the right steps. Proper care helps it last longer and stay fun.

This guide shows you how to pick a good spot, inflate it safely, and keep it clean and stored well.

### Choosing The Right Spot

Pick a flat area free of sharp objects like rocks or sticks. This keeps the inflatable safe and stable.

Make sure the spot gets some sun but also has shade to avoid too much heat on the inflatable.

* Clear the ground of stones and debris
* Use a soft surface like grass or a tarp
* Avoid areas with direct strong wind
* Choose a place near a water source if possible

### Inflation Tips

Use an electric or hand pump to fill the pool toy. Avoid mouth inflation to save time and energy.

Inflate slowly and check for firm but not over-tight areas. Over-inflation can cause damage.

* Check the air valve is clean and closed properly
* Stop inflating if the inflatable feels too hard
* Press gently to test firmness
* Fix any small leaks before use

### Cleaning And Storage

Clean the inflatable with mild soap and water after each use. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the material.

Dry it completely before folding to prevent mold. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

* Use a soft cloth or sponge for cleaning
* Rinse well to remove soap residue
* Air dry fully before packing
* Fold gently without sharp creases
* Keep away from heat sources and sharp tools
