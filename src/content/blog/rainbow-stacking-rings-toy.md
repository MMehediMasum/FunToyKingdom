## Care And Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=rainbow-stacking-rings-toy-needs-regular-care-to-stay-safe-and-fun.-taking-simple-steps-helps-keep-the-toy-in-good-shape.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Rainbow Stacking Rings Toy needs regular care to stay safe and fun. Taking simple steps helps keep the toy in good shape.

Follow these tips to clean, store, and check the toy for damage. This keeps it safe for children to use every day.

### Cleaning Methods

Clean the rings often to remove dirt and germs. Use mild soap and warm water. Avoid harsh chemicals that can harm the toy.

* Wipe each ring with a soft cloth dipped in soapy water.
* Rinse with clean water to remove soap.
* Dry the rings well with a towel or let air dry.
* Do not soak wooden rings for a long time to avoid swelling.

### Storage Suggestions

Store the rings in a dry, cool place. Keep them away from direct sunlight and heat to avoid fading or cracking.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Keep rings in a box or bag | Leave rings outside or in damp places |
| Stack rings neatly to avoid damage | Throw rings loosely in a drawer |
| Check storage area for pests | Ignore signs of mold or insects |

### Inspecting For Wear And Tear

Look over the rings regularly for cracks, splinters, or loose parts. Fix or replace damaged rings to keep children safe.

1. Examine each ring carefully for damage.
2. Check for sharp edges or rough spots.
3. Test the stability of stacked rings.
4. Discard any rings that look unsafe.
