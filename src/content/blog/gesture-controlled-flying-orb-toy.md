The Flying Orb toy moves using your hand gestures. It senses motion and reacts quickly. Learning to control it is simple and fun.

This guide covers the main ways to guide your orb and tips to keep it flying smoothly.

### Gesture Techniques

Use your hands near the orb to send commands. Move your palm up to make it rise. Move your palm down to lower it. You can also push sideways to change direction.

* Hold your hand about 6 inches below the orb to lift it.
* Move your hand slowly sideways to steer left or right.
* Wave your hand quickly below to make the orb drop.
* Keep your fingers spread for better sensor detection.
