**Oral sensory toys help children and adults with sensory processing needs stay calm and focused. These toys provide safe chewing options for people with autism, ADHD, SPD, and other special needs.**



Sensory chew toys come in many shapes and sizes, such as silicone chew tubes, chewy necklaces, and teething sticks. They offer oral motor stimulation that can reduce anxiety and help control fidgeting. Many designs suit different ages, from babies to adults, supporting oral sensory development.



These toys are made from non-toxic, durable materials to ensure safety during use. Using oral sensory toys regularly can improve concentration and comfort for sensory seekers. Choosing the right type helps meet individual needs and preferences. Overall, oral sensory toys serve as effective tools for managing sensory challenges in daily life.

### How Do Chew Necklaces Benefit Kids With Sensory Needs?



Chew necklaces offer a discreet, portable way for kids to manage oral sensory needs. They reduce fidgeting, biting, and anxiety. Made from safe silicone, these necklaces provide oral motor stimulation and help improve concentration for children with autism, ADHD, or SPD.



### Are Sensory Chew Toys Safe For Aggressive Chewers?



Yes, many sensory chew toys are made with durable, non-toxic silicone. Xtra tough chew sticks are designed for aggressive chewers. They withstand strong biting and provide long-lasting oral sensory relief without breaking or posing choking hazards.



### Can Oral Sensory Toys Help Reduce Fidgeting In Children?



Oral sensory toys redirect fidgeting by providing a focused oral outlet. They help children with ADHD, autism, and SPD stay calm and attentive. Using chewies or chew necklaces can improve concentration and decrease disruptive behaviors in sensory-sensitive kids.



### What Age Groups Benefit From Silicone Chew Toys?



Silicone chew toys are safe for babies, toddlers, and older children. They support oral motor development for ages 3 months and up. Sensory chew tools also help adults with sensory processing needs manage anxiety and improve oral habits safely.
