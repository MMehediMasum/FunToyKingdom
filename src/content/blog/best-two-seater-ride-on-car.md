## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-two-seater-ride-on-car-in-good-shape-is-easy-with-regular-care.-simple-maintenance-helps-it-last-longer-and-work-better.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your two-seater ride on car in good shape is easy with regular care. Simple maintenance helps it last longer and work better.

Follow these tips to take care of your ride on car safely and avoid common problems.

### Battery Care

Check the battery often to make sure it stays charged. A weak battery can stop the car from running.

Charge the battery after every use or if the car sits idle for many days. Use the charger that fits your battery type.

* Keep battery terminals clean and tight
* Do not let the battery drain completely
* Store battery in a cool, dry place

### Cleaning And Storage

Clean your ride on car to keep it looking good and to prevent dirt from causing damage. Use a soft cloth and mild soap.

Store the car inside or under a cover when not in use. Keep it away from rain and direct sunlight to protect its parts.

* Wipe off dust and mud after each use
* Remove the battery before long storage
* Keep tires inflated and check for cracks

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

If your ride on car does not start, check the battery first. Look for loose wires or dirt on the connections.

Listen for unusual sounds or check if the wheels spin freely. These signs can help find problems early.

* Test the battery voltage with a multimeter
* Clean or tighten battery connections
* Inspect motor and gears for damage
* Replace blown fuses or broken parts
