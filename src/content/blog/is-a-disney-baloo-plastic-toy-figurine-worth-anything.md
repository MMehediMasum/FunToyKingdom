## Authenticity Checks

Finding out if your Disney Baloo toy is genuine can be tricky. It involves careful observation and some research.

Knowing what to look for can help you avoid fakes and make sure your toy is worth something.

### Identifying Originals

Original Disney Baloo figurines have certain features. These details help you identify them as authentic.

Check the toy's packaging for official Disney logos. These logos are usually printed clearly and without errors.

* Look for copyright information on the toy or its packaging.
* Original toys often have a unique serial number.
* The paint should be smooth, with no smudges.

### Avoiding Counterfeits

Fake Disney toys can look real but have flaws. Knowing these flaws helps you avoid buying a counterfeit.

Counterfeits often have poor quality materials. They might feel light or cheap compared to real ones.

* Check for misspellings on the toy or box.
* Counterfeits might have blurry or faded logos.
* Authentic toys have consistent paint colors and details.
