## Capsule Toy Machines In Japan

Capsule toy machines, called "gachapon" or "gashapon," are very popular in Japan. They offer small toys or figures inside plastic capsules.

These machines are found in many places, such as shopping malls, arcades, and train stations. People of all ages enjoy using them.

### Popularity And Culture

Capsule toy machines are a big part of Japanese culture. They provide fun and surprise with every capsule.

* They include popular characters from anime and manga.
* They are affordable, usually costing 100 to 500 yen per capsule.
* Collectors often seek rare or limited-edition toys.
* Many machines change themes to match seasons or events.

### How They Work

Capsule toy machines work simply. You insert coins, turn a knob, and a capsule drops out.

| --- | --- |
| Step | Action |
| 1 | Insert the correct amount of coins |
| 2 | Turn the knob or handle |
| 3 | Capsule drops into the collection slot |
| 4 | Open the capsule to see the toy inside |

They offer unique, limited-edition collectibles not found elsewhere. The toys appeal due to their variety, quality, and cultural themes. Capsule toys combine surprise, affordability, and nostalgia, attracting collectors and casual buyers alike.
