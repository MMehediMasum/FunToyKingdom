**You’ve spent hours piecing together your jigsaw puzzle, and now it’s time to make it shine on your wall. But how do you frame a jigsaw puzzle without damaging it or losing its charm?**

Whether your puzzle is a stunning landscape or a fun family memory, framing it the right way can turn your hard work into a beautiful display. You’ll discover simple, step-by-step tips to frame your puzzle like a pro. Keep reading, and you’ll learn how to protect your masterpiece and showcase it with pride.



## Choosing The Right Frame

Framing a jigsaw puzzle keeps it safe and shows off your work. Picking the right frame makes a big difference.

Think about the puzzle size, style, and where you will hang it. These points help you choose the best frame.

### Types Of Frames

You can find many frame styles. Some fit puzzles better than others. Here are common types to consider.

* Shadow Box Frames: Deep frames that hold puzzles with space around.
* Floating Frames: Show the puzzle edges for a modern look.
* Standard Frames: Simple frames that fit puzzles exactly.
* Custom Frames: Made to fit your puzzle’s size and style.

### Measuring Your Puzzle

Measure your puzzle carefully. This ensures the frame fits without gaps or pressure.

| Step | How to Measure |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Place the puzzle on a flat surface. |
| 2 | Use a ruler or tape to measure width and height. |
| 3 | Write down the exact size in inches or centimeters. |
| 4 | Add extra space if using a shadow box frame. |

### Material Options

Frames come in many materials. Each has a look and feel that changes your puzzle’s display.

* Wood: Classic and warm. Can be painted or stained.
* Metal: Sleek and modern. Often lightweight and strong.
* Plastic: Affordable and versatile. Comes in many colors.
* Glass or Acrylic: Protects the puzzle surface from dust.




## Preparing The Puzzle

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=framing-a-jigsaw-puzzle-takes-care-and-patience.-preparing-the-puzzle-well-ensures-it-stays-intact-and-looks-great.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Framing a jigsaw puzzle takes care and patience. Preparing the puzzle well ensures it stays intact and looks great.

This guide covers cleaning, gluing, and drying your puzzle before framing it.

### Cleaning And Flattening

Before framing, clean the puzzle gently to remove dust and dirt. Use a soft brush or a dry cloth. Avoid using water because it can damage the pieces.

Flatten the puzzle by placing it under a heavy book or board. Leave it for several hours or overnight. This step stops the puzzle from bending or warping.

### Applying Puzzle Glue

Use special puzzle glue to hold the pieces together. Spread the glue evenly over the puzzle surface. You can use a sponge or a brush for this.

* Start from the center and move outward
* Apply a thin, even layer of glue
* Cover the entire front surface
* Do not soak the puzzle; use glue sparingly
* Flip the puzzle and glue the back for extra strength

### Drying Time Tips

| Drying Method | Time Needed | Tips |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flat Surface | 4–6 hours | Keep puzzle flat and avoid touching |
| Under Weight | Overnight | Use a heavy book to keep it flat |
| Air Dry | 12–24 hours | Place in a dry, dust-free area |

## Mounting The Puzzle

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=mounting-your-finished-jigsaw-puzzle-helps-protect-it-and-makes-it-easier-to-display.-it-keeps-the-puzzle-flat-and-prevents-pieces-from-shifting.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Mounting your finished jigsaw puzzle helps protect it and makes it easier to display. It keeps the puzzle flat and prevents pieces from shifting.

This guide explains how to mount your puzzle securely using the right backing board and mounting tape.

### Selecting A Backing Board

Choose a backing board that is sturdy and smooth. Foam board or cardboard works well for most puzzles.

The board should be slightly larger than your puzzle. This helps support the edges and keeps the puzzle flat.

* Foam board is light and strong
* Cardboard is easy to cut and affordable
* Avoid soft or bendable materials
* Use a clean, dry surface to mount

### Attaching The Puzzle Securely

Keep the puzzle in one piece before mounting. Use glue or adhesive sheets designed for puzzles if needed.

Place the puzzle on the backing board carefully. Press down gently to ensure it sticks without bending.

* Check puzzle edges line up with board edges
* Press evenly to avoid air bubbles
* Let glue dry completely before framing
* Handle the mounted puzzle with care

### Using Mounting Tape

Mounting tape helps hold the puzzle firmly on the backing board. Use double-sided tape for best results.

Apply small strips of tape under puzzle edges and in the center. This keeps the puzzle from shifting over time.

* Use acid-free tape to avoid damage
* Do not use too much tape to prevent bulging
* Press tape firmly to secure puzzle
* Check tape position before mounting




## Assembling The Frame

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=framing-a-jigsaw-puzzle-helps-protect-and-display-your-work.-it-makes-the-puzzle-look-neat-and-ready-to-hang.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Framing a jigsaw puzzle helps protect and display your work. It makes the puzzle look neat and ready to hang.

This guide covers how to fit the puzzle into the frame, secure the frame back, and add hanging hardware.

### Fitting The Puzzle Into The Frame

Make sure the puzzle is complete and glued together before framing. Let the glue dry fully.

Place the puzzle gently inside the frame. Check if it fits snugly without bending or forcing.

* Use a frame slightly larger than the puzzle size
* Lay the puzzle flat on a clean surface
* Slide the puzzle into the frame carefully

### Securing The Frame Back

After fitting the puzzle, secure the back of the frame to hold it in place. This keeps the puzzle safe.

Use frame clips, nails, or tabs depending on your frame type. Make sure the back fits tightly.

* Check for loose spots around the frame
* Press the back firmly against the puzzle
* Fix clips or tabs evenly on all sides

### Adding Hanging Hardware

Add hanging hardware to the back of the frame to hang your puzzle on a wall. Choose hardware that fits your frame size.

Attach hooks, wire, or D-rings securely. Check that the hardware holds well before hanging.

* Use two D-rings for large frames
* Use a sawtooth hanger for small frames
* Tighten screws firmly into the frame

## Displaying Your Puzzle

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=after-framing-your-jigsaw-puzzle,-you-want-to-show-it-off-nicely.-a-good-display-keeps-your-puzzle-safe-and-looks-great-in-your-home.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

After framing your jigsaw puzzle, you want to show it off nicely. A good display keeps your puzzle safe and looks great in your home.

Think about where and how to place it. The right spot and care make your puzzle last longer.

### Choosing The Right Location

Pick a place that is dry and free from direct sunlight. Avoid areas with high humidity or temperature changes. These can damage the puzzle over time.

* Choose a wall away from windows
* Avoid spots near heating or cooling vents
* Keep it out of reach of pets and children
* Consider a place where you can enjoy looking at it

### Lighting Tips

Good lighting helps show off the colors and details of your puzzle. Use soft, indirect light to avoid glare on the glass.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lighting Type | Best Use | Notes |
| Natural Light | Daytime display | Keep away from direct sun |
| LED Spotlights | Highlight puzzle | Use warm white bulbs |
| Fluorescent Lights | General room light | May cause glare |
| Incandescent Bulbs | Soft lighting | Can cause heat buildup |

### Maintaining The Display

Keep your puzzle clean and protected. Dust the frame and glass regularly. Check for any signs of damage or looseness.

#### Maintenance Checklist

* Dust frame weekly with a soft cloth
* Clean glass with glass cleaner monthly
* Check frame corners for tightness
* Keep away from moisture and heat
* Handle with care during cleaning




## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Prepare A Jigsaw Puzzle For Framing?

Start by completing the puzzle on a flat surface. Apply puzzle glue evenly on top. Let it dry completely before framing to ensure stability.

### What Materials Are Needed To Frame A Jigsaw Puzzle?

You’ll need puzzle glue, a frame with glass, a backing board, and mounting tape or adhesive. These protect and showcase your puzzle effectively.

### Can I Frame A Puzzle Without Glue?

Yes, but glue secures the pieces permanently. Without glue, the puzzle may fall apart or shift inside the frame over time.

### How Do I Choose The Right Frame Size For My Puzzle?

Measure your puzzle’s dimensions accurately. Pick a frame slightly larger for a mat border or exact size for a snug fit.
