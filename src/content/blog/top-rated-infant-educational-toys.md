## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Is Best For Infant Educational Stacking Toys?



Stacking toys are ideal for infants aged 6 months and up. They help develop motor skills and hand-eye coordination. Toys like Fisher-Price's blocks are perfect for this stage, promoting sorting and cognitive learning through play.



### How Do Montessori Toys Benefit Infant Development?



Montessori toys encourage independent learning and sensory exploration. They support fine motor skills, creativity, and problem-solving. For example, the 6-in-1 Montessori toy set helps babies aged 6-18 months build essential developmental skills naturally.



### Are Teething Toys Safe For Infants Under One Year?



Yes, teething toys made from silicone or soft materials are safe for infants. They soothe gums and improve sensory skills. Products like Yetonamr’s pull string teether are designed for babies 6-12 months, ensuring safe chewing and comfort.



### What Features Should I Look For In Infant Musical Toys?



Choose musical toys with lights, sounds, and interactive features. They stimulate auditory and visual senses while encouraging cause and effect understanding. The interactive pop-up animal toy with music suits babies aged 9-18 months perfectly.



### How Do High-contrast Toys Help Newborns?



High-contrast toys, like black and white sets, enhance newborns’ visual development. They attract attention and stimulate early brain growth. Toys such as URMYWO’s tummy time visual stimulation kit are ideal for babies 0-3 months.



### Can Bead Maze Toys Improve Toddler Skills?



Yes, bead maze toys improve fine motor skills, hand-eye coordination, and problem-solving. Melissa & Doug’s wooden bead maze is great for infants and toddlers, encouraging active play and cognitive development.
