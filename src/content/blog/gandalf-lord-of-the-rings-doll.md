### Cleaning And Maintenance

Keep dust and dirt off your Gandalf doll by cleaning it regularly. Use gentle methods to avoid damage to the paint and fabric.

* Use a soft brush or cloth to remove dust.
* Spot clean fabric parts with a damp cloth and mild soap.
* Do not soak the doll in water.
* Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners.
* Keep away from direct sunlight to prevent fading.

### Display Ideas For Fans

Show your Gandalf doll in a place where it can be admired safely. Choose a spot that suits the theme of your collection or room.

**Display Tips:**

* Place the doll on a shelf with good lighting but no direct sun.
* Use a clear acrylic case to protect it from dust and pets.
* Arrange the doll with other Lord of the Rings collectibles for a themed display.
* Add a small stand to keep it upright and stable.

### Storage Recommendations

Store the Gandalf doll properly if you do not display it. Good storage prevents damage from dust, moisture, and pests.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Storage Tip** | **Reason** |
| Keep in a cool, dry place | Prevents mold and fabric damage |
