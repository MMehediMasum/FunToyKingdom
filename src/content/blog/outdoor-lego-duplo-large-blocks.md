## Safety Tips For Outdoor Play

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=playing-with-outdoor-lego-duplo-large-blocks-is-fun-and-helps-children-learn.-safety-is-very-important-during-outdoor-play.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Playing with Outdoor Lego Duplo Large Blocks is fun and helps children learn. Safety is very important during outdoor play. Parents and caregivers should create a safe environment.

Use these tips to keep children safe while they enjoy building and playing outside. Watch them closely and keep their play area clean.

### Choosing Safe Play Areas

Pick a flat, open space free from sharp objects. Avoid places near roads or water. Grass or soft ground helps protect children from falls.

* Check for stones, sticks, or trash in the play area.
* Keep children away from busy streets and driveways.
* Make sure the area is shaded to prevent sunburn.
* Ensure the space is large enough for children to move safely.

### Supervision Guidelines

Always watch children closely during outdoor play. Stay within arm’s reach of toddlers. Older children still need regular checks.

1. Set clear rules for playing with blocks.
2. Keep small parts away from young children to avoid choking.
3. Intervene if children start rough play.
4. Encourage sharing and teamwork.

### Maintenance And Cleaning

Clean the blocks often to stop germs from spreading. Check blocks for damage that could hurt children.

| Cleaning Step | How to Do It | Frequency |
| Wipe Blocks | Use a damp cloth with mild soap | After outdoor play |
