## Materials Needed

Making a wooden toy necklace is a fun and creative project. You need the right materials to make it safe and beautiful.

This guide helps you choose wood, string, clasps, and decorations for your necklace.

### Choosing The Right Wood

Pick wood that is light and smooth to keep the necklace comfortable to wear. Avoid wood with splinters or rough edges.

Softwoods like pine or basswood work well. Hardwoods like maple or birch are strong but may need extra sanding.

* Lightweight for easy wear
* Smooth surface to avoid splinters
* Non-toxic and safe for children
* Easy to cut and shape

### Selecting String And Clasps

Choose string that is strong and flexible to hold the wooden pieces securely. Nylon or cotton cords are good options.

Clasps should be easy to open and close. Metal or plastic clasps work well and keep the necklace safe on the neck.

* Strong, durable string
* Soft texture for comfort
* Secure clasps that are easy to use
* Non-toxic materials

### Additional Decorative Elements

Add decorations to make the necklace unique. Paint, beads, or small charms can add color and fun details.

Make sure all decorations are safe and attached well. Avoid small parts that can fall off easily.

* Non-toxic paint or wood stain
* Colorful beads or buttons
* Small wooden or plastic charms
* Safe glue or sealant to hold decorations

## Preparing The Wood Pieces

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=preparing-the-wood-pieces-is-an-important-first-step-in-making-a-wooden-toy-necklace.-you-need-to-make-sure-each-piece-is-the-right-shape-and-size.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Preparing the wood pieces is an important first step in making a wooden toy necklace. You need to make sure each piece is the right shape and size. This makes the necklace safe and nice to wear.

Use simple tools and work carefully to get the best results. Focus on cutting, sanding, and drilling to prepare the wood properly.

### Cutting And Shaping

Start by cutting the wood into small shapes for the necklace. Use a small saw or a craft knife. You can cut circles, hearts, or any simple shape you like. Make sure the pieces are not too big or heavy.

* Choose soft wood like pine or basswood.
* Mark the shapes on the wood with a pencil.
* Cut slowly to keep the edges even.
* Use a small file to refine the shapes.

### Sanding For Smoothness

Sanding makes the wood pieces smooth and safe to touch. It removes rough spots and splinters. Use sandpaper of different grits, starting rough and finishing smooth.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Sandpaper Grit | Use |
| 80 | Remove big rough areas |
| 120 | Even out the shape |
| 220 | Make the surface very smooth |

### Drilling Holes For Stringing

Drill small holes in each wood piece to string them together. Use a thin drill bit that fits the string or cord. Be careful to drill in the right spot and keep the hole centered.

* Choose a drill bit about 2 to 3 mm wide.
* Hold the wood steady on a soft surface.
* Drill slowly to avoid cracking the wood.
* Check the hole size by threading the string through it.

## Assembling The Necklace

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=assembling-the-wooden-toy-necklace-is-a-fun-and-simple-process.-you-will-need-to-string-the-wooden-pieces,-secure-the-ends,-and-adjust-the-length-to-fit-perfectly.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Assembling the wooden toy necklace is a fun and simple process. You will need to string the wooden pieces, secure the ends, and adjust the length to fit perfectly.

Each part is important to make sure the necklace is safe and comfortable to wear.

### Stringing The Wooden Pieces

Start by threading the wooden beads or shapes onto a strong string or cord. Use a needle if the holes are small. Arrange the pieces in your desired pattern for a nice look.

* Cut a length of cord longer than the necklace size
* Thread the pieces one by one carefully
* Push pieces close together but not too tight
* Check the pattern before finishing

### Securing The Ends

Tie strong knots at both ends of the cord to keep the pieces from falling off. You can add a drop of glue on the knots for extra hold. Attach clasps or loops if you want an easy way to open and close the necklace.

| Type of End | Description | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simple Knot | Basic tie at cord ends | Holds beads securely |
| Glue Reinforced | Knot with glue drop | Extra strength |
| Clasp Attachment | Metal or plastic clasp | Easy to wear and remove |

### Adjusting The Length

Try the necklace on to see if the length fits well. You can add or remove wooden pieces to make it longer or shorter. Cut the cord and retie the ends if needed.

1. Put the necklace around your neck
2. Check the fit and comfort
3. Add or remove beads for length change
4. Cut excess cord carefully
5. Secure new ends with knots or clasps

## Finishing Touches

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=finishing-touches-make-your-wooden-toy-necklace-look-complete-and-polished.-these-steps-protect-the-wood-and-add-beauty.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Finishing touches make your wooden toy necklace look complete and polished. These steps protect the wood and add beauty.

Careful finishing helps your necklace last longer and feel smooth to wear.

### Applying Sealant Or Paint

Use a sealant to protect the wood from water and dirt. Choose clear sealants to keep the natural look.

You can also paint the necklace with non-toxic paint. Bright colors make the necklace fun and attractive.

* Apply sealant with a soft brush
* Let it dry completely before handling
* Use non-toxic, child-safe paint or sealant
* Apply multiple thin coats for better coverage

### Adding Charms Or Beads

Add charms or beads to give your necklace extra style. Pick pieces that match the wood and colors.

Secure charms well so they do not fall off during play or wear.

* Choose lightweight charms for comfort
* Use strong thread or wire to attach beads
* Mix shapes and sizes for a fun look
* Place charms evenly around the necklace

### Polishing For Shine

Polish the wood to make it smooth and shiny. Polishing also removes any rough spots.

Use a soft cloth and gentle polish made for wood. Rub in small circles until the shine appears.

* Use natural oils like beeswax or mineral oil
* Apply polish with a soft cloth
* Buff the wood after applying polish
* Repeat polishing for extra shine

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Materials Are Needed For A Wooden Toy Necklace?

You need wooden beads, string or cord, non-toxic paint, and sealant. Optional tools include sandpaper and a drill for bead holes.

### How Do I Safely Finish Wooden Beads For Necklaces?

Use non-toxic paint and sealants to ensure safety. Sand beads smooth to avoid splinters before painting and sealing.

### Can Children Wear Wooden Toy Necklaces Safely?

Yes, if beads are large enough to avoid choking hazards. Always supervise young children and use safe, non-toxic materials.

### How Long Does It Take To Make A Wooden Toy Necklace?

It typically takes 1-2 hours, including sanding, painting, drying, and assembling beads on the string.
