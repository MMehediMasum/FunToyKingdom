Rare Megatron toys have specific details that set them apart. Look carefully at these features when checking a toy.

## Caring For Megatron Figures

Megatron collectible toys need proper care to keep them looking great. Small details and moving parts require careful handling.

Following good care habits helps preserve your figure’s value and appearance over time.

### Storage Tips

Store your Megatron figure in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. This prevents fading and damage to plastic parts.

* Keep the figure upright to avoid warping
* Use dust covers or display cases
* Avoid areas with high humidity or extreme temperatures
* Separate parts to prevent scratches

### Cleaning And Maintenance

Regular cleaning keeps your Megatron figure free from dust and dirt. Use gentle methods to protect paint and joints.
