Dog rope toys help clean teeth and reduce plaque buildup. They also satisfy chewing instincts and promote healthy play. These toys strengthen the bond between you and your puppy through interactive games like tug of war.



### Are Cotton Rope Toys Safe For Small Dogs?



Yes, 100% natural cotton rope toys are safe for small dogs. They are non-toxic, durable, and gentle on teeth. Cotton fibers also help floss dogs’ teeth naturally during chewing and tugging sessions.



### How Do Rope Toys Help With Puppy Teething?



Rope toys soothe sore gums by providing a soft, chewable texture. Chewing these toys relieves discomfort and prevents destructive chewing habits. They also encourage healthy jaw development and keep puppies entertained.



### Can Dog Rope Toys Prevent Boredom And Anxiety?



Yes, interactive rope toys engage dogs mentally and physically. Tug of war and chewing reduce stress and destructive behaviors. They offer a fun outlet for energy and keep dogs stimulated indoors or outdoors.



### What Size Rope Toy Suits Medium Dogs Best?



Medium dogs benefit from rope toys around 11 to 14 inches long. Toys with multiple knots and tennis balls add extra durability and fun. Always choose toys that match your dog’s chewing strength to avoid choking risks.
