Charge the battery fully before first use. Avoid letting the battery drain completely.

Store the battery in a cool, dry place. Keep battery terminals clean and free from corrosion.

* Charge battery for 8-12 hours at a time
* Do not overcharge to prevent damage
* Remove battery if not used for long periods
* Check battery connections regularly

### Cleaning And Storage

Clean the jeep after each use to remove dirt and dust. Use a soft cloth and mild soap with water.

Store the jeep indoors or under a cover. Protect it from rain and direct sunlight to avoid damage.

* Wipe down plastic and metal parts gently
* Dry the jeep completely before storage
* Keep it in a dry, cool area
* Cover the jeep to prevent dust buildup

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the jeep does not start, check the battery charge first. Loose wires can also cause problems.

For slow movement, inspect the wheels and motor. Clean or tighten parts if needed.

* Ensure battery is fully charged
* Check all wiring connections
* Inspect tires for damage or dirt
* Test the motor and replace if faulty
