## Maintaining And Storing Clay Sets

Clay modeling toys provide endless fun and creativity. To keep them in great condition, proper care is needed.

Let's explore how to clean, store, and extend the life of your clay sets effectively.

### Proper Cleaning Techniques

Cleaning your clay sets is crucial for maintaining their quality. Regular cleaning prevents contamination and keeps the clay fresh.

* Use a damp cloth to wipe excess clay from tools.
* Remove dried bits gently with a soft brush.
* Keep hands clean to avoid dirt on clay.

### Storage Solutions To Preserve Clay

Proper storage is key to preserving the texture and color of your clay. It prevents drying and cracking.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Method | Benefit |
| Airtight containers | Prevents air exposure |
| Cool, dry place | Avoids moisture damage |
| Separate colors | Prevents mixing |

### Extending The Life Of Clay Toys

Extending the life of your clay toys ensures more creative playtime. Simple steps can help preserve their usability.

Ensure toys are stored away from direct sunlight to prevent fading. Rotate usage to evenly distribute wear on clay pieces.
