## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-ride-on-toy-with-a-princess-theme-in-good-shape-helps-it-last-longer.-regular-maintenance-keeps-it-safe-and-fun-for-children.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your ride-on toy with a princess theme in good shape helps it last longer. Regular maintenance keeps it safe and fun for children.

Simple care steps prevent damage and ensure smooth rides every time your child plays with it.

### Cleaning And Care

Wipe the toy often to remove dirt and dust. Use a soft cloth and mild soap with water for cleaning.

Check the surface for scratches or cracks. Fix small damages early to avoid bigger problems.

* Clean wheels and undercarriage to remove debris
* Use gentle cleaners to protect paint and decals
* Dry the toy after cleaning to stop rust

### Battery And Mechanical Upkeep

Check the battery regularly to keep it working well. Charge it fully before use and do not overcharge.

Inspect mechanical parts like wheels and steering. Tighten loose screws and oil moving parts to avoid noise and wear.

* Charge battery in a dry place away from heat
* Replace worn-out batteries promptly
* Test brakes and steering before each ride

### Storage Solutions

Store the ride-on toy in a dry, covered place to protect it from weather. Avoid leaving it under the sun or rain.

Keep the toy on a flat surface to prevent damage to wheels and frame. Cover it with a cloth if stored outside.

* Use a garage or shed for long-term storage
* Remove the battery if storing for many weeks
* Keep the toy away from sharp objects and pets
