Setting up your Outdoor Chameleon Inflatable Ride On is simple and fun. Follow some key steps to make sure it is safe and ready for play.

This guide covers inflation tips, choosing a safe area, and how to care for the ride on after use.

### Inflation Tips And Tools

Use a strong air pump to inflate the ride on. Avoid overinflating to keep the material safe and stretchy.

1. Connect the air pump nozzle firmly to the valve.
2. Inflate slowly and check firmness often.
3. Stop inflating when the surface feels tight but not hard.
4. Seal the valve tightly to prevent air leaks.

### Safe Play Area Selection

Choose a flat and clean space for the ride on. Avoid sharp objects and rough ground that can damage it.

**Safe Play Area Checklist:**

* Flat surface like grass or smooth pavement
* Clear of rocks, sticks, and glass
* Open space free from traffic
* Shade is preferred to avoid overheating
