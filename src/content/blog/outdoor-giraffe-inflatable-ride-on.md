## Safety Tips For Outdoor Inflatable Play

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=playing-on-an-outdoor-giraffe-inflatable-ride-is-fun-for-kids.-safety-is-very-important-to-prevent-injuries.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Playing on an outdoor giraffe inflatable ride is fun for kids. Safety is very important to prevent injuries. Follow key tips to keep playtime safe and enjoyable.

Proper care and rules help children have a good time without accidents. Adults should watch closely and check the inflatable often.

### Supervision Guidelines

Always have an adult nearby while children use the inflatable. Watching closely helps stop unsafe actions quickly. Adults should be alert and ready to help.

Limit the number of children on the ride at one time. This avoids crowding and collisions. Make sure kids follow the rules to keep play safe.

* Keep constant eye contact with children
* Stop play if children act rough or unsafe
* Help kids get on and off safely
* Keep a first aid kit nearby

### Avoiding Common Hazards

Check the area around the inflatable for sharp objects or uneven ground. Remove stones, sticks, or debris that could cause trips or punctures.

Do not place the inflatable near pools, roads, or hard surfaces. These places increase the risk of serious injury if a child falls off.

* Clear the play zone of any sharp objects
* Use the inflatable on soft grass or mats
* Keep the inflatable away from water and traffic
* Secure the inflatable with stakes or weights

### Proper Use And Weight Limits

Follow the manufacturer's weight and user limits. Overloading the inflatable can cause it to break or tip over. Children should use it one at a time if needed.

Make sure kids use the ride as intended. No climbing on the outside or rough play. Teach children to sit or stand only where allowed.

* Check weight limits before use
* Allow only the recommended number of children at once
* Use the inflatable only for its intended purpose
* Do not let children jump off from high parts
