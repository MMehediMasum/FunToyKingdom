## How To Use Shark Fin Toys Safely

Shark fin swimming toys are fun for kids but need safe use. They help children enjoy the water while learning to swim.

Follow safety tips to keep playtime safe and fun. This guide covers supervision, swimming skills, and cleaning advice.

### Supervision Tips

Always watch children closely when they use shark fin toys in the pool or water. Keep them within arm’s reach for quick help.

* Have an adult stay near the child at all times
* Do not allow unsupervised swimming
* Use life jackets for non-swimmers
* Check the pool area for hazards before play

### Proper Swimming Techniques

Teach children how to swim correctly with the shark fin toy. This helps them stay balanced and safe in the water.

1. Hold the fin firmly while swimming
2. Practice kicking legs steadily
3. Keep the head above water when starting
4. Use slow and smooth arm movements
5. Take breaks if tired or uncomfortable
