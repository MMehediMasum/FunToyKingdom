## Maintenance And Storage Tips

Keeping your Interactive Quiz Buzzers Toy Set in good shape helps it last longer. Proper care ensures the buzzers work every time you play.

Follow these simple tips on battery care, cleaning, and storage to enjoy your toy set for many fun sessions.

### Battery Care

Use the right batteries and check them often. Remove batteries if you do not plan to use the buzzers for a long time.

* Use recommended battery types only.
* Replace old batteries quickly to avoid leaks.
* Remove batteries if storing for more than a month.
* Do not mix old and new batteries together.
* Keep battery contacts clean and dry.

### Cleaning Instructions

Keep the buzzers clean to avoid dust build-up. Use gentle cleaning methods to protect the buttons and electronics.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Cleaning Tip | Method |
| Surface Dust | Wipe with a soft, dry cloth |
| Sticky Buttons | Use a damp cloth with mild soap; dry immediately |
| Battery Compartment | Clean with a dry cotton swab |
| Avoid | Water soaking or harsh chemicals |

### Organized Storage Solutions

Store your quiz buzzers in a safe place to prevent damage. Keep all parts together to avoid losing any pieces.

* Use a small box or container with compartments.
* Label the container for easy identification.
* Keep the set away from heat and moisture.
* Store in a dry, cool place out of direct sunlight.
* Wrap wires or cords neatly to avoid tangling.
