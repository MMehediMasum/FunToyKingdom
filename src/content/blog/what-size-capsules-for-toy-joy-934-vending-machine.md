## Measuring Capsules For Accuracy

### Tools Needed

You need some simple tools to measure capsules accurately. Using the right tools helps get exact sizes.

* Ruler or tape measure with millimeter units
* Caliper for precise diameter measurement
* Flat surface to place capsules
* Notebook or paper to record measurements

### Step-by-step Measuring Process

Follow these steps to measure the capsule size for the Toy & Joy 934 machine accurately. Take your time and be precise.

* Place the capsule on a flat surface.
* Use the caliper to measure the capsule’s diameter at its widest point.
* Record the diameter in millimeters.
* Measure the capsule’s height from base to top.
* Write down the height accurately.
* Repeat the process for a few capsules to check consistency.
* Compare measurements to the machine’s capsule size requirements.
