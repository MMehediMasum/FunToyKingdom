## What Is A Stomp Rocket Launcher

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=a-stomp-rocket-launcher-is-a-simple-toy-that-shoots-foam-rockets-into-the-air.-it-uses-the-power-of-a-foot-stomp-to-launch-the-rocket-high.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

A stomp rocket launcher is a simple toy that shoots foam rockets into the air. It uses the power of a foot stomp to launch the rocket high.

People of all ages enjoy watching the rockets fly. It is a fun outdoor activity that does not need batteries or motors.

### Basic Components

The stomp rocket launcher has a few main parts. These parts work together to make the rocket fly.

* **Launch Pad:**A flat surface that holds the launcher tube steady.
* **Launcher Tube:**A long tube connected to the air bladder where the rocket sits.
* **Air Bladder:**A strong, inflatable bag that fills with air when you stomp.
* **Rocket:**A lightweight foam rocket that fits on the launcher tube.

### How It Works

You place the rocket on the launcher tube. Then you stomp hard on the air bladder to push air.

The air rushes up the tube and pushes the rocket into the sky. The harder the stomp, the higher the rocket flies.

### Types Of Stomp Rockets

There are different stomp rockets to try. Each type varies by size, shape, and flight style.

* **Standard Rockets:**Basic foam rockets for general play and fun.
* **Glow-in-the-Dark Rockets:**Rockets that light up for evening use.
* **Multi-Rocket Launchers:**Sets that launch several rockets at once.
* **Target Rockets:**Designed to fly straight for target practice.
