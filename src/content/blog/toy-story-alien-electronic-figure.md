Clean your figure with a soft, dry cloth to avoid scratching its surface. Do not use water or cleaning liquids on electronic parts.

If needed, gently wipe plastic parts with a slightly damp cloth. Make sure the figure is off and dry before turning it back on.

* Dry the figure completely after cleaning

Store the figure in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. This helps protect the colors and electronic parts.

Remove batteries if you do not plan to use the figure for a long time. Keep it in a box or container to avoid dust and damage.

* Keep away from heat and moisture
* Remove batteries to prevent leaks
* Use a protective case or box
