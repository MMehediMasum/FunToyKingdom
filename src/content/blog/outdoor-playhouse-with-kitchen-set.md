## Maintenance And Care Tips

Clean the playhouse regularly. Use mild soap and water. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage surfaces.

Inspect the kitchen set for dirt and grime. Wipe it down frequently to maintain hygiene for children.

* Clean once a month
* Check for loose parts
* Remove debris from play area

### Weather Protection Strategies

Protect the playhouse from harsh weather. Use covers during heavy rain or snow. This prevents water damage.

Place the playhouse in a shaded area. It reduces sun exposure and prevents fading. Secure it to avoid wind damage.

* Use weather-resistant covers
* Anchor playhouse during storms

### Repair And Replacement Advice

Check for wear and tear. Tighten any loose screws or bolts. Replace broken parts immediately to ensure safety.

Consider replacing old parts with new ones. This keeps the playhouse sturdy and reliable for playtime.

* Inspect parts monthly
* Have spare parts ready
* Seek professional help for major repairs
