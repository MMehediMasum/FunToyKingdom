## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Rope Toys Good For Aggressive Chewers?



Rope toys made from durable cotton-poly blends resist strong chewing. They provide long-lasting fun and help clean teeth. Heavy-duty construction prevents easy tearing, ideal for large or aggressive dogs. These toys promote healthy teeth and gums while reducing destructive chewing behaviors.



### How Do Rope Toys Benefit My Dog's Dental Health?



Rope toys act like floss, cleaning teeth and gums during play. Chewing helps remove plaque and tartar buildup naturally. Regular use supports fresh breath and healthy gums. They offer a safe, interactive way to maintain oral hygiene without harsh chemicals.



### Are These Rope Toys Suitable For All Dog Sizes?



Many rope toys come in various sizes to fit small, medium, and large breeds. Always choose a size appropriate for your dog’s jaw strength. Heavy-duty options are especially designed for large or aggressive chewers to ensure durability and safety.



### Can Rope Toys Help Reduce My Dog’s Boredom?



Yes, rope toys engage dogs in tug-of-war and chewing activities. Interactive play keeps dogs mentally stimulated and physically active. This reduces anxiety, boredom, and destructive behavior, making them perfect for indoor and outdoor fun.



### How Do I Choose The Best Rope Toy For My Dog?



Look for tough, high-quality cotton or cotton-poly blends. Select toys with multiple knots for added durability. Consider your dog’s size and chewing habits. Packs with multiple toys offer variety and backup replacements for sustained play.
