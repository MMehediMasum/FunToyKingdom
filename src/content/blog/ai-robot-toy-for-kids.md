A.I. robot toys encourage kids to create new stories and ideas. They can program the robot to act in different ways.

Playing with these toys sparks imagination and helps children think outside the box. They enjoy making the robot do unique tasks.

### Simple Installation Steps

Set up the robot in just a few easy steps. Follow these instructions to begin using it fast.

### Tips For Maximizing Engagement

Keep kids interested by exploring the robot’s features together. Try different ways to play and learn.

* Use voice commands to talk to the robot

* Encourage creativity by making new commands

* Join your child during play to boost fun
* Take breaks to avoid robot or child fatigue
