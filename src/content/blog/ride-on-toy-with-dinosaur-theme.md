## Safety Features To Look For

Choosing a ride on toy with a dinosaur theme is fun for kids. Safety should be the top priority. Parents need to check key features before buying.

These safety features help keep children protected while they enjoy their ride on toy adventures.

### Stable Construction

A stable design prevents tipping and falling. Look for wide bases and low centers of gravity. Strong wheels and sturdy frames add safety.

* Wide wheelbase for balance
* Non-slip footrests
* Durable frame materials like metal or thick plastic
* Secure joints and fasteners
* Rubber wheels for smooth rides

### Non-toxic Materials

Children often touch and mouth toys. Non-toxic paints and plastics keep them safe from harmful chemicals. Check labels for safety certifications.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Material | Safety Feature |
| Paint | Lead-free and water-based |
| Plastic | BPA-free and phthalate-free |
| Fabric | Flame-retardant and hypoallergenic |
| Metal | Rust-resistant and smooth edges |

### Age-appropriate Design

Choose a toy that fits your child’s age and size. Small parts can be choking hazards. Controls and pedals should match their motor skills.

* Check manufacturer’s recommended age range
* Ensure seat size fits your child
* Look for easy-to-use steering and brakes
* Avoid small detachable pieces
* Verify weight limits to avoid accidents

## Maintenance And Care Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-ride-on-toy-with-dinosaur-theme-in-good-shape-makes-playtime-safe-and-fun.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your ride on toy with dinosaur theme in good shape makes playtime safe and fun.

Simple care steps help the toy last longer and look great.

### Cleaning The Toy

Clean the toy regularly to keep it free from dirt and grime.

* Use a soft cloth dampened with mild soap and water.
* Wipe down all plastic and metal parts gently.
* Do not soak electrical parts or the battery compartment.
* Dry the toy completely before use or storage.

### Battery Maintenance

Proper battery care ensures the toy runs smoothly and safely.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Action | Details |
| Charging | Charge fully before first use and avoid overcharging. |
| Storage | Remove batteries if not used for a long time. |
| Inspection | Check battery and wires regularly for damage. |
| Replacement | Use only manufacturer-approved batteries. |

### Storage Advice

Store the ride on toy properly to prevent damage and wear.

Follow these tips for safe storage:

* Keep the toy in a dry, shaded area away from direct sunlight.
* Cover the toy with a cloth or plastic sheet to protect from dust.
* Store indoors during cold or wet seasons to avoid rust and cracking.
* Place the toy on a flat surface to maintain balance and shape.
