## Yoda Figure History

The Yoda action figure is a popular toy from the Star Wars series. It represents the wise Jedi Master Yoda. Fans and collectors have enjoyed this figure for many years.

This history shows how the Yoda figure started, changed, and became iconic. Each stage tells a part of its story.

### First Releases

The first Yoda action figures appeared soon after the release of The Empire Strikes Back in 1980. These early toys were small and simple, made by Kenner.

* The original figure was about 2.5 inches tall.
* It had basic movable parts.
* The figure came with a small cane accessory.
* Packaging featured the Star Wars logo and Yoda’s image.

## Collecting Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collecting-a-star-wars-yoda-action-figure-can-be-very-fun.-knowing-how-to-check-if-your-figure-is-real-helps-you-avoid-fakes.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collecting a Star Wars Yoda action figure can be very fun. Knowing how to check if your figure is real helps you avoid fakes.

Also, grading the condition and storing your figure well keeps it valuable over time.

### Authenticity Checks

Look for official Star Wars and manufacturer marks on the toy. Check the packaging for copyrights and logos.

* Compare the figure with trusted photos online.
* Check the weight and plastic quality.
* Look for paint and sculpt details matching originals.
* Beware of unusual colors or missing parts.

### Condition Grading

Condition affects the value of your Yoda figure. Use simple grades to describe your item.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Grade | Description |
| Mint | No damage, unopened package |
| Near Mint | Minimal wear, may be opened |
| Excellent | Small scratches or paint wear |
| Good | Noticeable marks or missing accessories |
| Fair | Major damage or broken parts |

### Storage And Display

Keep your figure away from sunlight to avoid fading. Store it in a dry place to stop mold and rust.

**Tips for safe display:**

* Use glass cases to protect from dust.
* Keep away from direct heat sources.
* Use stands to keep the figure upright.
* Handle with clean hands or gloves.
