## Scrabble Junior Basics

Scrabble Junior is a simple word game designed for young kids. It helps children learn spelling and word recognition while having fun.

This version of Scrabble uses a colorful game board and easy rules to make learning enjoyable and easy to follow.

### Game Setup And Components

The game includes a board, letter tiles, and two-sided boards for different skill levels. Each player gets a board and a set of tiles.

* A double-sided game board with simple words on one side and blank spaces on the other
* 100 letter tiles with colorful letters
* Tile racks for each player
* A rule booklet with easy instructions

### How To Play

Players take turns placing letter tiles on their boards to complete words. The goal is to fill all the word spaces on the board first.

1. Each player chooses a game board side: easy or challenging.
2. Shuffle the letter tiles and place them face down.
3. Draw tiles and place them on your tile rack.
4. On your turn, place tiles to form words on your board.
5. Use clues from the board to help spell the words correctly.
6. The first player to fill all the words on their board wins.
