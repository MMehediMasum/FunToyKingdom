## Choosing The Right Obstacle Set

Outdoor laser tag is fun and exciting. Picking the right obstacle set makes the game better. You need obstacles that fit your space and players.

Think about materials, size, and safety when choosing obstacles. This guide will help you decide what works best.

### Materials And Durability

Obstacles must be made of strong materials. They should last long and handle outdoor weather. Plastic and metal are common choices.

* Plastic is light and resists water.
* Metal is very strong but heavier.
* Wood can look nice but may need more care.
* Look for UV protection to avoid fading.

### Size And Portability

Choose obstacles that fit your play area. Some sets are large, others small. Think about moving and storing them too.

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Obstacle Type | Size | Weight | Portability |
| Inflatable Barriers | Large | Light | Easy to carry |
| Plastic Panels | Medium | Medium | Moderate |
| Metal Frames | Small to Medium | Heavy | Hard to move |

### Safety Features

Safety is key for all players. Obstacles should not have sharp edges. They must stay stable and not tip over easily.

* Rounded corners to prevent cuts.
* Non-slip bases to keep obstacles in place.
* Materials that do not splinter or break.
* Clear space around obstacles for safe movement.

## Maintaining And Storing Obstacles

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=outdoor-laser-tag-obstacle-sets-need-care-to-last-long.-proper-maintenance-keeps-them-safe-and-fun.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Outdoor laser tag obstacle sets need care to last long. Proper maintenance keeps them safe and fun.

Storing obstacles the right way prevents damage and saves space. Regular checks help find problems early.

### Cleaning Tips

Clean obstacles often to remove dirt and dust. Use mild soap and water for the best results.

Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the materials. Soft brushes or cloths work well for scrubbing.

* Rinse obstacles with water after cleaning
* Dry completely before storing
* Clean all sides, including hard-to-reach areas
* Remove mud and grass promptly

### Storage Solutions

Store obstacles in a dry, shaded place to avoid sun and rain damage. Cover them with waterproof tarps if outside.

Stack obstacles carefully to keep their shape. Use pallets or shelves to keep them off the ground.

* Keep obstacles away from sharp objects
* Use labeled storage areas for easy access
* Ensure good air flow to prevent mold
* Store inflatable parts inflated slightly

### Regular Inspection And Repairs

Check obstacles before and after use to find any damage. Fix small tears or cracks quickly to prevent bigger problems.

Look for loose parts, broken seams, or worn-out materials. Replace damaged pieces to keep obstacles safe.

* Inspect seams and zippers for wear
* Test stability of hard obstacles
* Repair holes with patch kits or glue
* Keep repair tools handy for quick fixes

### Planning Game Modes

Choosing the right game modes keeps players interested. You can pick simple or complex modes based on the players’ age and skill.

Popular modes include team battles, free-for-all, and capture the flag. Each mode uses the obstacle set differently to add strategy.

* Team Battles: Two teams compete to tag opponents.
* Free-for-All: Every player fights alone to score points.
* Capture the Flag: Teams try to capture the opponent’s flag.

### Safety Guidelines

Safety is very important in outdoor laser tag. Set clear rules before the game starts to avoid accidents.

Make sure the play area is free of sharp objects and holes. Players should wear comfortable clothes and closed shoes.

* Check the obstacle set for stability.
* Keep first aid supplies nearby.
* Explain safe play and no physical contact.
* Use walkie-talkies or signals to communicate.

Laser tag helps players learn how to work as a team. Encourage players to plan strategies together before the game.

Friendly competition makes the game more fun and pushes players to do their best. Praise good teamwork during and after the game.
