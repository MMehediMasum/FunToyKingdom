## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Squishy Toys Used For?



Squishy toys are used for stress relief and sensory stimulation. They're perfect for kids with autism and adults needing relaxation. These toys help improve focus and provide a calming effect. Squishies are great gifts and party favors, offering fun and relief for everyone.



### How Do Squishies Benefit Children With Autism?



Squishies help children with autism by providing sensory stimulation and stress relief. Their soft, squeezable texture offers a calming effect. They improve focus and reduce anxiety, making them beneficial tools. Squishies are engaging and fun, perfect for enhancing sensory experiences and promoting relaxation in children.



### Are Squishy Toys Safe For Kids?



Yes, squishy toys are generally safe for kids. They're made from non-toxic materials. Always supervise young children to prevent choking. Check for small parts that could pose a risk. Ensure the toys meet safety standards for peace of mind. Enjoy these stress-relieving and sensory-friendly toys safely.



### How Do You Clean Sticky Squishy Toys?



To clean sticky squishy toys, gently wash them with warm soapy water. Rinse thoroughly and let them air dry. Avoid harsh chemicals that might damage the toy. Regular cleaning maintains their stickiness and ensures hygiene. Proper care extends the life of your squishies, keeping them fun and functional.



### What Makes Squishies Slow Rising?



Squishies are slow rising due to their special foam material. This material compresses easily and then slowly returns to its original shape. The slow rise adds a satisfying sensory experience. It enhances the calming effect, making them popular for stress relief and sensory play.
