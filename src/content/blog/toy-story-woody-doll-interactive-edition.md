## Tips For Maintaining Your Woody Doll

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=owning-a-toy-story-woody-doll-interactive-edition-is-fun.-taking-good-care-helps-it-last-longer.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Owning a Toy Story Woody Doll Interactive Edition is fun. Taking good care helps it last longer.

These tips will guide you on cleaning, battery care, and storage for your Woody doll.

### Cleaning And Care Instructions

Keep your Woody doll clean by wiping it gently with a soft cloth. Avoid using water on electronic parts.

Remove dust often and do not use harsh chemicals. Clean fabric areas carefully to avoid damage.

* Use a dry or slightly damp cloth for cleaning
* Do not soak or immerse the doll in water
* Avoid cleaning sprays or solvents
* Keep away from sharp objects to prevent scratches

### Battery Replacement Guide

Woody doll runs on batteries. Replace them when sounds or movements weaken.

Use the right battery type. Remove old batteries carefully to avoid damage.

* Open the battery compartment using a small screwdriver
* Take out old batteries and dispose of them properly
* Insert new batteries following the correct polarity (+ and -)
* Close the compartment tightly to protect the batteries
* Test the doll after replacing batteries to check function

### Storage Suggestions

Store your Woody doll in a cool, dry place. Avoid direct sunlight to keep colors bright.

Keep the doll away from dust and moisture. Use a box or cloth bag for extra protection.

* Choose a storage spot without heat or humidity
* Cover the doll with a soft cloth or place it in a box
* Do not store near liquids or food
* Remove batteries if storing for a long time
