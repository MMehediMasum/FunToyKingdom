## Setup And Maintenance Tips

Setting up your Toy Bowling Alley Electronic Set is simple. Proper care keeps it working well. Follow these tips to enjoy it longer.

### Easy Assembly Guide

Start by unpacking all parts from the box. Lay them on a flat surface to check everything is included.

Follow the instruction sheet to connect the pins, lanes, and electronic base. Use the clips and screws provided.

* Attach the bowling pins securely to the base.
* Connect the electronic sensors to the main lane.

* Test the set by rolling a ball to check pin reset.

### Battery And Power Management

Use fresh batteries for the best performance. The toy needs batteries to power the electronic scoring.

Turn off the set after play to save battery life. Remove batteries if you will not use it for a long time.

* Use the correct battery size as stated in the manual.
* Replace batteries when the lights dim or sound fades.
* Store batteries in a cool, dry place before use.
* Dispose of old batteries safely according to local rules.

### Cleaning And Storage

Clean the toy regularly to keep it working well. Use a soft cloth to wipe the lanes and pins.

Store the set in a dry place away from dust. Keep all parts together to avoid losing pieces.

* Do not use water or harsh cleaners on electronic parts.
* Remove dust with a dry cloth or soft brush.
* Keep the set in its box or a storage container when not used.
* Check parts for damage before each use to stay safe.
