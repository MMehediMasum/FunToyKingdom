## Tips For Perfect Snow Bricks

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-snow-bricks-is-fun-and-useful-for-building-snow-structures.-good-bricks-need-the-right-snow-and-packing-method.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making snow bricks is fun and useful for building snow structures. Good bricks need the right snow and packing method.

This guide shares tips on choosing snow, packing it well, and storing your snow brick maker.

### Choosing The Right Snow

Not all snow works well for making bricks. The best snow is slightly wet and packs easily.

* Look for snow that sticks when squeezed in your hand.
* Avoid powdery or very dry snow.
* Snow after a light thaw or during early winter is best.
* Cold, hard snow may not form bricks well.

### Packing Techniques

How you pack snow into the brick maker affects the brick’s strength.

1. Fill the mold completely with snow.
2. Press down firmly to remove air gaps.
3. Compact snow evenly on all sides.
4. Release the brick carefully to keep its shape.
5. Practice to find the right pressure for your snow type.

### Storage And Maintenance

Keep your snow brick maker in good shape by cleaning and storing it properly.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Care Task | Tip |
| Cleaning | Remove snow and dry after use to avoid rust. |
| Storage | Store in a dry place, away from direct sunlight. |
| Inspection | Check for cracks or damage before each use. |
| Lubrication | Apply light oil to hinges if any to keep smooth. |
