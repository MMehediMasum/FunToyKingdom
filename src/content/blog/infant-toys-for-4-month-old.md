## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Toys For 4-month-old Infants?



The best toys for 4-month-olds stimulate senses and motor skills. Choose soft rattles, teething rings, and sensory plush toys. These toys encourage grasping, cause-and-effect learning, and soothe sore gums safely.



### How Do Teething Toys Help 4-month-old Babies?



Teething toys relieve gum discomfort and promote healthy teeth development. They provide safe chewing surfaces made of silicone or gel. Toys like Nuby Ice Gel Teether Keys soothe pain and keep babies engaged.



### Are Montessori Sensory Toys Good For 4-month-olds?



Yes, Montessori sensory toys enhance cognitive and sensory development. They use high-contrast colors and varied textures. These toys encourage exploration and fine motor skills in infants aged 0-6 months.



### Which Toys Support Tummy Time For 4-month-old Babies?



Tummy time toys like soft mirrors and crinkle rattles motivate babies to lift their heads. These toys strengthen neck and shoulder muscles. Bright Starts’ plush caterpillar with a mirror is a great choice.



### What Makes A Toy Safe For 4-month-old Infants?



Safe toys are non-toxic, BPA-free, and have no small parts. They should be easy to clean and durable. Choose products designed specifically for infants under 6 months to ensure safety.
