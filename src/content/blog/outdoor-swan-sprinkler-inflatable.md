Strong material keeps the inflatable safe from tears and holes. Look for thick, high-quality vinyl or PVC that can handle rough outdoor use. Check if the seams are well sealed to avoid leaks.

### Water Pressure Compatibility

Your garden hose water pressure must match the sprinkler's needs. Too much pressure can damage the inflatable. Too little pressure will reduce water flow and fun.

* Check the required water pressure in PSI (pounds per square inch)
* Use a pressure regulator if needed
* Test the sprinkler with your hose before long use

## Tips For Safe Use

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-the-outdoor-swan-sprinkler-inflatable-safely-keeps-fun-without-accidents.-follow-these-simple-tips-for-best-results.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using the Outdoor Swan Sprinkler Inflatable safely keeps fun without accidents. Follow these simple tips for best results.

Proper care and attention help your inflatable last longer and stay safe for everyone.

### Proper Installation

Set up the swan inflatable on a flat, clean surface. Avoid sharp objects nearby to prevent damage. Secure the inflatable with stakes or weights to keep it stable during use.

* Clear the ground of rocks and sticks
* Use the provided anchor points for stability
* Keep the area free from overhead wires or branches
* Check that the sprinkler hose is connected correctly

### Supervision During Play

Always watch children while they use the inflatable. Make sure users follow the rules to avoid injuries. Limit the number of children playing at once to prevent crowding.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Safety Tip | Reason |
| Keep adult supervision | Adults can stop unsafe behavior quickly |
| Limit users to 3 at a time | Prevents collisions and overcrowding |
| Remove shoes before use | Protects the inflatable and users |
| No rough play | Reduces risk of falls and injuries |

### Maintenance And Storage

Keep the inflatable clean and dry before storing. Regularly check for holes or tears and repair them quickly. Store the swan in a cool, dry place to avoid mold and damage.

**Maintenance Checklist:**

* Inspect for leaks before and after use
* Clean with mild soap and water
* Dry completely before folding
* Store away from direct sunlight
