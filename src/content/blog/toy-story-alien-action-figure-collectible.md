## Care And Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-toy-story-alien-action-figure-collectible-in-top-shape-is-easy.-proper-care-helps-keep-its-colors-bright-and-parts-intact.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Toy Story Alien Action Figure Collectible in top shape is easy. Proper care helps keep its colors bright and parts intact.

Follow simple steps to clean, store, and protect your collectible. This guide will help you enjoy it for years.

### Cleaning Methods

Use a soft cloth or brush to remove dust gently. Avoid water on stickers or decals to prevent damage.

* Dust with a microfiber cloth weekly
* Use a soft brush for hard-to-reach areas
* Spot clean with a damp cloth if needed
* Do not use harsh chemicals or cleaners

### Storage Solutions

Keep your Alien figure away from direct sunlight and moisture. Proper storage prevents fading and warping.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Storage Option** | **Benefits** | **Notes** |
| Display Case | Keeps dust out and shows figure clearly | Choose UV-protected glass if possible |
| Closed Box | Protects from light and moisture | Use acid-free tissue paper inside |

### Protecting Against Damage

Handle your Alien figure with care. Rough play or dropping can break parts or scratch paint.

* Hold the figure by the body, not limbs
* Avoid exposing it to extreme temperatures
* Keep away from pets and small children
* Use soft padding when moving or storing
