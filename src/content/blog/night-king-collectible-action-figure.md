### Cleaning Tips

Dust and dirt can dull the figure’s paint and details. Use gentle cleaning methods to keep it safe.

* Use a soft brush or microfiber cloth to remove dust.
* Clean with a damp cloth using mild soap if needed.
* Avoid harsh chemicals or alcohol-based cleaners.
* Dry the figure thoroughly after cleaning.

### Storage Recommendations

Proper storage prevents damage from sunlight, moisture, and dust. Choose a safe place to keep your figure.

| Storage Condition | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Temperature | Keep in a cool, dry place below 75°F (24°C) |
| Light Exposure | Store away from direct sunlight to prevent fading |
| Humidity | Avoid high humidity to stop mold and paint damage |
| Dust Protection | Use display cases or sealed containers |

### Protecting The Figure

Handle your Night King figure with care to avoid scratches or breaks. Use extra protection during display and transport.

* Wear clean, dry hands or gloves to prevent oils on the figure.
* Place the figure on soft surfaces to avoid scratches.
* Keep small parts away from pets and children.
* Use padded boxes when moving or shipping the figure.
