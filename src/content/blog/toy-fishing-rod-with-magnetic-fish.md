## Magnetic Fishing Game Basics

The magnetic fishing game is a fun activity for kids. It uses magnets to catch small fish pieces. This game helps improve hand-eye coordination.

Players use a toy fishing rod with a magnet at the end. The goal is to catch as many fish as possible from a pool or board.

### How The Toy Works

The fishing rod has a small magnet attached to its hook. Each fish piece has a metal part that the magnet can attract. When the rod gets close to a fish, the magnet pulls it up.

1. Hold the rod steady above a fish.
2. Lower the magnet near the fish's metal part.
3. The magnet will attract and hold the fish.
4. Lift the rod to catch the fish.

### Materials And Durability

Look for toy rods made from sturdy materials like plastic or wood. These materials can handle rough play.

Durability means the toy will not break easily. This makes it safe and saves money by lasting longer.

* Plastic rods are lightweight and water-resistant
* Wood rods feel natural and are strong

### Size And Weight Considerations

Choose a rod that fits the child's age and hand size. A rod that is too big or heavy can be hard to hold.

Lightweight rods let kids play longer without getting tired. Shorter rods are easier for small hands to control.

* Small rods are best for ages 3 to 6

* Check the weight to ensure easy handling

### Safety Features

Safety is key when choosing a toy fishing rod. Look for smooth edges and secure magnets to prevent accidents.

Make sure the rod has no small parts that can come loose. The magnets should be strong but covered to avoid swallowing hazards.

* Rounded edges prevent cuts and scratches
* Magnets enclosed in plastic for safety
* Non-toxic materials are best for children

### Cleaning The Rod And Fish

Clean the rod and fish after each use. Remove dirt, dust, and water to avoid damage.

* Wipe the rod and fish gently with a damp cloth

### Storage Suggestions

Store your toy fishing rod and fish in a cool, dry place. Keep them away from direct sunlight to prevent fading.

Keep all parts together to avoid losing small pieces. Use a box or container with compartments if possible.

* Place the rod and fish in a storage box
* Keep the set away from heat and moisture
* Store in a place where kids can reach easily
* Check the toy regularly for any damage
