## Care Tips For Longevity

Keeping your baby plush toy with teether corners clean and safe helps it last longer. Proper care also protects your baby from germs and wear damage.

Follow simple steps to maintain the toy’s softness and safety. These tips cover cleaning, storing, and checking for damage.

### Cleaning Instructions

Clean the plush toy regularly to remove dirt and germs. Use gentle methods to avoid damaging the fabric and teether corners.

* Hand wash with mild soap and warm water.
* Use a soft cloth to gently clean the teether corners.
* Air dry fully before giving it back to the baby.
* Do not use bleach or harsh chemicals.
* Avoid machine washing to keep shape and softness.

### Storage Recommendations

Store the toy in a clean and dry place. Proper storage keeps it safe from dust, moisture, and damage.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Tip | Reason |
| Use a breathable fabric bag | Prevents moisture buildup |
| Keep away from direct sunlight | Protects colors and material |
| Store on a shelf or inside a drawer | Reduces dust and dirt exposure |
| Do not pile heavy items on top | Prevents crushing and deformation |

### Inspecting For Wear And Tear

Check the toy often for loose parts or damage. This keeps the toy safe for your baby and extends its life.

* Look for loose threads or seams.
* Check the teether corners for cracks or breaks.
* Ensure no small parts are coming off.
* Discard or repair if you find any damage.
