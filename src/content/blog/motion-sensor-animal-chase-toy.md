## How Motion Sensors Work

Motion sensors detect movement around them. They use different technologies to sense changes.

These sensors help toys react when animals or people move nearby. They make playtime more fun and interactive.

### Types Of Motion Sensors

There are several types of motion sensors used in toys. Each type works in a unique way to detect movement.

* **Passive Infrared (PIR) Sensors:**Detect heat changes from moving animals or humans.
* **Ultrasonic Sensors:**Use sound waves to sense objects nearby.
* **Microwave Sensors:**Emit microwaves and sense reflections from moving objects.
* **Tomographic Sensors:**Detect disruptions in radio signals across an area.

### Detecting Movement For Play

Motion sensors in animal chase toys trigger actions when movement is detected. This keeps pets interested and active.
