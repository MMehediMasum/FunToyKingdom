## Maintenance And Storage

Keeping your outdoor police car sprinkler inflatable in good condition ensures long-lasting fun. Proper care involves cleaning, deflating, and storing it correctly.

Learn how to maintain your inflatable with simple steps and ensure it's ready for the next playtime.

### Cleaning Tips

Regular cleaning prevents dirt build-up and prolongs the life of your inflatable. Follow these easy steps to clean it:

* Rinse the inflatable with clean water to remove surface dirt.
* Use mild soap and a soft brush to scrub any stains.
* Rinse thoroughly with water to remove all soap.
* Allow it to air dry completely before deflating.

### Proper Deflation

Deflating your inflatable properly is essential for safe storage. Incorrect deflation can cause damage or unnecessary wear.

**Tip:**Ensure all air is out before folding to avoid damage.

Gently press out the air, starting from one end and moving towards the valve. Roll it up tightly for compact storage.

### Storage Solutions

Storing your inflatable properly protects it from damage and extends its lifespan. Choose a suitable storage solution from the options below:
