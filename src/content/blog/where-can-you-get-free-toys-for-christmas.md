## Charity Organizations

Charity organizations play a big role in giving toys to children during Christmas. They collect donations and share them with families who need help. Many groups work hard to make sure every child feels joy during the holidays. These organizations often run special programs and drives that are easy to join.

### Local Toy Drives

Local toy drives collect new or gently used toys from the community. People donate toys at schools, churches, or stores. Volunteers organize these toys and give them to families in need. These drives often happen in November and December. Joining a local toy drive can bring smiles to many children’s faces.

### Nonprofit Holiday Programs

Nonprofit holiday programs offer free toys directly to families. They register children and provide gifts based on age and interest. Some programs also give food or clothes along with toys. These groups work with local partners to reach more families. Signing up early helps secure a spot in these programs.

Libraries often hold holiday-themed events. These include storytimes, craft sessions, and gift giveaways. Some events feature free toys for children. Families can join and enjoy festive activities.
