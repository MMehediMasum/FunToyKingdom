Nintendo Labo Toy Con VR Kit lets you explore virtual reality with your Nintendo Switch. It combines building and gaming for a fun experience.

### What’s Inside The Vr Kit

The VR Kit includes all parts you need to build Toy Cons. These are cardboard sheets and accessories that form VR devices.

* Straps to hold the VR headset
* Lenses for the headset
* Plastic parts and connectors

### Setting Up Your Switch For Vr

Before you start, update your Nintendo Switch system. Then install the Labo VR software from the eShop.

| Action | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Update System | Go to System Settings > System > System Update |
| Download Software | Open Nintendo eShop and search "Nintendo Labo VR" |

### Safety Tips For Vr Play

VR can cause dizziness or discomfort if not used properly. Follow safety rules for a safe playtime.

* Take breaks every 10 to 15 minutes
* Play in a clear, open space to avoid hitting objects
* Use the wrist straps on controllers
* Do not use VR if you feel sick or tired
* Keep children supervised during play

The Nintendo Labo Toy-Con VR Kit is fun, but sometimes problems occur. Here are solutions for common issues.

### Fixing Assembly Problems

Sometimes pieces may not fit together well. Check all parts for proper alignment.

Ensure all cardboard edges are folded correctly. This helps the parts to fit snugly.

* Inspect for missing pieces
* Re-read the instructions carefully
* Use gentle force if needed

### Resolving Display Glitches

The screen may not display properly. Check if the Switch console is inserted correctly.

Make sure the screen is clean and free from smudges. This can improve visibility.

* Adjust brightness settings
* Ensure the device is fully charged
* Restart the console

### Handling Controller Connectivity

Controllers may disconnect from the console. Check the Bluetooth settings first.

Ensure the Joy-Con controllers are fully charged. This helps maintain a stable connection.

* Reconnect the controllers manually
* Update the console firmware
* Remove any obstructions between console and controllers
