## Uno Deck Composition

Uno is a popular card game with a special deck. Understanding the deck helps you play better.

The Uno deck has different types of cards. Each type has a unique role in the game.

### Number Of Cards In A Standard Deck

A standard Uno deck has 108 cards in total. These cards include number cards, action cards, and wild cards.

### Breakdown Of Number Cards

Number cards are the most common in the deck. They come in four colors: red, green, blue, and yellow.

| Color | Number 0 | Numbers 1 to 9 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red | 1 card | 2 cards each |
| Green | 1 card | 2 cards each |
| Blue | 1 card | 2 cards each |
| Yellow | 1 card | 2 cards each |

### Action Cards Explained

Action cards add fun twists to the game. They include Skip, Reverse, and Draw Two cards.

* Skip: The next player loses their turn.
* Reverse: Changes the direction of play.
* Draw Two: The next player draws two cards and misses a turn.

Each color has two of each action card. That means eight Skip, eight Reverse, and eight Draw Two cards total.

### Wild Cards And Their Role

Wild cards let players choose the next color to play. They are important for strategy.

* Wild: Change the current color to any color.
* Wild Draw Four: Change the color and force the next player to draw four cards.

There are four Wild cards and four Wild Draw Four cards in a deck. These cards have no color until played.
