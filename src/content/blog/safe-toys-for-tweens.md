## Choosing Safe Toys

Choosing safe toys helps keep tweens happy and healthy. Parents and caregivers should look for toys that fit their child’s age and needs.

Safe toys reduce risks like choking and exposure to harmful materials. Knowing what to check is very important.



### Age-appropriate Options

Select toys that match the tween’s age and skills. Toys made for younger kids can be unsafe for older kids and vice versa.

* Look for age labels on toy packaging
* Choose toys that challenge but do not frustrate
* Avoid toys with parts too small for the child’s age
* Consider the child’s interests and development

### Material Safety

Toys should be made from non-toxic and durable materials. Avoid toys with paint or coatings that could peel or chip.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Material | Safety Tip |
| Plastic | Choose BPA-free and phthalate-free |
| Wood | Look for smooth edges and non-toxic finishes |
| Metal | Check for rust and sharp edges |
| Fabric | Ensure it is washable and flame resistant |

### Avoiding Choking Hazards

Small parts can block airways and cause choking. Always check toy parts before giving them to tweens.

Follow these safety tips:

* Keep toys with small parts away from children under 3 years
* Regularly inspect toys for loose or broken pieces
* Do not let tweens put toys in their mouths
* Read all warnings on the toy packaging

Check labels on toy packaging to see if they meet safety standards. Certifications show that toys passed tests for harmful materials and quality.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Safest Toy Materials For Tweens?



Safe toy materials for tweens include non-toxic plastics, organic cotton, and sustainably sourced wood. These materials reduce exposure to harmful chemicals. Always check for certifications like ASTM or EN71 to ensure safety standards are met.



### How To Choose Age-appropriate Toys For Tweens?



Choose toys labeled for tweens' age group, usually 9-12 years. Consider their interests, skill level, and safety features. Avoid toys with small parts that pose choking hazards. Age-appropriate toys encourage creativity and development.



### Are Electronic Toys Safe For Tweens?



Electronic toys can be safe if they meet safety standards and have parental controls. Look for low radiation, sturdy construction, and no sharp edges. Always supervise tween use to ensure safe and responsible play.



### What Safety Certifications Should Safe Tween Toys Have?



Look for certifications like ASTM F963, EN71, and CPSIA compliance. These standards ensure toys are tested for toxic substances and mechanical hazards. Certified toys offer peace of mind regarding safety and quality.
