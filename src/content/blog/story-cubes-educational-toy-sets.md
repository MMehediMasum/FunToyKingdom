## What Are Story Cubes

Story Cubes are small dice with pictures on each side. They help people create stories using the images.

These cubes encourage imagination and improve language skills for children and adults.

### Basic Components

Story Cubes sets include several dice. Each die has six different pictures on its faces.

The pictures show objects, actions, or symbols. They are simple and easy to understand.

* Multiple dice with unique images
* Compact size for easy handling
* Durable material for long use
* Images designed to spark ideas

### How They Work

Players roll the dice and look at the pictures on top. They then create a story using those images.

This process helps develop creativity and storytelling skills. There are no fixed rules for making stories.

* Roll the dice to get random images
* Use pictures as prompts for story parts
* Tell or write a story connecting the images
* Play alone or with friends for fun learning
