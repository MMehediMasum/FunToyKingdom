## Maintenance Tips

### Cleaning Guidelines

Clean the toy often to remove dirt and dust. Use a soft cloth and mild soap for wiping.

Avoid using water on electrical parts. Dry all surfaces before use to prevent damage.

* Wipe down plastic and metal parts gently
* Use a dry brush for hard-to-reach spots
* Do not soak the toy in water
* Keep sensors and buttons clean for good response

### Battery Care

Check the battery regularly to keep it working well. Charge it fully before the first use and after each ride.

Do not overcharge or leave the battery connected to the charger for a long time. This can reduce battery life.

* Charge the battery in a dry place
* Disconnect charger after full charge
* Replace battery if it no longer holds charge
* Keep battery contacts clean and dry

### Storage Recommendations

Store the toy in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. This prevents fading and damage.

Turn off the toy and remove the battery if storing for a long time. This keeps the battery safe and avoids leaks.

* Cover the toy with a cloth to keep dust off
* Store on a flat surface to avoid damage
* Avoid damp or humid places
* Check the toy before use after storage
