## Maintenance And Storage Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-your-outdoor-unicorn-sprinkler-toy-helps-it-last-longer.-proper-maintenance-keeps-it-clean-and-safe-for-kids.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of your outdoor unicorn sprinkler toy helps it last longer. Proper maintenance keeps it clean and safe for kids.

Storing the sprinkler correctly during off-season prevents damage. These tips make sure it works well next summer.

### Cleaning The Sprinkler

Clean the sprinkler after every use to stop dirt and mold buildup. Use mild soap and water to avoid damage.

Rinse all parts well and let them dry completely before storing. This keeps the sprinkler fresh and ready for play.

* Use soft cloth or sponge with mild soap
* Rinse thoroughly with clean water
* Dry all parts before storage

### Preventing Damage

Protect the sprinkler from sharp objects and rough surfaces. Avoid dragging it on the ground to keep it intact.

Check for leaks or cracks regularly. Fix small issues early to stop bigger problems later.

* Keep away from sharp tools and rocks
* Do not drag on rough or hard surfaces
* Inspect for leaks and cracks often
* Repair small damages quickly

### Off-season Storage

Empty the sprinkler of water before storing. Water left inside can freeze and cause cracks in cold weather.

Store the toy in a dry, cool place away from direct sunlight. Cover it with a cloth or plastic bag to keep dust off.

* Drain all water completely
* Store indoors or in a shaded area
* Use cover to protect from dust and dirt
* Avoid freezing temperatures

### Sun Protection For Kids

Sunburn can harm children's skin. Use sunscreen with at least SPF 30 before playtime.

* Apply sunscreen 15 minutes before outside play
* Reapply sunscreen every two hours
* Use wide-brimmed hats and sunglasses
* Choose breathable, long-sleeve shirts
* Play in shaded spots when possible

### Hydration Reminders

Kids lose water quickly when playing outdoors. Drinking water often keeps them safe and healthy.

Offer water breaks every 20 minutes. Avoid sugary drinks that can cause dehydration.

* Keep a water bottle nearby
* Encourage kids to drink small amounts often
* Watch for signs of thirst or tiredness
* Avoid caffeine and soda drinks

### Supervision Guidelines

Adults should watch children while they use the sprinkler toy. This prevents accidents and injuries.

Check the area for hazards like sharp objects or slippery surfaces before play.

* Stay close to children during play
* Keep the play area free of obstacles
* Ensure the sprinkler is set up on flat ground
