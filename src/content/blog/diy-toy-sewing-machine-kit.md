## How To Assemble The Kit

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=assembling-the-diy-toy-sewing-machine-kit-is-simple-and-fun.-you-will-build-a-working-sewing-machine-step-by-step.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Assembling the DIY Toy Sewing Machine Kit is simple and fun. You will build a working sewing machine step by step.

Follow the instructions carefully to make sure all parts fit well and work properly.

### Step-by-step Guide

Start by laying out all the parts on a clean surface. Check that you have everything listed in the kit.

1. Attach the base plate to the main body using the screws provided.
2. Insert the motor into the motor slot and secure it with clips.
3. Fix the needle arm onto its holder and tighten the screw.
4. Thread the sewing needle with the thread from the spool.
5. Connect the power switch wiring according to the diagram in the manual.
6. Place the bobbin in its case and slide it into the machine.
7. Test the machine by pressing the foot pedal to check movement.

## Maintaining And Storing The Sewing Kit

Taking care of your DIY toy sewing machine kit helps it last longer. Proper cleaning and storage keep all parts safe and ready to use.

Follow simple steps to maintain your kit. This will make sewing easier and more fun every time.

### Cleaning Tips

Keep your sewing machine clean to avoid jams and damage. Wipe dust and threads off after each use.

Use a soft cloth or small brush to clean hard-to-reach areas. Avoid using water or harsh chemicals.

* Remove loose threads from the needle and bobbin area
* Use a small brush to clean gears and moving parts
* Wipe the surface with a dry, soft cloth
* Check the manual for any special cleaning instructions

### Safe Storage Ideas

Store your sewing kit in a dry, clean place to protect it from dust and moisture. This prevents rust and damage.

Keep all pieces together in a box or container. Label it so you can find the kit easily when needed.

* Use a small box or plastic container with a lid
* Keep needles and small parts in a separate compartment
* Store the kit away from direct sunlight and heat
* Check the kit regularly for any signs of damage
