### Preparing The Base

Start by shaping the crown base from the included materials. Make sure it fits well on your head.

* Cut the strip to the correct length using scissors.
* Use the adhesive tape or glue to join the ends together securely.
* Let the base dry for a few minutes if you used glue.

### Adding Decorations

Decorate your crown using stickers, gems, and colorful paper shapes included in the kit.

Complete your crown by adding final details. Make sure everything is secure and comfortable to wear.

**Tips for finishing:**

* Press decorations firmly so they stick well.

* Try on the crown to check comfort before wearing.

## Safety Tips For Kids

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-a-toy-crown-can-be-fun-and-creative-for-kids.-safety-is-very-important-during-this-activity.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making a toy crown can be fun and creative for kids. Safety is very important during this activity. Parents and caregivers should know how to keep children safe.

This guide shares important tips to help kids enjoy crafting with less risk.

### Non-toxic Materials

Choose materials that do not have harmful chemicals. Non-toxic glue, paint, and decorations are best. These keep kids safe if they touch or accidentally taste them.

* Use water-based glue and paint
* Pick stickers and decorations labeled safe for children
* Avoid sharp or small parts that can be swallowed

### Supervision Guidelines

Always watch children closely during crafting. Adults can help prevent accidents and guide safe use of tools and materials.

1. Set clear rules for how to use each item
2. Stay near kids while they work on their crowns
3. Help with tricky or sharp parts of the project
4. Encourage taking breaks to avoid tiredness

### Safe Crafting Tools

| Tool | Safety Tip |
| --- | --- |
| Child-safe scissors | Use scissors with rounded tips to avoid cuts |
| Glue sticks | Choose glue sticks instead of liquid glue to reduce mess |
| Markers and crayons | Use non-toxic, washable markers and crayons |
| Decorative items | Pick large beads or foam shapes to prevent choking |

## Storing And Caring For Crowns

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=caring-for-your-diy-toy-crowns-ensures-they-last-longer.-proper-storage-and-cleaning-keep-them-looking-new.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Caring for your DIY toy crowns ensures they last longer. Proper storage and cleaning keep them looking new.

Learn how to store, clean, and fix your crowns to enjoy them for years.

### Proper Storage Methods

Store your toy crowns in a cool, dry place. Avoid direct sunlight to prevent fading.

* Use a box with a lid to keep dust away
* Line the box with soft cloth to prevent scratches
* Keep crowns separate to avoid tangling

### Cleaning Tips

Clean your crowns gently to maintain their shine. Use mild soap and water for best results.

Always dry thoroughly to prevent moisture damage. A soft cloth is ideal for drying.

### Repairing Damaged Crowns

Fix small breaks with glue designed for crafts. Let the glue dry completely before using the crown.

* Check for loose parts regularly
* Reattach any pieces that may fall off
* Handle crowns with care to avoid further damage
