## Collecting Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collecting-a-toy-story-buzz-lightyear-action-figure-can-be-fun-and-rewarding.-paying-attention-to-details-helps-keep-your-collection-valuable.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collecting a Toy Story Buzz Lightyear action figure can be fun and rewarding. Paying attention to details helps keep your collection valuable.

Knowing how to check authenticity, grade the condition, and store your figure properly protects your investment.

### Authenticity Checks

Look for official branding on the packaging and figure. Disney and Pixar logos are key signs of a real Buzz Lightyear toy.

Check the quality of paint and plastic. Fake figures often have blurry colors or rough edges.

* Verify manufacturer details on the box or figure
* Check for copyright marks and dates
* Compare with photos of known authentic figures
* Beware of unusually low prices

### Condition Grading

Condition affects the figure’s value. Mint condition means no scratches, marks, or missing parts.

Keep the original packaging intact. Opened or damaged boxes lower the figure’s grade.

* **Mint:**Perfect figure and packaging
* **Near Mint:**Minor flaws, almost perfect
* **Good:**Noticeable wear or small damage
* **Fair:**Major damage or missing parts

### Storage And Display

Store Buzz Lightyear in a cool, dry place. Avoid sunlight to prevent colors from fading.

Use display cases to protect the figure from dust and damage. Keep it away from pets and children.

* Use acid-free boxes for long-term storage
* Keep figures upright to avoid warping
* Clean gently with a soft cloth when needed
* Display in a place with stable temperature
