Most Jessie costume kits include a red cowboy hat, cow-print chaps or pants, a western-style shirt, and sometimes a belt. Accessories like felt hats and bows enhance the authentic cowgirl look.



### Are These Jessie Costumes Suitable For Halloween And Parties?



Yes, these costumes are perfect for Halloween, birthday parties, and cosplay events. They offer durable materials and detailed designs to bring the Toy Story character to life.



### Can Toddlers Comfortably Wear The Jessie Costume Kits?

### How Do I Choose The Right Jessie Costume Size?



Measure your chest, waist, and height before selecting a size. Refer to size charts provided by the seller to ensure an accurate fit for toddlers, kids, or adults.



### Are The Jessie Costumes Machine Washable?



Most costume kits recommend gentle hand washing or spot cleaning. Machine washing may damage delicate fabric and accessories, so always check care instructions for each product.
