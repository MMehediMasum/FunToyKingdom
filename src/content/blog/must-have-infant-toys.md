Selecting the right toys supports babies’ curiosity and developmental milestones. These toys also comfort teething infants and engage their senses. ## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Is Best For Infant Toys Like Wrist Rattles?



Infant toys such as wrist rattles are perfect for newborns up to 12 months. They help develop motor skills and sensory awareness.



### How Do Montessori Sensory Toys Benefit Infants?



Montessori sensory toys stimulate a baby’s senses and encourage exploration. They promote cognitive and motor development effectively in early months.



### Are Teething Toys Safe For Newborns And Older Babies?



Yes, teething toys made from BPA-free silicone and natural wood are safe. They soothe gums and improve hand coordination from 0 to 18 months.



### What Features Make A Good Baby Stacking Toy?



Good stacking toys have bright colors, different shapes, and easy grip design. They support hand-eye coordination and problem-solving skills for babies 6+ months.



### Why Choose Musical Toys For Infants?



Musical toys like Baby Einstein’s stimulate auditory senses and encourage early learning. They boost memory, rhythm recognition, and language development from 3 months onward.



### How Do High Contrast Toys Aid Newborns?



High contrast toys improve newborn visual tracking and focus. They are ideal for babies 0-3 months to enhance early brain development and attention span.
