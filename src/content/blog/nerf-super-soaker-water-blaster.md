Keep your water blaster clean to avoid clogs and leaks. After use, empty all water and rinse the tank. Let it dry before storing to stop mold and damage.

Check the seals and tubes often. Replace broken parts to keep strong pressure. Store the blaster in a cool, dry place.

* Empty water after each use
* Rinse and dry the tank
* Inspect seals and tubes regularly
* Replace worn or damaged parts
* Store away from heat and sun

### Effective Techniques

To shoot farther, pump the blaster fully before each shot. Aim at a slight upward angle for longer distance. Quick pumps keep a steady stream.

Use smooth, steady triggers instead of quick bursts. This keeps water pressure steady. Practice aiming to improve accuracy and hit your target.

* Pump fully before shooting
* Aim slightly upward for distance
* Use steady trigger pulls
* Practice aiming for better shots
