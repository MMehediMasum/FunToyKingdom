### Setting Time Limits

Short time limits keep the game fast and lively. They help players think quickly and stay engaged. Too much time can cause players to lose interest.

1. Set a timer for each turn, like one or two minutes.
2. Use a stopwatch or phone timer to keep track.
3. Encourage quick decisions to boost thinking skills.
4. Keep total game time reasonable to avoid boredom.
