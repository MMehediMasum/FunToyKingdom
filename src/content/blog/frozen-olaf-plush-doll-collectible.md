## Caring For Your Olaf Plush Doll

### Cleaning Techniques

Cleaning your Olaf plush doll gently removes dirt and dust. Use mild soap and water for spot cleaning. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage fabric.

If the doll is very dirty, hand wash it carefully. Let it air dry to keep its shape and softness. Do not use a dryer.

* Use a soft cloth or sponge for cleaning
* Spot clean small stains with mild soap
* Hand wash with cold water if needed
* Air dry in a well-ventilated area
* Keep away from direct sunlight when drying

### Repairing Minor Damages

Small tears or loose threads can be fixed at home. Use a needle and thread close to the doll’s color. Stitch gently to avoid further damage.

For loose buttons or eyes, sew them back securely. Check your doll often to catch small problems early. Quick repairs keep Olaf looking great.

* Sew small tears with matching thread
* Reattach loose buttons or eyes carefully
* Use small, even stitches to avoid holes
* Check for loose parts regularly

### Avoiding Common Wear Issues

Keep your Olaf plush doll safe from common wear problems. Avoid rough play or pulling on parts. Store Olaf in a clean, dry place.

Avoid exposing Olaf to moisture and dirt. This helps prevent mold and fabric damage. Handle Olaf gently to keep it soft and new-looking.

* Do not pull on arms, nose, or other parts
* Keep Olaf away from food and drinks
* Store in a dry area with good airflow
* Limit exposure to sunlight to prevent fading
* Encourage gentle play to avoid tears
