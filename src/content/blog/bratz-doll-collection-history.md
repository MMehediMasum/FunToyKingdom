## Origins Of Bratz Dolls

Bratz dolls became popular toys that changed the doll market. They showed a new style different from traditional dolls.

The dolls stood out with their big eyes, shiny lips, and trendy clothes. Many kids loved their unique look and attitude.

### Creation And Launch

The Bratz dolls were created by Carter Bryant. He worked for a toy company called MGA Entertainment.

The first Bratz dolls were launched in 2001. The company wanted to offer something new for kids who liked fashion and style.

### Initial Popularity

Bratz dolls quickly became popular among children. Their bold fashion and different look attracted many buyers.

The dolls grew fast in sales and gained a strong fan base. They stood out from other dolls on the market.
