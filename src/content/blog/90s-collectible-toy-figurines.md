### Plastic Types

Different plastics gave 90s toys their shape and strength. Manufacturers picked plastics for safety and look. Some plastics were soft for flexible parts. Others were hard for sturdy bodies.

* Polypropylene: Resistant to wear and used in joints

## Identifying Rare Figurines

Collecting 90s toy figurines is fun and rewarding. Some figurines are rare and very valuable.

Knowing how to spot these rare pieces helps collectors find special items. Look closely at details and history.

### Limited Editions

Limited edition figurines were made in small numbers. This makes them hard to find today.

They often have unique features like special colors or accessories. Check the packaging for "limited edition" labels.

* Look for unique packaging or stickers
* Check production numbers on the box
* Verify special features like paint or accessories

### Factory Errors

Factory errors happen when toys are made incorrectly. These mistakes can make figurines rare.

Errors include wrong colors, missing parts, or odd shapes. Collectors often prize these because they are one-of-a-kind.

* Check for paint outside the lines
* Look for missing or extra parts
* Notice unusual shapes or sizes

### Exclusive Releases

Exclusive releases were sold only at certain stores or events. They are rare because of limited availability.

These figurines may have special markings or tags showing where they were sold. They often come with unique packaging.

* Look for store or event logos on packaging
* Check for unique accessories included only in exclusives
* Research the release history for rare stores or events

## Condition And Grading

Collectible toy figurines from the 90s are popular among fans and collectors. Their condition plays a big role in value and appeal.

Understanding how to grade these toys helps buyers and sellers make fair deals. It also protects the toy's history and quality.

### Mint Vs Used

Mint condition means the toy looks brand new with no damage or wear. Used toys show signs of play or aging but can still be valuable.

* **Mint:**No scratches, dents, or discoloration.
* **Near Mint:**Very slight signs of handling or aging.
* **Used:**Visible wear, paint chips, or minor damage.
* **Poor:**Broken parts or heavy damage affecting value.

### Packaging Importance

Packaging adds value and shows if the toy is complete. Original boxes keep the toy safe and prove authenticity.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Packaging Type | Value Impact |
| Sealed in Original Box | Highest value, collector's favorite |
| Box Included but Opened | Good value, shows care |
| No Box | Lower value, harder to verify |

### Professional Grading Services

Experts grade toys using strict rules. They check condition, packaging, and originality. Grading helps buyers trust quality.

Common grading companies offer:

* Condition score from 1 to 10
* Sealed grading labels
* Authentication of rare figurines
* Protection with tamper-proof cases

## Care And Storage Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collectible-toy-figurines-from-the-90s-need-careful-handling.-proper-care-keeps-them-looking-great-for-years.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collectible toy figurines from the 90s need careful handling. Proper care keeps them looking great for years.

Storing and cleaning these toys right will protect their colors and shapes. Follow these tips to keep your collection safe.

### Cleaning Techniques

Use soft cloths or brushes to clean dust from your figurines. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage paint or plastic.

* Use a soft brush for tight spots
* Dab gently with a damp cloth for surface dirt
* Do not soak the figurines in water
* Use mild soap if needed, then dry immediately

### Display Options

| Display Type | Advantages | Considerations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glass Cabinet | Keeps dust out and shows off figurines | Can cause heat buildup from sunlight |
| Open Shelves | Easy to access and rearrange | More dust and risk of falls |
| Storage Boxes | Protects from dust and light damage | Figurines not visible |

### Preventing Damage

Keep your toys away from direct sunlight and heat. Avoid placing them in damp or humid places to stop mold and warping.

* Store figurines upright on stable surfaces
* Keep away from children and pets
* Do not use strong adhesives on them
* Check regularly for any signs of wear or damage
