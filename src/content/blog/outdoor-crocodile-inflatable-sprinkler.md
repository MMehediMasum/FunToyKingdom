## Maintaining And Storing Your Sprinkler

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-your-outdoor-crocodile-inflatable-sprinkler-helps-it-last-longer.-proper-maintenance-keeps-it-clean-and-ready-to-use.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of your outdoor crocodile inflatable sprinkler helps it last longer. Proper maintenance keeps it clean and ready to use.

Storing the sprinkler correctly protects it from damage and weather effects. Follow simple steps to keep it in good shape.

### Cleaning Tips

Clean the sprinkler after each use to remove dirt and debris. Use a soft cloth and mild soap with water.

Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the material. Rinse well and dry completely before storing.

* Wipe the surface gently
* Use mild soap and water
* Rinse off all soap residues
* Let it dry in the shade

### Proper Deflation

Deflate the sprinkler carefully to avoid tears. Open the air valves and press out the air slowly.

Do not fold or roll the sprinkler while it still has air inside. Make sure it is fully flat before folding.

* Open all air valves
* Press out air gently
* Wait until fully deflated
* Fold carefully without forcing

### Storage Solutions

Store the sprinkler in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. This prevents fading and material damage.

Use a storage bag or box to protect it from dust and pests. Keep it off the ground to avoid moisture.

* Choose a dry storage area
* Avoid direct sunlight
* Use a clean storage bag or box
* Keep off damp floors

## Safety Precautions For Kids

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=playing-with-an-outdoor-crocodile-inflatable-sprinkler-is-fun-for-kids.-it-helps-them-stay-cool-on-hot-days.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Playing with an outdoor crocodile inflatable sprinkler is fun for kids. It helps them stay cool on hot days. Safety is very important to keep playtime happy and safe.

Adults must watch kids closely. Knowing simple safety tips helps prevent accidents and injuries.

### Supervision Guidelines

Always watch children while they play with the sprinkler. Kids can slip or get hurt if left alone. Stay close and ready to help at any time.

Make sure the play area is safe and clear of obstacles. Remind kids to be gentle and take turns to avoid pushing or rough play.

* Keep an adult nearby during play
* Set rules for safe play
* Stop play if kids become too rough
* Check the sprinkler for damages regularly

### Avoiding Slips And Falls

Wet areas can be slippery. Help kids move carefully around the sprinkler. Teach them to walk, not run, on wet surfaces.

Clear the ground of toys or debris that can cause tripping. Use non-slip mats near the sprinkler if possible.

* Encourage slow walking on wet grass or concrete
* Remove sharp or hard objects from play area
* Keep shoes on if recommended by parents
* Dry off wet spots if they get too slippery

### Sun Protection Tips

Kids play outside under the sun. Protect their skin by applying sunscreen before playtime. Reapply sunscreen every two hours or after swimming.

Use hats and sunglasses to guard kids’ eyes and faces. Play in shaded areas when possible to avoid strong sun rays.

* Use broad-spectrum sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher
* Apply sunscreen 15 minutes before playing
* Wear wide-brimmed hats and UV-protective sunglasses
* Take breaks in shaded or indoor spots
