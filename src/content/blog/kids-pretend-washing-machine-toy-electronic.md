## Safety Tips For Electronic Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=electronic-toys-like-kids-pretend-washing-machines-are-fun-but-need-safety.-follow-these-tips-for-a-safe-playtime.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Electronic toys like kids pretend washing machines are fun but need safety. Follow these tips for a safe playtime.

Understanding how to manage and maintain electronic toys ensures your child’s safety. Let's explore some key guidelines.

### Supervision Guidelines

Always keep an eye on your child while they play with electronic toys. This prevents accidents and ensures safe use.

Teach children the right way to handle the toy. Explain what they should not do, like putting it in water.

* Monitor playtime activities
* Set clear rules for toy usage
* Keep toys away from water

### Proper Maintenance

Regularly check the toy for damage. Broken parts can be dangerous. Fix or replace them promptly.

Follow the manufacturer's instructions for cleaning. Keep the toy clean to ensure it works well and safely.

* Inspect for broken parts
* Follow cleaning instructions
* Store in a dry place

### Avoiding Small Parts Hazards

Electronic toys may have small parts. Ensure they are securely attached to prevent choking hazards.

Keep toys with small parts away from younger children. Check the age recommendations on the toy's packaging.

* Check for loose small parts
* Store away from young children
* Follow age guidelines
