## Tips For First-time Flyers

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=flying-toy-drones-is-fun-and-exciting-for-beginners.-learning-some-tips-helps-you-enjoy-flying-safely-and-easily.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Flying toy drones is fun and exciting for beginners. Learning some tips helps you enjoy flying safely and easily.

This guide shares basic controls, safety rules, and care tips for your new drone under $50.

### Basic Flight Controls

Most toy drones have simple controls for easy flying. You use two sticks on the remote to move the drone.

One stick controls the height and rotation. The other controls forward, backward, left, and right movements.

* Left stick: up/down and spin left/right
* Right stick: move forward/backward and left/right
* Practice in open areas with no obstacles

### Safety Precautions

Safety is very important for new flyers. Always check your area to avoid hitting people or animals.

Keep the drone away from water and strong winds. Fly only during the day and in good weather.

* Fly in open spaces with few obstacles
* Stay away from roads, crowds, and power lines
* Do not fly near airports or restricted zones
* Keep the drone within your sight at all times

### Maintenance And Care

Taking care of your toy drone helps it last longer. Clean the drone after each use to remove dust and dirt.

Charge the battery fully before flying and avoid overcharging. Store the drone in a dry place at room temperature.

* Clean propellers and body with a soft cloth
* Check for loose or damaged parts regularly
* Use the correct charger to protect the battery
* Store drone away from heat and moisture
