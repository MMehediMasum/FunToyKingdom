### Puzzle Sizes And Piece Counts

Puzzles come in many sizes and piece counts. Small puzzles have fewer pieces and are easier to complete. Large puzzles have more pieces and take longer to solve.

* Small puzzles: 100 to 300 pieces, good for beginners and kids.
* Medium puzzles: 500 to 1,000 pieces, suitable for regular puzzlers.
* Large puzzles: 2,000 pieces and above, for experienced puzzlers.
* Mini puzzles: 50 pieces or less, great for very young children.

## Creating Your Custom Puzzle

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=custom-photo-jigsaw-puzzles-make-great-gifts-and-fun-projects.-you-can-create-a-puzzle-from-your-favorite-picture.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Custom photo jigsaw puzzles make great gifts and fun projects. You can create a puzzle from your favorite picture. This guide will help you make a perfect puzzle.

Follow these tips to select, edit, and order your puzzle easily and with good results.

### Selecting The Perfect Photo

Choose a photo that is clear and bright. Avoid blurry or dark images. The puzzle pieces show every detail, so pick a picture with simple shapes and colors.

* Use high-resolution photos for sharp images
* Choose pictures with good lighting
* Avoid too many small details that are hard to see
* Select images with strong contrast between colors
* Pick photos that are meaningful or fun to you

### Editing Tips For Best Results

Edit your photo to improve its look before ordering. Simple edits can make a big difference on the puzzle.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Edit Type | Why It Helps |
| Crop | Focus on the main subject and remove distractions |
| Adjust Brightness | Make the image lighter or darker for clarity |
| Increase Contrast | Make colors stand out more |
| Sharpen | Enhance details for clearer puzzle pieces |
| Remove Red-Eye | Fix eye color for a natural look |

### Ordering Process Explained

Ordering your custom puzzle is simple and fast. Follow the steps to complete your purchase without trouble.

1. Upload your edited photo to the puzzle maker website
2. Choose the puzzle size and number of pieces
3. Preview your puzzle before finalizing
4. Enter your shipping and payment details
5. Confirm your order and wait for delivery

## Care And Storage Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=custom-photo-jigsaw-puzzles-are-fun-and-engaging.-they-bring-personal-memories-to-life.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Custom photo jigsaw puzzles are fun and engaging. They bring personal memories to life. Keeping them clean and stored well helps them last longer.

Proper care and storage ensure your puzzles stay vibrant and intact. Here are some simple tips to follow.

### Cleaning Your Puzzle

Dust and dirt can settle on puzzle pieces. Clean them gently to maintain their look.

* Use a soft, dry cloth to wipe the pieces.
* Avoid using water or liquid cleaners.
* Brush gently to remove any stuck particles.

### Safe Storage Solutions

Storing puzzles properly keeps them safe from damage. It also makes them easy to find later.

* Keep puzzles in their original box.
* Store in a dry place away from sunlight.
* Use resealable plastic bags for loose pieces.

### Preserving Puzzle Quality

Quality puzzles are fun to solve over and over. Preserve them to enjoy them for years.

* Avoid bending or folding the pieces.
* Handle the pieces with clean, dry hands.
* Consider using a puzzle mat for unfinished puzzles.
