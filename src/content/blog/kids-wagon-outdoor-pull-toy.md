## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-kids-wagon-outdoor-pull-toy-in-good-shape-helps-it-last-longer.-regular-care-makes-playtime-safe-and-fun.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Kids Wagon Outdoor Pull Toy in good shape helps it last longer. Regular care makes playtime safe and fun.

Follow simple maintenance steps to clean, check, and fix the wagon as needed.

### Cleaning And Storage

Clean the wagon after each use to remove dirt and dust. Use mild soap and water with a soft cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the paint or materials.

* Wipe down all surfaces with a damp cloth
* Dry the wagon completely to prevent rust
* Store it in a dry, shaded area to protect from sun and rain
* Cover the wagon with a cloth or tarp if kept outdoors

### Regular Safety Checks

Check the wagon often to keep it safe for children. Look for loose parts, sharp edges, or worn wheels.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| What to Check | What to Look For |
| Wheels | Spin smoothly, no cracks |
| Handle | Secure attachment, no bends |
| Body | No sharp edges or splinters |
| Fasteners | Tight screws and bolts |

### Repairing Common Issues

Fix minor problems quickly to avoid bigger damage. Tighten loose screws and replace broken parts as needed.

* Use a screwdriver to tighten screws
* Lubricate wheel axles to keep them moving smoothly
* Replace worn or cracked wheels with new ones
* Sand rough or splintered wooden parts carefully
