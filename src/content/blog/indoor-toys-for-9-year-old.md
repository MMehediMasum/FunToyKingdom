## Choosing Safe And Durable Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=finding-the-right-indoor-toys-for-a-9-year-old-can-be-fun.-parents-want-toys-that-are-both-safe-and-long-lasting.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Finding the right indoor toys for a 9-year-old can be fun. Parents want toys that are both safe and long-lasting.

It's important to consider the quality, safety, and suitability of toys. Let's explore the key aspects to look for.

### Material Quality

High-quality materials ensure toys last longer. They also make them safer for children. Check for non-toxic labels on toy packaging.

* Look for BPA-free plastics
* Choose toys made of natural wood
* Avoid toys with sharp edges

### Age Appropriateness

Toys should match the child’s age and abilities. A toy too advanced can frustrate them. A simple toy might bore them.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Age Group | Toy Type |
| 6-8 years | Building Blocks |
| 9-10 years | Puzzles and Board Games |

### Safety Certifications

Look for safety marks on toys. These marks show the toy meets safety standards. Certifications like CE or ASTM are important.

Always read the safety instructions provided with the toy.
