## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is The Largest Jigsaw Puzzle Ever Made?

The largest jigsaw puzzle ever made contained over 54,000 pieces. It was created by Ravensburger in 2011. The puzzle measured around 17,000 square inches. It set a world record for size and piece count.

### How Long Does It Take To Complete A Huge Puzzle?

Completing a massive jigsaw puzzle can take weeks or months. It depends on the puzzle's size and complexity. Some teams spend hundreds of hours piecing together record-breaking puzzles.

### Who Holds The World Record For Puzzle Size?

Ravensburger currently holds the world record for the largest jigsaw puzzle. Their 54,000-piece puzzle is the biggest officially recognized by Guinness World Records.
