## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Group Is Ideal For Foam Block Toys?



Foam block toys suit toddlers from 18 months to 5 years. They support early learning, sensory development, and motor skills. Different sets offer age-appropriate sizes and complexity. Always check product details for recommended ages to ensure safe and effective play.



### Are Foam Block Toys Safe And Non-toxic For Kids?



Yes, most foam block toys are made from non-toxic, BPA-free EVA foam. They are soft, lightweight, and safe for toddlers to handle. Always verify product certifications and avoid blocks with small parts for younger children to prevent choking hazards.



### How Do Foam Blocks Support Early Learning?



Foam blocks enhance cognitive skills like counting, color recognition, and shape identification. They boost hand-eye coordination, fine motor skills, and spatial awareness. Many sets include numbers, letters, or colors to combine fun with educational benefits.



### Can Foam Blocks Be Used As Bath Toys?



Yes, many foam block sets are waterproof and designed for bath time fun. They stick to wet surfaces and float, making them perfect for sensory play in water. Always supervise children during bath play to ensure safety.



### How Many Pieces Come In Typical Foam Block Sets?



Foam block sets vary widely, from 30 to over 100 pieces. Larger sets offer more building options and educational value. Choose a set based on your child’s age, interest, and the available storage space.



### Are Foam Block Toys Easy To Clean And Maintain?



Yes, foam blocks are washable and quick to dry. You can clean them with mild soap and water. Their durable material resists mold and mildew, making them ideal for both indoor and outdoor play.
