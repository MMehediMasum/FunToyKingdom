## Frequently Asked Questions



### Are Rope Toys Safe For Aggressive Chewers?



Yes, heavy-duty rope toys made from durable cotton blend fibers are safe for aggressive chewers. They resist shredding and reduce choking risks. Always supervise play to ensure safety and replace toys if they show excessive wear or damage.



### How Do Rope Toys Help With Dog Dental Health?



Rope toys naturally floss dogs’ teeth during chewing. They reduce plaque and tartar buildup. This supports healthier gums and fresher breath. Regular use promotes better oral hygiene as part of your dog’s dental care routine.



### Can Small Dogs Safely Use Large Breed Rope Toys?



Small dogs can use rope toys sized for their breed. Large breed toys may be too heavy or large. Choose toys designed for your dog’s size to ensure safe, comfortable play and effective chewing experience.



### What Materials Make Dog Rope Toys Safe And Durable?



Safe rope toys use 100% natural or cotton-poly blends. These materials are non-toxic, washable, and strong. They provide durability while being gentle on teeth and gums. Avoid synthetic fibers that could harm dogs if ingested.



### How Do Rope Toys Benefit Puppies During Teething?



Rope toys soothe puppies’ gums and ease teething discomfort. Chewing helps relieve irritation and strengthens jaw muscles. Choosing soft but durable ropes ensures safe, enjoyable chewing without damaging emerging teeth.



### Are Interactive Rope Toys Good For Dog Boredom?



Yes, interactive rope toys engage dogs through tug-of-war and fetch. They stimulate physical and mental activity, reducing boredom and destructive behavior. Using these toys regularly keeps dogs entertained and well-exercised.
