**Infant rattle toys help babies explore sounds and improve hand coordination. These toys keep little ones entertained while supporting early development.**



Rattles come in many shapes, colors, and materials. Bright Starts Oball Shaker and Baby Einstein Opus Octopus offer safe, BPA-free options for newborns and up. Easy-to-grasp designs like Bright Starts Oball Teal or iPlay’s 10-piece set encourage babies to hold and shake.



Some rattles also serve as teethers, soothing sore gums. Hanging crib rattles and wrist rattles add fun during playtime or travel. Soft, musical rattles like Fisher-Price Maracas introduce sound learning. Choosing the right rattle helps babies practice grip, cause-and-effect, and sensory skills. These simple toys make great gifts for newborn showers or birthdays. Overall, infant rattles are essential tools for early growth and joyful play.
