## Maintenance And Care Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-outdoor-leopard-inflatable-pool-toy-in-good-shape-makes-it-last-longer.-proper-care-helps-avoid-damage-and-keeps-it-safe-for-fun.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Outdoor Leopard Inflatable Pool Toy in good shape makes it last longer. Proper care helps avoid damage and keeps it safe for fun.

Follow these tips for cleaning, protecting, and storing your inflatable pool toy.

### Cleaning After Use

Clean your inflatable toy after each use to remove dirt and germs. Use mild soap and water. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the material.

* Rinse with clean water to remove sand and debris
* Use soft cloth or sponge to wash the surface
* Dry completely before storing to prevent mold
* Check all seams for dirt or damage

### Preventing Punctures

Protect your inflatable from sharp objects and rough surfaces. Avoid dragging it on the ground. Watch for pets that might bite.

**Quick Tips to Avoid Punctures:**

* Clear the area of sticks, stones, and sharp toys
* Keep away from pets with sharp claws or teeth
* Use a ground cloth or mat under the pool toy
* Handle gently during setup and takedown

### Proper Storage Methods

Store your inflatable pool toy in a cool, dry place. Avoid direct sunlight and heat, which can weaken the material. Fold it carefully to avoid creases.

| **Storage Tip** | **Reason** |
| --- | --- |
| Deflate fully | Prevents damage from pressure |
| Fold gently without sharp creases | Maintains shape and avoids cracks |
| Keep away from sharp objects | Prevents accidental punctures |
| Store in a sealed bag or container | Keeps dust and moisture out |
| Keep in a cool, dry place | Extends material life |
