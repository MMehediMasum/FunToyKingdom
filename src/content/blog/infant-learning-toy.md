Fisher-Price stacking toys and Sassy stacking rings encourage problem-solving and motor skills. Each toy offers unique benefits, making them ideal for gifts and everyday play.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Is Best For Infant Learning Toys?



Infant learning toys suit ages 0 to 18 months. Toys like Montessori sensory sets and stacking blocks support early brain development and motor skills for each growth stage.



### How Do Montessori Sensory Toys Benefit Babies?



Montessori sensory toys enhance cognitive and sensory skills. They stimulate touch, sight, and hearing, aiding in focused learning and fine motor development for infants 0-12 months.



### Are Teething Toys Safe For Newborns?



Yes, teething toys made from non-toxic, BPA-free materials are safe for newborns. They soothe gums and support oral development from 0 to 18 months.



### Which Toys Help Develop Motor Skills In Infants?



Stacking rings, blocks, and interactive walkers improve motor skills. These toys encourage hand-eye coordination, grasping, and movement in babies aged 6+ months.



### What Features Make A Good Developmental Toy Gift?



Good developmental toys include bright colors, sounds, and textures. They promote sensory exploration, cause-and-effect learning, and age-appropriate challenges for infants 1-2 years.



### How Do Musical Toys Support Infant Learning?



Musical toys boost auditory skills and memory. They engage babies through lights and sounds, encouraging interaction and cognitive growth from 6 months onward.
