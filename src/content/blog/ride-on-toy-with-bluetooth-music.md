## Maintenance And Care

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=proper-care-keeps-your-ride-on-toy-with-bluetooth-music-working-well.-follow-simple-tips-to-extend-its-life.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Proper care keeps your ride on toy with Bluetooth music working well. Follow simple tips to extend its life.

Regular checks and cleaning help maintain safety and fun during playtime.

### Battery Charging Tips

Charge the battery fully before first use. Avoid overcharging to protect battery life.

1. Use the charger that came with the toy.
2. Charge in a dry, cool place.
3. Unplug the charger once the battery is full.
4. Do not charge overnight.
5. Charge every few weeks if not in use.

### Cleaning And Storage

Keep your ride on toy clean and dry. Store it in a safe spot to avoid damage.

* Wipe the surface with a damp cloth.
* Do not use harsh chemicals or water jets.
* Remove dirt from wheels regularly.
* Store indoors away from sunlight and rain.

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

| Issue | Possible Cause | Solution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Toy does not turn on | Battery not charged | Charge battery fully |
| Bluetooth not connecting | Out of range or paired to another device | Move closer and reset Bluetooth |
| Wheels not moving smoothly | Dirt or debris stuck | Clean wheels and check for damage |
