## Caring For Wooden Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=handmade-wooden-toy-animals-are-loved-by-children-and-adults-alike.-they-are-durable-and-have-a-charming,-timeless-appeal.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Handmade wooden toy animals are loved by children and adults alike. They are durable and have a charming, timeless appeal.

Proper care ensures these toys last for generations. Here are some simple tips to keep them in good condition.

### Cleaning Tips

Cleaning wooden toys is easy and quick. Use a soft, damp cloth to wipe the surface gently.

Avoid soaking them in water to prevent damage. Mild soap can be used if needed.

* Use a soft cloth for dusting
* Use mild soap for sticky spots
* Avoid harsh chemicals

### Storage Suggestions

Store wooden toys properly to keep them safe. Choose a dry and cool place for storage.

Keep them away from direct sunlight to prevent fading. Use storage boxes to organize them neatly.

* Store in a dry place
* Avoid direct sunlight
* Use boxes for organization

### Repairing Minor Damages

Minor damages like scratches can be fixed. Sand the surface lightly with fine sandpaper.

Apply a natural oil or beeswax to restore the finish. This keeps the toys looking new.

* Sand with fine sandpaper
* Apply natural oil
* Use beeswax for shine
