## Game Basics

Clue Junior and Classic Clue are mystery board games. Both ask players to find out who did the crime. The games have different rules and themes.

This guide explains the main points of each game. You will learn about the goals, player setup, and game parts.

### Core Objectives

In both games, players try to solve a mystery. They collect clues to guess the suspect, location, and weapon. The first player to solve the case wins.

* **Classic Clue:**Find who committed the murder, where, and with what weapon.
* **Clue Junior:**Discover who took the cake, where they hid it, and what item they used.

### Player Setup

Players choose their characters and get ready to play. The number of players can change the game length and difficulty.

1. **Classic Clue:**3 to 6 players select characters like Miss Scarlet or Colonel Mustard.
2. **Clue Junior:**2 to 4 players pick kid-friendly characters with simpler roles.
3. Each player gets cards and a detective notepad to track clues.
4. Place the game board in the center for all to reach.

### Game Components

| Component | Classic Clue | Clue Junior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Game Board | Detailed mansion with rooms and hallways | Colorful house with kid-friendly rooms |
| Characters | 6 suspects with unique tokens | 4 child characters with simple designs |
| Cards | Suspect, Weapon, Room cards | Suspect, Item, Room cards |
| Weapons/Items | 6 classic weapons like candlestick | Fun objects like a soccer ball or hat |
| Notepad | Detective sheets for clues | Simple clue sheets for kids |

Clue Junior and Classic Clue are both detective board games. They differ in age suitability and how hard they are to play.

### Target Age Groups

Clue Junior is made for younger children. It is simpler and easier to understand.

Classic Clue is designed for older kids and adults. It needs more thinking and strategy.

* Clue Junior: Best for ages 5 to 8
* Classic Clue: Best for ages 8 and up

### Rule Simplicity

Clue Junior uses easy rules. Players guess which character did it with simple clues.

Classic Clue has more rules. Players collect clues about the weapon, room, and suspect.

* Clue Junior: Few rules, easy to learn
* Classic Clue: More rules, requires strategy

### Game Duration

Clue Junior games are shorter. They keep young players interested without long waits.

Classic Clue games last longer. They take more time because of detailed guessing.

* Clue Junior: About 15 to 30 minutes
* Classic Clue: About 45 to 60 minutes
