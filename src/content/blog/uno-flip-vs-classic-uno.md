## Game Basics

Uno Flip and Classic Uno are card games where players try to get rid of their cards first. Both games use colorful cards with numbers and special actions.

Players take turns matching cards by color or number. The goal is to be the first player with no cards left.

### Classic Uno Rules

Classic Uno uses a deck of 108 cards in four colors: red, green, blue, and yellow. Each color has numbers 0 to 9 and special cards.

Players start with seven cards. On your turn, play a card that matches the top card’s color or number. If you cannot play, draw a card.

* Special cards include Skip, Reverse, Draw Two, Wild, and Wild Draw Four
* When a player has one card left, they must say “Uno”
* The first player to get rid of all cards wins the round

### Uno Flip Rules

Uno Flip adds a double-sided deck with a light and a dark side. Players start on the light side but can flip the deck during play.

The dark side has different colors and stronger action cards. When a Flip card is played, all players turn their cards to the dark side.

* Each side has unique action cards like Skip, Draw One, Draw Five, and Reverse
* Players must match cards by color or number on the current side
* The game ends when a player discards all cards on either side

## Special Cards Impact

Special cards change the flow and strategy of Uno games. They add excitement and surprise to every turn.

Classic Uno and Uno Flip use different special cards. Each type affects the game in unique ways.

### Classic Uno Special Cards

Classic Uno has four main special cards that impact players directly. These cards can skip turns, reverse play, or force players to draw cards.

* **Skip:**The next player misses their turn.
* **Reverse:**Changes the direction of play.
* **Draw Two:**The next player draws two cards and loses their turn.
* **Wild:**Allows the player to choose the next color.
* **Wild Draw Four:**Lets the player pick a color, and the next player draws four cards and skips their turn.

### Uno Flip Special Cards

Uno Flip uses a double-sided deck with a light and a dark side. Special cards on each side create new challenges.

| Card Name | Effect | Side |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flip | Switches all cards to the opposite side of the deck. | Both |
| Skip | Next player loses their turn. | Light and Dark |
| Draw One | Next player draws one card and misses their turn. | Light |
| Draw Five | Next player draws five cards and misses their turn. | Dark |
| Wild | Player chooses the next color to play. | Both |
| Wild Draw Color | Player picks a color, and next player draws cards until they pick that color. | Dark |

### Is Uno Flip Better Than Classic Uno For Families?

Uno Flip offers more complexity, which some families enjoy for a fresh challenge. Classic Uno is simpler and ideal for younger kids or casual players. Choose Uno Flip for variety and Classic Uno for easy, quick gameplay.

### How Do The Action Cards Differ In Uno Flip?

### Can You Play Uno Flip With Classic Uno Rules?
