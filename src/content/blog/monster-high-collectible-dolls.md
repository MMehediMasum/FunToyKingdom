## Caring For Your Collection

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-monster-high-collectible-dolls-in-great-shape-takes-some-care.-proper-handling-and-storage-help-keep-them-looking-new-for-years.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Monster High collectible dolls in great shape takes some care. Proper handling and storage help keep them looking new for years.

This guide shares easy tips on storage, cleaning, and safe display. Follow these steps to protect your dolls from damage.

### Proper Storage Techniques

Store your dolls in a dry, cool place away from direct sunlight. This prevents colors from fading and materials from warping.

* Keep dolls in their original boxes if possible
* Use acid-free tissue paper to wrap dolls for extra protection
* Avoid plastic bags that can trap moisture
* Store dolls upright to keep their shapes intact

### Cleaning And Maintenance

Regular cleaning helps keep your dolls free from dust and dirt. Use gentle methods to avoid damage to clothes and paint.

1. Dust dolls with a soft brush or cloth
2. Spot clean clothes with a damp cloth and mild soap
3. Use cotton swabs for small, hard-to-reach areas
4. Never submerge dolls in water
5. Allow dolls to air dry if they get damp

### Displaying Dolls Safely

| Display Method | Benefits | Precautions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glass Cabinet | Protects from dust and accidents | Avoid direct sunlight exposure |
| Open Shelf | Easy access and viewing | Dust regularly and keep away from pets |
| Display Stand | Supports doll posture | Use soft materials to avoid scratches |
