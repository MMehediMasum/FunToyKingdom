## Origins Of Cabbage Patch Kids

Cabbage Patch Kids are popular dolls loved by many collectors. They have a unique story that began in the 1970s.

This article explores how these dolls started and became famous worldwide.

### Creation By Xavier Roberts

Xavier Roberts created the first Cabbage Patch Kids in 1978. He called them "Little People" at first. The dolls had soft bodies and hand-stitched faces.

Roberts wanted to make dolls that felt special and personal for children.

### Mass Production And Popularity

In 1982, the dolls became mass-produced by Coleco. This made them more available to the public. The name changed to Cabbage Patch Kids.

By the mid-1980s, these dolls were a huge hit with children and collectors alike.

### Unique Features And Appeal

* Each doll has a unique name and birth certificate.
* They have soft, fabric bodies with plastic heads.
* Their faces are hand-sculpted for individuality.
* They come in different skin tones and hair types.

These features made the dolls feel like real children to many buyers.

## How To Authenticate Your Doll

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=rare-cabbage-patch-kids-dolls-can-be-valuable.-knowing-how-to-check-if-your-doll-is-real-is-important.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Rare Cabbage Patch Kids dolls can be valuable. Knowing how to check if your doll is real is important. This guide helps you learn to tell real dolls from fakes.

Two main ways to check your doll are by looking at manufacturer marks and original features. Follow these tips to spot an authentic doll.

### Identifying Manufacturer Marks

Look for marks that show who made the doll. These marks are often on the back of the doll’s head or on its bottom. They prove the doll came from the original company.

* Check for the "Original Appalachian Artworks" stamp.
* Look for a copyright year from the 1980s or early 1990s.
* Find the name “Cabbage Patch Kids” printed on the doll or tags.
* Examine the mold number or batch code near the neck or back.
* Ensure the marks are clear and not smudged or missing.

### Checking For Original Features

Original dolls have special features that fake dolls often lack. These include the doll’s face, hair, and clothing style. Check these details to confirm your doll’s value.

## Care Tips To Preserve Value

Cabbage Patch Kids have been a beloved toy for generations, and rare ones can be highly valuable. To preserve their value, it's essential to provide proper care and maintenance.

### Storage Recommendations

When storing your rare Cabbage Patch Kids, make sure to keep them away from direct sunlight and moisture. This will help prevent damage to the doll's skin and prevent the growth of mold.

| Storage Tips | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- |
| Use acid-free tissue paper or cloth to wrap the doll. | This prevents damage to the doll's skin and prevents the growth of mold. |
| Store the doll in a sealed plastic bag or container. | This protects the doll from dust and other environmental factors that can cause damage. |
| Keep the doll in a cool, dry place. | This prevents the growth of mold and mildew, which can damage the doll's skin. |

### Cleaning And Maintenance

Cleaning and maintenance are essential to preserving the value of your rare Cabbage Patch Kids. Regular cleaning can help prevent dirt and dust from accumulating and causing damage to the doll's skin.

1. Clean the doll gently with a soft-bristled brush and mild soap.
2. Avoid using harsh chemicals or abrasive materials that can damage the doll's skin.
3. Dry the doll thoroughly after cleaning to prevent water spots.
4. Store the doll in a clean, dry place to prevent the growth of mold and mildew.

By following these care tips, you can help preserve the value of your rare Cabbage Patch Kids and keep them in great condition for years to come.
