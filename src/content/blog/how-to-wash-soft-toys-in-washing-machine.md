**Are your soft toys looking a little tired and in need of a fresh clean? You might be wondering if it’s safe to toss them in your washing machine without ruining their softness or shape.**

The good news is, you can easily wash your beloved plush friends at home—and keep them fresh and fluffy for years to come. In this guide, you’ll discover simple, step-by-step tips to clean your soft toys safely and effectively using your washing machine.

Keep reading, and you’ll never hesitate to refresh your favorite cuddly companions again!




## Preparing Soft Toys For Washing

Preparing soft toys for washing helps keep them safe and clean. Proper [steps stop](https://funtoykingdom.com/how-to-turn-off-toy-dog-that-walks/) damage during the wash. It also keeps the toy’s shape and colors strong. Follow simple checks before placing toys in the machine.

### Check Toy Labels

Look at the toy’s label for washing instructions. Some soft toys cannot go in the washing machine. Others may need a gentle cycle or cold water only. Labels show the best way to clean without harm.

### Remove Loose Parts

Take off any loose parts like ribbons or buttons. These can come off or damage the toy and machine. Removing loose parts keeps the toy intact and the wash safe.

### Secure Small Pieces

Small pieces like eyes or noses must be secure. Use a needle and thread to sew loose parts tightly. This stops them from falling off during washing. It also protects the washing machine from damage.

## Choosing The Right Washing Settings

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-washing-settings-is-key-to-keeping-soft-[toys-safe-and-clean](https://funtoykingdom.com/how-do-you-clean-battery-acid-out-of-a-toy/).-the-wrong-settings-can-damage-their-fabric-or-stuffing.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right washing settings is key to keeping soft [toys safe and clean](https://funtoykingdom.com/how-do-you-clean-battery-acid-out-of-a-toy/). The wrong settings can damage their fabric or stuffing. Proper settings protect the toy’s shape and colors. Follow these simple steps for the best results.

### Select Gentle Cycle

Pick the gentle or delicate cycle on your washing machine. This cycle moves the toy slowly. It stops rough spinning that can harm soft toys. Gentle cycles clean without shaking or stretching.

### Use Cold Water

Always use cold water for washing soft toys. Cold water stops colors from fading. It also protects the fabric from shrinking. Hot water can weaken the toy’s materials over time.

### Pick Mild Detergent

Choose a mild detergent with no strong chemicals. Harsh detergents can cause fabric damage or skin irritation. Mild detergent cleans softly and keeps the toy fresh. Use just a small amount for best care.

## Protecting Soft Toys During Wash

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=soft-toys-need-care-during-machine-washing-to-stay-safe-and-last-longer.-the-washing-machine-can-be-rough-on-them.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Soft toys need care during machine washing to stay safe and last longer. The washing machine can be rough on them. Protecting soft toys helps keep their shape and softness. Follow simple steps to protect your toys well.

### Use A Mesh Laundry Bag

Put soft toys inside a mesh laundry bag before washing. The bag [stops toys](https://funtoykingdom.com/are-there-any-gacha-capsule-toy-stops-in-the-usa/) from hitting the machine drum hard. It lowers the risk of tears and damage. The mesh lets water and detergent reach the toy. This keeps toys clean without rough handling.

### Add Towels For Cushioning

Place towels around soft toys in the washing machine. Towels act as cushions to absorb shocks. They stop toys from bouncing and rubbing against the drum. This reduces wear and tear during the wash. Towels also balance the load for a gentle cycle.




## Drying Soft Toys Safely

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=drying-soft-toys-properly-keeps-them-clean-and-fluffy.-it-stops-mold-and-bad-smells-from-forming.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Drying soft toys properly keeps them clean and fluffy. It stops mold and bad smells from forming. Soft toys need gentle drying to keep their shape and color. Use the right drying method to protect your child's favorite toy.

### Air Drying Tips

Air drying is the safest way to dry soft toys. Place the toy on a clean towel in a dry place. Turn the toy often to dry all sides. Avoid direct sunlight to stop colors from fading. Let the toy dry fully before use to prevent dampness.

### Using Dryer With Care

Some soft toys can go in the dryer on low heat. Use a gentle or air fluff setting without heat if possible. Put the toy inside a pillowcase or laundry bag to protect it. Check the toy often to avoid overheating or damage. Remove the toy as soon as it is dry.

### Fluffing And Reshaping

After drying, soft toys may look flat or misshaped. Gently squeeze and pat the toy to restore its fluff. Use your hands to reshape ears, arms, and legs. Brushing the toy with a soft brush can help remove clumps. Fluffing keeps the toy soft and cuddly.

## Extra Care For Delicate Or Vintage Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=delicate-and-vintage-soft-toys-need-gentle-care-during-washing.-these-toys-often-have-fragile-fabrics,-loose-parts,-or-special-materials.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Delicate and vintage soft toys need gentle care during washing. These toys often have fragile fabrics, loose parts, or special materials. Washing them in a machine without caution can damage their look and feel. Taking extra care protects their charm and keeps them safe for a long time.

### Spot Cleaning Alternatives

Spot cleaning helps remove small stains without full washing. Use a soft cloth or cotton swab dipped in mild soap and water. Gently dab the stained area. Avoid rubbing hard to prevent fabric damage. Let the toy air dry completely. This method keeps the [toy clean and safe](https://funtoykingdom.com/how-to-clean-teething-toys/).

### Hand Washing Methods

Hand washing is a safer option for delicate toys. Fill a basin with lukewarm water and mild detergent. Submerge the toy and gently squeeze it to clean. Avoid twisting or wringing. Rinse the toy well in clean water. Press out extra water softly using a towel. Dry the toy in a shaded, well-ventilated place. This method keeps the toy soft and intact.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I Wash All Soft Toys In A Washing Machine?

Not all soft toys are machine washable. Check the care label first. Delicate or electronic-filled toys need hand washing to avoid damage.

### What Washing Settings Are Best For Soft Toys?

Use a gentle or delicate cycle with cold water. This protects the toy’s fabric and stuffing from damage during washing.

### Should I Use Detergent Or Fabric Softener For Soft Toys?

Use a mild detergent only. Avoid fabric softeners as they may leave residue and harm the toy’s fabric or stuffing.

### How Can I Protect Soft Toys In The Washing Machine?

Place toys inside a mesh laundry bag or pillowcase. This prevents tearing and protects delicate parts during the wash cycle.

## Conclusion

Washing soft toys in a machine is easy with the right steps. Use a gentle cycle and mild detergent for best care. Place toys inside a pillowcase or laundry bag to protect them. Avoid washing toys with batteries or delicate parts.
