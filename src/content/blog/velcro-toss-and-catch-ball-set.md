The Velcro Toss And Catch is a fun outdoor activity. It involves throwing and catching balls with Velcro pads.

You play in pairs or groups, aiming to catch the ball on your Velcro pad. It helps improve hand-eye coordination and is great for all ages.

### Basic Rules

Players stand a short distance apart. One player throws the ball while the other tries to catch it on their Velcro pad.

* Throw the ball underhand or overhand.
* Catch the ball only on the Velcro pad.
* Step back after each successful catch to increase difficulty.
* If the ball misses the pad, the thrower gets a point.
* The first player to reach five points wins.

### Game Variations

You can change the game to suit the players’ skill levels or to add fun twists. Here are some popular variations:

* **Timed Catch:**See how many catches players can make in one minute.
* **Moving Target:**Players walk or jog while tossing and catching.
* **Team Play:**Form teams and pass the ball down a line of players.
* **Distance Challenge:**Increase the throwing distance after every successful catch.
