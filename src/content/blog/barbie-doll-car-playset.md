## Care And Maintenance

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-barbie-doll-car-playset-in-good-shape-makes-playtime-more-fun.-proper-care-helps-the-set-last-longer.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Barbie Doll Car Playset in good shape makes playtime more fun. Proper care helps the set last longer. It also keeps all parts working well.

This guide covers cleaning, storage, and fixing tips. Follow these steps to protect your playset from damage.

### Cleaning Tips

Use a soft cloth to wipe the car and doll surfaces. Avoid harsh chemicals that can fade colors or harm plastic parts.

* Wipe the car body with a damp cloth.
* Use mild soap for tough spots, then rinse well.
* Dry all parts with a soft towel to stop water damage.
* Clean small accessories gently with a soft brush.

### Storage Suggestions

Store your playset in a cool, dry place. Keep it away from direct sunlight to avoid color fading. Organize small pieces to prevent loss.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Storage Tip** | **Reason** |
| Use a box with compartments | Keeps small parts separated and safe |
| Cover with a soft cloth | Protects from dust and scratches |
| Avoid humid areas | Prevents mold and plastic warping |
| Store away from heat sources | Prevents damage to plastic and paint |

### Repair And Replacement

Check your playset often for broken or loose parts. Fix small cracks with safe glue made for toys. Replace lost or damaged pieces to keep play safe.

* Use non-toxic glue for repairs.
* Ask for replacement parts from the seller if available.
* Keep small screws and connectors in a safe place.
* Do not force broken parts; it may cause more damage.
