## Maintaining Your Collection

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=a-well-maintained-collection-of-mandalorian-action-figures-can-last-for-generations.-regular-care-helps-preserve-the-condition-and-value-of-your-figures.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

A well-maintained collection of Mandalorian action figures can last for generations. Regular care helps preserve the condition and value of your figures.

### Cleaning Techniques

Cleaning your Mandalorian action figures requires gentle techniques to avoid damaging the paint or removing the decals. Use soft-bristled brushes to remove dust and debris from the figure's surface.

* Use a soft-bristled brush to sweep away loose dust and debris.
* Dampen a clean cloth with water, but make sure it's not soaking wet.
* Gently wipe the figure's surface with the damp cloth, starting from the top and working your way down.

### Preservation Best Practices

Proper storage and handling are crucial to maintaining the condition of your Mandalorian action figures. Avoid exposing them to direct sunlight, moisture, or extreme temperatures.

Store your figures in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight. Use acid-free tissue paper or bubble wrap to prevent scratching and damage during storage.

Handle your figures with clean, dry hands to prevent oil and dirt from transferring to the figure's surface. Avoid touching the figure's paint or decals, as oils from your skin can damage them over time.
