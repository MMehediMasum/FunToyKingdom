Parents and caregivers can use these toys to make playtime both fun and educational. Selecting age-appropriate and safe toys ensures babies enjoy and benefit from each activity. STEM toys nurture curiosity and creativity from the very start.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Is Ideal For Stem Stacking Ring Toys?



STEM stacking ring toys are ideal for infants 6+ months. They enhance motor skills and hand-eye coordination. These toys also encourage problem-solving and sensory exploration in early development stages.



### How Do Teething Toys Benefit Infants?



Teething toys soothe sore gums and reduce discomfort during teething. They promote healthy chewing habits and oral motor skills. Silicone teething toys are safe, durable, and easy to clean for infants aged 0-18 months.



### Why Choose Montessori Sensory Toys For Babies?



Montessori sensory toys support natural learning through exploration. They develop fine motor skills and cognitive abilities. These toys encourage independent play and stimulate senses with textures, colors, and sounds suitable for 0-12 months.



### What Skills Do Shape Sorter Toys Develop?



Shape sorter toys enhance problem-solving, hand-eye coordination, and spatial awareness. They teach shape recognition and fine motor skills in toddlers aged 1 year and older. These toys foster cognitive development through interactive play.



### How Can Tummy Time Toys Support Infant Development?



Tummy time toys encourage infants to strengthen neck and shoulder muscles. They improve motor skills and sensory stimulation. Soft, high-contrast toys keep babies engaged during tummy time, promoting healthy physical growth and visual development.
