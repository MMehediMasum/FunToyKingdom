Clean your cement mixer sprinkler toy after every use. This prevents buildup and keeps it running smoothly.

Store the toy indoors during winter to prevent damage. Cold weather can cause parts to crack.

* Remove batteries if applicable

Ensure all parts are connected properly. Loose connections can cause the toy to malfunction.
