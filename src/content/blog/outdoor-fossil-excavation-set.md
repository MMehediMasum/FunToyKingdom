### Preparing The Area

Clear away leaves, sticks, and stones from the digging spot. This keeps the area tidy and easy to work in.

* Remove debris from the site
* Mark the digging boundary
* Check the ground for safety

### Organizing The Tools

Arrange your excavation tools like brushes, chisels, and small shovels near your site. Keep them within reach.

Use containers or trays to hold small fossils and dirt. This stops loss and keeps the site clean.

* Place tools close to the digging area
* Use containers for small items
* Keep tools clean and ready

## Tips For A Successful Dig

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=digging-for-fossils-is-an-exciting-outdoor-activity.-it-teaches-patience,-care,-and-observation.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Digging for fossils is an exciting outdoor activity. It teaches patience, care, and observation.

To have a good dig, use the right tools and follow careful steps. This helps find and keep fossils safe.

### Techniques For Excavation

Start by gently brushing away loose dirt from the fossil area. Use small tools like brushes and picks.

Work slowly to avoid breaking the fossils. Dig around the edges first before lifting the fossil out.

* Clear loose soil with a soft brush
* Use small picks for tight spots
* Remove dirt from edges carefully
* Lift fossils gently from the ground

### Preserving Fossil Integrity

Handle fossils with care to keep them whole. Avoid touching fragile parts directly with your fingers.

Use a soft cloth or gloves when moving fossils. Keep them in a safe box with padding to stop damage.

* Do not apply strong force to fossils
* Wear gloves or use a cloth
* Store fossils in padded containers
* Keep fossils dry and away from heat

### Documenting Finds

Write down where and when you find each fossil. This information helps understand the fossil’s history.

Take photos of the fossil before and after removal. Label each find with a number or code for easy tracking.

* Note location and date of discovery
* Take clear photos from different angles
* Label fossils with unique ID numbers
* Keep a logbook or digital record
