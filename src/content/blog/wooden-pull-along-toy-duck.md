## Care And Maintenance

A wooden pull along toy duck is a delightful companion for children. To keep it in good shape, proper care and maintenance are important.

Regular cleaning and careful storage help extend the life of the toy. Here are some tips for taking care of your wooden toy duck.

### Cleaning The Wooden Surface

Dust can collect on the wooden duck, so regular cleaning is necessary. Use a soft cloth to wipe the surface gently.

Avoid using water directly on the wood. If needed, dampen the cloth slightly for stubborn dirt.

### Storing The Toy

Store the toy in a dry place to prevent damage. Keeping it away from direct sunlight will help maintain its colors.

* Store in a box or on a shelf
* Avoid humid areas
* Keep out of direct sunlight

### Inspecting For Damage

Regularly check the toy for any signs of damage. Look for cracks or loose parts that may need repair.

Fix any issues promptly to ensure the toy remains safe for children to play with.

### Polishing The Wood

Polishing can help maintain the shine of the wooden duck. Use a natural oil like olive oil to polish the surface.

Apply a small amount of oil on a cloth and rub gently. This keeps the wood looking new and fresh.
