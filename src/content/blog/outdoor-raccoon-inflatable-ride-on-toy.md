## Maintenance And Storage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-outdoor-raccoon-inflatable-ride-on-toy-clean-and-stored-properly-helps-it-last-longer.-regular-care-protects-it-from-damage-and-dirt.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Outdoor Raccoon Inflatable Ride on Toy clean and stored properly helps it last longer. Regular care protects it from damage and dirt.

Follow these simple steps to keep your inflatable in good shape and ready to use every time.

### Cleaning Guidelines

Clean the inflatable after each use to remove dirt and debris. Use mild soap and water with a soft cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals that may damage the material.

* Rinse off loose dirt with water.
* Use a sponge with mild soap to wipe surfaces.
* Dry thoroughly with a clean towel.
* Do not use bleach or abrasive cleaners.
* Check seams for any dirt or mold growth.

### Proper Deflation And Folding

Deflate the toy completely before folding to avoid wrinkles and damage. Fold it gently along natural seams to keep its shape intact.

1. Release all air from the inflatable.
2. Lay it flat on a clean surface.
3. Fold sides towards the center carefully.
4. Make compact folds without sharp creases.
5. Store the folded inflatable in a dry bag or container.

### Storage Tips To Extend Lifespan

Proper storage protects the inflatable from weather and pests. Keep it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Condition | Recommended Practice |
| Temperature | Store between 50°F and 80°F (10°C - 27°C) |
| Humidity | Keep dry to avoid mold and mildew |
| Location | Avoid direct sunlight and sharp objects |
| Pest Protection | Use sealed containers to prevent insects |
| Cleaning Before Storage | Ensure the inflatable is clean and dry |
