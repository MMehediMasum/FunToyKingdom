## Care Tips For Your Plush Doll

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-lilo-and-stitch-plush-doll-clean-and-safe-helps-it-last-longer.-proper-care-also-keeps-it-soft-and-looking-great.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Lilo and Stitch plush doll clean and safe helps it last longer. Proper care also keeps it soft and looking great. Follow simple tips to protect your favorite plush.

These care tips include cleaning methods, storage ideas, and ways to fix small damages. Each section gives easy advice for your plush doll.

### Cleaning Methods

Regular cleaning removes dust and dirt from your plush doll. Use gentle methods to avoid damage.

* Spot clean with a damp cloth and mild soap.
* Hand wash in cold water for deeper cleaning.
* Air dry completely before use or storage.
* Do not use bleach or harsh chemicals.
* Brush gently to restore softness after drying.

### Storage Suggestions

Store your plush doll in a safe place to keep it clean and prevent damage. Avoid places with direct sunlight or moisture.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Tip | Reason |
| Keep in a cool, dry area | Prevents mold and mildew |
| Use a breathable fabric bag | Allows air circulation |
| Avoid heavy stacking | Maintains shape and softness |
| Keep away from pets | Prevents chewing or tearing |

### Repairing Minor Damages

Fix small tears or loose threads quickly to stop further damage. Use basic tools and gentle care.

* Sew small holes with a needle and matching thread.
* Use fabric glue for loose parts without stitches.
* Trim any pulled or frayed fibers carefully.
* Check seams regularly for early signs of wear.
