## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Motion Activated Dog Toys?



Motion activated dog toys keep dogs engaged by stimulating their natural instincts. They reduce boredom and destructive behavior. These toys also provide physical and mental exercise, improving overall health and happiness for dogs of all sizes.



### Are These Toys Safe For Aggressive Chewers?



Many motion activated dog toys are made with durable, anti-chew materials. For aggressive chewers, options like the JR JUMIAR 360° anti-chew ball are ideal. Always check product details to ensure the toy suits your dog's chewing habits.



### How Do Rechargeable Dog Toys Work?



Rechargeable dog toys use built-in batteries charged via USB. This feature ensures continuous playtime without replacing batteries. Models like PetDroid and Huimpet offer convenient USB charging for eco-friendly and cost-effective use.



### Can Motion Activated Toys Help With Puppy Training?



Yes, these toys encourage active play and obedience through stimulation and rewards. They help reduce anxiety and improve focus. Interactive toys with squeaky sounds and movement are perfect for training puppies effectively.



### Which Toys Are Best For Small Versus Large Dogs?



Toys like Salodal Peppy Pet Ball suit small dogs with gentle motion. For large dogs, durable options such as PetDroid and JR JUMIAR provide robust play. Always select toys based on size recommendations to ensure safety and fun.



### Do Motion Activated Toys Work Well Indoors And Outdoors?



Most motion activated dog toys function well both indoors and outdoors. Features like LED lights in ALL FOR PAWS toys enhance visibility for night play. Ensure the toy is weather-resistant for outdoor use to maximize fun.
