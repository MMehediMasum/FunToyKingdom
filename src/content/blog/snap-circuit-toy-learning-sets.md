## Snap Circuit Basics

Snap Circuit toy sets teach kids about electricity and circuits. They use easy-to-connect parts that snap together. This makes learning fun and hands-on.

Each set includes components that fit on a board. Kids can build many different projects by snapping pieces in place. This helps them see how circuits work.

### Key Components

Snap Circuits come with many parts. Each part plays a special role in building circuits. Here are the main components:

* Snap wires that connect parts easily
* A battery holder to power the circuit
* Switches to open or close the circuit
* Lights and buzzers that show circuit activity
* Motors that make things move
* Resistors to control the flow of electricity

### How Snap Circuits Work

The parts snap onto a plastic grid to form a circuit. The battery sends electricity through the wires. This powers the lights, buzzers, or motors in the set.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Component | Function |
| Battery | Supplies electrical power |
| Switch | Turns circuit on or off |
| Wire | Connects parts to carry electricity |
| Light | Lights up when powered |
| Buzzer | Makes sound when powered |
| Motor | Produces movement |

### Safety Features

Snap Circuits are made with safety in mind. The pieces are large and snap firmly to avoid loose parts. The batteries are low voltage to keep children safe.

The toy sets include:

* Rounded edges on all parts
* Secure battery compartments that need a tool to open
* Non-toxic plastic material
* Clear instructions to prevent misuse
