## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Range Is Best For Infant Toy Lots?



Infant toy lots typically suit babies from newborn to 12 months. They support developmental stages like grasping and teething. Toys often target sensory, motor skills, and early learning, making them ideal for infants and toddlers under one year.



### How Do Sensory Rattles Benefit Infant Development?



Sensory rattles stimulate a baby’s hearing and touch senses. They encourage hand-eye coordination and fine motor skills. These toys also help infants explore sounds and textures, essential for cognitive and sensory development during the first year.



### Are Teething Toys Safe For Newborns?



Yes, teething toys designed for newborns are made with non-toxic materials. They soothe sore gums and help babies learn to chew safely. Always choose BPA-free, soft, and easy-to-hold teething toys recommended for infants.



### What Features Should I Look For In Infant Toy Sets?



Look for easy-to-grip shapes, bright colors, and multiple textures. Musical elements and stacking features enhance learning. Safety, durability, and age-appropriate design are key when choosing infant toy lots for newborns and toddlers.



### Can Infant Toy Lots Make Good Gifts?



Absolutely! Infant toy lots are popular gifts for baby showers and birthdays. They offer variety, developmental benefits, and long-lasting play value. Sets with storage boxes and educational toys add extra convenience and appeal.
