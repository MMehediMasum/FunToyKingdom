### Setting Up A Mini Lab At Home

Create a small science space with easy-to-find items. A dedicated area helps kids focus and feel like real scientists.

1. Choose a table or desk with good lighting.
2. Gather simple tools like beakers, magnifiers, and safety goggles.
3. Store materials in labeled containers for easy access.
4. Include a notebook for recording observations and results.
5. Keep the space clean and organized after each experiment.

### Incorporating Play Into Learning

Play helps children understand science by doing fun activities. Mixing play with learning keeps kids engaged longer.

| Activity | Scientific Skill |

| Building simple circuits | Electricity and circuits |
| Growing plants | Biology and observation |

| Exploring magnets | Magnetism and forces |

### Encouraging Questions And Exploration

Ask children what they notice and why things happen. Questions help kids think deeper and learn better.

* Listen carefully to their ideas.
* Help them find answers using books or experiments.
* Encourage trying new things and making mistakes.
* Celebrate discoveries and progress.
* Use open-ended questions like “What do you think?”
