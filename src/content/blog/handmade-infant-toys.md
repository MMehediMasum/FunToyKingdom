## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Used In Handmade Infant Toys?



Handmade infant toys often use organic, natural materials like wood, cotton yarn, and non-toxic dyes. These materials ensure safety and comfort for babies, making them ideal for sensitive skin and early development stages.



### How Do Crochet Rattles Benefit Newborn Babies?



Crochet rattles stimulate sensory development through texture and sound. They improve grip strength and hand-eye coordination, providing soothing comfort while being safe and lightweight for newborns.



### Are Wooden Teething Toys Safe For Infants?



Yes, wooden teething toys are safe if made from organic, non-toxic wood. They help soothe sore gums and promote healthy chewing habits without harmful chemicals or plastics.



### Can Handmade Toys Support Early Development?



Handmade toys encourage sensory exploration, motor skills, and cognitive growth. Their unique textures and shapes engage babies, fostering curiosity and early learning in a natural way.



### How To Choose The Best Handmade Toy For Babies?



Select toys made from organic, hypoallergenic materials with safe, non-toxic finishes. Look for age-appropriate designs that support sensory and motor skills development for newborns and infants.
