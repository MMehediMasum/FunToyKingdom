## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Interactive Dog Toys?



Interactive dog toys boost mental stimulation, reduce boredom, and improve physical activity. They help prevent destructive behavior and keep dogs engaged for longer periods. These toys also strengthen the bond between pets and owners through play.



### Which Interactive Toy Suits Aggressive Chewers Best?



Carllg Dog Chew Toys are ideal for aggressive chewers. Made from indestructible, tough materials, they withstand strong bites. These toys also include squeaky features to keep dogs entertained and mentally stimulated, making them perfect for heavy chewers.



### How Do Motion-activated Dog Toys Work?



Motion-activated dog toys like QGI and PetDroid respond to your dog’s movements. They roll, jump, or make sounds to attract attention. This dynamic play encourages dogs to chase and interact, effectively reducing boredom and promoting exercise.



### Are Rechargeable Dog Toys Safe And Convenient?



Yes, rechargeable dog toys like Lollyx and JR JUMIAR are safe and eco-friendly. They offer continuous play without frequent battery changes, saving money. USB charging makes them easy to power up, ensuring your dog stays entertained longer.



### Can Interactive Dog Toys Help With Training?



Yes, dog puzzle toys and treat-dispensing toys improve problem-solving skills. They challenge dogs mentally and reward them for effort. These toys support IQ training, helping puppies and adult dogs develop focus and cognitive abilities.



### Which Toys Are Best For Small To Large Dog Breeds?



Toys like QGI, PetDroid, and BABORUI cater to all sizes. They come in adjustable sizes and durable materials suitable for small, medium, and large dogs. Always choose based on your dog’s size and chewing strength for safety.
