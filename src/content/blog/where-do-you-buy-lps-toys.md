## Tips For Safe Purchases

### Checking Seller Ratings

Check the seller’s ratings before buying. High ratings mean many happy buyers. Read reviews to see what others say. Avoid sellers with low ratings or bad feedback. Reliable sellers give better service and real products.

### Avoiding Scams

Beware of prices that seem too low. Scammers use cheap prices to trick buyers. Only [buy from trusted sites or stores](https://funtoykingdom.com/where-to-buy-a-fidget-toy/). Look for secure website signs like "https" in the address. Never share personal or payment information with suspicious sellers.

### Return Policies

Always check the return policy before purchase. Good stores offer easy returns if the toy is damaged or wrong. Know the time limit for returns. Keep receipts and packaging until sure about the toy. Clear return rules protect your money and satisfaction.
