## Origins Of Cabbage Patch Kids

Cabbage Patch Kids are famous dolls loved by many children. They have a unique story about how they began.

The dolls started as a small art project and grew into a popular toy around the world.

### Creation And Design

The dolls were created by artist Xavier Roberts in 1978. He wanted to make soft, unique dolls that looked like real babies.

* Each doll had a soft cloth body and a vinyl head.
* They had round faces with dimples and big eyes.
* Every doll came with a unique name and adoption papers.
* The design made kids feel like they were adopting a real baby.

### Rise To Popularity

The dolls became very popular in the early 1980s. Many parents wanted to buy them for their children.
