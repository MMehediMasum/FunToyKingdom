### Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the toy does not start, check the battery connection and charger first. Sometimes loose wires cause problems.

**Common Problems and Solutions:**

* **No power:**Check battery charge and connection.
* **Slow speed:**Clean wheels and check for dirt.
* **Remote control not working:**Replace remote batteries and check pairing.
* **Unusual noises:**Inspect motor and gears for damage.
