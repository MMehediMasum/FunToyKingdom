## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Sensory Toys For 12-month-old Infants?



Sensory toys like Montessori balls, stacking blocks, and crinkle stuffed animals stimulate touch and sight. They help develop motor skills and cognitive growth for 12-month-olds effectively.



### How Do Teething Toys Help Babies Aged 6-12 Months?



Teething toys soothe sore gums and encourage healthy chewing habits. Silicone pull-string teethers and soft textured toys provide relief and promote oral development safely.



### Which Musical Toys Are Ideal For Infants 6 To 12 Months?



Musical toys like light-up pianos and rain sticks engage auditory senses and motor skills. These toys promote early learning through sound and interactive play.



### What Montessori Toys Support Infant Learning At 12 Months?



Montessori toys such as stacking rings, sensory balls, and shape sorters foster problem-solving and hand-eye coordination. They encourage independent exploration and focus in babies.



### Are There Suitable Travel Toys For Toddlers Around 12 Months?



Yes, compact teething toys and interactive sensory toys are perfect for travel. They keep infants entertained while supporting developmental milestones on the go.



### What Makes Baby Piano Toys Good For Early Learning?



Baby piano toys with lights and sounds enhance auditory and visual stimulation. They develop fine motor skills and introduce cause-and-effect concepts to infants.
