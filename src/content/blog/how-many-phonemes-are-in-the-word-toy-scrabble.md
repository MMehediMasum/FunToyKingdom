### Breaking Down 'toy'

The word "toy" has three letters but not all letters make separate sounds. We look at the sounds made when we say the word out loud.

"Toy" has a start sound, a middle sound, and an ending sound. These sounds make the word easy to say.

### Individual Sounds

The sounds in "toy" are made up of three parts. Each part is a phoneme. They are:

* The "t" sound at the start
* The "oy" sound in the middle and end

The "oy" sound is a diphthong, which means it has two vowel sounds combined.

### Phoneme Count

The word "toy" has two phonemes. These are the smallest sounds you can hear when saying the word.

* /t/ - the initial consonant sound
* /ɔɪ/ - the diphthong vowel sound
