Use a pump to inflate the ostrich pool toy quickly and evenly. Avoid overinflating to prevent damage.

* Inspect the toy for holes before inflating

### Placement And Surface

Choose a flat, soft surface to place your ostrich inflatable pool toy. Grass or a smooth lawn is ideal.

Avoid sharp objects like rocks or sticks that can puncture the toy. Clear the area before setting up.

* Pick a shady spot to keep the toy cool
* Check the ground for debris or sharp items
* Keep the toy away from rough surfaces
* Place near the pool for easy access

### Maintenance During Use

Watch the inflatable toy while in use to prevent accidents. Keep children supervised at all times.

Wipe off water and dirt regularly. This helps keep the toy clean and extends its life.

* Check air pressure daily and re-inflate if needed
* Clean with mild soap and water after use

* Fix small holes promptly with repair kits

## Caring For Your Inflatable

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-good-care-of-your-outdoor-ostrich-inflatable-pool-toy-helps-it-last-longer.-proper-cleaning,-storage,-and-repair-keep-it-safe-and-fun-to-use.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking good care of your outdoor ostrich inflatable pool toy helps it last longer. Proper cleaning, storage, and repair keep it safe and fun to use.

Follow simple steps to keep your inflatable in top shape. This guide covers how to clean, store, and fix small damages.

### Cleaning Techniques

Clean your inflatable after each use to remove dirt and debris. Use mild soap and water to keep the material safe.

* Mix warm water with gentle soap in a bucket
* Use a soft cloth or sponge to wipe the surface
* Rinse well with clean water to remove soap
* Let it dry fully before storing to prevent mold

### Storage Tips

Store your inflatable in a cool, dry place away from sharp objects. Proper storage prevents damage and extends its life.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Advice | Reason |
| Keep out of direct sunlight | Prevents material from fading and weakening |
| Store in a sealed bag | Keeps dust and insects away |
| Fold gently without sharp creases | Avoids cracks and tears |
| Check for moisture before storing | Stops mold and mildew growth |

### Repairing Minor Damages

Fix small holes and tears quickly to stop leaks. Use a patch kit made for inflatables for the best results.

Follow these repair tips:

1. Clean the damaged area and dry it well.
2. Cut a patch slightly larger than the hole.
3. Apply adhesive to the patch and place it on the damage.
4. Press firmly and let it dry as directed.
5. Test the repair by inflating the toy slowly.
