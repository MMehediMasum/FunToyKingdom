Stuffed rattles use soft fabrics that feel gentle on baby skin. The fabrics must not cause allergies or irritation.

## Choosing The Perfect Rattle

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=stuffed-rattles-with-animal-shapes-make-great-toys-for-babies.-they-help-with-sensory-development-and-hand-eye-coordination.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Stuffed rattles with animal shapes make great toys for babies. They help with sensory development and hand-eye coordination.

Picking the right rattle is important to keep your baby safe and happy. Consider the baby’s age, the rattle’s size, and how easy it is to clean.

### Age Appropriateness

Choose a rattle that suits the baby’s age and skills. Some rattles are better for newborns, while others fit older babies.

Look for rattles with no small parts that could be a choking hazard. Soft materials are safer for young babies.

* Newborns need soft rattles with gentle sounds
* Babies 3-6 months enjoy rattles that are easy to grasp
* Older babies like rattles with varied textures and louder sounds

### Size And Weight

Pick a rattle that is light enough for a baby to hold comfortably. A heavy rattle can tire their hands quickly.

The size should fit small hands but not be so small it becomes a choking risk. Medium size rattles work well for most babies.

* Lightweight rattles support longer playtime
* Medium size rattles fit well in baby’s hands
* Avoid rattles with sharp edges or hard parts

### Washability

Babies put rattles in their mouths, so easy cleaning is a must. Stuffed rattles should be washable to keep them clean and safe.

Check the label for washing instructions. Machine washable rattles save time and keep germs away.

* Choose rattles with removable parts for easy cleaning
* Machine washable rattles dry quickly and thoroughly
* Spot clean rattles that cannot be machine washed
