## Caring For Your Wooden Toy

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=wooden-toys-need-special-care-to-last-a-long-time.-proper-care-keeps-the-toy-safe-and-beautiful.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Wooden toys need special care to last a long time. Proper care keeps the toy safe and beautiful.

This guide helps you clean and store your wooden toy fairy house correctly.

### Cleaning Tips

Use a soft, dry cloth to remove dust from the wooden toy. Avoid using water or harsh cleaners.

If needed, lightly dampen a cloth with water and gently wipe the surface. Dry it right away with a dry cloth.

* Do not soak the toy in water.
* Never use strong chemicals or bleach.
* Keep the toy away from direct sunlight while cleaning.

### Storage And Maintenance

Store the wooden toy in a dry, cool place. Avoid areas with high humidity or direct sunlight.

Check the toy regularly for loose parts or damage. Fix small issues early to prevent bigger problems.

* Use a soft cloth to dust the toy weekly.
* Keep the toy away from heat sources like radiators.
* Place the toy in a protective box or cover when not in use.
