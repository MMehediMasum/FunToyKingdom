Handling small Lego pieces trains finger strength and precision. This improves hand-eye coordination and control.

* Clicking pieces together
* Sorting different shapes and sizes
* Following detailed building instructions

Building Lego models helps tweens understand space and shapes. They learn how parts fit in three dimensions.
