### Inflation Tips

Use a pump to inflate the seahorse quickly. Avoid over-inflating to prevent damage.

Check the air valves for leaks before starting. Inflate until the toy feels firm but soft.

* Use a manual or electric pump
* Do not blow air by mouth
* Check for leaks at valves
* Stop inflating when firm but not hard

### Placement In Pool

Place the inflatable in a calm area of the pool. Avoid spots with strong currents or waves.

Keep the seahorse away from sharp edges and rough surfaces to prevent punctures.

* Choose shallow water for safety
* Keep away from pool drains
* Do not place near pool steps
* Check the area for sharp objects

### Maintenance And Care

After use, rinse the inflatable with fresh water to remove pool chemicals. Let it dry completely before storing.

Store the seahorse in a cool, dry place. Avoid direct sunlight to keep the colors bright.
