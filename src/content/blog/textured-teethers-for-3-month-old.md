## Choosing Safe Teethers

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-teether-for-a-3-month-old-baby-is-important-for-their-safety-and-comfort.-textured-teethers-can-soothe-sore-gums-and-help-with-early-development.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right teether for a 3-month-old baby is important for their safety and comfort. Textured teethers can soothe sore gums and help with early development.

Parents should focus on safety features before buying any teether. The right teether protects babies from choking and harmful chemicals.

### Material Safety

Teethers come in many materials like silicone, rubber, and wood. Each material has its own safety benefits and risks.

Soft silicone is gentle on babies’ gums and easy to clean. Natural wood is durable but must be smooth and untreated.

* Choose materials that do not break easily
* Check for smooth surfaces without splinters
* Pick materials safe for chewing and sucking

### Size And Shape Considerations

The size of the teether should prevent choking hazards. It should be large enough that a baby cannot swallow it.

Shape matters for easy gripping and gum relief. Textured surfaces help soothe gums and keep babies interested.

* Avoid small parts that can detach
* Look for teethers with handles for easy hold
* Check for rounded edges to prevent injury

### Non-toxic And Bpa-free Options

Non-toxic teethers keep babies safe from harmful chemicals. BPA-free products do not release dangerous chemicals when chewed.

Check labels to confirm teethers are free from toxins like phthalates and lead. This protects babies’ health during use.

* Choose BPA-free silicone or plastic
* Look for certifications from safety organizations
* Avoid teethers with artificial dyes or scents

## How To Use Textured Teethers

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=textured-teethers-help-soothe-your-3-month-old-baby’s-gums.-they-provide-safe-chewing-surfaces-to-ease-discomfort.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Textured teethers help soothe your 3-month-old baby’s gums. They provide safe chewing surfaces to ease discomfort.

Using textured teethers correctly makes teething easier for your baby. Learn how to introduce and care for them well.

### Introducing Teethers To Your Baby

Offer the textured teether when your baby shows signs of teething. Signs include drooling and fussiness.

Let your baby hold the teether and explore it at their own pace. Gently guide it to their mouth for chewing.

* Choose a soft, safe teether suitable for 3-month-olds
* Start with short chewing sessions
* Use the teether during calm, quiet times
* Watch your baby’s interest and comfort level

### Cleaning And Maintenance Tips

Keep textured teethers clean to protect your baby from germs. Clean them before and after use.

Use warm water and mild soap to wash the teether. Rinse it well to remove all soap.

* Wash teethers daily or after each use
* Check for cracks or damage before giving to baby
* Do not use harsh chemicals or boiling water
* Air dry completely before storing

### Monitoring Baby’s Reactions

Watch how your baby reacts when using the textured teether. Look for signs of comfort or discomfort.

Stop using the teether if your baby shows irritation or allergic reactions. Consult a doctor if needed.

* Notice if baby chews happily or fusses
* Check for redness or swelling in the mouth
* Observe if baby drops the teether often
* Keep teething sessions short to avoid overstimulation

## Signs Your Baby Needs A Teether

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=babies-start-teething-around-three-months-old.-this-stage-can-be-uncomfortable-for-them.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Babies start teething around three months old. This stage can be uncomfortable for them.

Using a teether helps soothe your baby’s gums. Watch for signs that your baby needs one.

### Common Teething Symptoms

Teething causes several signs you can notice easily. Your baby may have sore gums and drool a lot.

* Increased drooling
* Chewing on objects
* Swollen or tender gums
* Fussiness and crying more than usual
* Difficulty sleeping

### Behavioral Changes

Teething can change your baby’s mood and behavior. They may seem irritable and want more attention.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Behavior | Description |
| Increased fussiness | Baby cries more and is harder to soothe |
| Loss of appetite | Baby may refuse to eat or drink as usual |
| Sleep disruptions | Baby wakes often during the night |
| Chewing urge | Baby bites on fingers, toys, or clothes |

### Timing For Use

Most babies start teething around three to four months old. Signs may appear before teeth show.

1. Watch for early signs like drooling and gum rubbing
2. Offer a teether when your baby shows discomfort
3. Use teethers made for young babies with soft textures
4. Keep the teether clean and safe for chewing
5. Replace the teether if it breaks or wears out
