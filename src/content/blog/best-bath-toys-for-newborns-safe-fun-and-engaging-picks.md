No-hole bath toys prevent water from entering, stopping mold and bacteria growth. This keeps toys cleaner and safer for babies. Products like Tub Works® Marine™ and Hely Cancy Baby Bath Toys use this design to ensure healthier bath time fun.

Bath toys like Munchkin® Learn™ Bath Letters and Numbers promote fine motor skills and early learning. Squeezable toys like Munchkin Baby Bath Squirts encourage hand coordination. These toys engage babies while enhancing sensory and motor development.



### How To Choose Bath Toys For Newborns With Sensitive Skin?

Munchkin® Float & Play Bubbles™ are lightweight, colorful, and float on water. Their soft texture and easy-to-grip design make them perfect for newborns. They stimulate visual and tactile senses while ensuring safe, fun bath experiences.
