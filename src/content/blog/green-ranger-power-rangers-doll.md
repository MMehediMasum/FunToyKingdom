Good material makes the doll last longer. Look for strong plastic that does not break easily. The surface should feel smooth and sturdy.

* Non-toxic materials safe for children

### Articulation And Poseability

The doll should move well to create many poses. Check the joints for smooth movement without being too loose or tight.

Good articulation lets you recreate action scenes or display the doll in cool stances. Look for movable shoulders, elbows, knees, and head.

* Multiple joint points for flexibility
* Sturdy joints that hold poses
* Easy to move without damage risk
* Balance support for standing poses

### Authentic Detailing

The doll should look like the Green Ranger from the show. Check the helmet, armor, and suit colors closely.

Small details like the dragon emblem and chest armor add to the authenticity. Accurate shapes and textures make the doll special.

* True-to-show helmet design
