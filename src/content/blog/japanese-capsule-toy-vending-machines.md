## History Of Capsule Toy Machines

Capsule toy vending machines are small machines that give toys inside plastic capsules. These machines are very popular in Japan. They have a long and interesting history.

This article explains how capsule toy machines started, changed over time, and spread worldwide.

### Origins In Japan

The first capsule toy machines appeared in Japan in the 1960s. A company named Bandai helped start this trend. They made small toys and put them in plastic capsules.

People liked the surprise of getting a toy from a machine. The machines were placed in shopping malls and train stations. This made them easy to find and use.

### Evolution Over Decades

Capsule toy machines changed a lot over the years. The toys became more detailed and creative. Themes from cartoons, movies, and games became common.

New machines added features like lights and sounds. Some machines let you choose the toy before buying. The variety of toys also grew with many categories.

* 1960s: Basic capsule toys with simple designs
* 1980s: Popular cartoon and anime characters
* 2000s: More interactive machines and complex toys
* Today: High-quality toys with many themes

### Global Spread

Capsule toy machines became popular outside Japan too. Many countries started using them in malls and stores. People enjoyed the surprise and low cost of the toys.

Today, you can find capsule toy machines in places like the United States, Europe, and Asia. They often contain local and Japanese-themed toys to attract collectors.

* Asia: Common in shopping centers and arcades
* North America: Found in malls and comic stores
* Europe: Growing popularity in urban areas
* Online: Capsule toy sets sold through websites




## How Capsule Machines Work

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=japanese-capsule-toy-vending-machines-are-fun-devices-that-give-small-toys-inside-plastic-capsules.-you-put-a-coin-in,-turn-a-knob,-and-get-a-surprise-toy.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Japanese capsule toy vending machines are fun devices that give small toys inside plastic capsules. You put a coin in, turn a knob, and get a surprise toy.

These machines use a simple system to deliver a capsule toy. People of all ages enjoy them for their surprise and collectability.

### Mechanism Basics

The machine has a coin slot and a turning knob. Insert a coin to unlock the knob. Turn the knob to move the capsules inside.

Inside, capsules roll down a spiral or slide until one drops into the opening. The capsule falls out for you to take.

* Coin slot checks for the right coin size
* Knob turns gears inside the machine
* Capsules move along a path to the drop point
* One capsule is released per turn

### Types Of Capsules

Capsules come in many sizes and shapes. Most are round plastic balls that split in half. Some are egg-shaped or square.

Size depends on the toy inside. Small toys use tiny capsules. Larger toys need bigger capsules. Capsules protect the toy inside.

* Small capsules fit keychains or tiny figures
* Medium capsules hold small action figures
* Large capsules contain bigger toys or sets
* Some capsules have clear shells to see the toy

### Common Themes And Designs

Capsule toys often follow popular themes. Many relate to anime, animals, or everyday objects. Themes attract collectors.

Designs may be cute, funny, or realistic. Some capsules have parts that move or glow. Themes change often to keep interest.

* Anime characters and robots

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Are Japanese Capsule Toy Vending Machines?

Japanese capsule toy vending machines, also called Gashapon or Gachapon, dispense collectible toys in plastic capsules. They offer unique, themed mini-figures and items. These machines are popular in Japan and worldwide for their surprise element and affordable pricing.

### How Do Japanese Capsule Toy Machines Work?

Users insert coins into the machine, then turn a crank or press a button. A capsule containing a surprise toy is dispensed. Each capsule holds a random item from a specific series or collection, making it fun to collect.

You can find these machines in shopping malls, train stations, arcades, and tourist spots. Popular areas include Akihabara and Shibuya in Tokyo. Many stores specialize in capsule toys, offering a wide variety of themes and characters.
