## Care And Maintenance

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-your-what-do-you-meme-toys-helps-them-last-longer-and-stay-fun.-proper-care-keeps-the-colors-bright-and-the-parts-working-well.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of your What Do You Meme toys helps them last longer and stay fun. Proper care keeps the colors bright and the parts working well. Simple maintenance protects your investment and keeps the game ready for play.

### Cleaning Tips

Use a soft cloth to wipe down the cards and pieces. Avoid water on cards to stop damage. For plastic parts, a damp cloth works well. Dry everything completely before storing. Avoid harsh chemicals that can harm the materials.

### Storage Solutions

Keep your toys in a cool, dry place. Store cards in their box or a small container. Use zip-lock bags for loose pieces to prevent loss. Avoid direct sunlight to stop fading. Organize everything in one spot to find it easily next time.
