## Early Beginnings

The Barbie doll is a famous toy that many children love. It has a long history that began in the 1950s. This post looks at how Barbie started and what inspired her creation.

Barbie changed the way dolls were made. She was different from the baby dolls that were popular before. Barbie showed a grown-up woman’s life through play.

### Inspiration Behind Barbie

Ruth Handler, the creator of Barbie, noticed her daughter liked to play with paper dolls of adults. She saw a need for a doll that looked like a grown woman. This idea helped children imagine their future.

* Ruth Handler watched her daughter play with adult paper dolls.
* She wanted to make a 3D doll for girls to play with.
* The doll would show different adult roles and careers.
* Barbie was named after Ruth’s daughter, Barbara.

### Launch And Initial Reception

Barbie first appeared at the New York Toy Fair in 1959. At first, people were curious about this new doll. Kids quickly liked Barbie because she was different from other dolls.
