## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Robotic Toys For Dogs?



Robotic dog toys provide mental stimulation and physical exercise. They reduce boredom and destructive behavior. These toys keep dogs engaged and entertained for longer periods.



### How Do Motion-activated Dog Toys Work?



Motion-activated toys detect your dog's movement or touch. They respond by rolling, vibrating, or making sounds. This interaction encourages dogs to play actively and stay entertained.



### Are Robotic Dog Toys Safe For All Dog Sizes?



Yes, many robotic toys are designed for small, medium, and large dogs. Check the product specifications for size and durability. Always supervise your dog to ensure safe play.



### Can Robotic Toys Help With Dog Anxiety?



Robotic toys can reduce anxiety by providing distraction and engagement. They keep dogs busy and calm during stressful moments. These toys are useful for separation anxiety and boredom.



### How Do Rechargeable Dog Toys Improve Convenience?



Rechargeable toys eliminate the need for frequent battery replacements. They offer longer playtime and are environmentally friendly. USB charging makes recharging easy and quick.



### What Features Should I Look For In Interactive Dog Toys?



Look for durability, motion activation, sound effects, and safety. Toys with obstacle avoidance and waterproof rating are ideal. Choose toys suitable for your dog's size and play style.
