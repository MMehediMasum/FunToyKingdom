## Maintaining And Caring For Metal Detectors

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=outdoor-metal-detector-toys-for-kids-need-regular-care.-proper-maintenance-keeps-them-working-well.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Outdoor metal detector toys for kids need regular care. Proper maintenance keeps them working well.

Simple steps can protect the metal detector from damage. This helps your child enjoy it longer.

### Cleaning Tips

Clean the metal detector after each use to remove dirt and dust. Use a soft, dry cloth to wipe it.

Do not use water or harsh chemicals. These can damage the electronics inside the detector.

* Wipe the coil gently
* Clean the control box with a dry cloth
* Remove dirt from buttons carefully

### Battery Management

Check batteries before and after each play session. Replace weak batteries to keep the toy working well.

Remove batteries if the detector will not be used for a long time. This stops battery leaks and damage.

* Use fresh batteries recommended by the manufacturer
* Do not mix old and new batteries
* Store batteries in a cool, dry place

### Storage Recommendations

Store the metal detector in a dry and safe place. Avoid spots with high heat or moisture.

Keep the detector away from heavy items that can press or break it. Use a carrying case if available.

* Keep the coil off the ground
* Store in a cool, dry area
* Cover the detector to protect from dust
