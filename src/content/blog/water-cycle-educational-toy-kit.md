## Water Cycle Basics

The water cycle moves water around the Earth. It helps water change from liquid to gas and back.

This cycle keeps water moving through the air, land, and oceans. It supports life everywhere.

### Stages Of The Water Cycle

The water cycle has four main stages. Each stage changes water in a different way.

* **Evaporation:**Water from lakes, rivers, and oceans turns into vapor.
* **Condensation:**Water vapor cools and forms clouds.
* **Precipitation:**Water falls from clouds as rain, snow, or hail.
* **Collection:**Water gathers in bodies like rivers, lakes, and oceans.

These stages repeat over and over, moving water through nature.

### Importance Of Water Cycle

The water cycle is important for plants, animals, and people. It gives fresh water to drink.

This cycle also controls weather and keeps the Earth cool. It helps grow crops and plants.

* Supplies fresh water to rivers and lakes
* Keeps air clean by moving water vapor
* Supports plants and animals
* Regulates weather and climate
