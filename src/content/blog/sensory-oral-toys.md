## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Sensory Oral Toys For Autistic Children?



Sensory oral toys help autistic children with chewing and oral motor skills. They reduce anxiety and improve focus. These toys are made from safe, non-toxic silicone and provide calming sensory input for kids with autism, ADHD, and SPD.



### How Do Chew Necklaces Benefit Kids With Adhd?



Chew necklaces offer a discreet way for kids with ADHD to satisfy oral sensory needs. They help reduce fidgeting and biting habits. Made from durable silicone, these necklaces promote calmness and improve concentration during daily activities.



### Can Sensory Chew Toys Help Reduce Anxiety In Adults?



Yes, sensory chew toys can reduce anxiety in adults by providing a soothing oral outlet. They help manage stress and nervous habits like nail-biting. These chewies are portable, safe, and designed for both sensory needs and fidget control.



### Are Sensory Chew Toys Safe For Toddlers And Babies?



Sensory chew toys for toddlers and babies are made from BPA-free, non-toxic silicone. They support oral motor development and soothe teething pain. Always supervise young children during use to ensure safety and hygiene.



### How Do Chewies Assist Children With Spd?



Chewies provide deep pressure and oral sensory input, which helps children with Sensory Processing Disorder (SPD) self-regulate. They improve focus and reduce sensory overload. These tools are soft, durable, and designed to meet special sensory needs.



### What Features To Look For In Sensory Chew Toys?



Look for chew toys made from food-grade silicone, durable and easy to clean. Choose textures that offer varied sensory feedback. Size and shape should be safe and comfortable for the user’s age and chewing strength.
