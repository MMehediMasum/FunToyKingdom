## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Puzzles For One Year Olds?



The best puzzles for one year olds include chunky wooden peg puzzles and simple shape sorters. They improve motor skills and hand-eye coordination while being safe and engaging.



### Are Wooden Peg Puzzles Suitable For Toddlers 18+ Months?



Yes, wooden peg puzzles with large pieces are ideal for toddlers 18+ months. They promote fine motor skills and problem-solving without choking hazards.



### How Do Personalized Name Puzzles Benefit Toddlers?



Personalized name puzzles help toddlers recognize letters and develop early literacy. They boost confidence and make learning fun with custom animal or floral designs.



### Which Puzzles Encourage Pretend Play In Toddlers?



Pretend play puzzles like Toyster’s Wooden Cutting Fruit Puzzle engage toddlers in imaginative role-playing. They enhance creativity and fine motor skills simultaneously.



### Why Choose Montessori-style Puzzles For One Year Olds?



Montessori puzzles like facial recognition peekaboo or magnetic tangrams support sensory development and logical thinking. They encourage independent learning and cognitive growth in toddlers.



### What Makes Chunky Vegetable Puzzles Ideal For Babies?



Chunky vegetable puzzles offer easy grasping and bright colors that attract babies. They teach shapes, colors, and healthy food concepts in an interactive way.
