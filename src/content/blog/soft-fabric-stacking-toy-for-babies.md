## How To Use Stacking Toys Safely

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=stacking-toys-made-of-soft-fabric-are-great-for-babies.-they-help-develop-skills-like-hand-eye-coordination-and-problem-solving.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Stacking toys made of soft fabric are great for babies. They help develop skills like hand-eye coordination and problem-solving. Using them safely is very important to keep babies happy and healthy.

This guide shares easy tips on how to supervise play, clean the toys, and avoid risks. Follow these to make stacking toy time safe and fun.

### Supervision Tips

Always watch your baby while they play with stacking toys. Soft fabric toys are gentle, but babies can still put pieces in their mouths. Stay close to prevent choking or other accidents.

* Keep toys within your baby's reach but stay nearby.
* Remove any broken or loose parts right away.
* Limit playtime to short sessions to avoid fatigue.
* Teach your baby to stack gently to avoid falls or injuries.

### Cleaning And Maintenance

Clean stacking toys often to stop germs from spreading. Soft fabric can get dirty easily. Use safe cleaning methods to keep toys fresh and soft.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Cleaning Step | Method |
| Surface wipe | Use a damp cloth with mild soap |
| Machine wash | Wash on gentle cycle with cold water |
| Drying | Air dry completely before use |
| Check for damage | Look for tears or loose parts before giving to baby |

### Avoiding Common Hazards

Stacking toys can be safe if you avoid common dangers. Small parts or damaged toys might hurt babies. Keep the play area safe and check toys often.

**Hazards to watch for:**

* Loose stuffing that can be swallowed
* Broken seams exposing small pieces
* Toys with buttons or beads not sewn tightly
* Stacking too high causing toys to fall
