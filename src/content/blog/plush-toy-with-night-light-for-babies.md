## Choosing Safe Materials

### Non-toxic Fabric And Fillings

Materials used in the plush toy should be free from harmful chemicals. Non-toxic fabric and fillings reduce the risk of allergies or poisoning. Natural fibers like organic cotton are good choices.

Hypoallergenic toys reduce the risk of allergic reactions. Babies with sensitive skin need soft and gentle materials. Look for hypoallergenic certifications on the toy’s label.

Durable and washable toys last longer and stay clean. Babies often drool or spill on their toys. Easy washing helps remove dirt and germs safely.

* Choose fabrics that hold up in the wash.

## Safety Tips For Parents

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=plush-toys-with-night-lights-bring-comfort-to-babies.-parents-must-keep-safety-in-mind-when-using-them.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Plush toys with night lights bring comfort to babies. Parents must keep safety in mind when using them.

Following simple safety tips helps prevent accidents and keeps your baby safe at night.

### Avoiding Small Parts

Check the plush toy for small parts that can come loose. These parts can cause choking hazards for babies.

* Choose toys with securely stitched eyes and noses.
* Avoid toys with buttons or beads that can detach.
* Remove any small accessories from the toy before giving it to your baby.
* Pick toys labeled safe for infants under 3 years old.

### Regular Inspection And Maintenance

Check the toy regularly for wear and tear. Broken parts can be dangerous for babies.

| Inspection Area | What to Check | Action If Damaged |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fabric | Look for holes or loose threads | Repair or discard the toy |
| Light Unit | Ensure it is firmly attached and working | Replace batteries or fix securely |
| Seams | Check if seams are intact and strong | Sew or throw away the toy |

### Safe Battery Placement

Battery compartments must be secure and out of baby's reach. Loose batteries can cause harm.

* Use toys with battery compartments that require a screwdriver to open.
* Keep spare batteries stored safely away from children.
* Replace batteries regularly to avoid leaks.
* Never let your baby handle batteries directly.

## Care And Maintenance

Plush toys with night lights provide comfort and security to babies. Proper care ensures they last longer and remain safe for use.

Follow these guidelines to maintain your plush toy in top condition. This guide covers cleaning, battery replacement, and storage tips.

### Cleaning Instructions

Regular cleaning keeps your plush toy fresh and hygienic. Use gentle methods to avoid damage.

* Remove the battery before cleaning.
* Hand wash the plush surface with mild soap and water.
* Air dry completely before using again.

### Battery Replacement

Replacing the battery ensures the night light functions properly. Follow these steps carefully.

1. Open the battery compartment with a screwdriver.
2. Remove the old battery and dispose of it safely.
3. Insert a new battery, matching the plus and minus signs.
4. Close the compartment and secure it tightly.
