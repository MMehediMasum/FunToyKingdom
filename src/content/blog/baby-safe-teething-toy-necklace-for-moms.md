## How To Use Teething Necklaces Safely

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=teething-necklaces-can-help-soothe-babies-during-teething.-moms-should-use-them-carefully-to-keep-babies-safe.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Teething necklaces can help soothe babies during teething. Moms should use them carefully to keep babies safe.

Knowing how to use these necklaces safely is very important. Follow simple steps to protect your baby.

### Supervision Tips

Always watch your baby when they wear a teething necklace. Never leave your baby alone with it on.

Keep an eye on the necklace to make sure it stays safe and intact. Stop using it if you see any damage.

* Stay close while baby chews on the necklace
* Remove necklace during naps and sleep
* Check for broken beads or loose parts

### Proper Wearing Practices

Wear the necklace comfortably, not too tight or too loose. It should sit where baby can reach it easily.

Do not let the necklace hang low to avoid choking or strangulation risks. Remove it during active play.

* Fit necklace snugly around your neck
* Keep necklace length short and safe
* Remove before physical activities or sleeping

### Cleaning And Maintenance

Clean the teething necklace regularly to keep it safe and hygienic. Use mild soap and water only.

Check the necklace often for wear and tear. Replace it if you find any damage or loose beads.

* Wash necklace gently with warm water and soap
* Air dry completely before use
* Inspect for cracks or loose beads every time
