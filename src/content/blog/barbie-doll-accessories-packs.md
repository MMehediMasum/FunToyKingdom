## Caring For Barbie Accessories

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=barbie-accessories-make-playtime-more-fun-and-creative.-proper-care-keeps-them-looking-new-for-a-long-time.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Barbie accessories make playtime more fun and creative. Proper care keeps them looking new for a long time.

This guide will show you how to store, clean, and maintain the quality of Barbie accessories.

### Storage Solutions

Proper storage prevents Barbie accessories from getting lost or damaged. Use containers to keep them organized.

* Use small boxes for tiny items like shoes and jewelry.
* Label each container to easily find accessories.
* Store larger items in a dedicated toy bin or shelf.

### Cleaning Tips

Keeping Barbie accessories clean makes them last longer. Regular cleaning is simple and quick.

* Use a soft cloth to dust accessories.
* Wash plastic items with mild soap and water.
* Dry thoroughly to prevent mold or damage.

### Maintaining Quality

Maintaining the quality of Barbie accessories ensures they remain in good condition. Handle them gently during playtime.

* Avoid exposing them to direct sunlight for long periods.
* Check for loose parts and repair them promptly.
* Rotate use to prevent wear and tear on favorite items.
