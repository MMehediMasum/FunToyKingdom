Using the Outdoor Octopus Inflatable Kids Toy is fun and exciting. Safety should always come first to avoid accidents. Follow these tips to keep children safe while playing outside.

Proper care and supervision help make playtime enjoyable and secure. Read each section carefully to learn how to create a safe environment.

### Supervision Guidelines

Children need constant supervision while using the inflatable toy. Adults should watch closely to prevent rough play and injuries. Never leave kids unattended near the toy.

* Keep an adult nearby at all times
* Limit the number of children playing together
* Stop play if weather changes suddenly
* Remove shoes and sharp objects before play

### Proper Inflation And Maintenance

Check the inflatable toy before use. Proper inflation keeps it stable and safe for kids. Regular maintenance prevents damage and accidents.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Task | Frequency |
| Check air pressure | Before each use |
| Inspect seams and fabric | Weekly |
| Clean surface | After play sessions |
| Store in dry place | After each use |

### Safe Play Environment

Set up the inflatable toy on flat, soft ground. Avoid hard surfaces like concrete. Clear the area from objects that could cause harm.

**Safety Checklist:**

* Flat and even surface
* No sharp objects nearby
* Clear from overhead wires or branches
* Good weather conditions
* Easy access to first aid kit
