## Maintaining Your Race Set

Keeping your egg and spoon race set in good shape helps it last longer. Regular care makes sure it stays safe and fun to use.

Simple steps for cleaning and storing will protect your race set. You can also fix common problems quickly with easy tips.

### Cleaning And Storage

Clean your race set after each use. Dirt and food stains can damage the materials.

* Wipe spoons and eggs with a damp cloth and mild soap.
* Rinse well with clean water to remove soap.
* Dry all parts completely before storing to stop mold.
* Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
* Keep all pieces together in a box or bag to avoid loss.
