### Non-toxic Dyes

Non-toxic dyes color the felt safely. They do not harm babies if touched or mouthed.

### Organic Stuffing Options

Organic stuffing is filling made from natural materials. It is soft and safe for babies.

Common organic stuffing includes cotton, wool, and kapok. These materials are breathable and free from harmful chemicals.

* Cotton: soft and natural
* Wool: warm and fluffy

## Care And Maintenance

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=handmade-felt-rattle-toys-are-delicate-and-need-special-care.-keeping-them-clean-and-in-good-condition-ensures-they-last-longer.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Handmade felt rattle toys are delicate and need special care. Keeping them clean and in good condition ensures they last longer.

Proper maintenance involves regular cleaning, understanding durability, and storing them correctly. Let's explore these steps in detail.

### Cleaning Tips

Cleaning handmade felt rattles requires gentle handling. Use mild soap and lukewarm water for washing.

Avoid soaking them. Instead, gently spot clean the surface with a soft cloth.

* Use a damp cloth to wipe the toy
* Air dry completely before giving it back to a child
* Avoid harsh chemicals to prevent damage

### Durability Considerations

Felt rattles are crafted to be sturdy, yet delicate. Knowing their limits helps maintain their condition.

They can withstand gentle play but not rough handling. Supervise playtime to prevent any damage.

* Check seams regularly for wear
* Avoid pulling or stretching the toy
* Repair any minor damage promptly

### Storage Recommendations

Proper storage is key to preserving your felt rattle toy. Store them in a cool, dry place.

Avoid direct sunlight and humidity, which can cause fading and mildew. Use breathable bags for storage.

* Keep toys in a dust-free container
* Avoid crowded storage spaces
* Check stored toys regularly for any signs of damage
