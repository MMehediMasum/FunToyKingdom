## Safety Tips For Using Plush Teething Rings

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=plush-teething-rings-help-soothe-babies'-gums.-parents-must-keep-safety-in-mind-while-using-them.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Plush teething rings help soothe babies' gums. Parents must keep safety in mind while using them.

Following simple safety tips can protect babies from harm. Always check the teething ring before use.

### Regular Inspection For Wear

Check plush teething rings often for damage. Look for loose threads, holes, or tears that may form.

Damaged teething rings can expose stuffing or small parts. These can be a danger to babies.

* Inspect seams and edges carefully
* Remove the ring if you see damage
* Replace worn teething rings promptly

### Supervision During Use

Always watch babies when they use plush teething rings. Babies can chew too hard or put the ring in unsafe ways.

Supervision helps prevent accidents and choking. Keep the teething ring within reach but never leave babies alone with it.

* Hold or sit near your baby during use
* Remove the ring if baby becomes upset
* Stop use if baby shows signs of discomfort

### Avoiding Choking Hazards

Choose plush teething rings without small parts. Small pieces can break off and cause choking.

Keep the teething ring clean and dry. Wet or dirty fabric can break down and create hazards.

* Pick rings made from safe, durable materials
* Check that all parts are firmly attached
* Wash and dry rings regularly

## Care And Maintenance

Wash teething rings to remove germs and dirt. Use gentle soap and warm water.

### Storage Recommendations

Store teething rings in a clean, dry place. Avoid damp areas to prevent mold.

Keep them in a container or bag. This helps to protect them from dust and bacteria.

* Store in a dry, cool place
* Use a storage bag or container
* Avoid direct sunlight

### Replacing When Necessary

Inspect teething rings regularly for wear and tear. Replace them if you see any damage.

Cracks or loose parts can be dangerous. Ensure your baby uses only safe teething rings.

* Check for cracks or breaks
* Replace if any parts are loose
* Buy new rings if they look worn
