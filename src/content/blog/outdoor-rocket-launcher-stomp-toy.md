## Rocket Launcher Basics

The Outdoor Rocket Launcher Stomp Toy is a fun toy for kids. It lets them launch foam rockets into the air using their feet.

This toy helps children play outside and learn cause and effect by stomping on the launcher.

### How It Works

The toy has a soft air bladder connected to a tube and a rocket. When you stomp on the bladder, it pushes air into the tube.

The air pushes the rocket up into the sky. The harder you stomp, the higher the rocket goes.

* Stomp on the air bladder with your foot
* Air flows through the tube
* Rocket lifts off and flies into the air

The rocket launcher is made of durable materials to handle outdoor play. It is lightweight and easy for kids to use.

The foam rocket is soft and safe, reducing the chance of injuries during play. The launcher can be used on grass or hard surfaces.

Distance competitions are a great way to see who can launch the rocket the farthest. Players take turns stomping the launcher to send the rocket flying.

Mark the landing spots with cones or chalk. The player with the longest distance wins the round.

* Set a clear launch line

* Play multiple rounds for fun

### Target Practice

Target practice helps improve aim and control. Set up targets like buckets, boxes, or drawn circles to hit with the rocket.

Players try to land the rocket inside or on the target. Points are given based on how close the rocket lands to the center.

* Use different sized targets for variety
* Assign points for each target zone
* Keep score to see who hits best
* Challenge players to improve their accuracy

### Team Relay Races

Team relay races add teamwork and speed to the fun. Players form teams and take turns launching the rocket in a race against others.

The goal is to complete the course or reach a finish line as fast as possible while passing the rocket launcher to the next player.

* Create a start and finish line
* Divide players into equal teams
* Each player launches once per turn
* Fastest team to finish wins
