### How Scale Sizes Affect Antique Toy Car Collections?



Scale size determines the car’s model size relative to the original vehicle. Common scales include 1:38, 1:40, and 1:43. Smaller scales fit well in collections; larger scales show more detail. Collectors choose scales based on display space and detail preferences.



### Are Pullback Action Features Common In Classic Toy Cars?



Yes, pullback action is popular in vintage-style diecast cars like the 6-pack classic American vehicles. It adds playability by allowing cars to move forward after pulling back. This feature enhances fun and attracts both kids and collectors.
