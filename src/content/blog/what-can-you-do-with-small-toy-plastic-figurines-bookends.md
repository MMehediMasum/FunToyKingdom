## Choosing The Right Figurines

Small toy plastic figurines make fun and unique bookends. Picking the right ones can keep your books neat and add style to your space. Think about what works best for your needs and taste.

Many choices exist, so focus on key points. This helps you find figurines that last and look good.

### Materials And Durability

Figurines come in different plastics. Some plastics are stronger and last longer. Look for hard plastics that resist scratches and breaks.

Durable materials keep your bookends steady. They hold books without falling or bending.

* ABS plastic is tough and lasts long
* PVC plastic is flexible but less strong
* Polystone is heavy and very durable

### Size And Weight Considerations

Choose figurines that fit your shelf size. Too big or too small can look odd or not support books well.

Weight matters to keep books standing. Heavier figurines hold books better, so pick ones with enough weight.

* Check figurine height to match book height
* Medium weight prevents tipping
* Balance size and weight for best support

### Themes And Styles

Figurines come in many themes. Choose a style that matches your room or interests.

Matching themes create a nice look. Pick fun, classic, or modern styles to fit your decor.

* Cartoon characters add playfulness
* Animals bring a natural feel
* Abstract shapes suit modern rooms
* Classic figures fit traditional spaces

## Diy Bookend Assembly

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=small-toy-plastic-figurines-can-become-unique-bookends-with-simple-crafting.-this-guide-helps-you-build-fun-and-useful-bookends-using-these-figurines.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Small toy plastic figurines can become unique bookends with simple crafting. This guide helps you build fun and useful bookends using these figurines.

Follow the steps below to assemble your bookends easily and personalize them with paint and finishing touches.

### Tools And Supplies Needed

* Small toy plastic figurines
* Wooden or sturdy plastic bases
* Strong adhesive glue (like epoxy or hot glue)
* Sandpaper for smoothing surfaces
* Acrylic paint and brushes
* Clear sealant spray for protection
* Newspaper or protective sheet for workspace

### Attaching Figurines To Bases

First, prepare the base by sanding it lightly to help glue stick better. Clean any dust before you start gluing.

1. Apply a small amount of glue to the bottom of the figurine.
2. Press the figurine firmly onto the base and hold for 30 seconds.
3. Allow the glued parts to dry completely, usually for a few hours.
4. Check if the figurine is stable and add more glue if needed.

### Painting And Finishing Touches

Painting adds color and protects your bookends. Use acrylic paint for best results.

| Painting Step | Tip |
| --- | --- |
| Prime | Apply a thin coat of primer to the base and figurine |
| Paint | Use thin layers to avoid drips and let dry between coats |
| Detail | Add fine details with a small brush |
| Seal | Spray clear sealant to protect paint and add shine |

## Safety And Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=small-toy-plastic-figurines-can-be-fun-and-decorative-bookends.-keeping-them-safe-and-well-maintained-is-important.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Small toy plastic figurines can be fun and decorative bookends. Keeping them safe and well-maintained is important. Here are some simple tips to help.

These tips will ensure your figurines stay secure and last a long time. Let's explore how to care for them properly.

### Securing Figurines Safely

Ensure figurines are stable when used as bookends. A wobbly figurine can fall and break.

* Place figurines on a flat, steady surface
* Use non-slip pads to prevent movement
* Position them against heavier books for support

### Cleaning And Care

Keeping your figurines clean is simple. Dust and dirt can make them look old.

* Use a soft cloth to wipe dust away
* For sticky spots, dampen the cloth slightly
* Avoid using harsh chemicals that can damage plastic

### Preventing Damage Over Time

Preventing damage helps your figurines last longer. Simple habits can protect them.

* Keep them away from direct sunlight to prevent fading
* Store them in a safe place when not in use
* Check regularly for loose parts and fix them early
