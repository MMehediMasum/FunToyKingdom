Tummy time toys strengthen neck and shoulder muscles while promoting motor skills. They encourage sensory exploration and eye tracking. ### Which Toys Support Sensory Learning In Infants 0-12 Months?



Soft books, rattles, and teethers with varied textures stimulate touch and sound senses. High contrast items boost visual skills. Montessori sensory toys encourage cause-and-effect understanding. These toys help develop fine motor skills and sensory processing from newborn to toddler stages.

Teething toys are ideal from 3 months onward when babies start gum discomfort. They soothe sore gums and encourage biting and chewing. Safe, BPA-free teethers promote oral motor development. Early use helps babies explore textures and relieve teething pain naturally.



### Why Are Musical Activity Stations Good For Infants?



Musical activity stations stimulate auditory senses and hand-eye coordination. They promote cause-and-effect learning through sounds and lights. These toys encourage active play and cognitive development. Suitable for 3-36 months, they keep babies engaged and support early learning milestones.



### How Do High Contrast Flashcards Aid Newborn Brain Growth?



High contrast flashcards attract newborn attention and improve focus. They enhance visual tracking and memory skills. Flashcards introduce patterns, shapes, and colors for early learning. Ideal for 0-3 months, they support sensory development and cognitive stimulation effectively.
