## Origins Of Superhero Dolls

Superhero dolls have been popular for many years. They let fans play with their favorite heroes.

These dolls come from a mix of toys and comic book stories. Let's look at how they started.

### Early Action Figures

Action figures were the first types of superhero dolls. They were made from plastic with movable parts.

The 1960s saw the rise of these toys. Kids loved to act out battles with them.

* Smaller than regular dolls
* Usually 3.75 inches tall
* Made to look like heroes and soldiers

### Influence Of Comic Books

Comic books gave life to superhero characters. They showed stories and powers that inspired toys.

Popular heroes in comics became popular dolls. Fans wanted to collect their favorite characters.

* Superman and Batman started in comics
* Stories helped kids imagine new adventures
* Comic art influenced doll design

### First Iconic Releases

The first famous superhero dolls appeared in the 1970s. Toy makers made figures from popular TV shows.

These dolls were simple but loved by many. They set the stage for future superhero toys.

* Kenner’s Star Wars figures inspired superhero toys
* Marvel and DC heroes got their own dolls
* Costumes and logos were key features

Designers start by drawing the doll’s look. Sculptors then create the shape from clay or digital tools. They add details like muscles and costumes.

Good materials make dolls last through play. Strong plastics and tight joints keep dolls from breaking. Paints are safe and do not fade easily.
