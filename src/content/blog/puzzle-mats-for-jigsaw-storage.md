Puzzle mats help you store jigsaw puzzles safely. They keep your pieces organized and protected.

Puzzle mats stop pieces from falling off the table. They keep pieces flat and prevent damage.

This lets you work on puzzles at different places. You do not need to finish the puzzle in one spot.

Select a mat size that fits your largest puzzles comfortably. The mat should be slightly bigger than your puzzle.

Thickness matters for protection and ease of rolling. Thicker mats protect pieces better but can be harder to store.

### Material Quality

Good material keeps puzzle pieces from slipping or bending. It also makes the mat durable for long use.

* Foam offers a soft, cushioned surface

### Storage Compatibility

## Tips For Using Puzzle Mats

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=puzzle-mats-help-you-store-and-carry-jigsaw-puzzles-easily.-they-keep-pieces-safe-and-organized.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Puzzle mats help you store and carry jigsaw puzzles easily. They keep pieces safe and organized. Using puzzle mats well can save space and keep puzzles neat.

Here are some useful tips on rolling, storing, and cleaning your puzzle mats.

### Rolling Techniques

Roll your puzzle mat gently to avoid damaging the puzzle pieces. Start rolling from one edge, keeping the puzzle flat and tight. Use a soft tube or cardboard inside to keep the roll firm.

1. Place the puzzle mat on a flat surface.
2. Roll slowly and evenly from one side.
3. Insert a soft tube for support.
4. Secure the roll with straps or bands.
5. Store the rolled mat upright or flat.

### Storing In Small Spaces

Puzzle mats fit well in small spaces. You can store them in closets, under beds, or in cabinets. Rolling mats makes storage easier and saves room.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Storage Place | Best Position | Notes |
| Closet Shelf | Flat or upright | Keep away from moisture |
| Under Bed | Flat | Use a dust cover |
| Cabinet | Upright | Ensure enough space |
| Drawer | Flat | Check size before storing |

### Cleaning And Maintenance

Keep your puzzle mat clean to extend its life. Clean the mat surface after use. Avoid harsh cleaners that can damage the mat.

Cleaning tips:

* Use a damp cloth to wipe the mat.
* Spot clean with mild soap if needed.
* Let the mat air dry completely before rolling.
* Store in a dry place to avoid mold.
