## Tips For Choosing Authentic Plushies

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=finding-a-real-care-bear-plushy-can-be-tricky.-many-fake-versions-look-very-similar.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Finding a real Care Bear plushy can be tricky. Many fake versions look very similar. Use these tips to pick an authentic one.

Knowing what to check helps you get a soft, high-quality toy. This guide shows simple ways to spot the real Care Bear plushy.

### Check For Official Tags

Real Care Bear plushies have official tags. These tags show the brand and licensing details. Look closely at the tag design and spelling.

Fake plushies often miss tags or use wrong logos. The tag should feel sturdy and printed clearly. Some tags even have a hologram or special mark.

* Check for the Care Bears brand name on the tag
* Look for copyright year and licensing info
* Verify tag quality and print sharpness
* Watch out for missing or fake tags

### Compare Prices

Price can tell if a plushy is real or fake. Authentic Care Bears usually cost more because of quality and licensing fees. Very cheap plushies may be fake.

Compare prices from different sellers. If one price is much lower, check the product details carefully. Sometimes fakes are sold at very low prices.

* Look for price ranges of official Care Bear plushies
* Avoid very low prices that seem too good to be true
* Check if the seller explains why the price is low
* Remember, higher price often means better quality

### Read Customer Reviews

Customer reviews can reveal if a plushy is real. Buyers often mention if the product looks fake or feels cheap. Look for detailed reviews with photos.

Reviews can also share seller trustworthiness. Choose plushies with many positive reviews. Be careful if many reviews warn about fake products.

* Read multiple reviews to get a clear idea
* Look for comments on material and tag authenticity
* Check for photos uploaded by buyers
* Avoid sellers with many negative reviews about fakes
