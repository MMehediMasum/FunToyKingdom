Melissa & Doug started in 1988. The founders are Melissa and Doug Bernstein. They began by selling wooden puzzles from their home. Their goal was to create toys that inspire creativity. Over time, the company grew and added many toy types. Today, Melissa & Doug is a well-known name in children’s toys.
