## Collecting Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collecting-fantastic-beasts-figures-can-be-a-fun-hobby.-it-brings-magic-and-fantasy-into-your-home.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collecting Fantastic Beasts figures can be a fun hobby. It brings magic and fantasy into your home.

These tips help you find, check, and keep your figures in great shape for a long time.

### Where To Buy

You can buy Fantastic Beasts figures from many places. Check stores that sell toys and collectibles.

Online shops offer many options. Always compare prices and check seller ratings before buying.

* Official movie merchandise stores
* Specialty toy shops
* Online marketplaces like eBay or Amazon
* Fan conventions and collectible fairs

### Authenticity Checks

Check if the figure is real to avoid fakes. Look for official logos and quality details.

Packaging should have clear printing and proper seals. Compare your figure with pictures from trusted sources.

* Look for official Warner Bros or Fantastic Beasts logos
* Check the figure’s paint quality and details
* Verify serial numbers or certificates if included
* Avoid deals that seem too cheap or sellers without reviews

### Storage And Display

Keep your figures safe from dust and damage. Use display cases or shelves with good lighting.

Store figures away from direct sunlight to prevent fading. Use soft cloths to clean them gently.

* Use glass or plastic display boxes
* Place figures on sturdy shelves
* Keep away from heat and moisture
* Dust regularly with a soft brush or cloth
