## Safety Features To Look For

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-a-teething-toy-shaped-like-fruit-requires-attention-to-safety.-babies-explore-by-putting-toys-in-their-mouths.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing a teething toy shaped like fruit requires attention to safety. Babies explore by putting toys in their mouths. This means safety is very important.

Parents should check toys carefully before giving them to children. This guide explains key safety features to consider.

### Non-toxic Materials

Teething toys must be made from materials that are safe if chewed or sucked. Non-toxic plastics and silicone are common choices. Avoid toys with harmful chemicals like BPA, phthalates, or lead.

Look for products labeled as food-grade or medical-grade materials. These labels show the toy meets safety standards for infants.

### Choking Hazard Prevention

Small parts can break off and cause choking. The toy should be a size too large to fit in a baby’s mouth. Check for secure attachments and no loose pieces.

1. Ensure all parts are firmly attached
2. Avoid toys with small detachable pieces
3. Choose toys designed for babies under 12 months
4. Inspect the toy regularly for damage

### Easy To Clean Surfaces

Teething toys need regular cleaning to keep germs away. Smooth surfaces without cracks or crevices are easier to clean. Avoid fabric or fuzzy materials that trap dirt.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Surface Type | Cleaning Method |
| Silicone | Wash with warm soapy water or boil |
| Plastic | Wipe with disinfectant or wash with soap |
| Fabric | Machine wash or hand wash |

## Choosing The Right Size And Weight

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=teething-toys-shaped-like-fruit-come-in-many-sizes-and-weights.-picking-the-right-one-is-important-for-your-baby’s-comfort.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Teething toys shaped like fruit come in many sizes and weights. Picking the right one is important for your baby’s comfort. A toy that is too big or heavy can be hard to handle.

Consider your baby's age and strength. Choose a toy that fits well in their little hands and is easy to lift.

### Ergonomic Grips For Little Hands

Babies need teething toys that are easy to hold. Ergonomic grips are designed for little hands. These grips help babies grasp the toy without dropping it.

Look for toys with soft, rounded handles. They should not be too thick or too thin. This makes it easier for babies to chew and play.

* Soft grips are gentle on gums
* Rounded shapes prevent slipping
* Lightweight designs are easy to lift

### Balancing Portability And Usability

Teething toys should be portable and usable. A toy that is too large may be hard to carry. A very small toy might be easy to lose.

Choose a toy that can fit in a diaper bag. It should also be simple for babies to use while sitting or crawling.

## Tips For Cleaning And Maintenance

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=teething-toys-shaped-like-fruit-need-regular-cleaning.-keeping-them-clean-helps-protect-your-baby-from-germs.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Teething toys shaped like fruit need regular cleaning. Keeping them clean helps protect your baby from germs.

Proper maintenance also keeps the toy safe and lasts longer. Use simple steps to care for your teething toy.

### Safe Washing Techniques

Wash teething toys often with warm, soapy water. Avoid harsh chemicals that can harm your baby.

Rinse well to remove all soap. Dry the toy completely before giving it back to your baby.

* Use mild dish soap and warm water
* Gently scrub with a soft brush or cloth
* Rinse thoroughly to remove soap
* Air dry or pat dry with a clean towel
* Do not use bleach or strong cleaners

### Storage Recommendations

Store the teething toy in a clean, dry place. Avoid damp or dusty areas to keep it safe.

Use a small container or bag to protect the toy when not in use. This keeps it free from dirt.

* Keep the toy in a sealed plastic box or pouch
* Store away from direct sunlight and heat
* Check the toy for damage before each use
* Replace the toy if it shows signs of wear
