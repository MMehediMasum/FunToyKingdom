## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Ball Launcher Suits My Dog's Weight?



For dogs 20-60 pounds, use a medium 2. 5" ball launcher. Larger dogs (60-100 pounds) need a 3" ball launcher. Choose a size matching your dog’s weight for safe, comfortable fetch play and better performance.



### Are Automatic Ball Launchers Safe For Indoor Use?



Yes, many automatic launchers have adjustable distances from 10 to 30 feet. This makes them safe for indoor use in larger rooms or hallways. Always supervise your dog to avoid accidents and choose a model with soft, dog-friendly balls.



### How Far Can These Dog Ball Launchers Throw?



Most launchers can throw balls between 30 to 50 feet. For example, the Nerf Dog Tennis Ball Blaster launches up to 50 feet, ideal for energetic dogs needing long fetch sessions. Adjustable distances help tailor play to your space.



### Do Ball Launchers Come With Dog-safe Tennis Balls?



Yes, products like Chuckit! And ALL FOR PAWS include dog-safe tennis balls. These balls are non-toxic, soft, and sized appropriately to prevent choking. Always use balls designed for dogs to ensure safety during playtime.



### Can These Toys Improve My Dog's Mental Stimulation?



Absolutely. Automatic ball launchers promote active play and mental engagement. Dogs learn to fetch, wait, and focus, which reduces boredom. Many models have interactive features making fetch fun and mentally stimulating for energetic dogs.



### Are Ball Launchers Rechargeable Or Battery-operated?



Both options exist. Some models like the SOCO Dog Ball Launcher are USB rechargeable, offering convenience and eco-friendliness. Others use replaceable batteries. Rechargeable launchers save money long-term and are great for frequent use.
