## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-plush-toy-puppy-clean-and-working-well-helps-it-last-longer.-toddlers-can-be-rough-on-toys,-so-regular-care-is-important.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your plush toy puppy clean and working well helps it last longer. Toddlers can be rough on toys, so regular care is important.

Follow simple steps to keep the toy puppy safe and fun for your child. This guide covers cleaning, battery care, and storage tips.

### Cleaning Instructions

Use a soft cloth to wipe the surface of the plush toy puppy. Avoid soaking the toy in water to protect the electronics inside.

If the toy has stains, use mild soap mixed with water on the cloth. Gently clean the affected area and let it dry completely.

* Do not put the toy in a washing machine
* Keep water away from battery compartments
* Dry the toy in a well-ventilated area

### Battery Care

Check the batteries often to ensure the toy puppy works well. Remove batteries if the toy will not be used for a long time.

Replace old or weak batteries quickly to avoid leaks that can damage the toy. Use the correct battery size as listed in the manual.

* Remove batteries if toy is unused for over a month
* Use fresh batteries for better performance
* Dispose of batteries safely and properly

### Storage Recommendations

Store the plush toy puppy in a dry place away from direct sunlight. Avoid damp areas to prevent mold and bad smells.

Keep the toy in a spot where it will not get crushed or dirty. Using a clean box or bag helps protect it from dust and damage.

* Choose a cool, dry storage area
* Use a breathable bag or container
* Keep away from pets and pests
