## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Is Best For Baby Piano Toys?



Baby piano toys are ideal for infants aged 6 to 18 months. They help develop motor skills and sensory awareness. These toys introduce music and light interaction, promoting early learning and cognitive growth in babies.



### How Do Stacking Toys Benefit Infant Development?



Stacking toys improve hand-eye coordination and fine motor skills. They teach shape recognition and problem-solving for infants aged 6+ months. These toys encourage creativity and early STEM learning through engaging play.



### Why Choose Montessori Tissue Box Toys For Babies?



Montessori tissue box toys enhance sensory exploration and cause-effect learning. Suitable for 6-12 month infants, they support fine motor skills and hand dexterity. These soft, crinkle toys stimulate curiosity and early cognitive development.



### What Features Make Pop-up Animal Toys Educational?



Pop-up animal toys combine music, lights, and movement to teach cause and effect. Designed for 9 to 18 months, they develop auditory and visual senses. These toys encourage interaction and early STEM skills through playful learning.



### How Do Push Walkers Support Infant Mobility?



Push walkers aid infants aged 12+ months in learning to walk and balance. They provide stability and enhance leg strength while offering educational activities. These toys promote physical development and confidence during early walking stages.



### Are High Contrast Sensory Books Good For Newborns?



Yes, high contrast sensory books are perfect for 0-6 month babies. They stimulate visual development and focus. These soft, tactile books encourage early sensory exploration and bonding during tummy time and quiet moments.
