### Choosing The Right Felt

Selecting good felt is important for soft and durable hearts. Wool felt is thick and holds shape well. Acrylic felt is cheaper and comes in many colors.

* Wool felt for a sturdy and natural feel
* Acrylic felt for bright colors and easy cutting
* Choose colors that match your design
* Buy enough felt to cut two heart shapes per toy

## Basic Sewing Techniques

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-handmade-toy-hearts-from-felt-is-a-fun-project.-you-only-need-simple-sewing-skills.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making handmade toy hearts from felt is a fun project. You only need simple sewing skills. These skills help you create neat and strong toys.

Learning basic stitches and seam tips makes your work look better. It also keeps the toy safe for children to use.

### Simple Stitches For Beginners

Start with easy stitches to join felt pieces. The running stitch and whip stitch are good choices. These stitches hold the felt firmly.

* **Running Stitch:**Pass the needle in and out evenly along the edge.
* **Whip Stitch:**Sew around the edge, wrapping the thread over the edge each time.
* **Backstitch:**Sew backward a little to make a strong seam.

### Tips For Neat Seams

Neat seams improve the look of your felt hearts. Use the right thread and keep stitches close. Trim excess fabric to avoid lumps.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Tip** | **Details** |
| Thread Choice | Use polyester or cotton thread that matches felt color. |
| Stitch Length | Keep stitches small and even, about 3 to 4 mm long. |
| Pinning | Pin felt pieces before sewing to hold them steady. |
| Thread Tension | Pull the thread gently to avoid puckering the felt. |

## Step-by-step Crafting Process

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=creating-handmade-toy-hearts-from-felt-is-a-fun-and-simple-craft.-you-will-need-some-basic-materials-like-felt-fabric,-scissors,-needle,-and-thread.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Creating handmade toy hearts from felt is a fun and simple craft. You will need some basic materials like felt fabric, scissors, needle, and thread.

This guide shows you each step clearly. Follow along to make your own soft and lovely hearts.

### Cutting The Felt Shapes

Start by cutting two identical heart shapes from your felt fabric. Use a paper template for accuracy. Choose colors you like best for your hearts.

* Place the paper template on the felt
* Trace around the template with a fabric marker
* Cut carefully along the traced line
* Repeat to get two matching pieces

### Assembling The Heart

Put the two felt shapes together, aligning the edges. Use a needle and thread to sew around the heart. Leave a small opening for stuffing.

**Tips for sewing:**

* Use a simple running stitch or blanket stitch
* Keep stitches close to prevent gaps
* Secure thread ends with knots

### Adding Stuffing

Fill the heart with soft stuffing through the opening. Use small amounts at a time to keep the shape even. Do not overstuff to keep it soft.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Stuffing Materials** | **Benefits** |
| Polyester fiberfill | Lightweight, soft, easy to shape |
| Cotton balls | Natural, soft, affordable |
| Fabric scraps | Eco-friendly, firm fill |

### Finishing Touches

After stuffing, sew the opening closed using small stitches. Add decorations like buttons or embroidery if you want. Check all stitches for strength.

## Care And Durability

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=handmade-toy-hearts-from-felt-are-both-charming-and-delicate.-taking-proper-care-of-them-ensures-they-last-longer-and-remain-beautiful.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Handmade toy hearts from felt are both charming and delicate. Taking proper care of them ensures they last longer and remain beautiful.

Learn how to clean and maintain these toys to keep their shape and color vibrant. Simple steps can help in preserving these toys for years.

### Cleaning Handmade Felt Toys

Cleaning felt toys requires gentle handling to avoid damage. Always use mild soap and cold water.

Rinse carefully and avoid wringing the felt. Let the toy air dry completely to maintain its quality.

* Use a soft cloth for wiping dirt
* Spot clean with mild detergent
* Avoid soaking the toy in water

### Maintaining Shape And Color

Proper storage helps in maintaining the shape of felt toys. Store them in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

To retain color, avoid placing toys in areas with strong sunlight. This prevents fading and keeps the colors bright.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Keep away from sunlight | Prevents color fading |
| Store in a cool place | Maintains toy shape |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Materials Are Needed For Handmade Felt Toy Hearts?

You need soft felt sheets, embroidery thread, stuffing, scissors, and a needle. These basic materials ensure your toy hearts are soft and durable.

### How Do You Sew Felt Toy Hearts By Hand?

Cut two heart shapes from felt, stitch edges using a blanket stitch. Leave a small opening, stuff with filling, then close the gap carefully.

### Can Children Safely Play With Felt Toy Hearts?

Yes, felt toy hearts are safe for children if securely stitched. Use non-toxic materials and avoid small parts that could be choking hazards.

### How Long Does It Take To Make A Felt Heart Toy?

Making one felt heart toy usually takes about 30 to 60 minutes. Time depends on stitching complexity and decoration details you choose.
