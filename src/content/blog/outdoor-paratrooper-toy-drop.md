## Paratrooper Toy Basics

Outdoor paratrooper toys are fun toys that mimic real parachutes. Kids can throw them up and watch them float down slowly.

These toys help children learn about air resistance and gravity while playing outside.

### Design And Features

Paratrooper toys usually have a small plastic figure attached to a fabric parachute. The parachute is wide and light.

The toy is designed to float down gently from a height. It has strings connecting the figure to the parachute for balance.

* Lightweight parachute fabric
* Small plastic paratrooper figure
* Strong but thin suspension strings
* Bright colors for visibility

### Materials Used

The parachute is made of nylon or polyester, which are light and strong. This helps it catch air well.

The paratrooper figure is usually made from durable plastic. The strings are made from nylon thread to hold weight without breaking.

* Nylon or polyester fabric for parachute
* Plastic for the toy figure
* Nylon thread for strings

### How It Works

To use the toy, you hold the paratrooper figure and throw it into the air. The parachute opens as it falls.

Air fills the parachute, slowing the fall. The strings keep the figure steady while it glides down gently.

* Throw toy upward
* Parachute opens in air
* Air resistance slows descent
* Paratrooper floats down safely
