## Safety Tips For Play

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=playing-with-a-nerf-bow-and-arrow-toy-is-fun-and-exciting.-safety-should-always-come-first-to-avoid-accidents.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Playing with a Nerf Bow and Arrow toy is fun and exciting. Safety should always come first to avoid accidents.

Following simple safety tips helps keep everyone safe and happy during playtime.

### Proper Usage Guidelines

Use the Nerf Bow and Arrow as the instructions say. Never aim at people’s faces or animals.

Only shoot soft foam arrows that come with the toy. Do not modify the arrows or bow in any way.

* Load the arrows carefully to avoid jamming.
* Do not shoot arrows indoors near fragile items.
* Keep arrows away from small children to prevent choking.

### Protective Gear Recommendations

Wearing protective gear reduces the risk of injury. Eye protection is the most important gear to wear.

Consider using soft gloves to protect hands during play. Comfortable clothing that covers arms helps prevent scratches.

* Use safety glasses or goggles to protect eyes.
* Wear gloves if you plan long play sessions.
* Choose long sleeves to guard the skin.

### Supervision And Play Area Setup

Always have an adult watch children while they use the Nerf Bow and Arrow. Supervision helps stop unsafe actions.

Set up a safe play area. Clear the space of breakable things and sharp objects. Use soft targets for aiming practice.

* Choose open spaces away from windows and glass.
* Remove furniture that could cause injury if bumped.
* Use foam or fabric targets instead of hard ones.
* Keep other children and pets outside the play zone.
