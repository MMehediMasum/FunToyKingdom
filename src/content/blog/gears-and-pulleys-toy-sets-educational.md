### Types Of Gears Included

Gears are wheels with teeth that fit together to move parts. Toy sets often have many types of gears for learning.

Each gear type shows how machines change speed and direction. Kids can see how gears work in real life.

* Spur gears: Straight teeth for simple motion
* Bevel gears: Angled teeth to change direction
* Worm gears: Screw-like shape for slow movement
* Rack and pinion: Gear and straight bar for linear motion

### Varieties Of Pulleys

Pulleys are wheels that help lift or move objects. Toy sets include several pulley types to teach different uses.

These pulleys show how machines make work easier by changing force direction or size.

* Fixed pulleys: Stay in one place to change direction
* Movable pulleys: Move with the load to reduce effort
* Compound pulleys: Combine fixed and movable for less force

### Additional Accessories

Besides gears and pulleys, sets include extra parts to build and explore. These parts add fun and learning value.

Accessories help connect pieces and create different machines and experiments.

* Connectors and rods to join parts
