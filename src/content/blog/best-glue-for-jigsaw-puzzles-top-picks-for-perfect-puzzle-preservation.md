## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Glue For Preserving Jigsaw Puzzles?



Mod Podge Puzzle Saver is ideal for preserving jigsaw puzzles. It dries clear, is non-toxic, and seals pieces securely. It also works as a finish, making your puzzle durable and display-ready with a premium matte or gloss finish option.



### How Do I Apply Puzzle Glue Evenly?



Use a sponge head applicator for even glue application. Spread the glue gently over the puzzle surface without lifting pieces. Products like Datum Labs Jigsaw Puzzle Glue include easy-to-use sponges for smooth, quick-drying, and mess-free application.



### Can Puzzle Glue Be Used On Wood Puzzles?



Yes, many puzzle glues like Mod Podge and Ainiv are suitable for paper and wood puzzles. They dry clear and provide a strong, protective seal that keeps wooden puzzle pieces intact and enhances durability for display.



### Are Puzzle Glues Non-toxic And Safe For Kids?



Most puzzle glues mentioned, like PHILODOGS and Mod Podge, are non-toxic and safe for children. They allow safe crafting without harmful fumes, making them perfect for family puzzle projects and educational activities.



### How Long Does It Take For Puzzle Glue To Dry?



Puzzle glue typically dries within 15 to 30 minutes, depending on the brand and thickness applied. Quick-dry formulas like Mod Podge Gloss Sealer speed up the process, allowing faster handling and framing of your completed puzzle.



### Can Diamond Painting Sealers Be Used On Jigsaw Puzzles?



Yes, diamond painting sealers like VansyLife and KISREL work well on jigsaw puzzles. They provide a permanent hold with a shiny finish, enhancing puzzle appearance while protecting it from dust and damage.
