Rinse the inflatable with clean water to remove dirt and grass. Avoid using strong soaps that can harm the material.

* Let it air dry completely before storing

### Proper Deflation

Deflate the whale slowly to avoid damaging the seams. Open all valves to release air fully.

1. Locate all air valves on the inflatable
2. Open valves to let air escape naturally
3. Press gently to remove trapped air
4. Fold the inflatable neatly without sharp creases
