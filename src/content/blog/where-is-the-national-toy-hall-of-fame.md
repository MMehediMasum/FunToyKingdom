### Where Is The National Toy Hall Of Fame Located?

The National Toy Hall of Fame is in Rochester, New York. It is part of The Strong museum, a place dedicated to play and learning. This location brings together toys and history in one spot.

### About Rochester, New York

Rochester is a city in the western part of New York State. It has a rich history and many family-friendly attractions. Visitors can enjoy other museums, parks, and dining options nearby.

The Strong museum hosts the National Toy Hall of Fame. It has interactive exhibits and a large toy collection. The museum is open year-round and welcomes visitors of all ages.
