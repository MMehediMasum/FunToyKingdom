## Tips For Maintaining Batteries

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-spare-batteries-in-good-condition-helps-your-ride-on-toy-last-longer.-proper-care-keeps-batteries-safe-and-reliable.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping spare batteries in good condition helps your ride on toy last longer. Proper care keeps batteries safe and reliable.

Follow simple habits to get the best from your batteries and avoid common problems.

### Charging Best Practices

Always use the charger made for your battery type. Avoid overcharging by unplugging once fully charged. Charge batteries in a dry place at room temperature.

* Plug in the charger only after the battery is connected.
* Do not charge batteries overnight or for more than 12 hours.
* Keep the charger and battery away from water or heat sources.
* Check the battery for damage before charging.

### Storage Recommendations

Store batteries in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Avoid freezing or very hot spots to prevent damage.

| Storage Condition | Recommended | Not Recommended |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temperature | 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F) | Below 0°C or above 40°C |
| Humidity | Low to moderate | High moisture or damp areas |
| Charge Level | Store at 40%-60% charge | Fully charged or fully drained |

### Battery Lifespan Optimization

Follow these tips to make your battery last longer and stay healthy during use.

* Use the battery regularly, but do not drain it completely.
* Keep the battery clean and dry. Wipe with a soft cloth if needed.
* Avoid dropping or hitting the battery to prevent internal damage.
* Replace old batteries if they show signs of swelling or slow charging.
