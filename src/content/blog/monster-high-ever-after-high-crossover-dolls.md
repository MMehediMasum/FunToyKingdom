## Collector’s Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=monster-high-ever-after-high-crossover-dolls-are-unique-collectibles.-taking-care-of-them-helps-keep-their-value-and-beauty.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Monster High Ever After High crossover dolls are unique collectibles. Taking care of them helps keep their value and beauty.

This guide shares tips on checking authenticity, storing, displaying, and cleaning your dolls.

### Authenticity Checks

Fake dolls can look very similar to real ones. Check details carefully to avoid buying counterfeits.

Look for official logos and tags on the doll and packaging. Real dolls usually have clear brand marks.

* Check the quality of paint and stitching
* Look for a unique serial number or batch code
* Compare the doll with official pictures online
* Be cautious of very low prices
* Buy from trusted sellers or stores

### Storage And Display Ideas

Proper storage keeps dolls safe from dust, sunlight, and damage. It also helps keep their colors bright.

Display dolls in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Use glass cases or shelves with covers to protect them.

* Use acid-free tissue paper for wrapping
* Keep dolls standing or supported to avoid damage
* Rotate display dolls to prevent wear
* Store extra clothes and accessories separately
* Keep humidity low to avoid mold

### Maintenance And Cleaning

Regular cleaning keeps dolls looking fresh. Use gentle methods to avoid damage to paint and fabric.

Dust dolls often with a soft brush or cloth. Clean plastic parts with a damp cloth and mild soap if needed.

* Do not soak dolls in water
* Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners
* Let dolls air dry completely after cleaning
* Use a soft brush for hair styling and detangling
* Store dolls only when fully dry to prevent mold
