### Simple Science Experiments

Use the microscope to watch changes during easy experiments. Children learn cause and effect by seeing results up close.

1. Place a small drop of water on a slide and observe moving microorganisms.
2. Compare salt and sugar crystals under the microscope.
3. Watch how ice melts and changes shape on the slide.
4. Examine different fabric fibers to see how they differ.
