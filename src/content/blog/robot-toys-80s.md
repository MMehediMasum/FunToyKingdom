The 1980s introduced a variety of robot toys that captured the hearts of children everywhere. From wind-up clockwork models like the CHIMUYU vintage robot to building sets such as the Johnny 5 Robot compatible with Lego, these toys encouraged creativity and learning.



Kids enjoyed battling with Mattel’s Rock 'Em Sock 'Em Robots or controlling remote robots like the Hamourd models with gesture sensing and music features. Collectors value tinplate robots like the Fussbudget drumming robot for their nostalgic charm.
