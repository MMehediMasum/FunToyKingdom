## Safety Tips For Playtime

### Supervision Guidelines

Adults should watch children closely during play. This helps stop unsafe behavior before it starts.

1. Always have an adult nearby while kids use the set.
2. Explain safety rules clearly before play begins.
3. Stop play immediately if anyone gets hurt.
4. Check the area and equipment before and after play.

### Proper Use Of Equipment

Using the bow and arrows the right way prevents accidents and damage to the set.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Do | Don’t |
| Aim only at safe targets | Aim at people or animals |
| Use foam darts only | Use sharp or homemade arrows |
| Check darts for damage | Use broken or worn darts |
| Store equipment properly | Leave equipment where it can be a tripping hazard |

### Safe Play Areas

Choose a clear, open space to play. Avoid areas near windows or breakable items.

* Play outdoors or in a large room with soft flooring.
* Keep the area free of obstacles and sharp objects.
* Avoid crowded places with many people nearby.
* Ensure good lighting to see clearly.
