## Safety And Maintenance

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-a-toy-drawing-tablet-safe-and-in-good-condition-helps-it-last-longer.-proper-care-protects-children-and-the-device.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping a toy drawing tablet safe and in good condition helps it last longer. Proper care protects children and the device.

Learn how to handle the tablet, clean it, and charge it safely for the best experience.

### Non-toxic Materials

Choose a drawing tablet made with safe, non-toxic materials. This avoids harm if children touch or mouth the tablet.

Look for certifications or labels that prove the materials are free from harmful chemicals. This ensures the tablet is child-friendly.

### Cleaning And Care

Keep the tablet clean to maintain its look and function. Use a soft, dry cloth or a slightly damp cloth for stubborn dirt.

* Do not use harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners.
* Keep the screen free of scratches by avoiding sharp objects.
* Store the tablet in a dry place away from direct sunlight.
* Check the stylus tip regularly and replace if worn out.

### Battery And Charging Tips

| Tip | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Use the original charger supplied with the tablet. | Prevents damage to the battery and device. |
| Do not overcharge; unplug when fully charged. | Extends battery life and avoids overheating. |
| Charge in a cool, dry place. | Maintains battery efficiency and safety. |
| Do not charge near water or wet surfaces. | Reduces risk of electric shock or damage. |
