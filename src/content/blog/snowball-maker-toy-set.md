### Cleaning After Use

Clean the Snowball Maker Toy Set after each use to remove dirt and moisture. This stops mold and keeps parts working well. Use a soft cloth or rinse with water as needed.

* Wipe dry with a soft towel

Store the Snowball Maker Toy Set in a cool, dry place. Avoid spots with direct sunlight or high humidity. Proper storage stops parts from warping or cracking.

| Storage Tip | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Keep all pieces together in a box | Prevents loss of parts |
| Store away from heat sources | Avoids melting or warping |
| Use a sealed container if possible | Keeps dust and moisture out |
| Place in an easy-to-reach spot | Makes it simple to find and use |

### Extending Toy Lifespan

To keep your Snowball Maker Toy Set lasting many winters, handle it with care. Avoid dropping or bending parts. Check for damage before each use to fix small problems early.

1. Use the toy only as intended
2. Keep it away from sharp objects
3. Dry completely after each use
4. Store out of reach of pets and small children
5. Inspect regularly and replace broken parts
