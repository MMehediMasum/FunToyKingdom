## What Are Capsule Toy Keychains

Capsule toy keychains are small collectible toys inside plastic capsules. They are popular in vending machines and toy stores.

These keychains come in many shapes and themes. They are fun to collect and use on bags or keys.

### Origin Of Capsule Toy Keychains

Capsule toys started in Japan in the 1960s. They were sold in gashapon machines, which are coin-operated vending machines.

The toy keychains grew popular because they offer surprise and variety. Collectors enjoy finding rare items in the capsules.

* Animal figures
* Cartoon characters
* Miniature food items
* Robots and gadgets
* Seasonal and holiday themes

### How Capsule Toy Keychains Are Made

The toys are made from plastic or rubber. They are painted with bright colors to look attractive.

The keychain parts are added after the toy is made. This lets people use them on keys or backpacks.

### Why People Collect Capsule Toy Keychains

Collectors like capsule toy keychains for several reasons:

* They are small and easy to carry.
* Each capsule holds a surprise toy.
* Many themes attract different interests.
* They can be traded with friends.
* Some become rare and valuable.

### Cleaning And Maintenance

Keep your keychains free from dust and dirt by cleaning them gently. Avoid harsh chemicals to prevent damage.

* Use a soft, dry cloth to wipe the surface.

* Dry keychains completely before storing or displaying.
* Avoid soaking the keychains in water.
* Check metal parts for rust and clean carefully.
