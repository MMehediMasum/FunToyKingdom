## Tips For Setting Up Your Dart Board

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-up-your-electronic-dart-board-correctly-makes-playing-more-fun-and-safe.-follow-simple-tips-to-place-and-connect-your-board-properly.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting up your electronic dart board correctly makes playing more fun and safe. Follow simple tips to place and connect your board properly.

Good setup helps keep the board working well and lasts longer. It also improves your throwing experience.

### Ideal Placement And Height

Place your dart board on a flat wall with enough space around it. Avoid areas with bright sunlight or strong lights that can cause glare.

The standard height for the board’s bullseye is 5 feet 8 inches (173 cm) from the floor. This height suits most players for comfortable throws.

* Choose a wall free from obstacles and traffic
* Keep at least 5 feet clear in front of the board
* Set the bullseye 5 feet 8 inches from the floor
* Avoid placing near windows or bright lights

### Power And Connectivity

Electronic dart boards need power to work. Use a nearby outlet or an extension cord designed for indoor use to connect your board safely.

Some boards use batteries. Check your model and prepare fresh batteries to avoid interruptions during play.

* Plug the board into a stable power source
* Avoid overloading outlets with many devices
* Use fresh batteries if your board is battery-powered
* Keep cords away from walkways to prevent tripping

### Maintaining The Board

Clean your dart board regularly to keep it working well. Dust and dirt can affect the sensors and scoring.

Check for loose parts or damaged sections. Tighten screws and replace worn pieces to keep the board safe and accurate.

* Wipe the surface with a dry or slightly damp cloth
* Do not use harsh chemicals or water directly on the board
* Inspect screws and attachments monthly
* Store the board indoors away from moisture and heat
