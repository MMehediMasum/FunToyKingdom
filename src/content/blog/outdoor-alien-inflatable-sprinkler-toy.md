Use an electric or manual pump to inflate the alien sprinkler. Inflate it slowly to avoid leaks or damage.

Check all air valves for tightness once inflated. Do not overinflate, as this can stretch the material and cause tears.

* Place the toy on a flat surface before inflating

* Seal all valves tightly after inflation
* Keep sharp objects away during setup

### Connecting To Water Source

Attach your garden hose to the sprinkler’s water inlet. Make sure the connection is secure to prevent leaks.

Turn on the water slowly to check the flow. Adjust the hose or sprinkler if water does not spray evenly.

* Use a standard garden hose with correct fitting
* Check for leaks at the connection point
* Start water flow at low pressure
* Increase pressure as needed for spray distance

### Optimal Placement

Place the alien sprinkler on a soft, flat lawn area. Avoid rocky or uneven ground to keep it stable.

Position the sprinkler where water spray will not hit hard surfaces or neighbors’ property. Keep it away from electrical equipment.

* Choose a sunny spot for best outdoor fun

## Maintenance And Storage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-your-outdoor-alien-inflatable-sprinkler-toy-helps-it-last-longer.-proper-cleaning-and-storage-keep-it-safe.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of your Outdoor Alien Inflatable Sprinkler Toy helps it last longer. Proper cleaning and storage keep it safe.

Simple steps after each use can prevent damage and mold. Follow these tips to keep your toy ready for fun days.

### Cleaning After Use

Rinse the sprinkler toy with clean water to remove dirt and grass. Avoid using harsh chemicals that can harm the material.

Check all the small holes and seams for stuck debris. Use a soft cloth or sponge to gently clean these areas.

### Proper Drying Techniques

Dry the toy completely before storing it. Moisture can cause mold or mildew inside the inflatable parts.

Lay the toy flat in a shaded area. Avoid direct sunlight for long times to prevent color fading and material damage.

### Storage Tips For Longevity

Store the toy in a cool, dry place away from sharp objects. Folding it gently helps avoid creases and tears.

* Keep it in a storage bag or box to protect from dust.
* Avoid storing near heat sources to prevent material warping.
* Check the toy before each use for any damage or leaks.
