## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Dogs Are Compatible With These Tennis Ball Launchers?



Most tennis ball launchers suit small to medium dogs (20-60 pounds). Some models offer adjustable ball sizes for smaller breeds. Always check product specs for weight and ball size compatibility to ensure safe play and proper launcher performance.



### How Far Can These Dog Tennis Ball Launchers Throw Balls?



Launch distances vary from 10 to 65 feet depending on the model. Automatic launchers typically reach 30-50 feet, while manual ones may launch farther. Choose based on your dog’s play space and energy level for optimal fun and exercise.



### Are These Tennis Ball Launchers Safe For Dogs?



Yes, these launchers are designed with pet safety in mind. They use soft, dog-friendly tennis balls and prevent overexertion by controlling throw distance. Always supervise playtime to avoid accidents and ensure your dog enjoys fetch safely.



### Do Automatic Tennis Ball Launchers Require Batteries Or Charging?



Most automatic launchers are battery-operated or USB rechargeable. Battery life and charging time vary by model. Check product details for power requirements to ensure uninterrupted playtime and convenience during outdoor or indoor fetch sessions.



### Can These Launchers Be Used Indoors And Outdoors?



Yes, many tennis ball launchers work well both indoors and outdoors. Look for models with adjustable launch distances and soft balls to avoid damage inside. Outdoor use benefits from longer throw ranges and more open space for running.



### How Many Tennis Balls Come With Each Dog Ball Launcher?



Packages usually include 1 to 6 tennis balls, depending on the set. Some come with premium dog-safe balls designed for durability and safety. Extra balls ensure continuous play without frequent replacements or interruptions during fetch sessions.
