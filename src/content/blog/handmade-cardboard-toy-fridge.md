## Materials Needed

Making a handmade cardboard toy fridge is fun and simple. You just need a few basic materials. These materials help you build and decorate your toy fridge.

Gather the right tools and supplies before you start. This will make your project easier and more enjoyable.

### Cardboard Selection

Choose sturdy cardboard for your toy fridge. The cardboard should be thick enough to hold its shape. Corrugated cardboard works best because it is strong and easy to cut.

Look for cardboard that is clean and free of bends or holes. You can use old boxes from around the house or buy new sheets from a craft store.

### Cutting Tools

Using the right cutting tools helps you make clean cuts. This makes your cardboard pieces fit together well. Safety is important, so handle tools carefully.

* Craft knife or box cutter
* Metal ruler to guide cuts
* Scissors for small trims
* Cutting mat to protect surfaces
* Pencil for marking measurements

## Step-by-step Assembly

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=building-a-handmade-cardboard-toy-fridge-is-a-fun-and-simple-project.-this-guide-will-help-you-create-a-small-fridge-using-cardboard-pieces.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Building a handmade cardboard toy fridge is a fun and simple project. This guide will help you create a small fridge using cardboard pieces.

Follow these steps carefully to cut, build, and add details to your toy fridge.

### Cutting The Pieces

Start by gathering sturdy cardboard sheets. Use a ruler and pencil to mark the shapes you need.

Cut out the main parts: front, back, sides, top, bottom, doors, and shelves. Make sure all pieces are straight and smooth.

* Front and back panels of equal size
* Two side panels matching height and depth
* Top and bottom panels matching width and depth
* Two doors slightly smaller than the front panel
* Shelves sized to fit inside the frame

### Constructing The Frame

Use glue or tape to join the front, back, and side panels. This forms the fridge’s box shape.

Attach the top and bottom panels to complete the frame. Check that all edges line up well.

* Glue sides to front and back panels
* Add bottom panel and press firmly
* Attach top panel and let dry
* Ensure the frame is sturdy and square

### Attaching Doors And Shelves

Fix the doors to the front panel using tape or small paper hinges. Make sure they open and close smoothly.

Place shelves inside the frame. Use small strips of cardboard glued on the sides to hold shelves in place.

* Attach doors with tape or paper hinges
* Check doors open freely
* Glue small strips inside the frame for shelves
* Place shelves on strips firmly

## Safety Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-a-handmade-cardboard-toy-fridge-is-fun-but-needs-care.-safety-is-important-to-keep-kids-safe-and-enjoy-the-project.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making a handmade cardboard toy fridge is fun but needs care. Safety is important to keep kids safe and enjoy the project.

Follow these safety tips for handling tools, using safe materials, and supervising children during the project.

### Handling Tools

Use tools carefully to avoid injuries. Teach children to handle scissors and cutters under adult guidance.

* Always cut away from your body
* Keep blades sharp for clean cuts
* Store tools out of children's reach
* Use child-safe scissors for young kids
* Work on a stable surface to prevent slips

### Using Non-toxic Materials

Choose materials that are safe for children. Avoid paints, glues, or markers with harmful chemicals.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Material | Safety Tip |
| Glue | Use non-toxic, washable glue |
| Paint | Pick water-based, non-toxic paint |
| Markers | Choose child-safe markers |
| Cardboard | Use clean, smooth cardboard without sharp edges |

### Supervision Guidelines

Always watch children while they make the toy fridge. Help with tricky parts and keep the area safe.

* Stay close during cutting and gluing
* Explain safety rules clearly before starting
* Keep small parts away from very young children
* Encourage calm, focused work
* Clean up tools and materials after use

## Cleanup And Storage

Keeping your handmade cardboard toy fridge clean helps it last longer. Proper storage keeps all parts safe and easy to find.

Simple cleanup and good storage habits protect your toy fridge from damage.

### Keeping The Fridge Neat

Wipe the fridge gently with a dry cloth to remove dust. Avoid using water or wet cloths as cardboard can get soggy.

If there are small stains, use a soft eraser carefully. Keep the fridge in a clean, dry place to prevent dirt buildup.

### Storing Small Parts

Many toy fridges have small cardboard parts like shelves or magnets. Keep these parts organized to avoid losing them.

* Use small containers or zip bags for tiny pieces
* Label containers for easy identification
* Store parts inside the fridge or a box

### Maintaining Durability

Keep the fridge away from moisture and direct sunlight. Both can weaken the cardboard and cause colors to fade.

Handle the fridge gently to avoid bending or tearing. Fix small tears with glue to keep it sturdy.
