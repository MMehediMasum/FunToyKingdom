## Preparing The Coin Slot

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-a-coin-slot-is-an-important-part-of-your-toy-trash-can-coin-box.-it-lets-coins-go-inside-easily.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making a coin slot is an important part of your toy trash can coin box. It lets coins go inside easily. You need to make sure the slot is the right size and shape.

Careful preparation helps the coin box work well. It also keeps coins safe inside.

### Choose The Right Location

Pick a flat area on the toy trash can to cut the coin slot. The spot should be easy to reach. It must be strong enough to hold coins.

Usually, the top or near the lid is best. This lets coins drop inside without trouble.

### Measure The Coin Slot Size

Measure the size of the coins you want to use. The slot should be a little bigger than the largest coin. This helps coins fit inside without getting stuck.

* Use a ruler to measure coin width
* Mark a slot size about 1-2 mm wider
* Check that the slot is not too big

### Mark The Slot Shape

Draw the shape of the slot on the trash can. Usually, a narrow rectangle works best for coins. Use a pencil or marker for clear lines.

Make sure your marks are straight and centered. This helps when cutting the slot.

### Cut The Coin Slot Carefully

Use a small saw or craft knife to cut along your marks. Cut slowly and carefully. This prevents mistakes or damage.

Wear safety gloves if needed. Smooth the edges of the slot with sandpaper so coins slide in easily.

* Cut slowly to avoid cracks
* Sand edges to remove sharp parts
* Test slot with coins for fit
