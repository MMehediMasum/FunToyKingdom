## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Used In Puppy Rope Toys?



Puppy rope toys are typically made from 100% natural cotton or cotton-poly blends. These materials are safe, durable, and help clean teeth during chewing. They are non-toxic and gentle on puppies’ gums, making them ideal for teething and interactive play.



### How Do Rope Toys Benefit Puppy Dental Health?



Rope toys help clean puppies’ teeth by flossing and massaging gums as they chew. This reduces plaque and tartar buildup. Regular chewing on these toys promotes healthier teeth and fresher breath, supporting overall oral hygiene for growing puppies.



### Are These Rope Toys Suitable For Aggressive Chewers?



Many puppy rope toys, like the Mammoth Flossy Chews and SZEE ropes, are designed to withstand aggressive chewing. They use durable, tightly braided cotton fibers that resist shredding, making them long-lasting options for puppies who chew intensely.



### Can Rope Toys Be Used For Interactive Play?



Yes, rope toys are perfect for interactive games like tug-of-war. They encourage bonding between you and your puppy while providing physical and mental stimulation. Toys with knots and sturdy construction work best for safe, fun play sessions.



### Which Rope Toy Size Fits Small Breed Puppies Best?



For small breeds, choose smaller, lightweight rope toys such as the KONG Puppy Goodie Bone or Depets 4PCs set. These toys are sized to fit small mouths comfortably and are gentle on delicate teeth during teething stages.



### How Often Should I Replace My Puppy’s Rope Toy?



Replace rope toys when they show signs of fraying or damage. Worn-out toys can pose choking hazards or cause injury. Regularly inspect toys to ensure safety, especially if your puppy is an aggressive chewer or uses them daily.
