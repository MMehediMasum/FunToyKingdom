## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Range Is Ideal For Infant Teething Toys?



Infant teething toys are best suited for babies from 0 to 18 months. Different toys target specific developmental stages. For example, softer silicone teethers suit newborns, while more textured options are good for older infants. Age-appropriate toys help soothe sore gums effectively and promote sensory development.



### Are Silicone Teething Toys Safe For Babies?



Yes, silicone teething toys are safe when BPA-free and food-grade certified. They are gentle on sore gums and easy to clean. Silicone teethers like Nuby Ice Gel and Frida Baby 4-in-1 offer durability and hygiene, making them ideal for infants' teething needs.



### How Do Teething Mittens Prevent Toy Dropping?



Teething mittens have wristbands that secure the toy to the baby's hand. This prevents dropping and keeps the teether clean. Products like the 2-pack Food Grade Silicone Teething Mitten provide comfort and ease for babies aged 0-12 months, supporting healthy chewing habits.



### Can Teething Toys Aid Sensory Development?



Yes, Montessori sensory teething toys stimulate touch, sight, and coordination. They enhance infants' learning and motor skills from 0 to 12 months. Toys with varied textures and colors promote cognitive growth while soothing teething discomfort.



### What Materials Are Best For Infant Teething Toys?



Food-grade silicone and BPA-free plastics are best for safety and hygiene. These materials are non-toxic, durable, and easy to sterilize. Popular toys like Dr. Brown's Ridgees Giraffe and Baby Einstein Teether-pillar use these safe materials for infant use.



### How Do Teething Toys Relieve Gum Pain?



Teething toys provide gentle pressure on inflamed gums, reducing discomfort. Cooling teethers like silicone freezable options numb gums for extra relief. Regular chewing on safe teethers soothes pain and distracts babies during teething phases.
