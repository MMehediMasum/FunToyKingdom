## Safety Standards For Infant Plush Toys

Infant plush toys must be safe for babies to play with. Parents should know the safety standards for these toys.

Understanding materials and hazards helps keep infants safe. This guide covers important safety points.

### Materials To Avoid

Some materials can harm infants if used in plush toys. Avoid hard plastics or metals inside the toy.

Loose stuffing or sharp parts can also cause injury. Choose plush toys made from soft, safe materials only.

* Hard plastic pieces
* Metal parts or wires
* Loose stuffing or beads

### Choking Hazards

Infants put toys in their mouths. Small parts can break off and cause choking.

Toys must not have buttons, eyes, or decorations that can come loose easily.

* No small detachable parts
* Strongly sewn eyes and noses
* No beads or buttons

### Non-toxic Fabrics And Dyes

Infants often chew on plush toys. Non-toxic fabrics and dyes keep them safe from poisons.

Look for toys labeled as free from harmful chemicals like lead or phthalates.

* Use hypoallergenic fabrics
* Avoid toxic dyes and paints
* Check for safety certifications

## Tips For Cleaning And Maintaining Plush Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=plush-animal-toys-are-soft-and-cuddly-friends-for-infants.-keeping-them-clean-is-important-for-health-and-safety.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Plush animal toys are soft and cuddly friends for infants. Keeping them clean is important for health and safety. Proper care helps the toys last longer.

Learn simple ways to wash, dry, and store plush toys. These tips keep them fresh and safe for babies to enjoy.

### Washing Techniques

Check the toy’s label before washing. Some plush toys can go in the washing machine. Others need hand washing to stay safe.

Use mild detergent to avoid skin irritation for infants. Wash toys in cold or warm water to keep their shape and color.

* For machine wash: place toy in a pillowcase or laundry bag.
* Use gentle cycle and mild detergent.
* For hand wash: soak toy in soapy water, then gently scrub.
* Rinse well to remove all soap.
* Spot clean stains with a soft cloth and mild soap if needed.

### Drying Tips

Dry plush toys fully to prevent mold and smells. Avoid high heat because it can damage the fabric and stuffing.

Air drying is best. Lay the toy flat on a clean towel. Turn it often to dry all sides evenly.

* Do not use a dryer unless the label says it is safe.
* If using a dryer, choose low heat and add dryer balls.
* Keep the toy away from direct sunlight to avoid color fading.
* Make sure the toy is completely dry before giving it back to the infant.

### Storage Recommendations

Store plush toys in a clean, dry place to keep them fresh. Avoid damp areas where mold can grow.

Use breathable containers like fabric bins or open baskets. This helps air flow and prevents dust build-up.

* Keep toys away from pets to avoid damage.
* Regularly check toys for dirt and clean as needed.
* Remove any loose parts or broken pieces to keep toys safe.
* Rotate toys so infants always have clean ones to play with.
