## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-outdoor-space-shuttle-sprinkler-toy-in-good-shape-helps-it-last-longer.-regular-care-stops-issues-before-they-start.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Outdoor Space Shuttle Sprinkler Toy in good shape helps it last longer. Regular care stops issues before they start.

Follow simple steps to clean, store, and protect your sprinkler. This keeps it working well each season.

### Cleaning And Storage

After use, rinse the sprinkler with clean water. This removes dirt and debris that can clog holes.

* Use a soft cloth to wipe the surface dry.
* Check for stuck dirt inside the spray nozzles.
* Store in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.
* Keep it in a sealed container or bag to avoid dust.

### Preventing Damage

Handle the sprinkler gently. Avoid dropping or bending parts, which can cause cracks or leaks.

| Damage Type | Cause | Prevention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cracks | Dropping or hitting hard surfaces | Place on soft ground and handle with care |
| Leaks | Bent or broken nozzles | Inspect nozzles regularly and avoid twisting |
| Mold Growth | Wet storage | Dry completely before storing |

### Seasonal Care

Winter can harm your sprinkler. Drain all water before cold weather comes.

1. Disconnect the sprinkler from the hose.
2. Hold it upside down to empty water inside.
3. Store indoors away from freezing temperatures.
