## Tips For Safe Use

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-an-outdoor-scooter-sprinkler-inflatable-can-be-fun-for-kids.-safety-is-very-important-to-keep-playtime-safe-and-happy.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using an outdoor scooter sprinkler inflatable can be fun for kids. Safety is very important to keep playtime safe and happy.

Follow simple rules to avoid injuries and keep the inflatable in good condition. These tips help parents and caregivers provide a safe play space.

### Supervision And Age Appropriateness

Always watch children while they play on the inflatable. Make sure the toy fits the child’s age and size to prevent accidents.

* Only allow children in the recommended age range to use the inflatable.
* Keep an adult nearby to supervise play at all times.
* Limit the number of children on the inflatable to avoid crowding.
* Teach children to wait their turn and play gently.

### Proper Inflation And Maintenance

Check the inflatable for proper inflation before use. Too much or too little air can cause damage or unsafe conditions.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check | What to Look For | Action Needed |
| Inflation Level | Firm but not hard | Add or release air as needed |
| Leaks or Holes | Visible tears or air loss | Patch or repair before use |
| Anchoring | Secure to the ground | Use stakes or weights |

### Avoiding Slips And Falls

Wet surfaces can be slippery. Use these tips to help children stay safe while running and playing.

* Place the inflatable on flat, soft ground like grass.
* Keep the surrounding area clear of rocks and sharp objects.
* Use non-slip mats or towels near the entrance and exit.
* Remind children to walk, not run, around the inflatable.
* Dry the inflatable and ground if it becomes too slippery.
