## Maintenance And Care Tips

Proper care keeps your ride on toy convertible with roof safe and fun. Regular maintenance extends its life and performance.

Follow these simple tips to clean, store, and maintain the battery. Learn easy fixes for common problems.

### Cleaning And Storage

Clean the toy after each use to prevent dirt buildup. Use a soft cloth and mild soap with water.

* Wipe down plastic parts and roof gently
* Dry completely before storage to avoid rust
* Store indoors in a dry, cool place
* Cover with a cloth or plastic sheet if stored outside

### Battery Maintenance

Check the battery regularly to keep the toy running well. Charge the battery fully before first use.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Battery Care Tip | Details |
| Charge Time | 8-12 hours for full charge |
| Storage | Keep battery cool and dry |

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the toy does not start or stops suddenly, check these points first.

* Ensure the battery is fully charged
* Check all wiring connections for looseness
* Make sure the power switch is on
* Inspect tires for obstructions or damage
* Reset the toy by turning it off and on again




## Safety Tips For Kids

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=ride-on-toy-convertibles-with-roofs-are-fun-for-kids.-safety-is-important-while-they-play.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Ride on toy convertibles with roofs are fun for kids. Safety is important while they play. Here are some tips to ensure their safety.

These tips will help your child have a safe and enjoyable ride. Always pay attention to their safety needs.

### Proper Supervision

An adult should always supervise children when they ride. Watch them closely to prevent accidents.

Make sure the riding area is free from obstacles. This helps in avoiding collisions.

* Stay within arm's reach of the child
* Choose a safe, flat area for riding
* Ensure the toy's battery is charged properly

### Using Protective Gear

Protective gear is essential for safe riding. It reduces the risk of injury.

Always make sure your child wears a helmet. Knee and elbow pads add extra protection.

* Use a properly fitted helmet
* Wear knee and elbow pads
* Check gear regularly for damage

### Safe Riding Practices

Teach your child safe riding practices. This helps them understand how to ride responsibly.

Explain the importance of staying seated while riding. Show them how to use the toy correctly.

* Stay seated at all times
* Hold the steering wheel firmly
* Do not ride near streets or traffic
