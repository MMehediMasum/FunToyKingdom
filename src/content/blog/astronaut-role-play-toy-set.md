### Setting Up A Space Adventure

Start by creating a space-themed play area. Use blankets, pillows, and boxes as spaceship parts. Add stars or planets with paper or stickers.

* Choose a quiet spot with enough room to move.
* Set up your “space station” with toys and tools.
* Use a flashlight for space lighting effects.
* Include a timer to simulate mission countdowns.
* Play space sounds or music in the background.

### Role Play Scenarios To Try

Try different stories to keep play fresh. Each scenario encourages imagination and teaches teamwork or problem-solving.

1. Space Explorer: Discover new planets and collect samples.
2. Mission Control: Guide the astronaut through challenges from Earth.
3. Alien Encounter: Meet friendly aliens and learn their language.
4. Space Rescue: Save a stranded astronaut using teamwork.
5. Rocket Builder: Assemble the spaceship before takeoff.
