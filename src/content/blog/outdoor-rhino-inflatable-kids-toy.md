## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-outdoor-rhino-inflatable-kids-toy-in-good-shape-ensures-it-lasts-longer.-proper-care-helps-keep-it-safe-and-fun-for-children.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Outdoor Rhino Inflatable Kids Toy in good shape ensures it lasts longer. Proper care helps keep it safe and fun for children.

Follow simple steps for cleaning, storage, and fixing small issues. These tips make maintenance easy and effective.

### Cleaning Guidelines

Clean the inflatable regularly to avoid dirt build-up. Use mild soap and water for washing. Avoid harsh chemicals to protect the material.

* Use a soft cloth or sponge for cleaning.
* Rinse thoroughly with clean water.
* Dry completely before storing to prevent mold.
* Spot clean stains quickly to stop them from setting.

### Proper Storage

Store the inflatable in a dry, cool place away from direct sunlight. Folding it carefully helps avoid creases and damage.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Storage Tip** | **Reason** |
| Keep away from sharp objects | Prevents punctures and tears |
| Store in a sealed bag | Keeps dust and moisture out |
| Do not stack heavy items on top | Avoids deformation |
| Ensure it is fully dry before folding | Stops mold and mildew growth |

### Repairing Small Damages

Small holes or tears can be fixed quickly. Use a repair patch made for inflatables to keep the toy safe to use.

* Clean the damaged area well before repair.
* Cut a patch slightly larger than the hole.
* Apply adhesive evenly and press the patch firmly.
* Allow the glue to dry fully before inflating.
* Check the repair regularly for any leaks.
