## Trich Basics

### What Is Trich?

Trich, short for Trichomoniasis, is a common infection. It is caused by a tiny parasite called Trichomonas vaginalis. This parasite lives in the human body, mostly in the genital area. It can cause itching, burning, and unusual discharge. Some people do not show symptoms but can still spread it.

### Common Transmission Methods

Trich spreads mainly through sexual contact. The parasite moves from one person to another during sex. It can also spread through shared items, like towels or toys. The parasite survives outside the body for a short time. Cleaning and avoiding sharing personal items help reduce risk.
