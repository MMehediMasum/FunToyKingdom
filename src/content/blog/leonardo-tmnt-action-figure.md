Leonardo TMNT action figures need proper care to stay in good shape. Regular cleaning and correct storage can make them last longer.

Dust and dirt can collect on your action figure. Cleaning it regularly keeps it looking fresh and prevents damage.

### Storage Solutions

Storing your Leonardo TMNT figure properly keeps it safe from damage. Choose a good spot for your collection.

* Store in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.
* Use display cases to keep dust away.
* Keep figures upright to prevent bending.
* Avoid stacking heavy items on top of the figure.
