## How To Use Your Koala Inflatable Pool Toy

The Koala Inflatable Pool Toy is a fun way to enjoy the water. It is easy to set up and use for kids and adults alike.

This guide will help you set up, supervise, and take care of your koala pool toy for safe and lasting use.

### Setup Tips For Quick Fun

Find a flat area near your pool or in the backyard. Make sure the space is clear of sharp objects.

Inflate the koala toy slowly to avoid damage. Use an air pump for faster inflation.

* Check the toy for holes before inflating
* Keep the valve closed tightly after inflation
* Place the toy in shallow water for easy access

### Best Practices For Supervision

Always watch children when they play with the inflatable toy. Never leave them alone near water.

Make sure an adult is nearby to help if needed. Keep flotation devices close as extra safety.

* Stay within arm’s reach of young children
* Set clear rules for safe play
* Remove the toy from water when not in use

### Maintenance And Cleaning

Rinse the koala toy with fresh water after use. This removes chlorine and dirt that can cause damage.

Dry the toy completely before storing. Keep it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

* Use mild soap for cleaning if needed
* Check for leaks regularly and patch them quickly
* Store the toy folded to save space and prevent damage
