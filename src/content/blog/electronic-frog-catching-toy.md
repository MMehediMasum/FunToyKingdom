### How It Works

The toy has a motor that moves the frog’s tongue quickly. When the tongue hits a bug, the toy makes a snapping sound.

1. The frog waits in a resting position.
2. The motor pulls the tongue out fast.
3. The tongue hits the target (bug).
4. The toy plays a sound to show a catch.
5. The tongue retracts back to the frog’s mouth.
