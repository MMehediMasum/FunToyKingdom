**Choosing the best toys for your 23-month-old baby can feel overwhelming. You want something that sparks their curiosity, helps their growth, and keeps them happily engaged.**

## Choosing Safe Toys

Picking safe toys is important for a 23 month old baby. Safety helps protect your child from harm.

Parents should look for toys that fit the child’s age and are made from safe materials.

### Age-appropriate Materials

Toys must match the baby’s age to support safe play. Soft fabrics and smooth plastics work well.

* Choose toys labeled for toddlers or 1-3 years old.
* Look for rounded edges and no small parts.
* Avoid sharp or breakable materials.
* Wooden toys should be smooth and splinter-free.

### Non-toxic And Durable

Toys should be free from harmful chemicals. Durable toys last longer and stay safe.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material | Safety Feature | Durability |
| Silicone | Non-toxic, easy to clean | Very durable |
| Natural wood | Non-toxic finish | Long lasting |
| Food-grade plastic | Free from BPA and phthalates | Moderate durability |

### Choking Hazards To Avoid

Small parts can cause choking and must be avoided in toys for toddlers.

* Do not buy toys with pieces smaller than 1.25 inches.
* Avoid toys with loose buttons or beads.
* Check toys often for broken parts.
* Keep broken toys away from children.

## Tips For Toy Rotation

Toy rotation keeps playtime exciting for your 23-month-old baby. It also helps in developing various skills.

Switching toys allows children to explore different textures, shapes, and functions. This method can prevent boredom and promote learning.

### Maintaining Interest

Rotate toys every week to keep your child engaged. This makes old toys feel new again.

Observe which toys your child plays with the most. Keep these in rotation more often.

* Introduce a mix of toys, such as puzzles and blocks
* Include both indoor and outdoor toys
* Swap toys that have similar functions

### Encouraging Creativity

Offer toys that inspire creativity, like building blocks or art supplies. These types of toys help develop problem-solving skills.

Provide materials that let your child experiment. This can include crayons, paper, or even simple household items.

* Mix traditional toys with creative tools
* Encourage your child to create stories with toys
* Use everyday objects for imaginative play

### Organizing Playtime

Organize toys into bins or baskets. This makes it easy for your child to find and pick toys.

Label the storage with pictures or colors. This helps your child learn to clean up after playtime.

* Use clear bins for easy visibility
* Rotate storage locations to refresh interest
* Keep a variety of toys in each bin




## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Are The Safest Toys For 23-month-old Babies?

Safe toys for 23-month-olds are non-toxic, large enough to avoid choking, and free from sharp edges. Soft blocks, chunky puzzles, and sturdy push toys are ideal. Always check labels for age recommendations and avoid small parts to ensure your baby’s safety during play.

### Which Toys Help Develop Motor Skills At 23 Months?

Toys like stacking blocks, shape sorters, and ride-on cars enhance motor skills in 23-month-olds. These encourage hand-eye coordination, balance, and fine motor control. Choose toys that challenge your child while being easy to handle and engaging for effective skill development.

### How Do Toys Support Cognitive Growth In Toddlers?

Toys such as puzzles, interactive books, and cause-and-effect toys stimulate cognitive growth. They promote problem-solving, memory, and language skills. Selecting age-appropriate toys that encourage exploration and curiosity helps toddlers learn and develop critical thinking abilities naturally.

### Are Educational Toys Important For 23-month-old Babies?

Yes, educational toys are vital at 23 months. They foster learning through play, supporting language, motor, and social skills. Toys like alphabet blocks and musical instruments introduce concepts early, making learning fun and effective during this crucial developmental stage.

## Conclusion

Choosing the right toys helps your baby learn and grow every day. Toys that encourage movement and thinking are great for this age. Simple shapes, bright colors, and fun sounds keep toddlers interested. Playing with these toys also builds important skills like hand-eye coordination and problem-solving.

Remember to pick safe, age-appropriate toys for your child’s best experience. Enjoy watching your little one explore and discover through play. This time is special and full of learning. Keep the playtime fun and educational.
