Scrabble is a word game where each tile has a point value. Using high-value tiles well can increase your score.

Knowing how to place tiles like Q, Z, X, and J helps you get big points. Blank tiles also play a key role.

### Using Q, Z, X, And J Effectively

Q, Z, X, and J have the highest points in Scrabble. Using them on double or triple letter score squares raises your score.

Try to build words where these letters land on premium squares. Short words with these letters can still score high.

* Use Q in words like "qi" or "qat" to save tiles.
* Place Z on double or triple letter squares.
* X works well in short words like "ax" or "ox".
* J can add points in words like "jo" or "jab".

### Leveraging Blank Tiles Strategically

Blank tiles have no point value but can be any letter. Use them to complete difficult words or connect high-value tiles.

Save blank tiles for rare letters like Q or Z. They help you play these letters when no other options exist.

* Use blanks to form longer words for bonus points.
* Replace blanks early to free up space for new tiles.
* Plan ahead to use blanks on double or triple word scores.

Bonus squares can multiply your word or letter scores. Try to place your tiles on these squares whenever possible.

* Use triple word scores for long, high-value words

### Building Parallel Words

Playing words parallel to existing ones creates multiple new words at once. This can earn many points.

Check if your new word can form valid words with letters above or below it. This strategy uses the board well.

* Place words next to others to create two or more words
* Use short words to fit in tight spaces
* Ensure all new words formed are valid

### Balancing Offense And Defense

Scoring high is important, but blocking your opponent matters too. Avoid opening easy access to bonus squares.

Try to place words that limit your opponent’s options while giving you good scoring chances.

* Watch where you place tiles to block bonus squares
* Keep some good letters to defend on later turns
* Balance scoring big and stopping your opponent
