## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Toys For Infant Sensory Development?



The best infant sensory toys include high-contrast black and white items, soft textured books, and musical rattles. These stimulate sight, touch, and hearing, aiding brain growth for ages 0-12 months. Montessori and developmental toys like the URMYWO and Fisher-Price playmat are highly recommended.



### How Do Teething Toys Benefit Babies 0-18 Months?



Teething toys soothe sore gums and encourage healthy chewing habits. They improve hand-mouth coordination and keep babies engaged. Items like Baby Teether Teething Toys are safe and BPA-free, making them ideal for infants from 0 to 18 months to relieve discomfort.



### Which Toys Promote Tummy Time For Newborns?



Tummy time toys like high-contrast black and white items and soft rattles encourage babies to lift their heads. Toys such as URMYWO Tummy Time Toy and sensory caterpillar plush support muscle development and visual stimulation for newborns aged 0-6 months.



### What Are Good Developmental Toys For 6-12 Month Babies?



For 6-12 months, stacking rings, musical toys, and easy-to-grasp balls are excellent. Sassy Stacks of Circles and Bright Starts Oball help improve motor skills, coordination, and cognitive development, preparing babies for crawling and walking stages.



### Can Infant Toys Be Suitable As Baby Shower Gifts?



Yes, infant developmental toys make perfect shower gifts. Sets like iPlay iLearn rattles, Infinno wrist rattles, and sensory books are popular choices. They are practical, safe, and encourage early learning for babies from newborn to 12 months.



### How Do Musical Toys Aid Infant Development?



Musical toys develop auditory skills, rhythm, and cause-effect understanding. Toys like Fisher-Price Glow and Grow Piano Gym engage babies with lights and sounds, supporting sensory and motor skill growth from birth to 12 months.



## Conclusion



Choosing the right developmental toys helps your baby grow skills like coordination, senses, and thinking. Toys like rattles, teethers, and stacking rings encourage babies to explore and learn safely. High-contrast toys and soft books support early vision and touch development.
