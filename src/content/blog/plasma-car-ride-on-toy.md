A Plasma Car is a fun ride-on toy for kids. It moves without pedals or batteries.

### Design And Features

The Plasma Car has a unique design that makes it easy to use. It has a low seat and wide base.

* Made from durable plastic for safety
* Four wheels for stability
* Simple steering handle for control
* Lightweight and easy to carry
* No batteries or pedals needed

### How It Works

The Plasma Car moves when you turn the steering handle left and right. This action creates forward motion.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Action | Effect |

| Turn handle right | Car moves forward |
