### Transformation Process

The transformation process moves different parts step-by-step. Motors and gears work together to change the toy’s form.

1. The car’s front splits and folds out to form the robot’s chest.
2. The wheels rotate and tuck in to become the robot’s shoulders.
3. The sides of the car extend to create the robot’s arms.
4. The rear part shifts forward to make the robot’s legs.
5. Finally, the head rises up from the car roof to complete the robot look.

### Mechanical Design

The toy uses small hinges and joints for moving parts. These allow smooth folding and unfolding during change.
