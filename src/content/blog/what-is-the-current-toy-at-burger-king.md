### How To Store Toys

Store toys in a cool, dry place to avoid damage. Use clear plastic containers to keep them dust-free. Keep toys away from sunlight to prevent colors from fading. Separate toys with soft cloth or bubble wrap to stop scratches. Label each container to find toys quickly.

### Trading And Selling

Find other collectors to trade toys and complete your set. Always check the toy’s condition before trading or selling. Use social media groups or collector websites to connect with buyers. Set a fair price based on the toy’s rarity and condition. Be honest about any damage to keep trust.

### Spotting Authentic Toys

Look for official Burger King logos on the packaging and toy. Check the quality of plastic and paint for signs of authenticity. Fake toys often have rough edges or poor details. Compare your toy with images from the official Burger King site. Keep the original packaging to prove the toy is real.
