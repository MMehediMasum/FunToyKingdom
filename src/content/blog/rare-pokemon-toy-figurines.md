## Identifying Authentic Figurines

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collecting-rare-pokémon-toy-figurines-is-exciting.-knowing-how-to-spot-authentic-ones-helps-you-avoid-fakes.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collecting rare Pokémon toy figurines is exciting. Knowing how to spot authentic ones helps you avoid fakes.

This guide explains key signs to check if a figurine is genuine or counterfeit.

### Official Markings And Stamps

Authentic figurines have clear markings from the manufacturer. These include logos, copyright dates, and model numbers.

* Look for the official Pokémon or Nintendo logo.
* Check for a copyright year under the figurine.
* Find the manufacturer's name or initials.
* Examine the quality of the stamp—blurred marks may mean fake.

### Packaging Clues

Original packaging helps prove a toy’s authenticity. Pay attention to print quality and details on the box.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Packaging Feature | Authentic Signs |
| Print Quality | Sharp, clear images and text |
| Holographic Stickers | Present and well-placed |
| Barcodes | Valid and correctly formatted |
| Box Material | Sturdy and smooth finish |

### Common Counterfeits

Fake figurines often have poor paint jobs and wrong colors. They may miss official marks or have spelling errors.

**Signs of Fake Figurines:**

* Rough or uneven paint
* Wrong or faded colors
* Missing logos or stamps
* Spelling mistakes on packaging
* Light or cheap-feeling plastic
