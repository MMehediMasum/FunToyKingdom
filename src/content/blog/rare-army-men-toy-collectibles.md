## Origins Of Army Men Toys

Army men toys have been popular for many decades. They are small plastic figures shaped like soldiers. Kids and collectors enjoy them for their detail and play value.

These toys show different military poses and gear. Their history goes back to the early 20th century. Let’s explore how they started and who made them.

### Early Designs And Materials

The first army men toys were made from metal. Lead was the main material used before plastic became common. These metal figures were heavy and fragile.

In the 1930s, manufacturers switched to plastic for safety and cost. Plastic made the toys lighter and easier to produce. Early plastic figures were simple in design but captured soldier poses well.

* Metal figures were detailed but fragile.
* Lead was the common metal before plastics.
* Plastic introduced in the 1930s made toys safer.
* Early plastic toys had fewer details but were durable.
