## Iconic Toy Story Characters

Toy Story has many beloved characters that fans enjoy. Each toy has a unique personality and story.

Toy Story plush dolls bring these characters to life. They make great toys and collectibles.

### Woody: The Cowboy Hero

Woody is the brave cowboy and leader of the toys. He is loyal and always helps his friends.

Woody's signature look includes a cowboy hat, vest, and boots. He carries a friendly smile.

### Buzz Lightyear: Space Ranger

Buzz Lightyear is a fearless space ranger. He believes he is on a mission to save the galaxy.

His suit is white and green with purple accents. Buzz has wings that pop out when he flies.

### Jessie: The Yodeling Cowgirl

Jessie is a cheerful cowgirl with a big heart. She loves to sing and yodel with friends.

She wears a red hat and yellow shirt. Jessie is brave and always ready for adventure.

### Bullseye: The Loyal Steed

Bullseye is Woody's faithful horse. He is fast, strong, and loves to play.

Bullseye has big eyes and a friendly face. He is always by Woody's side.

## Tips For Maintaining Plush Dolls

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=plush-dolls-from-toy-story-bring-joy-and-comfort.-keeping-them-clean-and-safe-helps-them-last-longer.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Plush dolls from Toy Story bring joy and comfort. Keeping them clean and safe helps them last longer.

Follow easy care tips to maintain their soft texture and bright colors.

### Cleaning Techniques

Regular cleaning removes dust and dirt. Spot cleaning works well for small stains.

* Use a soft cloth with mild soap and water
* Gently dab the stained area without soaking
* Let the doll air dry completely before use
* Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage fabric

### Storage Solutions

Proper storage keeps plush dolls safe from dust and damage. Use breathable containers.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Method | Benefits |
| Cloth bags | Protect from dust and allow air flow |
| Plastic bins with holes | Keep dolls dry but prevent mold |
| Open shelves | Easy access but needs regular dusting |

### Handling And Preservation

Handle dolls gently to keep their shape and softness. Avoid pulling on limbs or accessories.

Tips for handling:

* Hold the doll by its body, not limbs
* Keep away from pets and small children
* Store away from direct sunlight to prevent fading
* Check for loose parts and repair promptly
