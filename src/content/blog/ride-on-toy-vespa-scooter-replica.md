### Battery Care

Charge the battery fully before the first use. Avoid letting the battery drain completely to keep it healthy. Use the charger that came with the scooter only.

* Charge the battery for 8-12 hours before first use.
* Do not overcharge; unplug when full.
* Store battery in a cool, dry place.
* Check battery connections monthly for tightness.
* Replace the battery if it holds charge poorly.

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the scooter does not start, check the battery and connections first. Loose wires can cause problems. Below are common issues and fixes.

* **Scooter won’t start:**Charge battery fully and check wires.
* **Slow speed:**Clean wheels and check for obstacles.
* **No sound:**Check speaker connections and volume settings.
* **Stuck throttle:**Clean throttle area and test movement.
* **Battery won’t charge:**Try a different charger or replace battery.
