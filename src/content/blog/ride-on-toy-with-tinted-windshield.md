Tinted windshields provide useful features for ride on toys. They help protect from sunlight and improve the riding experience.

Tinted windshields reduce the amount of sunlight that reaches the rider. This helps protect sensitive eyes and skin from harmful UV rays.

* Keeps the rider cooler on sunny days
* Prevents sunburn on the face and hands

Tinted windshields can improve visibility by cutting down glare from the sun or reflective surfaces. This helps kids see the path clearly.

## Maintenance Tips For Longevity

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-ride-on-toy-in-good-shape-helps-it-last-longer.-simple-care-steps-protect-its-parts-and-keep-it-working-well.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your ride on toy in good shape helps it last longer. Simple care steps protect its parts and keep it working well.

Follow these tips for cleaning, battery care, and regular checks. They will make your toy safer and more fun to use.

### Cleaning The Tinted Windshield

Clean the tinted windshield with a soft cloth and mild soap. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the tint.

* Use a damp microfiber cloth to wipe the surface gently.
* Rinse with clean water and dry with a soft towel.
* Do not use abrasive sponges or paper towels.
* Clean regularly to prevent dust and dirt buildup.

### Battery Care

Proper battery care keeps your ride on toy running longer. Charge the battery fully before first use and after each play.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Do | Do Not |
| Charge battery in a dry place | Overcharge the battery overnight |
| Use the charger that comes with the toy | Use a different or damaged charger |
| Disconnect battery if not in use for weeks | Leave battery connected for long storage |
| Check battery terminals for corrosion | Ignore corrosion or loose connections |

### Regular Inspections

Look over your ride on toy often to spot wear or damage. Fix small problems before they get worse.

* Check wheels and axles for smooth movement.
* Make sure screws and bolts are tight.
* Test brakes and steering if your toy has them.
* Inspect the body for cracks or broken parts.
* Replace worn-out parts quickly to avoid accidents.

## Safety Tips For Kids

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=ride-on-toys-with-tinted-windshields-are-fun-for-children.-safety-is-very-important-when-kids-play-with-these-toys.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Ride on toys with tinted windshields are fun for children. Safety is very important when kids play with these toys.

Parents and caregivers must follow safety tips to keep children safe while riding.

### Supervision Guidelines

Always watch children closely when they use ride on toys. Stay near to help if needed. Never leave kids alone while riding.

* Check the area is free from traffic and obstacles.
* Make sure the ground is flat and smooth.
* Set clear rules for riding time and place.
* Teach kids to stop riding if they feel unsafe.

### Proper Use Of Safety Gear

Safety gear protects kids from injuries. Always use helmets and pads when riding.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Safety Gear | Purpose |
| Helmet | Protects head from impact |
| Knee Pads | Shields knees from scrapes |
| Elbow Pads | Guards elbows during falls |
| Closed-Toe Shoes | Protects feet and toes |

### Safe Riding Practices

Teach children how to ride safely. Show them how to start and stop gently.

Follow these safe riding rules:

1. Ride only in safe, open areas.
2. Keep both hands on the steering wheel.
3. Do not stand while riding the toy.
4. Stop riding when the toy battery is low.
5. Do not ride near stairs or pools.
