## Care And Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=sensory-tags-blanket-toys-need-gentle-care-to-keep-them-safe-and-clean.-proper-care-helps-them-last-longer-and-stay-soft.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Sensory tags blanket toys need gentle care to keep them safe and clean. Proper care helps them last longer and stay soft.

Follow these tips to wash, handle, and store your sensory tags blanket toys the right way.

### Washing Instructions

Wash sensory tags blanket toys by hand or on a gentle machine cycle. Use cold water and mild detergent only.

Avoid bleach and fabric softeners. They can damage the fabric and tags.

* Use a laundry bag for extra protection in the machine
* Air dry flat to keep the shape
* Do not iron the tags

### Handling Wear And Tear

Check sensory tags often for loose threads or tears. Fix small issues quickly to stop bigger damage.

Gently pull on tags to avoid ripping. Teach children to play carefully with the toy.

* Trim loose threads with scissors
* Sew small tears with a needle and thread
* Replace damaged tags if needed

### Storage Recommendations

Store sensory tags blanket toys in a clean, dry place. Keep them away from direct sunlight to stop fading.

Use breathable storage bags or containers to avoid moisture buildup. This keeps the toys fresh and safe.

* Do not store in plastic bags for long periods
* Keep away from pets and small children when not in use
* Fold gently to avoid crushing the tags
