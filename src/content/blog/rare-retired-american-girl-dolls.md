## Authenticity And Verification

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=rare-retired-american-girl-dolls-are-valuable-to-collectors.-it-is-important-to-check-if-a-doll-is-real-before-buying.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Rare retired American Girl dolls are valuable to collectors. It is important to check if a doll is real before buying.

Verifying authenticity helps protect your investment and avoid fakes. This guide explains how to identify genuine dolls.

### Identifying Genuine Dolls

Look closely at the doll’s face, clothes, and markings. Original dolls have clear details and high-quality materials.

Check the doll’s body for proper seams and joints. Genuine dolls feel sturdy and well-made.

* Face mold matches official American Girl designs
* Clothing has neat stitching and correct fabric
* Body has manufacturer marks or stamps
* Hair is rooted or styled as per the original

### Common Counterfeits

Fake dolls often have cheap materials and sloppy details. Faces might look blurry or different from originals.

Clothing on counterfeit dolls may use wrong fabrics or colors. The body might feel light or poorly made.

* Blurry or uneven facial paint
* Incorrect clothing styles or fabrics
* Missing official markings or stamps
* Loose joints and flimsy parts

### Using Certificates And Documentation

Original American Girl dolls often come with certificates of authenticity. These documents confirm the doll’s origin.

Look for original boxes, manuals, or receipts. These help prove the doll is genuine and increase its value.

* Certificate of authenticity with matching doll details
* Original packaging with correct logos and labels
* Instruction booklets or care guides
* Purchase receipts or proof of sale

## Preserving Doll Value

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=rare-retired-american-girl-dolls-can-keep-their-value-for-years.-careful-preservation-is-important-to-protect-your-investment.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Rare retired American Girl dolls can keep their value for years. Careful preservation is important to protect your investment.

Proper storage, cleaning, and handling help maintain a doll’s condition and worth.

### Storage Tips

Keep dolls in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Sunlight can fade fabric and plastic parts.

* Store dolls in acid-free boxes or breathable fabric bags.
* Use padded supports to keep dolls in shape and avoid creases.
* Avoid plastic bags that can trap moisture and cause mold.
* Keep dolls away from heat sources and humidity.

### Cleaning And Maintenance

Regular cleaning prevents dust buildup and preserves fabric quality. Use gentle methods to avoid damage.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Cleaning Method | Recommended Use |
| Soft brush | Remove dust from hair and fabric |
| Damp cloth | Wipe vinyl parts gently |
| Mild soap solution | Clean tough stains carefully |
| Air dry | Dry dolls naturally after cleaning |

### Handling Best Practices

Handle dolls with clean hands to avoid oils and dirt transferring. Support the doll fully to prevent joint stress.

**Tips for Handling:**

* Wash hands before touching dolls.
* Hold dolls by the torso, not limbs.
* Avoid excessive bending of arms and legs.
* Keep dolls away from food and liquids.
