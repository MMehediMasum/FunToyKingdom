## Setting Up Your Float

Setting up the Outdoor Cheetah Inflatable Kids Float is easy and fun. Follow simple steps to get it ready for your child’s playtime.

This guide covers inflation tips, the best locations to use the float, and safety precautions to keep in mind.

### Inflation Tips

Inflate the float on a clean, flat surface to avoid damage. Use a pump for faster and even inflation.

* Check all valves are closed before inflating.
* Inflate slowly to prevent overexpansion.
* Do not use high-pressure air sources.
* Make sure the float is firm but still soft to the touch.
* After inflation, close the valve tightly to avoid leaks.

### Best Locations For Use

### Safety Precautions

Keep your child safe by following these important safety rules when using the inflatable float.

* Always supervise children while they are using the float.
* Do not allow rough play or jumping on the float.

## Maintenance And Care

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-your-outdoor-cheetah-inflatable-kids-float-helps-it-last-longer.-proper-maintenance-keeps-it-clean-and-safe-for-kids.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of your Outdoor Cheetah Inflatable Kids Float helps it last longer. Proper maintenance keeps it clean and safe for kids.

Simple steps can protect the float from damage and wear. Follow these tips to enjoy the float for many seasons.

### Cleaning Techniques

Clean the float after each use to remove dirt and debris. Use mild soap and water for best results.

A soft cloth or sponge works well to avoid scratches on the surface. Rinse thoroughly with clean water.

* Mix mild soap with warm water
* Wipe the float gently with a soft cloth
* Rinse with fresh water to remove soap
* Let it air dry completely before storing

### Storage Advice

Store the float in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Sunlight can weaken the material over time.

Make sure the float is fully dry before folding it. This prevents mold and unpleasant odors.

* Keep away from sharp objects
* Fold gently without creasing
* Use a storage bag if available
* Avoid places with extreme heat or cold

### Repairing Minor Damages

Small holes or tears can be fixed easily to prevent air leaks. Use a patch kit made for inflatables.

Clean and dry the area before applying the patch. Press firmly and let it set according to instructions.

* Locate the hole or tear
* Clean the damaged area with soap and water
* Dry completely before repair
* Apply patch and press firmly
* Wait for the adhesive to cure fully
