## Choosing Safe Materials

Choosing the right teething toy is important for your baby’s safety. The materials used in the toy should not harm your newborn.

Safe materials help protect babies from allergies and toxins while they chew during teething.

### Non-toxic Ingredients

Teething toys made from non-toxic ingredients keep babies safe from harmful chemicals. Look for toys made with natural rubber, food-grade silicone, or untreated wood.

### Hypoallergenic Options

Babies can have sensitive skin or allergies. Hypoallergenic teething toys reduce the chance of skin irritation or allergic reactions.

* Choose toys labeled hypoallergenic
* Avoid materials like latex if your baby is allergic
* Test new toys on a small skin area first

### Bpa And Phthalate Free

BPA and phthalates are chemicals found in some plastics. They can harm your baby’s health. Always pick toys that clearly state they are free of these chemicals.

| **Chemical** | **Possible Harm** |
| --- | --- |
| BPA (Bisphenol A) | Hormone disruption and developmental issues |
| Phthalates | Respiratory problems and allergies |

## Tips For Using Teething Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=teething-toys-can-soothe-a-newborn's-gums.-using-them-correctly-ensures-safety-and-hygiene.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Teething toys can soothe a newborn's gums. Using them correctly ensures safety and hygiene.

Here are some tips to help you use teething toys effectively for your little one.

### Supervised Use

Always watch your baby when they use teething toys. This ensures they do not choke or hurt themselves.

* Stay close while your baby chews.
* Ensure the toy is not too small.
* Check for any broken parts before use.

### Cleaning And Storage

Clean teething toys regularly to keep germs away. Proper storage also matters.

* Wash with warm, soapy water after each use.
* Dry completely before storing.
* Store in a clean, dry place.

### When To Replace

Teething toys wear out over time. Know when to replace them for safety.

* Replace if there are cracks or tears.
* Check for loose parts regularly.
* Consider replacing every few months.




## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is The Best Teething Toy For Newborns?

The best teething toy is soft, safe, and BPA-free. It soothes gums and is easy to grip. Look for toys with different textures to stimulate babies’ senses and relieve discomfort effectively.

### When Should I Introduce Teething Toys To My Baby?

Introduce teething toys around 3 to 4 months old. This timing helps soothe early gum discomfort. Always supervise your baby when using teething toys to ensure safety and prevent choking hazards.

### How Do Teething Toys Help Newborns?

Teething toys provide relief by massaging sore gums. They distract babies from discomfort and promote healthy oral development. Using a teething toy can reduce fussiness and encourage sensory exploration safely.

### Are Silicone Teething Toys Safe For Newborns?

Yes, silicone teething toys are safe if BPA-free and non-toxic. They are soft, durable, and easy to clean. Silicone toys are gentle on gums and ideal for newborns during teething.
