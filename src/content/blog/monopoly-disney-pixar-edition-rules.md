## Basic Gameplay

Monopoly Disney Pixar Edition is a fun twist on the classic game. Players buy properties and collect rent to win.

The game follows simple rules to move around the board and take turns. Understanding these basics helps you play smoothly.

### Taking Turns

Each player takes a turn in order, going clockwise around the table. During a turn, a player rolls dice and moves their token.

After moving, the player may buy properties, pay rent, or follow instructions on the board. Then the next player takes their turn.

### Rolling The Dice

Players roll two dice at the start of their turn. The dice show numbers from one to six. The total moves the player’s token.

If a player rolls doubles, they get another turn. But rolling doubles three times in a row sends them to Jail.

* Roll two six-sided dice
* Add the numbers to find total steps
* Move token forward that many spaces
* Roll doubles for an extra turn
* Three doubles in a row sends you to Jail

### Moving Around The Board

Move your token forward the number rolled on the dice. Pass through spaces like properties, Chance, or Community Chest.

If you pass Go, collect $200. Landing on a property lets you buy it if available or pay rent if owned by another player.

* Move token forward the dice total
* Follow the instructions on the space landed
* Buy properties to build your collection
* Pay rent if property belongs to others
* Collect $200 when passing Go

## Property Rules

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=in-monopoly-disney-pixar-edition,-properties-are-key-to-winning.-you-buy,-build,-and-collect-rent-to-earn-money.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

In Monopoly Disney Pixar Edition, properties are key to winning. You buy, build, and collect rent to earn money.

Knowing the rules about properties helps you play better and make smart choices.

### Buying Properties

You can buy a property when you land on an unowned space. Pay the price listed on the board to the bank.

If you do not want to buy, the property goes to auction. Other players can bid to buy it.

* Landing on an unowned property lets you buy it at listed price.
* If you pass, the property is auctioned to the highest bidder.
* Buy properties to collect rent from other players later.

### Building Houses And Hotels

When you own all properties of a color, you can build houses. Houses increase rent value.

Build evenly across your color group. After four houses, you can build a hotel.

* Own all same-color properties to start building.
* Build one house on each before adding more.
* Four houses can be traded for a hotel.
* Hotels give the highest rent value.

### Collecting Rent

When another player lands on your property, they pay rent. Rent depends on buildings on the property.

Rent increases with houses and hotels. Some properties have special rent rules.

* Collect rent every time a player lands on your property.
* Rent grows as you add houses or hotels.
* Check the card for exact rent amounts.
