## Installation Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=installing-a-nest-swing-saucer-for-multiple-kids-takes-some-planning.-you-want-it-safe-and-fun-for-all-users.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Installing a nest swing saucer for multiple kids takes some planning. You want it safe and fun for all users. Use these tips to set it up correctly.

Pay attention to the location, anchoring, and height. These factors help keep the swing stable and enjoyable.

### Choosing The Best Location

Pick a spot with enough open space around the swing. Avoid areas near walls, trees, or fences. This prevents accidents and gives kids room to move.

* Choose flat ground to keep the swing steady.
* Make sure the area is free of sharp objects or debris.
* Select a shaded spot to keep the swing cool on sunny days.
* Check the surface; grass or soft ground is best.

### Proper Anchoring Methods

Secure anchoring is key for safety. Use strong hardware and support structures made for heavy loads. Attach the swing firmly to avoid movement or slipping.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Anchor Type** | **Best Use** | **Notes** |
| Ceiling Hook | Indoor or covered porch | Must be fastened to a strong beam |
| Tree Straps | Outdoor trees | Use wide straps to protect bark |
| Metal Frame | Open yards without trees | Ensure frame is rated for weight |
| Ground Anchors | Soft soil areas | Drive anchors deep for firm hold |

### Height And Clearance Guidelines

Set the swing at a safe height to avoid injuries. Allow enough clearance around and below the swing for free motion.

1. Hang the swing seat about 24 inches off the ground.
2. Keep at least 6 feet of clearance in front and behind the swing.
3. Allow 8 feet of clearance above the swing to avoid hitting overhead objects.
4. Check the swing’s ropes or chains for proper length and strength.
