## Setting Up Your Putting Course

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=creating-a-fun-putting-course-with-your-toy-golf-putting-set-can-be-simple-and-enjoyable.-this-guide-helps-you-set-up-the-perfect-course-indoors.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Creating a fun putting course with your toy golf putting set can be simple and enjoyable. This guide helps you set up the perfect course indoors. Follow easy tips to make your game exciting and safe.

Focus on space, challenge, and safety. Each part is important to have a great time playing.

### Indoor Space Tips

Choose a flat area with enough room to swing your putter. Clear the space from furniture and breakable items. Use a carpet or mat to keep balls from rolling too far.

* Pick a spot with good lighting
* Make sure the floor is even and smooth
* Leave at least 6 feet for putting strokes
* Use soft barriers to catch balls

### Creating Challenging Holes

Design holes that test your skills by adding slopes and obstacles. Change the hole distance to keep it interesting. Use ramps or small objects to create curves and angles.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Hole Feature | Description |
| Slope | Place a slight incline to add difficulty |
| Obstacle | Use cups or blocks to block the path |
| Distance | Adjust how far the ball must travel |
| Curves | Set up curved paths with ramps or books |

### Safety Precautions

Keep safety in mind while setting up and playing. Avoid sharp objects near the course. Make sure players do not stand too close to the ball’s path.

* Clear the area of fragile items
* Use soft balls if possible
* Keep putting strokes slow and controlled
* Supervise children during play
