## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Used In Infant Sloth Toys?



Infant sloth toys are made from soft plush fabrics, BPA-free plastic, and safe silicone teethers. These materials ensure safety and comfort for babies while providing gentle textures for tactile exploration and soothing during teething.



### Are These Sloth Toys Safe For Newborns?



Yes, these sloth toys are designed specifically for newborns. They use non-toxic materials, have no small parts, and meet safety standards, making them ideal for infants to play with and chew on safely.



### How Do Sloth Rattles And Teethers Benefit Babies?



Sloth rattles and teethers stimulate baby’s senses with soft sounds and textures. They promote motor skills, soothe sore gums, and encourage hand-eye coordination in newborns and infants.



### Can These Sloth Toys Be Used For Stroller Or Travel?



Yes, many sloth toys like Bright Starts Plush Playful Pals are portable and stroller-friendly. Their compact size and secure attachments make them perfect for on-the-go entertainment and comfort.



### What Age Range Suits These Sloth Baby Toys?



Most sloth baby toys are suitable from newborn to 12 months and beyond. They grow with the baby, supporting early development stages with safe teethers, rattles, and plush comfort.
