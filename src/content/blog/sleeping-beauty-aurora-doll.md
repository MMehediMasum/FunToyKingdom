## History Of Sleeping Beauty Aurora

Sleeping Beauty Aurora is a beloved character known worldwide. She has a rich history that spans centuries. Her story has inspired many books, movies, and toys.

This content explores Aurora's origin and her journey through popular culture. Understanding her history helps appreciate the doll's significance.

### Origin Of The Character

Aurora first appeared in classic fairy tales. The story’s roots trace back to old folk tales from Europe. The most famous version comes from Charles Perrault’s 1697 tale.

* She is a princess cursed to sleep for 100 years.
* The curse is broken by a prince’s kiss.
* The story teaches themes of good versus evil and hope.
* Disney adapted the story into an animated film in 1959.

### Evolution In Popular Culture

Aurora’s image changed over time with different media. She became a symbol of grace and kindness in many adaptations. The doll version reflects these traits.
