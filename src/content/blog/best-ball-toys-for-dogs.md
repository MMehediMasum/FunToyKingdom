Toys like the DISSKNIC Indestructible Squeaky Ball offer mental stimulation and chewing satisfaction. Chewing helps dogs release stress and anxiety naturally. These balls also clean teeth, making them multifunctional for both emotional and dental health.

Yes, balls like Chuckit! Ultra and Rugged Balls are designed to float. They make water fetch games more exciting and safe. These balls are easy to spot on water, encouraging swimming and active play for water-loving dogs.
