## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-your-rc-honda-civic-toy-car-ensures-it-runs-smoothly-and-lasts-longer.-regular-maintenance-helps-keep-the-car-in-top-condition.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of your RC Honda Civic toy car ensures it runs smoothly and lasts longer. Regular maintenance helps keep the car in top condition.

Follow these simple tips to maintain your RC car and enjoy endless fun driving it around.

### Battery Care

The battery is the heart of your RC car. It is important to charge it properly and store it correctly.

* Always use the charger that came with the car.
* Do not overcharge the battery.
* Store the battery in a cool, dry place.

### Cleaning And Storage

Keeping your RC car clean prevents dust and dirt from damaging parts. Proper storage also keeps it safe when not in use.

* Wipe the car with a damp cloth after use.
* Check for dirt in the wheels and axles.
* Store the car in a dry and dust-free place.

### Troubleshooting

If your RC car is not working, don't worry. Simple troubleshooting can often fix common problems.

* Check the battery connections.
* Ensure the remote control has fresh batteries.
* Look for loose wires or connections.
