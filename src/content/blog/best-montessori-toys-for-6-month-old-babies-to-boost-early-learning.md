## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Montessori Toys For 6-month-olds?



The best Montessori toys for 6-month-olds include stacking rings, sensory balls, and teething toys. These encourage motor skills and sensory development while being safe and engaging.



### How Do Stacking Ring Toys Benefit 6-month Babies?



Stacking ring toys enhance hand-eye coordination and fine motor skills. They also teach color recognition and problem-solving in a fun, interactive way.



### Are Sensory Toys Important For Infants Aged 6-12 Months?



Yes, sensory toys stimulate a baby’s senses like touch, sight, and sound. They promote cognitive growth and help babies explore their environment confidently.



### Can Teething Toys Improve A Baby’s Comfort?



Teething toys soothe sore gums and reduce discomfort. They also encourage safe chewing habits and help develop oral motor skills in infants.



### What Makes Montessori Toys Suitable For Baby Development?



Montessori toys focus on natural materials, simplicity, and open-ended play. They foster independence, creativity, and learning through hands-on experience for babies.



### Which Toys Are Ideal For Travel With 6-month-old Babies?



Compact, multi-functional toys like pull-string silicone teethers and stacking blocks are perfect for travel. They keep babies entertained and aid developmental milestones on-the-go.
