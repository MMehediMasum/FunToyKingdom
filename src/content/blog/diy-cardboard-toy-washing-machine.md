## Materials Needed

Building a DIY cardboard toy washing machine is a fun project. You will need simple materials that are easy to find.

This guide covers all the materials needed to create your toy washing machine model step by step.

### Cardboard And Tools

Cardboard is the main material for the toy washing machine. You will also need some basic tools to cut and shape it.

* Sturdy cardboard sheets (large enough for the washing machine body)
* Scissors or a craft knife
* Ruler for measuring
* Pencil for marking cut lines
* Glue stick or white glue
* Cutting mat or safe surface to cut on
* Paintbrush (optional, for glue application)

### Decorative Supplies

Decorative supplies help make your toy washing machine look real and colorful. Use simple items for decoration.

* Colored markers or crayons
* Colored paper or construction paper
* Stickers or printed labels to add details
* Clear tape to protect decorations
* Glue for attaching paper decorations

### Additional Components

Extra parts add realism and help your toy function better. These are optional but useful.

* Plastic bottle caps for buttons or knobs
* Small pieces of string or yarn
* Paper fasteners or brads for movable parts
* Transparent plastic sheet for the washing machine door
* Rubber bands to create spinning action




## Designing The Washing Machine

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-a-cardboard-toy-washing-machine-starts-with-the-design.-planning-the-parts-helps-build-a-fun-and-sturdy-toy.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making a cardboard toy washing machine starts with the design. Planning the parts helps build a fun and sturdy toy. Focus on size, shape, and details for a good look.

This guide covers three key design areas: the basic shape and size, door and window placement, and the control panel layout.

### Basic Shape And Size

Choose a simple box shape for the washing machine. It should be big enough for small hands to play with but not too large to handle. Think about the height, width, and depth before cutting the cardboard.

* Height: About 12 inches works well for kids.
* Width: Around 8 inches gives good space.
* Depth: Keep it near 8 inches for stability.
* Use thicker cardboard for strength.
* Mark edges clearly before cutting.

### Door And Window Placement

Place the door on the front side of the machine. A round door looks like a real washing machine. Add a clear window inside the door to see the clothes.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feature | Placement | Size |
| Door | Front center | 5 inches diameter |
| Window | Inside door | 4 inches diameter |
| Hinges | Right side of door | 1 inch wide |
| Handle | Left side of door | 1.5 inches long |

### Control Panel Layout

Design the control panel on top of the machine. It should have buttons and dials that kids can pretend to use. Keep the layout simple and clear.

**Control Panel Features:**

* Start button: big and easy to press
* Cycle dial: round and turns smoothly
* Water level switch: small slide switch
* Power indicator light: small red dot

## Adding Functional Details

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=adding-functional-details-to-your-diy-cardboard-toy-washing-machine-makes-it-more-fun.-it-helps-kids-pretend-to-use-a-real-machine.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Adding functional details to your DIY cardboard toy washing machine makes it more fun. It helps kids pretend to use a real machine.

Simple features like buttons, knobs, and a spinning drum bring the toy to life. These parts also teach how washing machines work.

### Simulating Buttons And Knobs

Use small cardboard circles or bottle caps to make buttons and knobs. Glue them on the front panel for easy reach.

Draw labels with a marker to show different settings. Kids can press or turn these to pretend they control the machine.

* Cut small circles or squares from cardboard
* Paint or color them for contrast
* Glue them firmly but allow slight movement
* Label with marker for fun and learning

### Crafting The Drum

The drum is the part that holds clothes. Use a cardboard tube or roll to make the drum shape.

Cover the tube with paper and decorate it to look like a real drum. Cut small holes or lines to add detail.

* Find a sturdy cardboard tube or roll
* Wrap with colored paper or paint
* Draw or cut small holes for texture
* Attach inside the machine body securely

### Incorporating A Spin Mechanism

Create a simple spin mechanism using a pencil or skewer as an axle. Insert it through the drum to allow rotation.

Secure the axle ends to the machine frame so the drum spins smoothly. This adds realistic movement to the toy.

* Use a pencil or wooden skewer for the axle
* Make holes on the sides of the machine for the axle
* Insert the drum onto the axle
* Check that the drum spins without wobbling

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Materials Are Needed For A Diy Cardboard Washing Machine?

You need sturdy cardboard, glue, scissors, markers, and a small plastic container. Optional items include buttons and string for added features. Using recycled materials makes the project eco-friendly and cost-effective.

### How Do You Assemble The Cardboard Toy Washing Machine?

Cut cardboard pieces for the body and drum. Glue parts firmly and let dry. Attach a small container inside as the drum. Decorate with markers to mimic a real washing machine.

### Can Children Safely Make The Cardboard Washing Machine?

Yes, children can safely make it with adult supervision. Use child-friendly scissors and non-toxic glue. The project encourages creativity and fine motor skills in a fun way.

### How Long Does It Take To Build The Toy Washing Machine?

Building the toy typically takes 30 to 60 minutes. Drying glue might require extra time. The project duration depends on complexity and child involvement.
