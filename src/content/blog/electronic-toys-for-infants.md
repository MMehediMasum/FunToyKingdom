### How Do Musical Toys Benefit Infant Development?



Musical toys stimulate auditory senses and improve cognitive skills. They encourage interaction and enhance memory. These toys promote early learning through fun sounds and rhythms.

### How Do Electronic Storybooks Help Toddlers Learn?



Electronic storybooks combine lights, sounds, and stories to engage toddlers. They improve language skills and sensory development. Interactive features make learning fun and memorable.
