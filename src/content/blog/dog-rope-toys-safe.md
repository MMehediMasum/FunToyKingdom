## Frequently Asked Questions



### Are Dog Rope Toys Safe For Aggressive Chewers?



Yes, dog rope toys made from durable cotton are safe for aggressive chewers. They withstand heavy chewing and reduce tooth damage. Choose indestructible options designed for large breeds to ensure safety and longevity during play and chewing sessions.



### How Do Rope Toys Benefit My Dog’s Dental Health?



Rope toys help clean teeth by flossing and reducing plaque buildup. Chewing on them massages gums and promotes healthy teeth. Regular use can decrease bad breath and improve overall oral hygiene for dogs of all sizes.



### Can Rope Toys Prevent Destructive Chewing Behavior?



Yes, rope toys provide a safe outlet for chewing urges. They reduce boredom and anxiety, minimizing destructive habits. Interactive tug toys also engage dogs mentally and physically, promoting better behavior at home.



### What Size Rope Toy Suits Large Breed Dogs?



For large breeds, choose rope toys that are thick, sturdy, and at least 3 feet long. Larger knots and heavy-duty cotton fibers ensure durability and safety during vigorous play and tug-of-war games.



### Are Cotton Rope Toys Safe For Puppies?



Cotton rope toys are safe for puppies as they are non-toxic and gentle on teeth. They help soothe teething discomfort and encourage healthy chewing habits. Always supervise puppies to prevent swallowing loose threads.



### How Do I Clean And Maintain Dog Rope Toys?



Hand wash rope toys with mild soap and warm water regularly. Air dry completely to prevent mold. Replace toys showing frayed fibers or damage to keep your dog safe and maintain hygiene.
