## Displaying And Caring For Your Doll

Displaying your Star Wars Princess Leia collectible doll properly keeps it safe and beautiful. Caring for your doll helps it last for years.

This guide shows easy ways to store and clean your doll to protect its quality and detail.

### Storage Solutions

Choose a storage spot that avoids dust, sunlight, and extreme temperatures. These factors can damage your doll’s colors and materials.

* Use a glass display case to keep dust away and show off your doll.
* Store your doll in a cool, dry place to prevent mold and fading.
* Avoid direct sunlight to stop colors from fading over time.
* Use acid-free tissue paper to wrap your doll if you store it in a box.
* Keep the doll away from pets and small children to avoid damage.

### Cleaning And Preservation

Cleaning your doll gently helps keep its paint and fabric safe. Use soft tools and mild solutions for the best care.
