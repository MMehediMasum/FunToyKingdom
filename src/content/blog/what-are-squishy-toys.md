## What Squishy Toys Are

Squishy toys are soft and squeezable items that bring joy and calm. They offer a fun way to reduce stress and keep hands busy. These toys slowly return to their original shape after being squished. They come in many sizes and colors, making them popular with kids and adults.

People use squishy toys for relaxation, focus, and even as collectibles. Their soft texture and slow bounce create a satisfying feeling. This simple toy type has grown in popularity worldwide.

### Materials Used

Squishy toys are made from foam, silicone, or rubber. Foam is light and soft, perfect for gentle squeezing. Silicone offers a smooth, stretchy feel that lasts longer. Rubber toys are durable and can handle rougher play. Some squishies include a gel filling for extra bounce. The materials are safe and non-toxic to touch.

### Common Shapes And Designs

Squishy toys come in many shapes. Food shapes like cupcakes, fruits, and bread are very popular. Animals such as cats, dogs, and bears also appear often. Cartoon characters and cute faces attract children. Some squishies look like everyday objects, like phones or plants. The designs focus on being fun and appealing to all ages.

## Care And Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=squishy-toys-bring-fun-and-stress-relief-to-many-people.-to-keep-them-soft-and-fresh,-proper-care-is-key.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Squishy toys bring fun and stress relief to many people. To keep them soft and fresh, proper care is key. Good care helps them last longer and stay safe to use. Follow these simple tips to clean and store your squishy toys well.

### Cleaning Methods

Clean squishy toys gently to avoid damage. Use a soft cloth with mild soap and warm water. Rub the surface lightly to remove dirt and stains. Avoid soaking the toy in water. Rinse the cloth and wipe off soap residue. Let the toy air dry completely before use. Never use harsh chemicals or bleach on squishies. These can harm the material and color.

### Storage Suggestions

Store squishy toys in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Sunlight can cause colors to fade. Keep them away from sharp objects to avoid tears. Use a clean box or drawer for storage. Avoid placing heavy items on top of squishies. This keeps their shape soft and intact. Regularly check stored toys for dust or dirt. Clean them before putting back in storage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Are Squishy Toys Made Of?

Squishy toys are made from soft, flexible materials like foam or silicone. These materials allow the toys to compress and slowly return to their original shape, providing a satisfying tactile experience.

### How Do Squishy Toys Relieve Stress?

Squeezing squishy toys helps release tension by engaging hand muscles. This repetitive action promotes relaxation and reduces anxiety, making them effective stress-relief tools.

### Are Squishy Toys Safe For Children?
