## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Cotton Rope Dog Toys Safe For Pets?



Cotton rope dog toys are non-toxic and natural. They are free from harmful chemicals, making them safe for chewing. Their soft texture helps protect dogs’ teeth and gums during play. These toys also reduce the risk of choking compared to plastic or rubber options.



### How Do Cotton Rope Toys Help With Dental Health?



Cotton rope toys clean teeth by flossing during chewing. The fibers remove plaque and tartar buildup naturally. This action promotes healthier gums and fresher breath. Regular use supports overall oral hygiene for dogs, especially aggressive chewers.



### Are Cotton Rope Toys Durable For Aggressive Chewers?



Yes, many cotton rope toys are designed for durability. Brands like Flossy Chews and Fida offer tough, heavy-duty ropes. These withstand strong chewing and tugging without easily fraying or breaking. They provide long-lasting fun for medium and large aggressive dogs.



### Can Cotton Rope Toys Reduce Dog Boredom Effectively?



Cotton rope toys encourage interactive play like tug-of-war and fetch. This mental and physical stimulation helps reduce boredom and destructive behaviors. Their texture and knot designs keep dogs engaged longer. Using these toys regularly improves your dog’s happiness and exercise.



### What Sizes Of Cotton Rope Toys Suit Different Dog Breeds?



Cotton rope toys come in various sizes for small to large breeds. Smaller ropes like SunGrow’s are ideal for puppies and small dogs. Larger ropes, such as the 25-inch Flossy Chews, fit medium and large aggressive chewers. Choose size based on your dog’s breed and chewing strength.
