Some chew toys have vibration modes to soothe teething pain. Others offer different firmness levels for gentle or strong chewers.



Many are made from BPA-free silicone, ensuring safety for little mouths. These toys also help develop oral motor skills and hand strength. Choosing the right sensory chew toy can make daily activities easier for children with special needs. Parents appreciate options that combine fun, comfort, and therapy in one simple tool.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Sensory Chew Toys Used For?



Sensory chew toys help children with autism, ADHD, and SPD. They provide oral motor stimulation and reduce anxiety. These toys are beneficial for teething babies, offering comfort and relief. They also help in improving focus and concentration by providing a safe outlet for chewing needs.



### Are Chew Toys Safe For Toddlers?



Yes, sensory chew toys are designed with safety in mind. They're made from non-toxic, BPA-free materials. These toys are specifically crafted to withstand aggressive chewing, ensuring they are durable and safe for toddlers. Regular supervision is recommended to ensure safety while using these toys.



### How Do Chew Necklaces Help Children?



Chew necklaces offer a discreet, wearable solution for children needing oral stimulation. They help manage anxiety, improve focus, and reduce fidgeting. Made from safe, food-grade silicone, they provide a durable option for kids who chew. These necklaces also support oral motor skills development.



### Can Sensory Toys Aid In Teething?



Yes, sensory toys are great for teething relief. They provide a safe, comforting surface for babies to chew on. This aids in soothing sore gums and encourages oral exploration. The textures and designs of these toys stimulate babies’ senses and help in their cognitive development.



### What Age Group Are Chew Toys Suitable For?



Chew toys are suitable for a wide age range. From infants aged 0-6 months to toddlers and children with special needs. These toys cater to various developmental stages, offering oral motor stimulation and anxiety relief. Always choose age-appropriate toys to ensure safety and effectiveness.
