## Authenticating Your Doll

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=knowing-if-your-toy-story-bo-peep-doll-is-real-helps-protect-its-value.-collectors-want-dolls-with-original-features.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Knowing if your Toy Story Bo Peep doll is real helps protect its value. Collectors want dolls with original features.

Fake dolls can look similar but have small differences. Learning to spot real dolls keeps your collection safe.

### Identifying Genuine Features

Look closely at the doll’s details. Real Bo Peep dolls have clear marks and quality materials.

Check the tags, stitching, and paint. These parts show if the doll is made by the official maker.

* Official Disney or Pixar logo on the tag
* High-quality fabric and paint
* Clear facial expressions matching the movie
* Correct colors for dress and accessories
* Proper size and weight for the doll model

### Avoiding Counterfeits

Fake dolls often use cheaper materials. They may have blurry logos or wrong colors.

Buy from trusted sellers and check reviews. Always ask for detailed photos before buying.

* Look for misspelled words on tags or packaging
* Check if paint looks smudged or uneven
* Beware of dolls that feel too light or flimsy
* Avoid deals that seem much cheaper than usual
* Compare with official images online before buying
