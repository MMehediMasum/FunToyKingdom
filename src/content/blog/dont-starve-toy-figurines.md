## Maintenance And Care

Don’t Starve toy figurines are fun collectibles. Keeping them clean and safe helps them last long.

Proper care protects your figurines from damage and keeps them looking good over time.

### Cleaning Techniques

Clean your Don’t Starve figurines gently to avoid scratches. Use soft materials and mild cleaners.

Remove dust with a soft brush or microfiber cloth. For dirt, use a damp cloth with water only.

* Use a soft brush for dust in small areas
* Damp cloth with water cleans surface dirt
* Avoid harsh chemicals or strong soaps
* Dry figurines fully after cleaning

### Handling Best Practices

Handle figurines with clean, dry hands to prevent oils or dirt transfer. Hold them by the base or sturdy parts.

Keep figurines away from direct sunlight and heat. Store them in a safe place to avoid falls or bumps.

* Wash hands before handling
* Hold figurines by strong parts only
* Keep away from sunlight and heat
* Use display cases or shelves for storage

### Repair Tips

If your figurine breaks, act quickly to fix it. Use glue made for plastic or resin materials.

Apply glue carefully with a small brush or toothpick. Hold parts together until dry to ensure a strong bond.

* Use plastic or resin glue only
* Apply glue in small amounts
* Hold parts steady until glue dries
* Avoid using super glue on painted areas
