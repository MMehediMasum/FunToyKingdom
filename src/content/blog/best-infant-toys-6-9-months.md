Sensory toys help develop a baby’s motor skills, hand-eye coordination, and cognitive abilities. They provide tactile, visual, and auditory stimulation, aiding early learning. Toys like silicone teethers and shape sorters are perfect for sensory exploration at 6-9 months.

Yes, teething toys made from food-grade silicone are safe for infants. They soothe gums and encourage chewing. Products like Yetonamr’s pull string teether and silicone baby remote control teethers meet safety standards and are ideal for this age.



### Which Toys Encourage Motor Skill Development In 6-9 Months Old?



Stacking blocks, rattles, and grasp toys promote fine and gross motor skills. Toys like Fisher-Price’s first blocks and Bright Starts Oball help babies practice grasping, rolling, and crawling. These toys support active exploration and physical growth.



### Can Musical Toys Aid Infant Development At 6-9 Months?



Yes, musical toys like Baby Einstein’s light bar and baby piano stimulate auditory senses and cognitive development. They encourage cause-and-effect learning and improve focus. Musical toys also help infants develop rhythm and coordination during play.
