## Care Tips For Plush Toys And Blankets

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=plush-toys-and-blankets-bring-comfort-to-babies.-keeping-them-clean-and-safe-is-important.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Plush toys and blankets bring comfort to babies. Keeping them clean and safe is important.

Follow simple care tips to keep these items soft and fresh for longer use.

### Cleaning Methods

Regular cleaning removes dirt and germs from plush toys and blankets. Use gentle methods to avoid damage.

* Hand wash with mild soap in cold water.
* Use a gentle cycle on the washing machine with cold water.
* Avoid bleach and strong chemicals.
* Air dry to keep softness and shape.
* Spot clean stains quickly with a damp cloth.

### Maintenance For Longevity

Taking care of plush toys and blankets helps them last longer and stay safe for babies.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Tip | Reason |
| Brush plush toys gently | Removes dust and keeps fabric soft |
| Check for loose threads or parts | Prevents choking hazards |
| Wash blankets regularly | Keeps them clean and fresh |
| Use fabric softener sparingly | Maintains softness without buildup |

### Safe Storage Practices

Store plush toys and blankets in a clean, dry place to avoid damage and germs.

* Keep items in sealed containers or bags.
* Store away from direct sunlight to prevent fading.
* Do not place heavy objects on top to avoid crushing.
* Ensure storage areas are free from pests and moisture.
