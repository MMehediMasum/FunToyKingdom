## Basic Rules

Tic Tac Toe is a simple game played on a 3x3 grid. Two players take turns marking X or O.

The goal is to get three marks in a row. Rows can be horizontal, vertical, or diagonal.

### The Game Board

The board has nine empty squares. Players place their marks in one square per turn.

* Three rows
* Three columns
* Two diagonals

### Player Turns

One player uses X and the other uses O. X always goes first. Players alternate turns.

Each turn, a player chooses an empty square to place their mark. No skipping turns allowed.

### Winning The Game

A player wins by placing three of their marks in a row. The row can be horizontal, vertical, or diagonal.

* Three marks across one row
* Three marks down one column
* Three marks along a diagonal

### Game Draw

If all squares fill without a three-in-a-row, the game ends in a draw. Neither player wins.

Players must try to block each other to avoid a loss and aim to create their own row.
