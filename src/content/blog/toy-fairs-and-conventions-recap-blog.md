### What Are The Top Toy Fairs To Attend Yearly?

### How Do Toy Conventions Benefit Collectors And Sellers?

Toy conventions provide collectors exclusive access to rare items and limited editions. Sellers gain exposure to large audiences and industry contacts. Both groups enjoy firsthand insights on market trends and upcoming releases.

### What Trends Emerged From Recent Toy Fairs?

Recent toy fairs highlighted sustainability, tech integration, and nostalgia. Eco-friendly materials and smart toys grew popular. Classic brands also launched retro-inspired collections, appealing to both kids and adults.
