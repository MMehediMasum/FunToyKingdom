## Game Variations For Kids

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=horseshoe-toss-sets-are-great-for-kids-to-enjoy-outdoor-fun.-changing-the-game-rules-keeps-kids-excited-to-play-again.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Horseshoe toss sets are great for kids to enjoy outdoor fun. Changing the game rules keeps kids excited to play again.

There are many ways to make horseshoe toss easier and more fun. Kids can try different styles to find what they like best.

### Basic Toss Rules

Kids stand a short distance from the stake to throw the horseshoes. The goal is to land the horseshoe close to or around the stake.

Each player takes turns throwing two horseshoes. Points are given for landing horseshoes near the stake or ringers.

* A ringer scores 3 points
* Closest horseshoe scores 1 point
* Only one player scores per round

### Team Play Options

Kids can play in teams to make the game social and cooperative. Teams alternate turns and add their points together.

Teams can be small, with two or three players each. This helps kids practice teamwork and cheering for friends.

* Two teams take turns throwing
* Team members alternate throwing
* Team with highest total points wins
* Rotate players to keep everyone involved

### Creative Scoring Methods

Changing scoring rules can make the game more fun. Kids enjoy trying new ways to earn points and win.

Try giving points for trick throws or landing horseshoes in special spots. This adds challenge and excitement.

* Give bonus points for hitting the stake directly
* Score extra for landing horseshoes in a circle around the stake
* Allow negative points for missing the board
* Use time limits to add pressure
