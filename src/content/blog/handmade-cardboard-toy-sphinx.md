### Choosing The Right Cardboard

Pick cardboard that is strong but easy to cut. Corrugated cardboard with one smooth side works well. Avoid very thick or very thin sheets.

* Use clean, dry cardboard pieces
* Recycled boxes are good if undamaged
* Flat sheets are easier to work with
* Check for no stains or tears

## Assembly Steps

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=building-a-handmade-cardboard-toy-sphinx-is-fun-and-easy.-follow-these-steps-to-assemble-your-toy-correctly.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Building a handmade cardboard toy sphinx is fun and easy. Follow these steps to assemble your toy correctly.

Each step focuses on a different part of the assembly process. Take your time and enjoy crafting.

### Folding Techniques

Proper folding creates a strong and neat structure for your sphinx. Use sharp creases to keep edges crisp.

* Fold along all scored lines carefully.
* Use a ruler to guide straight folds.
* Make valley folds where the paper bends inward.
* Make mountain folds where the paper bends outward.
* Press firmly to keep folds sharp and clean.

### Gluing And Securing

Use glue to hold the parts firmly in place. Make sure the glue dries before moving on.
