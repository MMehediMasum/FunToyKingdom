## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Sensory Toys For Infants 0-6 Months?



Sensory toys like Montessori sets stimulate infants' senses and motor skills. They encourage exploration, touch, and sound, aiding early brain development. Choose soft, textured, and colorful toys safe for babies under 6 months to enhance sensory learning effectively.



### Which Teething Toys Are Safest For Babies Under 12 Months?



Safe teething toys are BPA-free, non-toxic, and easy to grasp. Silicone and natural rubber teethers soothe gums and reduce discomfort. Look for toys designed for 0-12 months that are durable, easy to clean, and free from small parts to ensure baby safety.



### How Do Stacking Toys Benefit Infants Aged 6+ Months?



Stacking toys improve hand-eye coordination, fine motor skills, and problem-solving. They also introduce shapes and colors, promoting cognitive growth. Toys like Fisher-Price blocks or Sassy Stacks provide fun, educational play for infants 6 months and older.



### What Makes Musical Toys Ideal For Babies Over 3 Months?



Musical toys stimulate auditory senses and encourage early speech development. Toys like Baby Einstein Take Along Tunes engage babies with melodies and rhythms. They also enhance memory and focus, making them perfect for infants aged 3 months and up.



### Are Push And Pull Car Toys Good For Toddlers 1-2 Years?



Yes, push and pull car toys develop gross motor skills and coordination. They encourage walking and imaginative play. Toys like iPlay, iLearn Press to Go Cars are safe, engaging, and suitable for toddlers aged 1-2 years.



### What Features Should I Look For In Newborn Baby Socks With Rattles?



Rattle socks like Infinno’s combine comfort and sensory stimulation. They are soft, breathable, and include gentle rattles to engage babies. Ideal for newborns up to 12 months, these socks promote movement awareness and early auditory learning.
