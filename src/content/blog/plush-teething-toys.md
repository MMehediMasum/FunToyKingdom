* Lightweight and easy to grasp

### Texture Variety For Comfort

Different textures help soothe sore gums. Soft plush combined with firmer surfaces works well.

Look for teethers with bumps, ridges, or crinkles. These textures give babies comfort and interest.

* Soft plush for gentle chewing
* Rubber or silicone parts for gum massage
* Varied textures to keep baby engaged

### Hypoallergenic And Non-toxic Features

Babies have sensitive skin and mouths. Pick teethers made from safe, non-toxic materials.

Hypoallergenic fabrics reduce allergy risks. Avoid chemicals like BPA, phthalates, and lead.

* Certified BPA-free and phthalate-free
* Made with hypoallergenic fabrics

## Cleaning And Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=plush-teething-toys-need-regular-cleaning-to-keep-babies-safe.-dirt-and-germs-can-build-up-quickly-on-these-toys.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Plush teething toys need regular cleaning to keep babies safe. Dirt and germs can build up quickly on these toys.

Proper care helps the toys last longer and stay soft and safe for babies to chew on.

### Washing Guidelines

Check the toy’s label for washing instructions. Most plush teething toys can be hand-washed or machine-washed.

Use mild soap and warm water to clean the toy. Avoid harsh chemicals that can harm your baby.

* Hand wash with gentle detergent
* Use a laundry bag if machine washing
* Wash on a gentle cycle with cold or warm water
* Rinse thoroughly to remove soap

### Drying And Storage

Dry plush teething toys completely after washing. Moisture can cause mold or mildew to grow.

Store the toys in a clean, dry place away from dust and dirt to keep them fresh and safe.

* Air dry toys flat or hang to dry
* Avoid high heat from dryers to protect fabric
* Keep toys in a sealed container or box
* Regularly check toys for dirt before storing

### When To Replace

Replace plush teething toys if they show signs of wear or damage. Broken parts can be choking hazards.

Toys that lose softness or have stains that won't wash out should be replaced to keep babies safe.

* Tears or holes in fabric
* Loose or missing parts
* Persistent stains or bad smells
* Loss of shape or softness

Plush teethers work well with gentle rocking and soft singing. These methods help your baby feel safe and calm.

You can also use a cool washcloth or a pacifier along with the plush teether. This mix gives your baby more ways to find relief.

* Rock your baby while they hold the teether

### Monitoring Baby’s Response

Watch how your baby reacts to the plush teether. Some babies may like it right away, others need time to adjust.

If your baby seems upset or ignores the toy, try a different texture or shape. Always check the teether for safety before use.

* Look for signs of comfort or distress
