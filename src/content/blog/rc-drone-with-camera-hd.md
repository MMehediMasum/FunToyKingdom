## Camera Specifications

The camera on an RC drone is very important for capturing clear images and videos. Knowing the camera specifications helps you choose the right drone for your needs.

This section explains the key camera features like resolution, lens types, and image stabilization.

### Resolution And Frame Rates

Resolution shows how many pixels the camera captures. Higher resolution means sharper images.

Frame rate is how many pictures the camera takes per second in video mode. A higher frame rate makes videos look smoother.

* 1080p resolution is common and good for clear videos
* 4K resolution offers very detailed and sharp video quality
* 30fps is standard for smooth video
* 60fps and above are better for fast action shots

### Lens Types And Field Of View

The lens type affects how much the camera can see. Different lenses give different views of the scene.

Field of view (FOV) is the angle the camera covers. A wider FOV shows more area in the frame.

* Wide-angle lenses capture more of the scene
* Standard lenses focus on a smaller area with more detail
* FOV ranges from 70° to 120° depending on the lens

### Image Stabilization Techniques

Image stabilization helps keep photos and videos steady and clear. It reduces blurriness from drone movement.

There are different ways drones stabilize images to improve quality.

* Mechanical gimbals use motors to balance the camera
* Electronic stabilization adjusts the video with software
* Some drones use both for the best results

## Maintenance And Troubleshooting

Taking care of your RC drone with an HD camera helps it last longer. Regular checks and cleaning keep it flying well.

Troubleshooting common problems saves time and keeps your drone ready for action. Learn simple fixes and upgrades here.

### Routine Checks And Cleaning

Inspect your drone before and after each flight. Clean dirt and dust from propellers, camera lens, and body to avoid damage.

* Check propellers for cracks or chips
* Wipe the camera lens with a soft cloth
* Remove dust from motors and vents
* Make sure battery contacts are clean
* Test remote control buttons for response

### Common Issues And Fixes

If your drone won’t connect to the controller, check the signal and battery levels. Reset the connection if needed.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| Drone won’t start | Battery low or not connected | Charge battery or reconnect it |
| Camera not working | Loose cable or lens dirty | Check cable and clean lens |
| Drone drifts in air | Calibration needed | Recalibrate using the app |
| Remote control lag | Interference or weak signal | Fly in open area, replace batteries |

### Upgrading Parts
