## Popular Stranger Things Figures

Stranger Things action figures are popular among fans of the show. They bring favorite characters and creatures to life. Collectors enjoy their detailed designs and unique poses.

These figures come in many styles and sizes. Fans can find main characters, villains, and special editions. Each figure captures the spirit of the series well.

### Main Characters

Main characters from Stranger Things are often made into action figures. These include the kids and adults who fight the Upside Down. Their outfits and accessories match scenes from the show.

Figures of Eleven, Mike, Dustin, and Lucas are especially popular. Fans enjoy collecting the whole gang to recreate favorite moments. Some figures come with extra hands or items like Eggo waffles or walkie-talkies.

* Eleven with her iconic pink dress or Hawkins jacket
* Mike Wheeler with his bike and walkie-talkie
* Dustin Henderson with his cap and backpack

Stranger Things features many scary villains and strange creatures. Action figures of these add excitement to any collection. They show the dark side of the Upside Down world.

Popular villain figures include the Demogorgon and Mind Flayer. These figures often have detailed textures and poseable limbs. Fans can display heroes and villains side by side for dramatic scenes.

## Collecting Tips And Tricks

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collecting-stranger-things-action-figures-can-be-fun-and-rewarding.-knowing-how-to-care-for-them-helps-keep-their-value-high.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collecting Stranger Things action figures can be fun and rewarding. Knowing how to care for them helps keep their value high.

Use these tips to spot real figures, store them safely, and keep their worth intact over time.

### Authenticity Checks

Check for official logos and trademarks on packaging. Look for clear printing and correct colors.

* Examine the figure’s paint job for neat details
* Confirm the manufacturer’s name on the box
* Use a UV light to spot hidden marks or stamps
* Compare with official images from the brand’s website

### Storage And Display

Keep your figures away from direct sunlight to avoid fading. Use dust covers or clear cases for protection.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Method | Benefit |
| Display Case | Protects from dust and damage |
| Original Box | Keeps figure in mint condition |
| Soft Cloth Wrap | Prevents scratches during storage |
| Cool, Dry Place | Avoids warping and discoloration |

### Maintaining Value

Keep figures in original packaging if you want to keep value. Avoid removing price tags or stickers.

Here are ways to maintain value:

* Handle figures with clean hands or gloves
* Do not repaint or alter the figure
* Store in a stable environment without humidity
* Keep all accessories and parts together

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Are Stranger Things Action Figures Made Of?

Stranger Things action figures are typically made from durable plastic materials. These materials allow for detailed designs and safe play. Collectors appreciate the high-quality finish and accurate character likenesses.

### Where Can I Buy Official Stranger Things Action Figures?

You can buy official Stranger Things action figures from major retailers like Amazon, Target, and Walmart. Specialty toy stores and online collectible shops also stock them. Always check for authentic branding to avoid counterfeit products.

### Which Characters Are Available As Stranger Things Action Figures?

Popular characters like Eleven, Mike, Dustin, and the Demogorgon are available as action figures. The collection also includes newer characters from recent seasons. Each figure often comes with unique accessories and detailed costumes.

### Are Stranger Things Action Figures Suitable For All Ages?

Most Stranger Things action figures are recommended for ages 8 and up. They contain small parts that may pose choking hazards for younger children. Always check the packaging for age guidelines before purchase.
