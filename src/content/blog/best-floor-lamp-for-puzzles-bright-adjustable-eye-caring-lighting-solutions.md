## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Floor Lamp Ideal For Puzzles?



An ideal puzzle floor lamp offers bright, adjustable light that reduces eye strain. Features like dimmable brightness and color temperature help create the perfect lighting environment for detailed work.



### How Does Adjustable Brightness Benefit Puzzle Lighting?



Adjustable brightness lets you customize light intensity to match puzzle complexity. It prevents glare and fatigue, improving focus and comfort during long puzzle sessions.



### Why Is Eye-caring Technology Important In Floor Lamps?



Eye-caring lamps minimize blue light and flicker, reducing eye strain and fatigue. This helps maintain eye health during extended puzzle or craft activities.



### Can Remote Control Improve Puzzle Lamp Usability?



Yes, remote control allows easy light adjustments without interrupting your puzzle work. It adds convenience and flexibility, enhancing your overall lighting experience.



### Which Features Enhance Floor Lamps For Craft And Puzzle Tasks?



Gooseneck design, stepless dimming, color temperature control, and timer functions enhance usability. These features provide targeted, comfortable lighting tailored to detailed puzzle and craft tasks.
