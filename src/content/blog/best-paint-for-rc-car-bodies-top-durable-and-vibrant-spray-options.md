## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Paint For Rc Car Bodies?



The best paint for RC car bodies is polycarbonate spray paint like Tamiya or Pro-Line. They offer durability, flexibility, and vibrant colors. These paints bond well to plastic, resist chipping, and provide a smooth finish, ideal for RC car customization and protection.



### How To Choose Paint For Rc Car Bodies?



Choose paint designed specifically for polycarbonate or lexan bodies. Look for sprays like Tamiya 86001 or Pro-Line Racing for strong adhesion and flexibility. Consider color options, drying time, and durability to match your RC car style and performance needs.



### Are Lacquer Primers Suitable For Rc Car Painting?



Yes, lacquer primers like Tamiya lacquer primers prepare RC car bodies well. They enhance paint adhesion and create a smooth base. However, ensure compatibility with polycarbonate paints to avoid damage or poor finish on your RC car body.



### What Colors Are Popular For Rc Car Body Paint?



Popular RC car body paint colors include metallic blue, fluorescent orange, white, black, and candy magenta. Brands like Traxxas and Pro-Line offer vibrant options like Race Red and Sky Blue, giving your RC car a standout, customized look.



### Can I Use Regular Spray Paint On Rc Car Bodies?



Regular spray paint is not recommended for RC car bodies. It often lacks flexibility and adhesion needed on polycarbonate surfaces. Use specialized RC car paints like Tamiya or Pro-Line to avoid cracking and peeling during use.



### How Long Does Rc Car Body Paint Take To Dry?



RC car body paint typically dries within 15 to 30 minutes. Full curing can take up to 24 hours depending on the paint brand and environment. Follow manufacturer instructions for best drying and curing results.
