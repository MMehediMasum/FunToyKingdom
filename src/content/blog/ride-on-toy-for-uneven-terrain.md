## Safety Measures For Uneven Surfaces

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=riding-a-toy-on-rough-or-uneven-ground-needs-extra-care.-safety-should-be-the-top-priority-to-avoid-accidents-or-injuries.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Riding a toy on rough or uneven ground needs extra care. Safety should be the top priority to avoid accidents or injuries.

Understanding how to protect yourself and keep the ride smooth helps you enjoy it more safely.

### Protective Gear Recommendations

Wearing the right gear reduces the risk of injury on uneven terrain. It helps protect your body if you fall or hit bumps.

* Always wear a helmet that fits snugly
* Use knee and elbow pads for extra protection
* Wear closed-toe shoes with good grip
* Consider gloves to protect your hands

### Safe Riding Techniques

Good riding skills make navigating uneven surfaces safer. Slow down and stay alert to changes in the terrain.

1. Keep both hands firmly on the handlebars
2. Shift your weight slightly back on steep slopes
3. Look ahead to spot bumps or holes early
4. Use smooth, controlled movements to avoid tipping
5. Stop riding if the surface looks too rough or unsafe

### Maintenance Checks Before Use
