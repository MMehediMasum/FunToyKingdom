## Customization And Modding

Transformers Bumblebee action figures can be personalized to show your style. Customization lets you change the look and feel of your figure. Modding adds unique features and improves play value.

Many fans enjoy painting, adding lights, and creating custom parts. These changes make your Bumblebee stand out in your collection.

### Painting Techniques

Painting helps give your Bumblebee a fresh look or new details. Use acrylic paints for best results. They dry fast and stick well to plastic.

Light sanding before painting helps the paint hold better. Use thin layers to avoid covering small details. Seal your work with a clear coat to protect the paint.

* Use fine brushes for small parts
* Mask areas you want to keep original
* Apply primer before painting bright colors
* Let each layer dry before adding another

### Adding Led Lights

LED lights can make your Bumblebee look more dynamic. Small LED kits fit inside the figure’s chest or eyes. You need a tiny battery and wires for power.

Drill holes carefully to place the LEDs without breaking the figure. Use hot glue to hold parts in place. Turn off the lights when not in use to save battery.

* Choose low-power LEDs for safety
* Use thin wires to keep parts flexible
* Test the circuit before final assembly
* Hide wires in joints or hollow spaces

### Custom Accessories

Custom accessories add new features to Bumblebee. You can create weapons, armor, or gadgets with plastic or clay. Paint and attach these to your figure for a unique look.

Use magnets or small screws to make accessories removable. This lets you change your figure’s style easily. Adding custom decals can also personalize your figure.

* Build parts using modeling clay or plastic sheets
* Paint accessories to match or contrast with Bumblebee
* Use magnets for easy attachment and removal
* Add decals for logos or details
