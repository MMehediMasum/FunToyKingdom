## Maintenance And Care Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-your-toy-projector-with-stars-and-planets-helps-it-last-longer.-proper-maintenance-keeps-the-images-clear-and-bright.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of your toy projector with stars and planets helps it last longer. Proper maintenance keeps the images clear and bright.

Regular cleaning and careful storage prevent damage. Follow simple steps to keep your projector working well.

### Cleaning The Lenses

Clean the lenses gently to keep the pictures sharp. Dust and fingerprints can make the images blurry or dim.

Use a soft, dry cloth or lens cleaning tissue. Avoid using harsh chemicals or water on the lenses.

* Turn off the projector before cleaning
* Use a microfiber cloth to wipe the lens
* Do not press too hard to avoid scratches
* Store the cloth in a clean place

### Replacing Light Sources

The light source in your projector may need replacement after long use. A dim light affects the projection quality.

Check the user manual for the correct replacement bulb or LED. Use only recommended parts to avoid damage.

* Unplug the projector before replacing the light
* Handle bulbs with care to avoid breakage
* Install the new light firmly but gently
* Test the projector after replacement

### Storage Recommendations

Store the projector in a dry, dust-free place. Proper storage helps keep the parts safe and clean.

Use the original box or a soft bag to protect the device. Keep it away from heat and direct sunlight.

* Turn off and unplug before storage
* Cover the projector with a dust cloth
* Keep it in a cool, dry area
* Avoid stacking heavy items on top
