Choose math cubes made of non-toxic materials. They should be sturdy and free from sharp edges. Safety is the top priority for children's toys.

* Use BPA-free plastic or natural wood
* Check for smooth finishes without splinters
* Avoid small parts that can be swallowed
* Look for certifications like ASTM or EN71
