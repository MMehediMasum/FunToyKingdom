## Caring For Your Eevee Plush

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=your-eevee-plush-is-soft-and-cuddly.-taking-good-care-helps-it-last-a-long-time.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Your Eevee plush is soft and cuddly. Taking good care helps it last a long time.

This guide shares easy tips to keep your plush clean, stored well, and fluffy.

### Cleaning Tips

Clean your Eevee plush gently to keep its colors bright. Avoid harsh chemicals or hot water.

* Use a soft cloth with mild soap and cold water.
* Spot clean dirty areas only.
* Do not soak the plush in water.
* Let it air dry away from sunlight.

### Storage Advice

Store your Eevee plush in a dry, cool place. Avoid tight spaces that can squash it.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Tip | Reason |
| Keep away from direct sunlight | Prevents color fading |
| Avoid damp areas | Stops mold and mildew |
| Store loosely in a box or shelf | Protects shape and fluff |

### Maintaining Plush Shape

Keep your Eevee plush fluffy and soft with simple care habits.

Try these tips to keep its shape:

* Fluff the plush regularly by gently squeezing and reshaping.
* Do not put heavy objects on top of it.
* If flattened, let it rest in open air for a few hours.
