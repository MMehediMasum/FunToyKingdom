## Care And Maintenance

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-good-care-of-your-legolas-action-figure-bow-set-keeps-it-looking-great.-proper-maintenance-helps-the-set-last-a-long-time.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking good care of your Legolas Action Figure Bow Set keeps it looking great. Proper maintenance helps the set last a long time.

Follow simple tips to clean, store, and handle your bow set safely. This protects the details and materials.

### Cleaning Tips

Clean the bow set gently to avoid damage. Use a soft cloth to remove dust and dirt.

For tougher spots, slightly dampen the cloth with water. Avoid using harsh chemicals or soaking the figure.

* Use a dry microfiber cloth for dusting
* Dampen cloth lightly for sticky dirt
* Do not use alcohol or strong cleaners
* Avoid water contact with painted areas

### Storage Advice

Store the bow set in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Sunlight can fade colors and weaken plastic.

Keep the figure in a display case or box to protect it from dust and accidental damage.

* Use a closed display case if possible
* Avoid humid areas to prevent mold
* Keep away from direct sunlight
* Store parts separately if needed

### Handling Recommendations

Handle the Legolas bow set with clean hands. Oils and dirt from skin can harm the paint and plastic.

Hold the figure by sturdy parts, not thin or fragile pieces. Move slowly to avoid breaking small parts.

* Wash hands before touching the figure
* Hold by the main body or thick parts
* Avoid pulling on strings or thin pieces
* Be gentle during play or display changes
