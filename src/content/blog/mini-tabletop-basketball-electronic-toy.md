### Assembly Tips

Start by unpacking all parts carefully. Make sure you have the backboard, hoop, electronic scoreboard, and base. Use the included screws and screwdriver to attach the hoop to the backboard.

* Place the electronic scoreboard at the top center of the backboard.
* Secure the base to the bottom of the backboard for stability.
* Insert batteries into the scoreboard as instructed in the manual.
* Test the electronic functions before playing.

### Basic Rules

Players take turns shooting the small basketball into the hoop. Each player gets five shots per turn. The goal is to score as many baskets as possible.

Make sure to shoot from the same side to keep the game fair. If the ball misses, the turn passes to the next player.
