### Basic Grind Moves

Begin with these foundational grind moves. They are essential for any skateboarder using a grind rail.

* **50-50 Grind**: Balance with both trucks on the rail.
* **Boardslide**: Slide the deck sideways along the rail.
* **5-0 Grind**: Use the back truck to grind, lifting the front.

Once comfortable with basics, try these intermediate techniques. They require more balance and skill.
