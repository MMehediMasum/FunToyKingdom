## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Scale Of Toy Cars?



The scale indicates the size ratio compared to the real vehicle. For example, a 1:48 scale means the toy is 48 times smaller than the actual car. It's a popular scale for collectors and enthusiasts, offering detailed replicas without taking up too much space.



### Are These Toy Cars Suitable For Kids?



Yes, most of these toy cars are suitable for children aged 3 and above. They are made from durable die-cast metal. However, always check the manufacturer's age recommendations to ensure safe play for younger children.



### Do These Toy Cars Have Moving Parts?



Yes, many of these toy cars feature moving parts. Common features include pull-back action, opening doors, and rotating wheels. These interactive elements enhance the play experience, making the cars more engaging and realistic for both kids and collectors.



### What Materials Are These Toy Cars Made From?



These toy cars are primarily made from die-cast metal. This material provides durability and a realistic look. Some parts, such as tires and windows, may be made from plastic. The combination ensures a sturdy and detailed model that appeals to collectors and kids alike.



### How Can I Maintain Die-cast Toy Cars?



To maintain die-cast toy cars, regularly dust them with a soft cloth. Avoid using harsh chemicals, which can damage the finish. Store them in a dry, cool place to prevent rust. Display cases can help protect from dust and accidental damage, keeping your collection pristine.
