## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Teething Toys?



Teething toys soothe sore gums and promote healthy oral development. They provide sensory stimulation, aiding cognitive and motor skill growth. Safe materials ensure babies can chew without risk. Many are designed to be easy for little hands to hold, enhancing playtime and learning experiences for infants.



### How Do Silicone Teethers Help Infants?



Silicone teethers provide a gentle chewing surface to relieve gum discomfort. They're durable and can be chilled for extra relief. Silicone is non-toxic and easy to clean, ensuring a hygienic option for infants. These teethers also help improve hand-eye coordination and sensory skills.



### Are Teething Toys Safe For Newborns?



Yes, most teething toys are designed with newborns in mind. They are made from non-toxic, BPA-free materials. Always choose age-appropriate toys and supervise your baby during use. Ensure the toys are easy to clean and inspect them regularly for wear and tear.



### Why Choose A Teething Mitten For Babies?



Teething mittens prevent babies from dropping their teethers and keep hands safe. They are made from food-grade silicone, offering a safe chewing surface. The textured fabric provides sensory stimulation, and they're adjustable, ensuring a snug fit. Mittens are especially useful for younger infants who struggle to hold traditional teethers.



### How Do Chilled Teethers Provide Relief?



Chilled teethers soothe inflamed gums by providing a cooling sensation. They help numb the pain and reduce swelling. Simply place them in the refrigerator, not the freezer, to avoid extreme cold. Always ensure the teether is made from materials safe for chilling and suitable for your baby's age.



## Conclusion



Choosing the right teething toy can make a big difference for your baby’s comfort and development. Soft, safe, and easy-to-hold toys help soothe sore gums during teething. Toys made from BPA-free silicone or food-grade materials are gentle and safe for infants.



Different shapes and textures can keep your baby interested while supporting sensory growth. Many teething toys also double as learning tools, helping babies explore and develop fine motor skills. It’s important to pick toys designed for your baby’s age to ensure safety and effectiveness.



Keeping teething toys clean and in good condition also helps protect your baby’s health. Remember, every baby is unique, so try different options to find what works best. With the right teething toys, you can help your baby feel better and enjoy playtime more.



Teething can be easier for both babies and parents with the right choices.
