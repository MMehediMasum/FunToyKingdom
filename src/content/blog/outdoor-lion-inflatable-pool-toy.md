## Setting Up The Inflatable Pool Toy

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-up-your-outdoor-lion-inflatable-pool-toy-is-easy-and-fun.-follow-simple-steps-to-make-sure-it-is-ready-for-play.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting up your outdoor lion inflatable pool toy is easy and fun. Follow simple steps to make sure it is ready for play.

Proper setup helps keep the toy safe and lasts longer. Take time to prepare the space and inflate it correctly.

### Inflation Tips

Use an electric or hand pump to inflate the lion pool toy quickly. Avoid using your mouth to prevent damage.

Do not overinflate the toy. Stop once it feels firm but still soft to touch. This prevents leaks and tears.

* Check all valves before inflating
* Inflate in a shaded area to avoid heat expansion
* Close valves tightly after inflation
* Inspect for leaks by listening or feeling for air escaping

### Placement And Safety

Place the inflatable lion pool toy on a flat, smooth surface. Clear away sharp objects like stones or sticks.

Keep the pool toy away from direct sunlight for long hours. This helps prevent material damage and overheating.

* Use a ground cloth or tarp under the toy
* Set up in a shaded or partly shaded area
* Ensure adult supervision during play
* Keep pets away to avoid punctures

### Maintenance And Cleaning

Clean the inflatable pool toy after each use with mild soap and water. Avoid harsh chemicals that harm the material.

Dry the toy completely before storing it. Store in a cool, dry place away from sharp objects and sunlight.

* Rinse off dirt and debris gently
* Use a soft cloth or sponge for cleaning
* Check for small holes or damage regularly
* Repair leaks with a patch kit if needed
