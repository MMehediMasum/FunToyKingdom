**Imagine bringing the magic of a campfire right into your home—without any smoke, sparks, or danger. With a DIY cardboard toy campfire, you can create a fun, safe, and creative project that sparks your imagination and keeps you entertained for hours.**

## Materials Needed

Building a DIY cardboard toy campfire is fun and simple. You only need a few materials to get started.

Gather all items before you begin to make the process smooth and easy.

### Cardboard Essentials

Cardboard is the main material for this project. Choose thick pieces that hold shape well.

You will cut and shape the cardboard to create logs and the campfire base.

* Corrugated cardboard sheets (large size)
* Cardboard tubes (optional for logs)
* Recycled boxes

### Decorative Supplies

Decorative supplies make the campfire look real and colorful. Use safe, easy-to-handle items.

These items help create flames and add texture to the campfire.

* Colored tissue paper (red, orange, yellow)
* Paints (acrylic or poster paint)
* Markers or crayons
* Glue or craft glue sticks
* Optional: glitter or stickers for extra detail

### Tools For Assembly

You need simple tools to cut and join cardboard pieces safely and neatly.

Make sure to use child-safe scissors or ask for adult help.

* Scissors or craft knife
* Ruler or measuring tape
* Pencil or pen for marking
* Hot glue gun or strong tape
* Cutting mat or safe surface

## Step-by-step Construction

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=building-a-cardboard-toy-campfire-is-a-fun-and-easy-project.-you-only-need-simple-materials-and-some-patience.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Building a cardboard toy campfire is a fun and easy project. You only need simple materials and some patience. Follow these steps to create your own campfire toy.

This guide will show you how to cut, assemble, and decorate the cardboard pieces for a realistic look.

### Cutting The Cardboard Pieces

Start by preparing all the pieces you will need. Use a ruler and pencil to mark the cardboard before cutting.

* Cut out a circle for the base, about 12 inches wide.
* Cut 6-8 logs about 8 inches long and 2 inches wide.
* Cut flame shapes from red, orange, and yellow cardboard.

### Assembling The Base

Use the circle piece as the ground for your campfire. Arrange the logs upright around the edge of the circle.

| Log Position | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Glue the first log at the circle’s edge. |
| 2 | Place the second log next to the first, slightly leaning in. |
| 3 | Repeat until all logs form a ring. |

Let the glue dry well before moving on.

### Creating The Flames

Stack the flame shapes by size and color. Start with the largest yellow flames at the back.

* Glue the orange flames on top of the yellow ones.
* Add the red flames on top for depth.
* Shape the flames by curling the edges slightly upward.

### Adding Final Touches

Place the flame stack in the center of the log ring. Secure it with glue or tape.

* Paint the logs with brown and black to add texture.
* Add small stones or moss around the base for realism.
* Let everything dry completely before play.

## Safety Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=building-a-diy-cardboard-toy-campfire-is-fun-and-creative.-safety-is-very-important-during-the-project.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Building a DIY cardboard toy campfire is fun and creative. Safety is very important during the project.

Follow these safety tips to keep everyone safe and enjoy making your toy campfire.

### Handling Tools Safely

Use scissors and knives carefully to avoid cuts. Always cut away from your body.

Keep tools out of reach of small children. Store them safely after use.

* Use child-safe scissors if possible
* Cut on a flat, stable surface
* Keep fingers clear of blades
* Never run with scissors or sharp tools

### Non-toxic Materials

Choose non-toxic glue and paint to keep the toy safe. Avoid materials with strong smells or harmful chemicals.

Check labels to make sure all supplies are child-friendly and safe to use indoors.

* Use white glue or school glue
* Pick water-based paints
* Avoid spray paints or solvents
* Choose natural or recycled cardboard

### Supervision Guidelines

Adults should supervise children during the whole project. Help with cutting and gluing tasks.

Watch for any small parts that could be a choking hazard. Keep the workspace clean and organized.

* Stay close while children use tools
* Explain safe ways to handle materials
* Remove sharp objects after use
* Encourage careful and slow work
