## Caring For Your Dolls

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collectible-dolls-from-game-of-thrones-need-special-care-to-stay-in-good-shape.-proper-care-helps-keep-their-colors-bright-and-details-clear.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collectible dolls from Game of Thrones need special care to stay in good shape. Proper care helps keep their colors bright and details clear.

Simple steps in cleaning, storage, and damage prevention will protect your dolls for years to come.

### Cleaning And Maintenance

Clean your dolls gently to avoid damage. Use a soft cloth or brush to remove dust and dirt from their surfaces.

For tougher spots, slightly dampen the cloth with water. Avoid harsh chemicals or soaking the dolls.

* Dust dolls weekly to prevent buildup
* Use a soft brush for small details
* Spot clean with a damp cloth only
* Never use bleach or strong cleaners

### Storage Tips

Store your Game of Thrones dolls in a cool, dry place. Keep them away from direct sunlight to stop colors from fading.

Use dust covers or display cases to protect them from dust and pets. Keep dolls standing or lying flat to avoid misshaping.

* Place dolls in a shaded area
* Use cases or covers to keep dust out
* Store dolls upright or flat to keep shape
* Keep away from heat and moisture

### Preventing Damage And Wear

Handle your dolls with clean hands to avoid oils and dirt. Avoid dropping or bending parts that can break easily.

Check your dolls regularly for loose pieces or fading. Fix small problems quickly to stop bigger damage.

* Wash hands before touching dolls
* Hold dolls by sturdy parts only
* Inspect dolls for damage often
* Repair small issues early

## Investing In Collectibles

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collecting-game-of-thrones-dolls-can-be-a-smart-way-to-invest.-these-dolls-may-increase-in-value-over-time.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collecting Game of Thrones dolls can be a smart way to invest. These dolls may increase in value over time.

Knowing how to evaluate and track the market helps you make better choices. This guide explains key points for collectors.

### Evaluating Doll Value

Look at the doll's condition first. Mint condition dolls usually cost more.

Check if the doll comes with original packaging and certificates. These items add to the value.

* Condition: New, used, or damaged
* Packaging: Original box or missing
* Authenticity: Official license and certificates
* Character popularity

### Rarity And Demand

Rare dolls from limited editions usually have higher value. The fewer dolls made, the more valuable they become.

Demand depends on the character and fan interest. Popular characters keep value higher over time.

* Limited edition dolls
* Exclusive release dolls
* Fan-favorite characters
* Time since release

### Resale Market Trends

Track recent sales to understand price changes. Online auctions show what buyers pay now.

Prices may rise after shows end or during special events. Watch for trends and act at the right time.

* Check auction results regularly
* Follow collector forums and groups
* Note price spikes after new releases
* Sell when demand is high
