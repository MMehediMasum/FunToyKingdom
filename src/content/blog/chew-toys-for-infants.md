## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Is Best For Baby Chew Toys?



Chew toys suit infants from 0 to 18 months. They support teething and sensory development during early stages. Choose age-specific toys for safety and effectiveness.



### Are Silicone Teething Toys Safe For Babies?



Yes, silicone teething toys are BPA-free and non-toxic. They are soft, durable, and gentle on sore gums. Food-grade silicone is ideal for infant use.



### How Do Montessori Sensory Toys Help Infants?



Montessori sensory toys promote motor skills, hand-eye coordination, and cognitive growth. They engage babies through touch, sight, and sound. These toys encourage independent exploration.



### Can Teething Mittens Prevent Toy Dropping?



Teething mittens with wrist straps keep toys within reach. They reduce choking hazards and mess from dropped toys. Ideal for babies learning to grasp.



### What Shapes Are Best For Infant Teething Toys?



Ring, carrot, tube, and fruit shapes are popular. These shapes are easy to hold and stimulate sensory exploration. Variety helps keep babies interested.



### How To Clean And Maintain Baby Chew Toys?



Wash toys with warm, soapy water regularly. Some silicone toys are freezer-safe for soothing relief. Always check manufacturer cleaning instructions for safety.
