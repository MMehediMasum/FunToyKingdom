### Turning On And Off

To turn on Jessie, press the button on her back or under her hat. You will hear her speak phrases from the Toy Story movies. To turn off the doll, press the same button again until the sound stops.

### Battery Requirements

| Battery Type | Quantity | Placement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AAA | 3 | Inside the back compartment |

Use new AAA batteries for the best sound. Always insert the batteries with the correct polarity. Remove batteries if the doll will not be used for a long time to avoid damage.

### Care And Maintenance

* Keep Jessie away from water to protect the electronics.
* Clean the doll with a dry or slightly damp cloth only.
* Store Jessie in a cool, dry place when not in use.
* Check batteries regularly and replace if sounds get weak.
* Do not open the doll’s internal parts to avoid damage.
