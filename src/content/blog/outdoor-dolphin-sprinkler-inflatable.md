## Setting Up The Inflatable Sprinkler

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-up-the-outdoor-dolphin-sprinkler-inflatable-is-easy.-this-guide-helps-you-prepare-it-quickly-for-fun-water-play.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting up the outdoor dolphin sprinkler inflatable is easy. This guide helps you prepare it quickly for fun water play.

Follow these simple steps to get your sprinkler ready and running smoothly in your yard.

### Choosing The Right Location

Pick a flat area free of sharp objects. The ground should be soft to protect the inflatable.

Make sure the spot has good drainage. Avoid places where water can pool or cause mud.

* Use a grassy area or a smooth lawn
* Keep away from rocks, sticks, or fences
* Choose a place near a water source
* Allow enough space for the inflatable to expand

### Inflation Tips And Tricks

Use an electric or manual pump for fast inflation. Avoid using your mouth to save energy.

Check all air valves before inflating. Close them tightly after filling with air to prevent leaks.

* Inflate on a smooth surface to avoid punctures
* Do not overfill; keep the inflatable firm but soft
* Inspect for holes or tears before use
* Keep the pump nearby for quick adjustments

### Connecting To Water Source

Attach a garden hose to the sprinkler’s water inlet securely. Use a hose washer to prevent leaks.

Turn on the water slowly. Adjust the flow for the best spray and to avoid damage.

* Check hose for cracks or holes before connecting
* Use a water timer to control sprinkler time
* Ensure the connection is tight to avoid water loss
* Turn off water when not in use to save resources

## Maintenance And Storage Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-outdoor-dolphin-sprinkler-inflatable-clean-and-stored-properly-helps-it-last-longer.-regular-care-prevents-damage-and-keeps-it-safe-to-use.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Outdoor Dolphin Sprinkler Inflatable clean and stored properly helps it last longer. Regular care prevents damage and keeps it safe to use.

Follow these easy steps after each use to maintain your inflatable’s condition and enjoy it for many seasons.

### Cleaning After Use

Clean your dolphin sprinkler carefully after every use. Dirt, grass, and water can cause mold or wear if left on the surface.

* Use a soft cloth or sponge with mild soap and water.
* Gently wipe all surfaces, including inside the water holes.
* Rinse well with clean water to remove soap residue.
* Allow the inflatable to air dry completely before storing.
* Avoid harsh cleaners or abrasive brushes that can damage the material.

### Proper Deflation And Folding

Deflate the dolphin slowly to avoid damage to the seams. Folding it correctly helps save space and prevents creases.

1. Turn off the water supply and let the inflatable dry completely.
2. Open the air valves and press gently to remove all air.
3. Fold the inflatable neatly, avoiding sharp bends in the material.
4. Store the folded inflatable in a dry, cool place away from direct sunlight.
5. Check for any leaks or tears before folding and fix them if needed.

### Storing For Longevity

Proper storage keeps your dolphin sprinkler safe from damage and extends its life. Use these tips to protect your inflatable during off-seasons.

| Storage Tip | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Keep in a dry area | Prevents mold and mildew growth |
| Avoid direct sunlight | Stops material from fading or cracking |
| Store away from sharp objects | Prevents accidental punctures |
| Use a breathable bag or container | Allows air circulation and reduces moisture |

## Safety Precautions For Outdoor Play

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=outdoor-play-with-an-inflatable-dolphin-sprinkler-is-fun-and-refreshing.-safety-should-always-come-first-to-keep-kids-safe.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Outdoor play with an inflatable dolphin sprinkler is fun and refreshing. Safety should always come first to keep kids safe.

Simple safety steps help prevent accidents and ensure a happy playtime for everyone.

### Supervision Guidelines

Adults should watch children at all times while using the inflatable sprinkler. This helps stop accidents before they happen.

Keep young kids close and check frequently that they are playing safely.

* Stay within arm’s reach of young children
* Do not leave children unattended near water or slippery surfaces
* Make sure all kids understand the rules before playing

### Slip And Fall Prevention

Wet surfaces can be slippery. Take steps to reduce the risk of falls around the sprinkler.

Use a soft mat or grass area under and around the inflatable to cushion falls.

* Keep the play area free of toys and objects that cause tripping
* Check that the sprinkler is firmly secured and stable
* Encourage kids to walk, not run, near the sprinkler

### Sun Protection Measures

Playing outdoors means exposure to the sun. Protect skin and eyes from sun damage.

Use sunscreen and wear hats or sunglasses during playtime with the sprinkler.

* Apply sunscreen 30 minutes before play
* Reapply sunscreen every two hours or after swimming
* Choose shaded play areas if possible
* Encourage wearing UV-protection clothing
