## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Sensory Regulation Toys Used For?



Sensory regulation toys help manage anxiety, stress, and sensory processing issues. They support focus and calmness in children and adults. These toys are ideal for ADHD, autism, and OCD. Using them can improve emotional regulation and classroom behavior.



### Which Sensory Toys Are Best For Autism?



Toys like Scientoy Fidget Sets, silicone sensory boards, and textured worry stones are great for autism. They provide tactile and visual stimulation. These toys promote calmness and reduce sensory overload. They are safe and suitable for various age groups.



### Can Sensory Toys Help With Anxiety Relief?



Yes, sensory toys like stress balls, fidget cubes, and calming bottles reduce anxiety. They offer tactile distraction and relaxation. Using these toys can improve focus and emotional control. They are effective tools for both kids and adults.



### Are Sensory Toys Suitable For Classroom Use?



Absolutely. Sensory toys help students stay focused and manage stress in class. Items like cool down cubes and fidget sets are quiet and mess-free. Teachers use them as rewards or calming tools. They support a positive learning environment.



### What Age Group Benefits From Sensory Toys?



Sensory toys suit toddlers, children, teens, and adults. Many products cater to ages 3 and up. They assist with developmental and emotional needs across all ages. Versatile designs ensure safety and engagement for all users.



### How Do Sensory Activity Boards Support Stress Relief?



Sensory activity boards provide tactile and visual stimulation to calm nerves. They engage multiple senses through touch and movement. These boards improve focus and reduce anxiety. Portable designs make them great for travel or classroom use.
