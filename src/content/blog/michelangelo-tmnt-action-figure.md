## Maintenance And Care Tips

Your Michelangelo TMNT action figure is a valuable collector's item. Proper care keeps it looking great for years.

Follow these simple maintenance tips to ensure your action figure remains in top condition.

### Cleaning And Storage

Regular cleaning prevents dust build-up and keeps colors vibrant. Store your figure properly to avoid damage.

* Dust your figure weekly with a soft brush.
* Use a damp cloth for stubborn dirt, but avoid soaking.
* Store in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.
* Keep in a display case to reduce exposure to dust.

### Repairing Minor Damages

Minor damages can occur over time. Simple repairs can restore your figure to its original condition.

Glue small cracks with plastic-safe adhesive. Paint scratches can be touched up with acrylic paint matching the original colors.
