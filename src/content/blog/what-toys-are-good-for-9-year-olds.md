## Safety And Durability Tips

Choosing toys for 9 year olds means thinking about safety and durability. Toys should be strong to last long and safe to avoid accidents. These tips help pick toys that keep kids happy and parents worry-free.

### Check For Non-toxic Materials

Toys must be made from safe, non-toxic materials. Avoid toys with harmful chemicals. Look for labels that say “BPA free” or “phthalate free.” These ensure the toy is safe to touch and put in the mouth.

### Choose Age-appropriate Toys

Buy toys made for 9 year olds. Toys for younger kids may have small parts that cause choking. Toys for older kids might be too hard to use. Age-appropriate toys reduce safety risks.

### Inspect For Small Or Loose Parts

Small parts can break off and cause choking. Check toys for tiny pieces before buying. Make sure all parts are firmly attached. Avoid toys that look fragile or easily breakable.

### Look For Durable Construction

Toys should withstand rough play. Choose toys made from strong plastic, wood, or metal. Avoid cheap, thin materials that break fast. Durable toys last longer and stay safe over time.

### Check For Sharp Edges And Points

Sharp edges can cut skin or cause injury. Inspect toys for smooth, rounded edges. Avoid toys with exposed nails, screws, or sharp plastic parts. Safety comes first in every toy.

### Follow Manufacturer’s Instructions

Always read the instructions before use. Some toys need assembly or special care. Proper use helps keep the toy safe and working well. Teach kids how to use toys correctly.
