## Diy Weather Experiments

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=weather-forecast-toy-kits-let-you-explore-weather-science-at-home.-you-can-build-simple-tools-to-watch-nature's-changes.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Weather forecast toy kits let you explore weather science at home. You can build simple tools to watch nature's changes.

These experiments teach you how to measure rain, wind, pressure, and temperature. Each project uses easy materials and clear steps.

### Building A Rain Gauge

A rain gauge helps you measure how much rain falls. You only need a plastic bottle and a ruler.

Cut the bottle and mark measurements on it. Place it outside to collect rainwater. Check the level after each rain.

### Creating A Simple Barometer

A barometer measures air pressure. You can make one using a jar, balloon, and straw.

Stretch the balloon over the jar’s opening. Attach a straw on top as a pointer. Watch the straw move when pressure changes.

### Making A Wind Vane

A wind vane shows wind direction. Use a straw, paper, and a pencil with an eraser.

Cut arrow shapes from paper and attach them to a straw. Stick the straw on the pencil eraser. Place it outside and watch it spin.

### Tracking Temperature Changes

Track temperature using a simple thermometer made from water, rubbing alcohol, and a clear tube.

Fill a bottle with colored water and alcohol mix. Insert a tube and seal it. Watch the liquid rise or fall as temperature changes.
