## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Stuffed Animal Safe For Babies To Sleep With?



A safe stuffed animal is soft, hypoallergenic, and free from small detachable parts. It should be lightweight and machine washable. These features reduce choking hazards and allergens, providing a secure sleep companion for babies.



### How Do Sleep Soothers With Heartbeat Sounds Help Babies?



Heartbeat sounds mimic the womb environment, calming newborns and reducing anxiety. They promote faster sleep onset and longer naps by creating a soothing, familiar ambiance that comforts babies throughout the night.



### Which Stuffed Animals Have Built-in Night Lights And Lullabies?



Products like BEREST Bunny and PureBaby Unicorn include night lights and lullabies. These features help create a calming bedtime routine, easing babies into sleep with gentle light and soothing sounds.



### Are Security Blankets Or Loveys Better For Newborn Sleep Comfort?



Both provide comfort, but security blankets like Pro Goleem’s offer soft fabric for tactile soothing. Loveys combine plush toys with blankets, adding emotional security and helping babies self-soothe effectively.



### How Does A Cry Sensor Improve Baby Sleep Soothers?



Cry sensors detect a baby’s cries and activate sounds or lights automatically. This helps soothe babies quickly without parental intervention, minimizing sleep disruptions and promoting longer restful sleep.



### Can Stuffed Animals With White Noise Aid In Reducing Bedtime Wake-ups?



Yes, stuffed animals like BEREST Heartbeat Bear use white noise to mask environmental sounds. This continuous sound soothes infants, reducing nighttime awakenings and helping babies stay asleep longer.
