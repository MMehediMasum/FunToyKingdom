## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Bath Toys For 3-month-olds?



The best bath toys for 3-month-olds are soft, safe, and easy to grasp. Look for floating toys like Munchkin® Float & Play Bubbles™. These stimulate sensory development and keep babies entertained during bath time.



### Are Floating Bath Toys Safe For Infants?



Yes, floating bath toys like swimming turtles and light-up whales are safe when made from non-toxic materials. Always supervise your baby during bath time to prevent choking hazards.



### How Do Bath Toys Help Infant Development?



Bath toys improve sensory skills, hand-eye coordination, and motor skills. Toys like wind-up turtles and light-up sprinklers engage babies and encourage interactive play during bathing.



### What Age Range Suits These Bath Toys?



Most toys listed are ideal for babies 3 months to 3 years. For example, the Nuby Floating Octopus suits 18+ months, while others like silicone teethers fit newborns.



### Can Bath Toys Soothe Teething Babies?



Yes, food-grade silicone bath teethers provide relief for teething babies aged 3 to 12 months. They are safe to chew and also serve as fun bath companions.



### How To Clean And Maintain Baby Bath Toys?



Clean toys regularly with warm soapy water and rinse well. Avoid mold by drying toys thoroughly and storing them in a ventilated area after use.
