Yes, light-up bath toys like ZHEDNUO rechargeable whales are safe when BPA-free and waterproof. They enhance bath time fun with gentle lights and are designed for toddler use.

Yes, bath toys such as sensory fidget sets and foam puzzles stimulate touch, sight, and coordination. These toys support toddlers' sensory exploration in a fun water environment.

### Are Wind-up Bath Toys Suitable For Toddlers?



Yes, wind-up bath toys like swimming turtles are safe and engaging for toddlers. They encourage hand-eye coordination and add excitement to bath time play.
