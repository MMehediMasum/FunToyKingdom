Yes, these bunny toys are safe for toddlers. They are made from non-toxic materials and have child-safe designs. ### What Features Do Interactive Bunny Toys Offer?



Interactive bunny toys offer lifelike movements like hopping, ear twitching, and nose wiggling. Some models include sound effects, enhancing the play experience.
