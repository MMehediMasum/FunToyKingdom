## Setting Up The Sprinkler

Setting up your Outdoor Toucan Sprinkler Pool Toy is quick and easy. Follow simple steps to get it ready for fun summer play.

### Choosing The Right Location

Find a flat area with good drainage. Avoid places with sharp objects or rough surfaces. Make sure there is enough space for the sprinkler to spray water freely.

* Keep away from electrical outlets and wires
* Check that the area is free of rocks and sticks
* Leave room for children to run around safely

### Connecting To Water Source

Attach the sprinkler hose to a garden faucet or outdoor tap. Use a tight connection to stop leaks. Turn on the water slowly to check pressure and flow.

## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-outdoor-toucan-sprinkler-pool-toy-in-good-shape-helps-it-last-longer.-regular-care-stops-damage-and-keeps-it-working-well.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Outdoor Toucan Sprinkler Pool Toy in good shape helps it last longer. Regular care stops damage and keeps it working well.

Simple maintenance steps make your sprinkler safe and fun to use every time.

### Cleaning The Sprinkler

Clean the sprinkler after each use to remove dirt and debris. This stops clogging and keeps water flowing freely.

Use mild soap and water with a soft cloth or sponge. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the material.

* Rinse with clean water after washing
* Check spray nozzles for blockages
* Use a toothpick or soft brush to clear small holes
* Let it dry completely before storing

### Storage Suggestions

Store the sprinkler in a dry place away from direct sunlight. This prevents fading and material wear.

Keep it folded or rolled gently to avoid cracks or tears. Avoid heavy items on top to keep its shape.

* Drain all water before storing
* Keep away from sharp objects
* Store indoors during cold weather
* Use a storage bag or box for protection

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the sprinkler does not spray water well, check for blockages or leaks. Fix small holes with waterproof tape.

If water pressure is low, make sure the hose is fully connected and the water source is open.

* Clear clogged nozzles gently
* Check hose connection and tighten if loose
* Patch small holes with waterproof tape
* Replace damaged parts if needed
