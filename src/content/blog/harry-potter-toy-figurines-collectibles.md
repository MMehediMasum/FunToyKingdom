## Materials And Craftsmanship

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=harry-potter-toy-figurines-are-made-with-different-materials.-each-material-affects-how-the-figurine-looks-and-feels.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Harry Potter toy figurines are made with different materials. Each material affects how the figurine looks and feels.

Craftsmanship is important. It shows in the details and quality of each figurine.

### Plastic Vs. Resin Figurines

Plastic figurines are lightweight and durable. They are common for everyday play.

Resin figurines are heavier and more fragile. They show fine details better and are for collectors.

* Plastic: affordable, less detailed, good for kids
* Resin: expensive, highly detailed, made for display

### Hand-painted Details

Hand-painting adds a unique touch to each figurine. It brings characters to life with colors and shading.

Skilled painters use fine brushes to add small details. This work makes figurines look more realistic.

* Eyes, hair, and clothes get special attention
* Small imperfections show the hand-made quality
* Hand-painting increases the value of collectibles

### Size And Scale Variations

Figurines come in many sizes. Some are small for easy display, others are large for detail.

Scale means how big the figurine is compared to the real character. Common scales include 1:12 and 1:6.

| Scale | Size Range | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1:12 | 5 to 6 inches | Standard display size |
| 1:6 | 11 to 12 inches | Highly detailed collectors’ pieces |
| Miniature | Under 3 inches | Keychains and small sets |

## Caring For Your Figurines

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=harry-potter-toy-figurines-bring-magic-to-any-collection.-keeping-them-clean-and-safe-helps-them-last-long.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Harry Potter toy figurines bring magic to any collection. Keeping them clean and safe helps them last long. Simple care steps protect your favorite characters.

Follow these tips to keep your figurines looking their best. Proper care also keeps their details sharp and colors bright.

### Cleaning Tips

Dust and dirt can build up on figurines. Clean them gently to avoid damage. Use soft tools and mild cleaners.

* Use a soft brush to remove dust
* Wipe with a damp microfiber cloth
* Avoid strong chemicals or abrasive cleaners
* Dry figurines completely after cleaning
* Clean regularly to prevent buildup

### Storage Solutions

Proper storage keeps figurines safe from dust and damage. Choose the right place and containers to protect your collection.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Storage Type | Best Use | Notes |
| Display Case | Showcasing figurines | Protects from dust and sunlight |
| Plastic Containers | Storing many figurines | Use soft padding inside |
| Original Boxes | Long-term storage | Keep in cool, dry place |
| Shelves with Covers | Easy access display | Use glass or acrylic covers |

### Preventing Damage

Figurines can break or fade if not handled well. Follow these tips to avoid accidents and keep colors fresh.

* Handle figurines with clean, dry hands
* Keep figurines away from direct sunlight
* Do not expose to extreme heat or cold
* Place figurines on stable surfaces
* Keep away from pets and small children
