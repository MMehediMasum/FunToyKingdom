## Caring For Your Funko Pop Anime Figures

Funko Pop anime action figures are prized collectibles. They need proper care to keep them looking great. This guide will help you maintain your figures.

Learn how to clean, protect, and store your Funko Pop figures. Simple steps will ensure they remain in top condition.

### Cleaning Techniques

Dust can make your Funko Pop figures look dull. Cleaning them regularly keeps them fresh and vibrant.

* Use a soft brush to remove dust.
* Wipe with a microfiber cloth for gentle cleaning.
* For stubborn dirt, use a damp cloth with mild soap.

### Protective Cases

Protective cases shield your figures from damage. They prevent scratches and keep dust away.

Choose cases that fit your figures perfectly. Clear plastic cases are popular because they allow you to display your figures while keeping them safe.

### Avoiding Sunlight Damage

Sunlight can fade the colors of your Funko Pop figures. Keep them out of direct sunlight to preserve their vibrant colors.

Store your figures in a cool, shaded place. Curtains or blinds can help block harmful UV rays.
