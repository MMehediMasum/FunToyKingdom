## Tips For Safe Riding

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=riding-a-toy-car-with-a-realistic-steering-wheel-is-fun.-safety-is-very-important-for-kids-while-riding.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Riding a toy car with a realistic steering wheel is fun. Safety is very important for kids while riding.

Follow these safety tips to keep your child safe and happy during playtime.

### Helmet And Protective Gear

Always make sure your child wears a helmet. It protects the head from injuries.

Use extra protective gear like knee pads and elbow pads. These reduce scrapes and bruises.

* Choose a helmet that fits well and is comfortable
* Check straps to keep the helmet secure
* Wear knee and elbow pads for added protection

### Supervision Guidelines

An adult should watch children while they ride. This helps prevent accidents and injuries.

Stay close enough to help if the child loses control or needs assistance.

* Keep an eye on the child at all times
* Teach children basic rules before they start
* Stop play immediately if unsafe behavior occurs

### Safe Riding Areas

Choose flat, smooth surfaces for riding. Avoid steep slopes and busy roads.

Safe areas reduce the risk of falls and collisions with obstacles or vehicles.

* Use driveways, playgrounds, or parks with clear space
* Keep away from streets and parking lots
* Remove toys and objects that block the path
