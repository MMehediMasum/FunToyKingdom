## Setting Up The Sprinkler

### Inflation Tips

Use an electric pump for quick and full inflation. Avoid over-inflating the peacock to keep it safe.

Check for leaks by listening for hissing sounds. Secure the valve tightly after inflation to keep air inside.

* Place the inflatable on a flat surface before inflating
* Keep the blower connected during use
* Do not use sharp objects near the inflatable

### Connecting To Water Source

Attach a garden hose to the sprinkler’s water inlet. Make sure the connection is tight to avoid leaks.

Turn on the water slowly. Adjust the water pressure for the best spray effect without damaging the sprinkler.

* Use a standard garden hose connector
* Check for leaks at the connection point
* Turn off water before disconnecting

### Placement For Best Coverage

Place the peacock sprinkler on a flat, open area. Make sure nothing blocks the water spray.

Check the spray direction and adjust the position to cover your lawn or garden evenly.

## Maintenance And Storage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=proper-care-helps-your-outdoor-peacock-sprinkler-inflatable-last-longer.-clean-and-store-it-well-after-use.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Proper care helps your Outdoor Peacock Sprinkler Inflatable last longer. Clean and store it well after use.

Follow these tips to keep your inflatable in good shape and ready for next season.

### Cleaning Tips

Clean the inflatable after each use to remove dirt and debris. Use a soft cloth and mild soap with water.

Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the material. Rinse well and let it dry completely before storing.

* Wipe gently with soap and water
* Rinse off all soap residue
* Dry in a shaded area to avoid sun damage
* Check for small tears or holes

### Deflation And Packing

Turn off the blower and open the air valves to deflate the inflatable. Press gently to remove trapped air.

Fold the inflatable neatly without sharp creases. Packing it loosely helps prevent damage during storage.

* Fully deflate before folding
* Fold in sections to avoid stress points
* Store in a dry, cool place
* Use the original bag if possible

### Seasonal Storage Advice

Store the inflatable indoors during winter or long breaks. Cold and moisture can weaken the material over time.

Keep it away from sharp objects and rodents. Check the inflatable before each use for any needed repairs.

* Choose a dry, temperature-controlled storage spot
* Avoid folding in the same place repeatedly
* Inspect for damage before storing and after removal
* Keep it off the ground if possible
