## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Is Best For Stacking Toys Development?



Stacking toys are ideal for babies aged 6 months to 3 years. They support motor skills and cognitive growth. Early use improves hand-eye coordination and problem-solving abilities. Different toys suit various age groups, from soft blocks for infants to wooden puzzles for toddlers.



### How Do Stacking Toys Improve Fine Motor Skills?



Stacking toys encourage grasping, lifting, and placing objects precisely. These actions strengthen finger muscles and hand coordination. Fine motor skills develop as children learn to manipulate shapes and sizes. This foundation aids writing, buttoning, and other daily tasks later.



### Which Stacking Toys Support Sensory Development?



Toys like the Jyusmile Montessori set and SpringFlower blocks feature varied textures and colors. These stimulate touch, sight, and spatial awareness. Sensory balls and teething blocks also enhance tactile and oral sensory skills, crucial for early brain development and exploration.



### Can Stacking Toys Aid Early Stem Learning?



Yes, stacking toys teach basic STEM concepts like counting, sorting, and spatial reasoning. Sets like Sassy Stacks of Circles introduce shapes and size differentiation. These activities build logical thinking and early math skills naturally through play.



### What Materials Are Best For Safe Stacking Toys?



Wooden and BPA-free plastic toys are safest for babies. They are durable, non-toxic, and easy to clean. Montessori wooden toys and Fisher-Price sets use child-safe finishes. Avoid small parts that pose choking hazards for younger children.



### How Do Stacking Toys Support Language Development?



Stacking toys encourage interaction and naming shapes, colors, and numbers. Caregivers often describe actions during play, boosting vocabulary. This social engagement fosters communication skills and cognitive connections related to language learning.
