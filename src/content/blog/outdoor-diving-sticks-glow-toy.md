## Care And Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=outdoor-diving-sticks-glow-toys-are-fun-to-use-in-pools-and-lakes.-taking-good-care-helps-them-last-longer-and-glow-brighter.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Outdoor diving sticks glow toys are fun to use in pools and lakes. Taking good care helps them last longer and glow brighter.

Simple care and cleaning keep the toys safe and ready for the next dive.

### Cleaning After Use

Rinse the diving sticks with fresh water after each use. This removes dirt and chemicals that can damage the glow surface.

Use a soft cloth or sponge to wipe them clean. Avoid harsh soaps or scrubbing too hard to keep the glow layer safe.

* Rinse with cool, fresh water
* Wipe gently with a soft cloth
* Do not use strong detergents
* Dry completely before storing

### Storage Recommendations

Store diving sticks in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Sunlight can fade the glow and weaken the plastic.

Keep them separated from sharp objects to avoid scratches. Use a mesh bag or box with soft lining for protection.

* Keep away from direct sunlight
* Store in a dry, cool area
* Avoid contact with sharp items
* Use soft storage bags or boxes

### Extending Glow Life

Charge the glow sticks under bright light before use. Natural sunlight or strong indoor light works best.

Do not leave the sticks glowing all the time. Turn off lights and store them to save their glow for longer playtime.

* Charge under bright light for at least 10 minutes
* Avoid overexposure to light when not in use
* Store in a dark place to keep glow longer
* Handle gently to prevent damage to glow surface
