## Getting Started With Toy Story Monopoly

Toy Story Monopoly is a fun board game for friends and family. It brings the Toy Story world to life with familiar characters and places.

This guide will help you get ready to play. We will cover the game parts, how to set up the board, and choosing your character.

### Game Components

The game has many pieces to enjoy. Knowing each part helps you play better and faster.

* Game board featuring Toy Story locations
* Character tokens representing Toy Story heroes
* Property cards for buying and trading
* Play money in various denominations
* Chance and Community Chest cards with surprises
* Houses and hotels to build on properties
* Dice to move your token around the board

### Setting Up The Board

Place the board on a flat surface. Make sure everyone can reach it easily.

| Item | Where to Place | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Game board | Center of the table | Flat and stable surface |
| Money | Near the banker | Organize by denomination |
| Property cards | Next to the board | Shuffle and stack face down |
| Chance and Community Chest cards | On their spaces on the board | Shuffle separately |
| Houses and hotels | Close to the board | Keep in easy reach |
| Dice | Near the players | Ready for rolling |

### Choosing Your Character

Each player picks a character token. Your token shows your place on the board.

* Woody
* Buzz Lightyear
* Jessie
* Mr. Potato Head
* Rex
* Alien

Pick the character you like best. Place your token on the “Go” space to start.




## Basic Rules And Gameplay

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=toy-story-monopoly-is-a-fun-board-game-based-on-the-toy-story-movies.-players-buy-properties,-collect-rent,-and-try-to-earn-the-most-money.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Toy Story Monopoly is a fun board game based on the Toy Story movies. Players buy properties, collect rent, and try to earn the most money. The goal is to be the richest player by the end of the game.

Each player takes turns moving around the board, buying properties, and using special cards. The game uses familiar Monopoly rules with a Toy Story twist.

### Turn Sequence

Players take turns in order, rolling two dice to move their token. After moving, they follow the instructions on the space they land on. Then the next player takes a turn.

Each turn includes these steps:

* Roll the dice and move your token.
* Follow the space’s instructions.
* Buy property if available.
* Pay rent if you land on another player’s property.
* Use special cards if you want.

### Buying Properties

When you land on an unowned property, you can buy it. Pay the bank the price shown on the board. Then place your token on the property.

If you do not want to buy the property, the bank sells it at auction. Players can bid to get the property at a lower price.

### Collecting Rent

If another player lands on your property, they must pay you rent. The rent amount depends on the property type and if you own all properties of the same color.

Rent increases if you add houses or hotels to your properties.

| Property Type | Rent Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Single Property | Base Rent |
| Full Color Set | Double Rent |
| With Houses/Hotels | Higher Rent |

### Using Special Cards

Toy Story Monopoly has special cards that add fun rules. These cards can help you move, avoid rent, or earn money.

Players draw special cards when landing on certain spaces. You can use these cards on your turn or save them for later.

* Move cards let you jump to specific spaces.
* Rent-free cards protect you from paying rent once.
* Money cards give you extra cash from the bank.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is The Objective Of Toy Story Monopoly?

The objective is to buy properties and collect rent. Players aim to bankrupt opponents by smart trading and investing. It’s a fun twist on classic Monopoly with Toy Story themes.

### How Do I Start Playing Toy Story Monopoly?

Each player chooses a character token, receives starting money, and places their token on “Go. ” Players roll dice to move and follow board instructions. The game begins with player turns in clockwise order.

### Can I Trade Properties In Toy Story Monopoly?

Yes, players can trade properties, money, or get creative deals. Trading is key to building property sets and winning. Make strategic trades to gain an advantage over opponents.

### What Happens When I Land On A Chance Or Community Chest?

Draw a card from the respective deck and follow instructions. Cards can provide money, force payments, or move your token. These add excitement and unpredictability to the game.
