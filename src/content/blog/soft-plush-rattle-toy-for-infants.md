## Maintaining And Cleaning Plush Rattles

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=soft-plush-rattles-for-infants-need-regular-care-to-stay-clean-and-safe.-proper-cleaning-helps-keep-germs-away-and-maintains-the-toy's-softness.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Soft plush rattles for infants need regular care to stay clean and safe. Proper cleaning helps keep germs away and maintains the toy's softness.

Following simple steps can extend the life of the plush rattle. This guide covers washing, durability, and storage tips.

### Washing Instructions

Check the label on the rattle before washing. Most plush rattles can be hand washed or machine washed on a gentle cycle.

Use mild detergent and cold water to avoid damage. Rinse well to remove all soap. Air dry the rattle completely before giving it back to the baby.

* Remove batteries or noise makers if possible
* Use a mesh laundry bag for machine washing
* Do not use bleach or strong chemicals
* Dry flat or hang to avoid misshaping

### Durability Tips

Inspect the rattle often for loose threads or parts. Fix small tears early to prevent bigger damage. Avoid rough play that can break the toy.

Keep the rattle away from pets and sharp objects. Use gentle cleaning methods to protect the fabric and stuffing.

* Check seams and stitching regularly
* Repair small holes with needle and thread
* Do not pull or tug on attached parts
* Keep away from pets and sharp items

### Safe Storage Practices

Store plush rattles in a clean, dry place. Avoid damp areas to stop mold and mildew growth. Use a dedicated basket or box for infant toys.

Keep rattles out of reach of older children and pets. Regularly clean the storage area to keep it free of dust and dirt.

* Use a breathable container for storage
* Keep away from moisture and heat sources
* Clean storage space regularly
* Separate infant toys from other household items
