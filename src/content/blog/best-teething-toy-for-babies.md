## Signs Of Teething In Babies

Teething is a natural process when babies start to grow their first teeth. It can cause different signs and symptoms. Parents often want to know what to expect during this stage.

Recognizing teething signs helps parents comfort their babies. It also helps to choose the best teething toys to ease discomfort.

### Common Symptoms

Babies show many signs when they begin teething. These signs can vary from one baby to another.

* Increased drooling
* Chewing on objects
* Swollen or tender gums
* Fussiness or irritability
* Changes in sleeping patterns
* Loss of appetite or refusal to eat
* Rubbing their face or ears
* Occasional mild fever

These symptoms usually last until the tooth breaks through the gums. If symptoms get worse, it is good to check with a doctor.

### When Teething Usually Starts

Teething usually begins when babies are around six months old. Some babies start earlier or later, which is also normal.

Most babies get their first tooth between four and seven months. The bottom front teeth usually come in first.

* 4 to 7 months: First teeth appear
* 6 to 10 months: More front teeth arrive
* 9 to 13 months: Molars may start to come
* 12 to 18 months: Canines usually grow
* 18 to 24 months: More molars appear

## Criteria For Safe Teething Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-teething-toy-is-important-for-your-baby's-safety.-not-all-toys-are-safe-to-chew-on.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right teething toy is important for your baby's safety. Not all toys are safe to chew on. Parents should look for certain features before buying.

Safe teething toys help soothe babies without causing harm. This guide explains key criteria to check when selecting toys.

### Material Safety

Materials must be free of harmful chemicals. Babies put toys in their mouths, so avoid toxic substances. Look for BPA-free, phthalate-free, and lead-free materials.

Natural materials like food-grade silicone or untreated wood are good choices. They are gentle on gums and safe to chew.

* Check for non-toxic labels
* Avoid plastic with harmful chemicals
* Choose soft, chewable materials
* Prefer natural or medical-grade silicone

### Size And Shape

Toys should not be small enough to swallow or choke on. They need to fit comfortably in baby’s hands without sharp edges.

Rounded shapes reduce injury risk. Avoid toys with detachable small parts or long strings that could cause choking.

* Choose large enough toys to avoid choking
* Check for smooth, rounded edges
* No small or removable parts
* Avoid long cords or strings

### Ease Of Cleaning

Teething toys must be cleaned often to prevent germs. Look for toys that are easy to wash or dishwasher safe.

Avoid toys with hard-to-reach areas where dirt can hide. Simple designs make cleaning faster and more effective.

* Choose dishwasher-safe toys if possible
* Simple shapes clean better
* Check for mold-resistant materials
* Avoid toys with tiny crevices

## Types Of Teething Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=teething-toys-help-babies-ease-the-pain-when-new-teeth-come-in.-different-types-of-toys-offer-unique-benefits-and-textures.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Teething toys help babies ease the pain when new teeth come in. Different types of toys offer unique benefits and textures.

Parents can choose from silicone, wooden, gel-filled, and fabric teethers. Each type suits different baby needs and preferences.

### Silicone Teethers

Silicone teethers are soft and flexible. They are gentle on babies’ gums and easy to clean.

Many silicone teethers come in fun shapes and bright colors. Babies enjoy chewing on them to relieve discomfort.

### Wooden Teethers

Wooden teethers are firm and natural. They provide a different texture that some babies prefer.

These teethers are often made from safe, untreated wood. They are durable and free from harmful chemicals.

### Gel-filled Teethers

Gel-filled teethers can be cooled in the fridge. The cold helps soothe swollen gums and reduce pain.

They are usually soft and easy for babies to hold. The gel inside stays cool longer than plain plastic.

### Fabric Teethers

Fabric teethers are soft and often combined with crinkly or textured parts. They are gentle for babies to chew.

Many fabric teethers can be wet and frozen for extra relief. They are lightweight and easy to carry around.

## Tips To Soothe Teething Discomfort

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=teething-can-be-uncomfortable-for-babies-and-stressful-for-parents.-finding-ways-to-ease-this-discomfort-helps-your-baby-feel-better.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Teething can be uncomfortable for babies and stressful for parents. Finding ways to ease this discomfort helps your baby feel better.

Try gentle methods to calm your baby during this stage. Here are some helpful tips to soothe teething pain.

### Cold Therapy

Cold objects can reduce swelling and numb sore gums. Use safe, chilled items to comfort your baby.

* Use a clean, damp washcloth cooled in the fridge
* Offer a chilled teething ring made of safe materials
* Avoid freezing teething toys as they can become too hard

### Massage Techniques

Gently massaging your baby’s gums can ease pain and pressure. Use clean hands and soft touch.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Technique | How to Do It |
| Finger Massage | Wash hands, rub gums softly in circular motions |
| Teething Brush | Use a soft-bristled brush designed for babies |
| Cold Finger | Chill your finger in cold water, then massage gums |

### Safe Chewing Practices

Chewing helps relieve teething pain. Choose safe toys and supervise your baby closely.

Tips for safe chewing:

* Pick BPA-free, non-toxic teething toys
* Check toys for small parts or loose pieces
* Clean toys regularly to prevent germs
* Never leave your baby unattended with a teething toy




## When To Consult A Pediatrician

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-best-teething-toy-for-your-baby-is-important.-sometimes,-teething-can-cause-issues-that-need-a-doctor's-help.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the best teething toy for your baby is important. Sometimes, teething can cause issues that need a doctor's help.

Parents should know when to consult a pediatrician. This ensures the health and comfort of their baby.

### Signs Of Infection

Teething can sometimes lead to infections. Watch for these signs to know if you need to call a doctor.

* Fever that doesn't go away
* Swollen, red gums
* Pus or unusual discharge
* Refusal to eat or drink

### Severe Discomfort

Teething can be uncomfortable, but severe pain needs attention. If your baby shows these signs, consult a pediatrician.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Symptom | Description |
| Constant Crying | More than usual, even after comfort |
| Disturbed Sleep | Waking up frequently and not settling down |
| Excessive Drooling | More than typical teething drool |

If you notice these symptoms, contact your pediatrician. They can provide advice and treatment to help your baby feel better.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Features Make A Teething Toy Best For Babies?

The best teething toys are safe, non-toxic, and easy to grasp. They provide soothing relief and stimulate sensory development. Soft textures and chewable materials help comfort sore gums effectively.

### When Should I Start Giving My Baby A Teething Toy?

You can introduce teething toys around 3 to 4 months of age. This timing helps soothe gums as teeth begin to emerge. Always supervise your baby while using the toy to ensure safety.

### Are Silicone Teething Toys Better Than Plastic Ones?

Silicone teething toys are generally safer and more flexible. They are BPA-free, soft on gums, and easy to clean. Plastic toys may be harder and less comfortable for babies to chew on.

### How Do I Clean And Maintain Teething Toys Safely?

Clean teething toys with warm, soapy water regularly. Some toys are dishwasher safe; check manufacturer instructions. Avoid harsh chemicals to keep toys safe and hygienic for babies.
