## Collecting Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collecting-guardians-of-the-galaxy-action-figures-is-fun-and-rewarding.-you-can-build-a-great-set-with-care-and-knowledge.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collecting Guardians of the Galaxy action figures is fun and rewarding. You can build a great set with care and knowledge.

This guide helps you find, check, and keep your figures safe. Follow these tips to enjoy your collection for years.

### Where To Buy

You can find action figures in many places. Check stores, online shops, and collector fairs for the best finds.

Look for sellers who have good reviews and clear photos. This helps you avoid bad purchases.

* Local toy stores and comic shops
* Online marketplaces like eBay and Amazon
* Collector conventions and fairs
* Official Marvel merchandise stores

### Authenticity Checks

Always check if the action figure is real. Fake toys may look similar but have lower quality.

Look for official logos, serial numbers, and packaging details. These signs show your figure is authentic.

* Check for Marvel or licensed branding
* Inspect the packaging for seals or holograms
* Compare details with official product photos
* Ask for proof of purchase if possible

### Maintaining And Storing

Keep your action figures clean and safe. Dust and sunlight can damage them over time.

Use display cases or boxes to protect your figures. Store them in a cool, dry place away from direct light.

* Dust regularly with a soft cloth
* Use plastic display cases to avoid damage
* Keep figures away from heat and sunlight
* Store in original boxes if possible
