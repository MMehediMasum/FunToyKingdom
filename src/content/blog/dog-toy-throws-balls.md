## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Ball Launcher Suits My Dog's Weight?



Choose a ball launcher based on your dog's weight. For dogs 0-20 lbs, use the Chuckit! Sport 14S. For 20-60 lbs, the Chuckit! Sport 12" with Medium ball fits well. For 60-100 lbs, the Chuckit! Sport 26L with a large ball is ideal.



### Are Automatic Ball Launchers Safe For Indoor Use?



Yes, many automatic ball launchers like ALL FOR PAWS and Franklin Pet Supply Co. models are designed for indoor use. They have adjustable launch distances and soft balls to prevent damage or injury indoors, making them safe and fun for your pet.



### How Far Can Dog Ball Throwers Launch Balls?



Distances vary by model. Nerf Dog Tennis Ball Blaster launches up to 50 feet. Franklin Pet Supply Co. Ready Set Fetch throws up to 40 feet. MEKAPLE launcher has settings from 10 to 30 feet. Choose based on your outdoor space.



### What Materials Make Durable Dog Fetch Balls?



Durable balls like Chuckit! Ultra Ball are made from tough rubber. They withstand chewing and are safe for dogs. Many balls float, making them great for water play. Always pick non-toxic, pet-safe materials for long-lasting fun.



### Can Small Dogs Use Standard Tennis Ball Launchers?



Small dogs need smaller balls and shorter launchers. The Chuckit! Sport 14S with a 2-inch ball suits dogs under 20 lbs. Using appropriate size prevents injury and ensures your dog can comfortably catch and carry the ball.
