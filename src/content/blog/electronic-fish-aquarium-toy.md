## Maintenance And Care Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-your-electronic-fish-aquarium-toy-helps-it-last-longer.-regular-maintenance-keeps-it-working-smoothly.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of your electronic fish aquarium toy helps it last longer. Regular maintenance keeps it working smoothly.

Follow simple steps to replace batteries, clean the aquarium, and fix common problems.

### Battery Replacement

Check the battery level often to keep the fish moving. Replace batteries when the toy slows down or stops.

Use the right battery type and insert it correctly. Turn off the toy before changing batteries to avoid damage.

* Open the battery cover carefully
* Remove old batteries safely
* Insert new batteries with the correct polarity
* Close the battery cover securely

### Cleaning The Aquarium

Clean the aquarium to keep the toy looking nice and working well. Dirt and dust can block sensors or parts.

Use a soft cloth or sponge to wipe the inside and outside gently. Avoid harsh chemicals that can harm the toy.

* Turn off and unplug the toy before cleaning
* Use mild soap and water for cleaning
* Dry the aquarium completely before use
* Clean regularly to prevent build-up

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the fish do not move or the toy does not turn on, try simple fixes first. Many problems have easy solutions.

Check batteries, clean the toy, and look for loose parts. If problems continue, consult the manual or customer support.

* Replace old or weak batteries
* Clean sensors and moving parts
* Ensure no water or moisture inside electronics
* Check for broken or stuck pieces
