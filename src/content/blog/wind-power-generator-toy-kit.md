Wind moves the blades of the toy kit. The blades are connected to a small generator. As the blades spin, they generate electricity.

The blades catch the wind. They are shaped to spin easily. The spinning motion turns the generator.

The generator is the heart of the kit. It converts mechanical energy from the blades into electricity. This electricity can light up a small bulb or power a motor.
