## Safety Tips For Kids

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=ride-on-toys-with-working-headlights-are-fun-for-kids.-they-add-excitement-and-make-riding-feel-real.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Ride on toys with working headlights are fun for kids. They add excitement and make riding feel real.

Safety is very important when kids use these toys. Parents and caregivers should follow some simple tips.

### Proper Supervision

Always watch children while they ride on the toy. Stay close to help if needed.

Supervision prevents accidents and keeps kids safe. Never leave young children alone with ride on toys.

### Protective Gear

Kids should wear protective gear every time they ride. Helmets protect their heads from injury.

Use knee pads, elbow pads, and closed shoes to protect against falls and scrapes.

* Helmet that fits well
* Knee and elbow pads
* Closed-toe shoes

### Safe Riding Areas

Choose flat, smooth areas away from roads and traffic. Avoid steep hills and rough surfaces.

Clear the area of obstacles like rocks, sticks, and toys. This reduces the chance of falls.

* Flat pavement or driveway
* Quiet parks or playgrounds
* Avoid streets and busy areas
