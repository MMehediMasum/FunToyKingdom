## Caring For Your Figurines

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=toy-soldier-figurines-add-charm-to-holiday-decor.-keeping-them-clean-and-safe-helps-them-last-longer.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Toy soldier figurines add charm to holiday decor. Keeping them clean and safe helps them last longer. Proper care keeps their colors bright and details sharp.

Simple steps can protect your figurines from dust, damage, and fading. Follow these tips to enjoy your collection year after year.

### Cleaning Tips

Dust your figurines often with a soft brush or cloth. Avoid using water or strong cleaners that can damage paint.

Use a small, dry paintbrush to reach tiny details. Be gentle to prevent scratches or paint loss.

* Use a microfiber cloth for larger surfaces
* Compressed air can remove dust from hard spots
* Do not soak or scrub figurines

### Storage Solutions

Store figurines in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Sunlight can fade colors and weaken materials over time.

Use padded boxes or display cases to prevent scratches and chips. Keep figurines separated to avoid collisions.

* Wrap figurines in soft cloth or bubble wrap
* Label boxes for easy finding
* Store in stable temperature and humidity

### Preserving Paint And Finish

Keep figurines away from heat sources like radiators. Heat can cause paint to crack or peel.

Avoid touching painted surfaces often. Oils from skin can dull the finish and cause damage.

* Handle figurines by the base or unpainted parts
* Apply a clear, matte sealant if recommended by the manufacturer
* Check for any chips or cracks regularly
