## Frequently Asked Questions



### Are Rope Dog Toys Safe For Aggressive Chewers?



Yes, rope dog toys made from durable cotton like Feeko and Fida are safe for aggressive chewers. They resist fraying and help clean teeth while providing strong tugging fun.



### How Do Rope Toys Help With Dog Dental Health?



Rope toys like Mammoth Flossy Chews support dental health by flossing teeth during chewing. The braided cotton fibers remove plaque and reduce tartar buildup naturally.



### Can Rope Toys Prevent Boredom In Dogs?



Absolutely. Interactive rope toys such as Aitmexcn and Sunglow engage dogs in tug-of-war and chewing. This mental stimulation reduces destructive behaviors caused by boredom.



### Are Rope Dog Toys Suitable For Puppies?



Yes, puppy-specific rope toys like HESUILONG are designed for teething relief. They are soft enough for puppies’ developing teeth but tough enough for chewing.



### What Size Rope Toy Is Best For My Dog?



Choose rope toys like SZEE or TLAZZ based on your dog’s size. Small breeds need lighter, shorter ropes; large breeds require thicker, longer durable ropes.



### How Do I Clean And Maintain Rope Dog Toys?



Most rope toys made from natural cotton are washable. Hand wash or machine wash on gentle cycles, then air dry to maintain durability and hygiene.
