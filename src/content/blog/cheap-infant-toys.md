## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Safest Materials For Infant Toys?



Safe infant toys are made from BPA-free silicone, soft fabric, and non-toxic plastics. These materials prevent choking and allergic reactions. For example, Bright Starts Oball toys use BPA-free plastic, ensuring safety for newborns and up to 2-year-olds. Always check product labels for safety certifications.



### How Do Teething Toys Relieve Infant Discomfort?



Teething toys provide gentle pressure on gums, soothing pain during teething. Silicone teethers like the 3 Pack Ring Teether are soft and easy to grip. They also promote healthy chewing habits and help infants develop hand coordination while offering relief.



### Which Toys Support Infant Sensory Development?



Sensory toys with varied textures, colors, and sounds enhance infant brain growth. Soft stuffed toys with crinkles and rattles stimulate touch and hearing. Montessori sensory toys encourage exploration and learning, aiding motor skills and cognitive development for babies aged 0-18 months.



### What Age Range Suits Bright Starts Oball Toys?



Bright Starts Oball toys are designed for newborns and up to toddlers aged 2 years. Different models cater to specific stages, such as rattles for 0-6 months and crawling toys for 3 months and up. Always choose toys labeled for your child's age.



### Are Multi-pack Toy Sets Cost-effective For Infants?



Multi-pack toy sets, like the 3 Pack Ring Teether or 18Pcs Baby Toys, offer variety and value. They keep infants engaged with different textures and shapes. Buying sets often reduces cost per toy, making them budget-friendly for parents seeking affordable infant entertainment.



### How Do Montessori Toys Benefit Infant Learning?



Montessori toys promote independent play and sensory exploration. They improve fine motor skills and problem-solving abilities. Toys like the Tissue Box Toy encourage curiosity and hand-eye coordination. These toys support natural development by allowing babies to discover at their own pace.
