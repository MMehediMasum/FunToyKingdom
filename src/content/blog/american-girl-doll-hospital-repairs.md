## Common Doll Damages

American Girl dolls are loved by many children. Over time, these dolls may get damaged from play or storage. Knowing common damages helps in fixing them properly.

Some damages happen often and can be repaired at the doll hospital. Below are typical issues found in American Girl dolls.

### Worn Hair And Tangling

The dolls’ hair can get worn out after lots of brushing and playing. Hair may become tangled and hard to comb.

Hair can also lose its shine and look messy. This happens when hair strands break or get dirty.

### Cracked Or Broken Limbs

Doll arms and legs can crack or break from falls or rough play. These damages make the doll hard to pose or hold.

Sometimes, the limbs may loosen and not stay attached firmly to the body. This affects the doll’s balance and use.

### Faded Facial Features

The doll’s face can fade after being in sunlight or getting wet. Eyes, lips, and cheeks lose their color over time.

Faded features make the doll look older and less bright. Restoring colors needs special care to keep the doll natural.

### Loose Or Missing Eyes

Eyes may become loose inside the head or fall out completely. This happens if the doll is dropped or handled roughly.

Missing eyes change the doll’s look and can be hard to replace without the right parts. Fixing eyes keeps the doll’s original charm.

## Hair Restoration Techniques

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=american-girl-dolls-need-special-care-to-keep-their-hair-looking-nice.-restoring-their-hair-takes-gentle-work-and-the-right-tools.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

American Girl dolls need special care to keep their hair looking nice. Restoring their hair takes gentle work and the right tools. This guide covers key methods for hair care and repair.

We will explain how to detangle and wash the hair, re-root hair when needed, and style the hair to keep the doll looking beautiful.

### Detangling And Washing

Start by carefully detangling the doll’s hair with a wide-tooth comb. Use gentle strokes from the ends toward the scalp. This prevents breakage and damage.

* Use lukewarm water to wet the hair.
* Apply a small amount of mild shampoo made for dolls or gentle baby shampoo.
* Rinse the hair thoroughly to remove all soap.
* Pat the hair dry with a soft towel. Do not rub or twist.
* Let the hair air dry completely before styling.

### Re-rooting Hair

If the doll has bald spots or missing hair, re-rooting is a good fix. This means adding new hair strands to the scalp.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Tool** | **Purpose** |
| Re-rooting needle | Pushes hair strands into the scalp holes |
| Replacement hair | Matches original hair color and texture |
| Glue | Secures hair inside the scalp |
| Scissors | Trims hair to the right length after re-rooting |

Insert each hair strand carefully and glue it inside the head. Let the glue dry fully before trimming or styling the hair.

### Styling Tips

After washing or re-rooting, style the hair gently. Avoid heat tools as they can melt synthetic hair fibers.

**Tips for Styling:**

* Use soft brushes made for dolls or baby brushes.
* Try simple styles like braids, ponytails, or loose waves.
* Keep accessories light to avoid pulling on the hair.
* Store the doll with hair covered to prevent tangles.

## Clothing Repairs And Care

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=american-girl-dolls-are-beloved-companions.-keeping-their-clothing-in-good-shape-is-important.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

American Girl Dolls are beloved companions. Keeping their clothing in good shape is important. This guide helps you care for and repair their clothes.

Learn to mend tears, clean fabrics, and replace buttons. These tips will keep your doll looking beautiful.

### Mending Tears And Seams

Doll clothes can tear during play. Mending these tears is simple. Use a needle and matching thread to sew them up.

Focus on small stitches. This makes the repair almost invisible. Always sew on the inside to hide the seam.

* Thread the needle with matching color
* Sew small, tight stitches
* Knot the thread securely at the end

### Cleaning Fabrics

Dirty clothes can make dolls look worn out. Cleaning fabrics is easy with gentle hand washing. Use mild soap and cold water.

Rinse thoroughly and let them air dry. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage delicate fabrics.

* Use mild soap only
* Rinse in cold water
* Air dry completely

### Replacing Buttons And Accessories

Buttons and accessories can fall off with time. Replace them with similar or matching pieces. Sewing new buttons is easy and quick.

Ensure the new button matches the old one's size. Use strong thread to keep it secure.
