## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Rc Bait Boat For Surf Fishing?



The best RC bait boat offers long-range control, strong load capacity, and GPS auto-return. Models like JABOBOAT Dolphin excel for surf fishing with dual motors and waterproof hulls.



### How Far Can These Rc Bait Boats Operate Remotely?



Most RC bait boats for surf fishing have a remote control range of 500 to 600 meters. This allows anglers to deploy bait far offshore for better fishing results.



### What Load Capacity Should A Surf Fishing Bait Boat Have?



A good surf fishing bait boat should carry around 4. 4 lbs (2 kg) of bait. This ensures enough bait is delivered to attract fish effectively.



### Why Is Gps Auto Return Important In Bait Boats?



GPS auto return helps the bait boat safely return to the angler if it loses connection or battery is low, preventing loss in open water.



### How Long Do The Batteries Last In Rc Bait Boats?



High-capacity batteries like 12000mAh or 22000mAh provide several hours of operation. Dual battery systems extend fishing time without frequent recharging.



### Are Led Lights Useful On Surf Fishing Bait Boats?



Yes, LED lights improve visibility during low light or night fishing sessions. They help track the bait boat’s position in surf conditions easily.
