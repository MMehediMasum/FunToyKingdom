## Packing Tips For Travel Games

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=travel-size-board-games-make-trips-more-fun.-they-bring-friends-and-family-together.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Travel size board games make trips more fun. They bring friends and family together. Packing them right keeps all parts safe and easy to carry.

Use smart packing methods to protect pieces and save space. Organize your games so you can find everything fast. Follow these tips to make travel game packing simple.

### Protecting Game Pieces

Small pieces can get lost or broken. Use containers that keep pieces in place. Soft bags or small boxes work well. Wrap fragile pieces in cloth or bubble wrap.

* Store pieces in zip-lock bags
* Keep cards in sleeves or small envelopes
* Label bags to avoid mixing pieces
* Use foam or cloth padding for delicate parts

### Organizing Multiple Games

Bring more than one game for variety. Keep each game’s parts separate to avoid confusion. Use a box or bag with compartments. Group similar games together.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Game Type | Storage Method | Tip |
| Card Games | Small boxes or sleeves | Label each deck |
| Dice Games | Drawstring bags | Keep dice together |
| Board Games | Compact boxes | Store boards flat |

### Maximizing Luggage Space

Use every inch of your luggage well. Pack games between clothes or shoes for extra protection. Stack flat games at the bottom. Fill empty spaces with small items.

1. Place flat games at the base of your bag
2. Put soft games or bags between clothes
3. Use small containers for tiny pieces inside shoes
4. Compress clothes to create more room
5. Carry fragile games in your carry-on bag
