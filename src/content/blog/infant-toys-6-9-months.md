## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Toys For Infants Aged 6 To 9 Months?



The best toys for 6 to 9-month infants include sensory Montessori toys, stacking blocks, and teething toys. These toys support motor skills, sensory development, and teething relief. Examples are Yetonamr pull string teethers, Fisher-Price stacking blocks, and Bloobloomax rattles.



### How Do Montessori Toys Benefit Babies 6 To 9 Months?



Montessori toys encourage independent learning and sensory exploration. They help develop fine motor skills, hand-eye coordination, and cognitive abilities. Toys like shape blocks and sensory cubes stimulate curiosity and problem-solving in infants aged 6 to 9 months.



### Are Teething Toys Safe For 6 To 9-month-old Babies?



Yes, teething toys made from food-grade silicone are safe and effective. They soothe sore gums and promote healthy chewing habits. Products like the Yetonamr silicone teether are designed for infants 6 to 9 months, ensuring safety and comfort.



### Which Toys Support Early Learning For 6 To 9-month-olds?



Early learning toys include musical activity stations, light-up pianos, and cause-and-effect toys. These stimulate auditory senses, cognitive skills, and hand coordination. Examples are Baby Einstein Glow & Discover and Baby Piano Toy for infants 6 to 9 months.



### Can Rattles And Wrist Toys Help Infant Development?



Yes, rattles and wrist toys improve sensory perception and motor skills. They encourage reaching, grasping, and hand movement in infants. Bloobloomax wrist rattle socks and easy-grasp Oball rattles are ideal for babies aged 6 to 9 months.
