## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Chuckit! Ultra Ball Durable For Dogs?



Chuckit! Ultra Balls are made from tough rubber that resists chewing. They float on water and bounce high. These features make them perfect for active dogs weighing 20-60 lbs. They come in medium size, ideal for fetch games both indoors and outdoors.



### Are Squeaky Balls Safe And Fun For Dogs?



Yes, squeaky balls like Outward Hound Squeaker Ballz and Hartz DuraPlay provide interactive fun. They engage dogs by stimulating their natural instincts. Made from durable materials, they withstand moderate chewing and encourage active play, helping reduce boredom and anxiety.



### Which Dog Balls Suit Aggressive Chewers Best?



For aggressive chewers, choose DISSKNIC Indestructible Squeaky Dog Balls or VITEVER Spikey Dog Balls. These toys are made from tough, non-toxic materials. They clean teeth and relieve anxiety while lasting longer, making them ideal for large breeds and heavy chewers.



### Can Dog Balls Be Used For Water Play?



Yes, balls like Chuckit! Ultra Ball and Chuckit! Dog Balls float well. Their durable rubber construction ensures they survive water fetch games. They offer fun and exercise for dogs during swimming or pool play, keeping pets active and entertained.



### What Size Dog Ball Is Best For Medium Breeds?



Medium dog balls typically range from 2. 5 to 4. 75 inches in diameter. Chuckit! Medium balls (2. 5 inches) and Outward Hound Medium balls suit medium breeds perfectly. Choosing the right size prevents choking hazards and ensures comfortable play.



### Are Non-toxic Dog Balls Safe For Puppies?



Non-toxic dog balls like VITEVER and INSUM Tennis Balls are safe for puppies. They are BPA-free and SGS certified. These balls help with teething and provide safe chewing outlets. Always supervise playtime to ensure your puppy's safety.
