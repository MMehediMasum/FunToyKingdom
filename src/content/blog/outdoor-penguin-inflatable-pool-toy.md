## Tips For Maintenance And Storage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-care-of-your-outdoor-penguin-inflatable-pool-toy-helps-it-last-longer.-good-maintenance-keeps-it-clean-and-safe.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking care of your Outdoor Penguin Inflatable Pool Toy helps it last longer. Good maintenance keeps it clean and safe.

Proper storage protects the toy from damage and makes it easy to use again next time.

### Cleaning After Use

Clean the inflatable pool toy after each use to remove dirt and germs. Use mild soap and water for washing.

Rinse well to remove soap and let it dry completely in the shade before storing it.

* Use a soft cloth or sponge to avoid scratches
* Do not use harsh chemicals or bleach
* Dry fully to prevent mold and mildew

### Proper Deflation And Folding

Deflate the toy slowly to avoid damage. Press out the air gently from all parts.

Fold the inflatable neatly in sections. Keep it smooth to stop creases that can cause cracks.

* Start folding from the smallest end
* Keep folds loose, not tight
* Avoid sharp bends or pressure points

### Safe Storage Practices

Store the inflatable in a cool, dry place. Avoid direct sunlight and heat sources.

Keep the toy away from sharp objects and heavy items that might cause tears.

* Use a storage bag or container
* Check for moisture before storing
* Keep it off the floor if possible
