## Safety And Durability Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=outdoor-toys-for-tweens-should-be-safe-and-last-a-long-time.-choosing-the-right-toys-helps-keep-kids-happy-and-injury-free.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Outdoor toys for tweens should be safe and last a long time. Choosing the right toys helps keep kids happy and injury-free.

Knowing how to pick strong, safe toys and care for them extends their use. This guide covers key tips for parents and caregivers.

### Choosing Safe Materials

Select toys made from non-toxic and sturdy materials. Avoid small parts that can break off and cause choking hazards.

Look for labels showing the toy meets safety standards. Materials like BPA-free plastic, wood with smooth finishes, and metal without sharp edges are best.

* Non-toxic plastics and paints
* Rounded edges and smooth surfaces
* Strong parts that do not easily break
* No small detachable pieces
* Certified safety marks or labels

### Weather-resistant Toys

Outdoor toys face sun, rain, and wind. Choose toys made to resist these elements to avoid damage and keep them safe.

Materials like treated wood, rust-proof metal, and UV-resistant plastics last longer outdoors. These toys keep their color and strength through different weather.

* Treated wood resists rot and splinters
* Rust-resistant metals prevent corrosion
* UV-protected plastics avoid fading
* Waterproof coatings help prevent damage

### Maintenance Advice

Regular care keeps outdoor toys safe and durable. Check toys often for damage or wear that could cause injuries.

Clean toys with mild soap and water. Store them in a dry place when not in use to protect from weather damage.

* Inspect toys monthly for cracks or loose parts
* Wash dirt and debris off regularly
* Dry toys before storing to avoid mold
* Keep toys out of direct sun when possible
* Repair or discard broken toys promptly
