Electric ride ons use batteries to move on their own. Kids can control the speed and direction using simple buttons or pedals.

### Push And Pedal Models

Push and pedal ride ons need kids to use their legs to move. This type helps build strength and coordination.

### Balance Bikes

Balance bikes have no pedals. Kids use their feet to push and balance while riding.

These bikes teach balance and prepare children for riding a regular bike. They are lightweight and easy to handle.

## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-ride-on-toy-with-cup-holders-in-good-shape-is-important.-regular-care-helps-it-last-longer-and-work-better.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your ride on toy with cup holders in good shape is important. Regular care helps it last longer and work better.

Follow simple maintenance steps to protect the toy and ensure safe play for kids every time.

### Cleaning And Care

Clean the toy often to remove dirt and dust. Use a soft cloth and mild soap with water. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the surface.

Check the cup holders and other parts for cracks or breaks. Fix or replace any damaged parts quickly to keep the toy safe.

* Wipe down after each use
* Use gentle cleaning solutions
* Inspect for wear and tear regularly
* Dry completely to prevent rust or mold

### Battery Maintenance

Charge the battery fully before the first use. Avoid overcharging by unplugging when full. This keeps the battery healthy.

Check battery connections often. Clean any dirt or corrosion with a dry cloth. Replace batteries that do not hold charge well.

* Charge battery after each use
* Do not leave battery unused for long
* Keep battery contacts clean and dry
* Use the charger made for the toy

### Storing Safely

Store the ride on toy in a dry, cool place. Keep it away from direct sunlight to avoid fading and damage.

Remove the battery if you will not use the toy for a long time. Cover the toy to protect it from dust and moisture.

* Choose a shaded storage area
* Keep off wet or damp floors
* Remove batteries for long storage
* Cover with a cloth or plastic sheet
