### Choosing The Right Location

Pick a flat and open area to set up the toy. The ground should be clean and free of sharp objects.

Make sure there is enough space around the toy for safe play. Avoid placing it near walls or furniture.

* Use grass or soft ground to protect the toy

### Storage And Maintenance
