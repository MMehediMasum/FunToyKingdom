## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=proper-care-helps-your-ride-on-toy-excavator-work-well-for-a-long-time.-regular-maintenance-keeps-it-safe-and-fun.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Proper care helps your ride on toy excavator work well for a long time. Regular maintenance keeps it safe and fun.

Follow these simple tips to clean, store, and care for the battery. Fix small problems early to avoid bigger issues.

### Cleaning And Storage

Keep the toy clean to avoid dirt build-up. Store it in a dry place to prevent damage.

* Wipe the excavator with a damp cloth after use.
* Remove any mud or debris from the shovel and wheels.
* Dry the toy completely before storing it.
* Store indoors or under a cover to protect from rain and sun.
* Check for loose parts before storing.

### Battery Care

Good battery care improves performance and lengthens battery life. Charge and store the battery the right way.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Battery Care Tip** | **How to Do It** |
| Charge Fully | Charge the battery before first use and after each ride |
| Avoid Overcharging | Disconnect charger once battery is full |
| Store Properly | Keep battery in a cool, dry place when not in use |
| Check Connections | Make sure battery terminals are clean and tight |
| Use Correct Charger | Only use the charger that came with the toy |

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

Some problems may happen. Check these tips to fix common troubles quickly.

**Troubleshooting Tips:**

* If the excavator won't start, check the battery charge first.
* For slow movement, clean the wheels and check for stuck debris.
* If the shovel does not move, test the controls and wiring connection.
* Reset the toy by turning it off and on again if it acts strange.
* Consult the manual for error codes or warning lights.
