## Care Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=american-girl-dolls-need-gentle-care-to-stay-nice.-taking-care-helps-them-last-a-long-time.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

American Girl Dolls need gentle care to stay nice. Taking care helps them last a long time.

Learn simple ways to clean dolls and store their accessories safely.

### Cleaning Dolls

Use a soft cloth to wipe the doll's face and body. Avoid water on cloth parts to stop damage.

For hair, use a wide-tooth comb gently. If hair is tangled, spray a little water before combing.

* Do not use harsh cleaners or bleach
* Spot clean stains with mild soap and water
* Let the doll air dry fully after cleaning

### Storing Accessories

Keep accessories in small boxes or bags to avoid losing them. Group similar items together for easy use.

Store accessories in a dry place. Avoid direct sunlight to prevent colors from fading.

* Use clear containers to see accessories quickly
* Label boxes or bags for better organization
* Check accessories regularly for damage or dirt
