## Basic Rules

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=monopoly-junior-toy-story-version-is-a-fun-board-game-for-kids.-it-uses-simple-rules-to-help-players-learn-and-enjoy.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Monopoly Junior Toy Story Version is a fun board game for kids. It uses simple rules to help players learn and enjoy. This guide explains the basic rules to get started quickly.

Players take turns moving around the board and buying properties. The goal is to collect the most money by the end of the game.

### Starting The Game

At the start, each player chooses a character token from Toy Story. Then, every player gets the same amount of money to begin with. The banker shuffles the property cards and places them on the board.

Place the tokens on the "Go" square. The youngest player goes first. Play moves clockwise around the board.

### Taking Turns

1. Roll the dice and move your token forward that many spaces.
2. If you land on a property, you may buy it if it is available.
3. If you choose not to buy, the property stays open for others.
4. Collect money if you land on a space with coins or pass "Go".
5. If you land on a space with an action, follow the instructions on the card.

### Moving Around The Board

| Board Space | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Property | Buy if available, or pay rent if owned |
| Chance | Draw a Chance card and follow instructions |
| Go | Collect $2 each time you pass |
| Jail | Miss one turn if sent to Jail |
