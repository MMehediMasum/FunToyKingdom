## Safety Features To Consider

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=rolling-toys-help-infants-develop-motor-skills-and-have-fun.-choosing-safe-toys-is-very-important.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Rolling toys help infants develop motor skills and have fun. Choosing safe toys is very important. Parents should check key safety features before buying.

Good safety features protect babies from harm. This guide covers three important points to watch for in rolling toys.

### Non-toxic Materials

Infants often put toys in their mouths. Toys made from **[non-toxic materials](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=non-toxic+infant+toys&tag=gearcookware-20)** keep babies safe from harmful chemicals. Look for toys labeled BPA-free and phthalate-free.

Paints and finishes should also be safe. Avoid toys with strong smells or peeling paint. These can be dangerous for infants.

### Stable And Durable Design

Rolling toys must be sturdy and balanced. A stable base prevents tipping over and accidents. Durable materials resist breaking or cracking.

Check wheels and axles for strong attachment. Loose parts can cause injuries or choking risks. The toy should support the baby’s weight safely.

### Size And Choking Hazards

Small parts can block an infant’s airway. Rolling toys should not have detachable tiny pieces. All parts must be large enough to avoid swallowing.

* Avoid toys with buttons, beads, or small wheels that detach
* Make sure all components are securely fastened
* Test parts by trying to pull or bite them gently

## Tips For Choosing The Right Toy

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=rolling-toys-help-infants-develop-motor-skills-and-have-fun.-choosing-the-right-toy-makes-playtime-safe-and-enjoyable.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Rolling toys help infants develop motor skills and have fun. Choosing the right toy makes playtime safe and enjoyable.

Consider your infant’s age, the toy’s material, and where you will use it. This guide covers important points for picking the best rolling toy.

### Age Appropriateness

Infants grow quickly, so choose toys that fit their developmental stage. Toys for younger babies should be simple and safe.

1. Toys for 6-9 months help with grasping and rolling.
2. Toys for 9-12 months encourage crawling and standing.
3. Toys for 12 months and older support walking and pushing.

### Material Preferences

Materials affect safety and durability. Parents often prefer natural or non-toxic materials to protect infants.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material | Advantages | Considerations |
| Wood | Durable, natural feel | May have rough edges if not well-made |
| Plastic | Lightweight, easy to clean | Check for BPA-free and non-toxic labels |
| Rubber | Soft, safe to chew | May wear out faster |

### Space And Portability

Think about where your child will play with the toy. Size and weight matter for small spaces and travel.

* Choose compact toys for limited space at home.
* Look for rolling toys with handles for easy carrying.
* Check if the toy fits in car seats or strollers for trips.
