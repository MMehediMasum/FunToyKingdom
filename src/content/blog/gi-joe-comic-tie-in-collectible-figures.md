## Gi Joe Comic Origins

The Gi Joe comic series began in the 1980s as a tie-in to the toy line. It introduced readers to a world of heroes and villains fighting for control. The comics helped build the story behind the action figures.

These comics gave fans a chance to explore the characters’ backgrounds and missions. They became a key part of the Gi Joe brand’s success and lasting appeal.

### Early Comic Series

The first Gi Joe comics were published by Marvel Comics. They featured military stories with a mix of action and adventure. The series showed the fight between the Gi Joe team and the evil Cobra organization.

* Started in 1982 by Marvel Comics
* Focused on military and spy missions
* Introduced the conflict between Gi Joe and Cobra
* Included detailed character backstories
* Ran for over 150 issues
