## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Is Best For Magnetic Building Toys?



Magnetic building toys suit children aged 3 and above. They promote creativity and STEM learning in toddlers. Sets like Miereirl and JTNFSH offer safe, engaging play for this age group.



### How Do Building Toys Help Toddler Development?



Building toys improve fine motor skills and cognitive abilities. They encourage problem-solving, creativity, and hand-eye coordination in young children. Educational sets like MISS. CHEN and PREXTEX support these developmental areas effectively.



### Are Wooden Blocks Better Than Plastic Ones?



Wooden blocks offer a natural, durable option promoting tactile exploration. Plastic blocks like magnetic sets provide varied shapes and interactive play. Both types support learning but cater to different sensory experiences.



### What Safety Features Should I Look For In Toys?



Look for non-toxic materials, rounded edges, and secure magnets. Toys like Adena Montessori and MEIEST blocks meet safety standards for toddlers. Always check age recommendations and avoid small parts choking hazards.



### Which Building Toys Encourage Stem Learning?



Toys such as JTNFSH magnetic sticks and 80-piece marble run kits enhance STEM skills. They teach engineering, physics, and spatial reasoning through hands-on play. These sets are ideal educational gifts for ages 3 and up.



### Can Building Toys Be Good Birthday Gifts For Toddlers?



Yes, building toys are excellent birthday gifts. They offer fun, educational value and encourage independent play. Popular options include Miereirl magnetic tiles and MEIEST mini vehicle blocks, perfect for kids aged 3 to 5.
