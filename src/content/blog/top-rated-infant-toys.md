Sensory toys also help babies explore their world safely. Parents often prefer BPA-free, non-toxic materials to protect their little ones. Popular items include plush rattles, teething rings made of silicone or wood, and musical toys that encourage early hearing skills. Choosing the right toy supports learning and keeps babies entertained. This guide highlights top rated infant toys that meet these needs effectively.
