## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-ride-on-toy-with-real-engine-start-sound-in-good-condition-extends-its-life.-regular-care-helps-the-toy-work-properly-and-safely.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your ride-on toy with real engine start sound in good condition extends its life. Regular care helps the toy work properly and safely.

Follow simple steps to maintain the battery, clean the toy, and fix sound issues. This guide makes maintenance easy and clear.

### Battery Care

Check the battery often to keep the toy running well. Charge it fully before use and avoid overcharging. Disconnect the charger after the battery is full.

* Charge the battery for 8 to 12 hours before first use.
* Avoid using the toy while charging the battery.
* Store the battery in a cool, dry place.
* Replace the battery if it no longer holds a charge.

### Cleaning And Storage

Clean the toy after each use to remove dirt and dust. Use a soft cloth and mild soap. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the toy’s surface.

| Cleaning Task | Recommended Method |
| --- | --- |
| Exterior surfaces | Wipe with damp cloth and mild soap |
| Wheels | Remove debris and rinse with water |
| Sound buttons | Clean gently with dry cloth |
| Storage | Keep in a dry, shaded area |

### Troubleshooting Sound Features

If the engine start sound does not work, check the battery connection first. Loose wires can stop sound from playing. Make sure the volume is turned on.

* Check if the battery is charged.
* Inspect wires for damage or loose connections.
* Test the sound button to confirm it clicks properly.
* Replace batteries in the sound module if needed.
* Consult the user manual for reset instructions.
