## Stages Of The Butterfly Life Cycle

The butterfly life cycle is a fascinating process that shows how butterflies grow. It has four clear stages that change the butterfly’s shape and form.

Butterfly life cycle toy models help children see and learn each stage in a fun way. These models show the changes from egg to adult butterfly.

### Egg Stage

The butterfly life cycle starts with the egg. The female butterfly lays tiny eggs on leaves. These eggs are very small and round or oval.

The eggs stay on the leaf until the caterpillar inside is ready to hatch. This stage lasts a few days depending on the butterfly type.

### Caterpillar Stage

When the egg hatches, a caterpillar comes out. This stage is also called the larva stage. The caterpillar eats leaves and grows very fast.

The caterpillar sheds its skin several times as it grows bigger. It stores energy for the next stage.

* Hatches from egg
* Eats leaves constantly
* Sheds skin multiple times
* Grows quickly

### Chrysalis Stage

The caterpillar forms a chrysalis or pupa. This stage is when the caterpillar changes inside the hard shell. It does not eat during this time.

Inside the chrysalis, the caterpillar’s body transforms into a butterfly. This process can take several days or weeks.

### Butterfly Stage

The adult butterfly comes out of the chrysalis. It waits for its wings to dry before it can fly. This is the last stage of the life cycle.

Butterflies live for a few weeks to months. They lay eggs to start the cycle again.
