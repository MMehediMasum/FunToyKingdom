## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Is Best For Infant Standing Toys?



Infant standing toys suit babies from 4 months to 18 months. They support early standing and walking skills. Choose toys matching your baby's developmental stage for safety and engagement.



### How Do Standing Toys Help Baby’s Motor Skills?



Standing toys encourage balance, coordination, and muscle strength. They promote crawling, standing, and walking milestones. Interactive features also boost hand-eye coordination and fine motor skills.



### Are Interactive Features Important In Standing Toys?



Yes, interactive features like music and lights stimulate senses and keep babies engaged. They enhance learning through play and support cognitive development while encouraging movement.



### What Safety Features Should Standing Toys Have?



Look for sturdy construction, non-toxic materials, and stable bases. Toys should have smooth edges and no small detachable parts to prevent choking hazards. Always supervise your baby during play.



### Can Standing Toys Aid Early Walkers?



Absolutely. Standing toys provide support and motivation for babies learning to walk. Push toys and activity centers help build confidence and muscle strength necessary for independent walking.



### Which Standing Toy Is Best For Multi-stage Use?



Toys like Skip Hop Baby Activity Center offer 3-stage grow-with-me functionality. These adapt as your baby grows from sitting to standing, providing long-term value and continuous engagement.
