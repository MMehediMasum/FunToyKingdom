Babies often chew and throw toys, so durability is important. The plush cube should resist tears and stains.

## Safety Tips For Parents

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-baby-safe-while-playing-with-a-plush-cube-toy-is-very-important.-parents-should-follow-simple-safety-steps-to-prevent-accidents.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your baby safe while playing with a plush cube toy is very important. Parents should follow simple safety steps to prevent accidents.

This guide offers clear tips on how to ensure safe use of the toy with crinkle sounds.

### Supervised Play

Always watch your baby during playtime with the plush cube toy. Supervision helps you react quickly if the toy becomes damaged or if your baby puts it in their mouth.

### Regular Toy Inspection

Check the plush cube toy often for any signs of wear or damage. Look for loose threads, torn fabric, or broken parts that could harm your baby.

* Examine seams and edges for tears
* Ensure the crinkle sound parts are securely inside
* Remove the toy if any damage is found
* Clean the toy regularly to avoid germs

### Avoiding Small Parts

The plush cube toy should not have small detachable parts. Small pieces can cause choking hazards if your baby puts them in their mouth.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Risk | Precaution |
| Loose buttons | Remove or sew tightly |
| Small beads | Do not use with babies under 1 year |
| Crinkle inserts | Make sure they are sealed inside |
