## Iconic Toy Soldier Brands

Toy soldiers have been popular with collectors for many years. Some brands stand out because of their history and quality.

This article looks at three famous toy soldier brands. Each brand has its own style and story.

### Britains Ltd

Britains Ltd is one of the oldest toy soldier makers. It started in the UK in 1893. The brand is famous for its detailed metal figures.

The soldiers often show historical armies and battles. Collectors like the realistic look and quality paint.

* Founded in 1893
* Known for metal toy soldiers
* Focus on historical accuracy
* Popular among collectors worldwide

### Marx Toys

Marx Toys was a top American toy company. It made many plastic toy soldiers in the mid-1900s. These toys were affordable and popular with kids.

Marx soldiers often had bright colors and unique poses. Many collectors seek these vintage toys today.

* American brand started in 1919
* Famous for plastic toy soldiers
* Bright colors and creative designs
* Highly collectible vintage items

### King & Country

King & Country is a modern brand from Hong Kong. It began in the 1980s. Their toy soldiers are hand-painted and very detailed.

Collectors value King & Country for their quality and craftsmanship. The brand makes soldiers from many different armies and times.

* Started in the 1980s
* Known for hand-painted figures
* High detail and craftsmanship
* Focus on historical and military themes

Famous toy soldier brands often use different materials to create their figures. The choice of material affects the look and feel of each piece. Collectors pay close attention to these details.

Design trends also change over time. They reflect both the materials used and the style preferences of the era. This guide looks at metal versus plastic figures and paint and finish styles.

### Metal Vs Plastic Figures

Metal figures are known for their weight and durability. They often have fine details and a classic look. Plastic figures are lighter and can be made in large quantities. They allow more color options and flexible designs.

* Metal figures use materials like lead, tin, or pewter.
* Plastic figures are mostly made from PVC or polystyrene.
* Metal models often have sharper edges and finer details.
* Plastic models can include movable parts and accessories.
* Collectors value metal for its traditional and sturdy feel.
* Plastic offers more variety in shapes and sizes.

## Collecting Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collecting-toy-soldiers-is-a-fun-hobby.-it-brings-history-and-art-together-in-small-figures.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collecting toy soldiers is a fun hobby. It brings history and art together in small figures.

Knowing how to care for your collection helps keep its value and beauty.

### Authenticity And Condition

Check if the toy soldiers are real or copies. Authentic pieces are more valuable.

Look closely at paint, markings, and materials. Original brands have specific details.

* Examine manufacturer marks or stamps
* Compare with official brand catalogs
* Avoid pieces with repainting or repairs
* Keep soldiers away from moisture and sunlight
* Store them in original packaging if possible

### Where To Buy And Sell

Buy toy soldiers from trusted shops or collectors. Check online and local stores.

Selling is easier if you know the market. Use auctions, fairs, or collector groups.

* Visit toy fairs and collector conventions
* Use online auction sites with buyer protection
* Join social media groups for collectors
* Ask for appraisals before selling
* Keep clear photos and descriptions ready
