## Basic Folding Techniques

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=origami-is-the-art-of-paper-folding.-it-uses-simple-folds-to-make-many-shapes-and-toys.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Origami is the art of paper folding. It uses simple folds to make many shapes and toys.

Learning basic folding techniques helps beginners create neat and accurate models. This guide covers key folds and symbols.

### Essential Folds To Know

Origami uses a few basic folds to build complex figures. Knowing these folds makes folding easier.

* **Valley Fold:**Fold the paper inward, creating a valley shape.
* **Mountain Fold:**Fold the paper outward, forming a mountain peak.
* **Squash Fold:**Open a flap and press it flat to form a new shape.
* **Reverse Fold:**Fold a section inside or outside to change direction.
* **Petal Fold:**Lift a flap and fold sides to the center line.

### Common Origami Symbols

Origami diagrams use symbols to show folding steps. Learning these helps follow instructions well.

* **Dashed Line (---):**Indicates a valley fold.
* **Dash-Dot Line (-·-·-):**Shows a mountain fold.
* **Arrow:**Shows the direction to fold or move paper.
* **Circle:**Marks a point to hold or start folding.
* **Squiggly Arrow:**Means to open or unfold a flap.

### Tips For Precise Folding

Precise folds make origami models look clean and professional. Use these tips for best results.

* Use a flat, clean surface to fold on.
* Align edges carefully before making a crease.
* Press folds firmly with your fingernail for sharp lines.
* Work slowly to avoid mistakes.
* Practice folds on scrap paper to improve skill.

## Troubleshooting Folding Challenges

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=origami-toy-kits-are-fun-but-can-be-tricky-for-beginners.-folding-paper-the-right-way-takes-practice.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Origami toy kits are fun but can be tricky for beginners. Folding paper the right way takes practice.

This guide helps you fix common problems while folding your origami toys. It also shares tips for better paper handling and clean folds.

### Fixing Common Mistakes

Beginners often fold in the wrong places or crease paper too hard. These mistakes can ruin the toy shape.

To fix this, check your folds carefully before pressing down. Use light folds first, then make them sharp once you are sure.

* Align edges before folding
* Use your fingernail to sharpen creases
* Unfold and refold if shapes look off

### Improving Paper Handling

Good paper handling helps make neat origami toys. Hold the paper gently but firmly to avoid tears.

Use flat, clean surfaces to fold on. This keeps the paper smooth and easier to work with.

* Keep hands dry and clean
* Turn pages slowly to avoid wrinkles
* Use tweezers for small folds

### Maintaining Clean Folds

Clean folds make origami toys look better and last longer. Press folds firmly for sharp edges.

Use a bone folder or the side of a fingernail to smooth creases. Avoid folding the same spot many times.

* Press folds evenly from center out
* Keep paper flat during folding
* Do not rush the folding process
