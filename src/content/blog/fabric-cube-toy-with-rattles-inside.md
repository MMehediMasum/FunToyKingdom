## Safety Tips For Fabric Cube Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=fabric-cube-toys-with-rattles-are-fun-for-babies-and-toddlers.-these-toys-help-develop-motor-skills-and-senses.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Fabric cube toys with rattles are fun for babies and toddlers. These toys help develop motor skills and senses.

Safety is very important when choosing and using these toys. Follow simple tips to keep children safe.

### Checking For Secure Attachments

Make sure all parts of the toy are tightly attached. Loose pieces can be dangerous if a child pulls them off.

Check the seams, rattles, and any decorations often. Fix or remove parts that look weak or loose.

### Avoiding Small Parts Hazards

Small parts can cause choking. Avoid toys with tiny buttons, beads, or pieces that can come off easily.

Choose fabric cubes with rattles that are built-in or sewn inside. This keeps small parts out of reach.

* Never give fabric cubes with detachable small parts to children under 3 years.
* Inspect toys regularly for missing or broken parts.
* Replace toys that have parts that can break off.

### Regular Cleaning Practices

Keep fabric cube toys clean to avoid germs and allergies. Wash them as recommended by the maker.

Use gentle soap and warm water for hand washing. Some fabric cubes are machine washable, check the label.

* Dry toys fully before giving them back to children.
* Clean toys often to remove dirt and bacteria.
* Do not use harsh chemicals that can harm children.
