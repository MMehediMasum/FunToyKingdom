## Game Setup

Setting up Toy Story Monopoly is easy and fun. Follow these simple steps to prepare for the game.

Make sure all players understand the setup before starting. This helps the game run smoothly.

### Choosing Your Character

Each player picks a Toy Story character token. This token will move around the board during the game.

* Look at all the character tokens available.
* Decide which character you want to play as.
* Take the matching token and place it in front of you.
* Each player must have a different character token.

### Preparing The Board

Place the board on a flat surface where all players can reach it easily.

| Item | Where to Place |
| --- | --- |
| Chance and Community Chest Cards | On their marked spaces near the board center |
| Property Cards | Organized near the board for easy access |
| Bank Money | In a pile next to the bank space |
| Tokens | With each player, ready to move |

### Distributing Money And Tokens

Each player starts with a set amount of money and one character token. The bank holds the rest.

1. Give each player $1500 in Monopoly money.
2. Make sure the money is divided into correct denominations:

* 2 x $500 bills
* 4 x $100 bills
* 1 x $50 bill
* 1 x $20 bill
* 2 x $10 bills
* 1 x $5 bill
* 5 x $1 bills

Place the remaining money in the bank. Each player keeps their money visible.

## Basic Gameplay

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=toy-story-monopoly-is-a-fun-board-game-based-on-the-toy-story-movies.-players-take-turns-rolling-dice-and-moving-around-the-board.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Toy Story Monopoly is a fun board game based on the Toy Story movies. Players take turns rolling dice and moving around the board.

The goal is to buy properties and earn money while avoiding bankruptcy. Here are the basic rules for playing the game.

### Rolling The Dice

Each player rolls two dice to move their token around the board. The total number on the dice shows how many spaces to move.

If a player rolls doubles, they get another turn. But rolling doubles three times in a row sends the player to jail.

### Moving Around The Board

Players move their tokens forward the number of spaces rolled. Depending on where they land, different actions happen.

* If a player lands on a property, they can buy it.
* Landing on Chance or Community Chest means drawing a card.
* Landing on tax spaces means paying money to the bank.
* Players can go to jail if they land on the jail space or roll doubles three times.

### Buying Properties

When a player lands on an unowned property, they may buy it from the bank at the listed price.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property Type | How to Buy | Price |
| Street | Buy immediately | Varies by property |
| Railroad | Buy immediately | Fixed price |
| Utility | Buy immediately | Fixed price |

If a player chooses not to buy, the property goes to auction. Other players can bid to buy it.

## Jail Rules

In Toy Story Monopoly, the jail space works like in classic Monopoly. It changes how you play and can slow down your progress.

Understanding the jail rules helps you plan your moves better. It also adds fun twists to the game.

### Getting Sent To Jail

You go to jail if you land on the "Go to Jail" space. You can also be sent if you draw a card that says so.

Landing on this space means you move your token directly to jail. You do not collect money for passing "Go."

### Ways To Get Out

You have three ways to leave jail. You can try to roll doubles on your turn, use a "Get Out of Jail Free" card, or pay a fee.

* Roll doubles on your dice within three turns
* Use a "Get Out of Jail Free" card if you have one
* Pay $50 to leave jail immediately

If you fail to roll doubles after three tries, you must pay the fee and move forward.

### Strategic Jail Use

Staying in jail can sometimes help you avoid paying rent. You can stay for up to three turns without moving.

Use jail time to save money while others move around the board and pay rents or buy properties.

* Stay in jail to skip expensive rent spaces
* Try to get out quickly if you want to buy properties
* Use "Get Out of Jail Free" cards wisely
