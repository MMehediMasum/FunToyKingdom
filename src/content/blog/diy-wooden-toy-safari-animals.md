## Materials Needed

Creating DIY wooden toy safari animals is a fun project. You need the right materials to make safe and beautiful toys.

This guide covers the types of wood, basic tools, and paints or finishes needed for your project.

### Types Of Wood

Choose wood that is safe and easy to work with. Softwoods and hardwoods both work well for toys.

* Basswood – soft, smooth, and easy to carve
* Birch – strong with a fine grain
* Maple – hard and durable
* Pine – affordable and easy to cut
* Alder – light and has a smooth finish

### Basic Tools

You need simple tools to shape and finish your wooden safari animals. Safety is important when using tools.

* Hand saw or jigsaw for cutting shapes
* Sandpaper or sanding block for smooth edges
* Wood carving knives for details
* Clamps to hold wood pieces steady
* Drill with small bits for holes

### Paints And Finishes

Use safe paints and finishes made for children’s toys. These protect the wood and add color.

* Non-toxic water-based paints
* Food-safe wood oils like mineral oil
* Natural beeswax for a soft finish
* Clear water-based sealers for protection

## Basic Craft Techniques

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-wooden-toy-safari-animals-is-a-fun-and-creative-project.-you-can-use-simple-tools-and-materials.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making wooden toy safari animals is a fun and creative project. You can use simple tools and materials. Basic craft techniques help you create safe and beautiful toys.

This guide covers cutting, sanding, and painting. These skills are easy to learn and improve your final product.

### Cutting And Shaping Wood

Start by choosing soft wood like pine or basswood. These woods are easier to cut and shape. Use a saw or craft knife to cut out the animal shapes.

After cutting, use files or carving tools to shape details. Keep shapes simple for safety and easier crafting. Always cut away from your body for safety.

### Sanding For Smooth Edges

Sanding removes rough spots and splinters. Use coarse sandpaper first to shape edges. Then use finer sandpaper to smooth the surface.

Check the wood by running your fingers over it. Smooth edges make toys safe for children. Take your time to get a nice finish.

* Start with 80-grit sandpaper for rough sanding
* Use 120-grit for medium smoothing
* Finish with 220-grit for a soft touch

### Painting Tips

Choose non-toxic, water-based paint. This paint is safe for kids and easy to clean. Apply thin coats to avoid drips and uneven surfaces.

Let each coat dry before adding another. Use small brushes for details and larger ones for big areas. Seal the paint with a clear, child-safe finish.

* Use bright colors for a fun look
* Paint simple patterns like stripes or spots
* Seal with a non-toxic varnish for protection

### Lion Figurine

Start by choosing a soft wood block. Cut the shape of a lion’s body and head carefully. Sand the edges smooth for safety. Use a wood burner or paint to add the eyes and mane details.

* Finish with a non-toxic sealant

### Elephant Toy

Use a larger wood piece for the elephant. Cut out the body, trunk, and ears separately. Attach ears and trunk with wood glue. Paint the toy in gray or your favorite colors.
