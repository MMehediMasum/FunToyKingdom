## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Fidget Sensory Blankets For Dementia?



Fidget sensory blankets provide tactile stimulation that helps reduce anxiety. They improve focus and promote calmness in dementia patients. These blankets engage multiple senses, aiding memory and emotional comfort. They are practical tools for daily dementia care and therapy.



### How Do Sensory Toys Help Alzheimer’s And Dementia Patients?



Sensory toys stimulate the brain through touch, sight, and sound. They encourage cognitive engagement and reduce agitation. These toys promote fine motor skills and emotional well-being. Regular use can slow cognitive decline and improve quality of life.



### Which Dolls Are Best For Seniors With Dementia?



Realistic dolls with soft textures and weighted bodies are ideal. They offer comfort, stimulate nurturing behavior, and reduce loneliness. Choose dolls designed specifically for dementia care with sensory features. These dolls help improve mood and provide companionship.



### Are Busy Boards Effective For Dementia Therapy?



Yes, busy boards improve hand-eye coordination and cognitive function. They offer engaging activities that stimulate problem-solving skills. Busy boards reduce restlessness and promote focus. They are excellent tools for occupational therapy in dementia care.



### Can Matching Games Benefit Dementia Patients?



Matching games enhance memory and attention in dementia patients. They provide mental stimulation and encourage social interaction. These games help maintain cognitive skills and delay progression. They are enjoyable and meaningful activities for seniors.
