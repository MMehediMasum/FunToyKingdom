## Care And Maintenance

### Cleaning Techniques

Use gentle cleaning methods to avoid scratching or fading. Soft cloths and mild soap work best for cleaning the figures and accessories.

* Use a soft, dry cloth to remove dust regularly.
* If needed, dampen the cloth with water and mild soap.
* Avoid soaking the toys in water.
* Dry the toys completely before storing.
* Use a soft brush for small crevices and details.

### Avoiding Damage

Handle the action figures carefully to prevent paint wear and broken parts. Keep them away from heat and direct sunlight.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cause | Effect | Prevention |
| Direct sunlight | Fading colors | Store in shaded area |
| Heat exposure | Warping plastic | Keep toys cool |
| Rough handling | Broken joints | Handle gently |
| Water soaking | Paint damage | Use damp cloth only |

### Long-term Preservation

Store your Power Rangers set in a clean, dry place. Use protective cases or boxes to keep dust and moisture away.

**Storage Tips:**

* Use acid-free tissue paper to wrap each figure.
* Place desiccant packets in storage boxes to reduce moisture.
* Keep the set out of direct sunlight and away from heat sources.
* Check the set regularly for any signs of damage or wear.
