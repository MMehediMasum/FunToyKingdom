## Choosing Safe Materials

Newborn photo props must be safe and gentle. Choosing the right materials is very important.

Safe materials protect babies from harm and keep them comfortable during photos.

### Non-toxic And Baby-friendly Options

Use materials free from harmful chemicals. Babies often put toys in their mouths.

Choose natural fibers like organic cotton or untreated wood. These are gentle and safe.

* Look for BPA-free plastics
* Use water-based, non-toxic paints
* Avoid small parts that can detach
* Check for certifications on safety

### Soft And Lightweight Designs

Soft rattles reduce risk of injury. Lightweight toys are easier for newborns to hold.

Choose plush or fabric rattles with gentle fillings. Avoid hard or heavy materials.

* Soft fabrics like fleece or cotton
* Light plastic parts with smooth edges
* Secure stitching to avoid loose threads
* Easy to clean materials

### Easy Handmade Rattles

Handmade rattles are simple to make with basic materials. Use soft fabrics and safe items for babies.

* Fill with plastic beads, rice, or dried beans
* Close securely with strong thread or glue
* Add ribbons or buttons for decoration
* Ensure all parts are safe and non-toxic

### Customizing Store-bought Rattles

Store-bought rattles can look plain. You can easily customize them to fit your photo theme.

Use fabric, paint, or stickers to change the rattle’s look. Make sure decorations are secure and safe.

* Wrap the handle with colorful ribbons or yarn

* Attach small bells or soft charms carefully
