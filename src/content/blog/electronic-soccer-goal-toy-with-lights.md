## Safety Considerations

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=electronic-soccer-goal-toys-with-lights-offer-fun-and-excitement.-safety-is-important-to-prevent-accidents.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Electronic soccer goal toys with lights offer fun and excitement. Safety is important to prevent accidents.

Parents and caregivers should watch how children use the toy. Following safety tips helps keep playtime safe.

### Age Recommendations

Check the age label before buying the toy. Electronic soccer goal toys may have small parts.

These toys are best for children aged 3 and older. Younger children might choke on small pieces.

* Recommended age: 3 years and above
* Keep away from children under 3 years
* Supervise children near small parts

### Safe Play Environment

Choose a clear area without obstacles for playing. Avoid hard floors or sharp objects nearby.

Play indoors or on soft surfaces like carpet or grass. This reduces risk of injury from falls.

* Use the toy in open, clutter-free spaces
* Soft floors lower injury risks
* Keep away from stairs and furniture edges

### Maintenance And Care

Regularly check the toy for loose parts or damage. Broken parts can cause injuries or stop lights from working.

Clean the toy with a dry cloth. Avoid water to protect electronic parts and lights.

* Inspect the toy before each use
* Do not use if parts are broken or loose
* Clean with dry cloth only
* Store in a dry place away from heat
