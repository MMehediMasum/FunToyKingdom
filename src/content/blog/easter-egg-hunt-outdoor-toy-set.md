## Setting Up The Hunt

Organizing an Easter egg hunt outdoors is fun and easy. Planning helps make the event smooth and exciting for kids.

Choosing the right place and hiding spots lets children enjoy the search safely and happily.

### Choosing The Perfect Location

Select a safe and open area for the egg hunt. Parks, backyards, or gardens work well. Make sure the space is free from hazards like sharp objects or busy roads.

Check for enough shade to keep kids cool and spots with soft grass to avoid injuries.

### Creative Egg Placement Ideas

* Place eggs inside flower pots for a colorful surprise.
* Hide eggs under garden benches or picnic tables.
* Use tree branches to hang eggs at low heights.
* Put eggs near outdoor toys or play equipment.
* Scatter eggs along a winding path to guide the hunt.

Clean the toy set after each use. Dirt and grass can stick to the toys and cause damage over time.

Use mild soap and warm water. Gently scrub the toys with a soft cloth or sponge. Avoid harsh chemicals that can hurt the plastic.

* Rinse toys well to remove soap
* Dry each piece completely before storing
* Check for cracks or damage during cleaning

### Proper Storage Solutions

Store the toy set in a dry place. Keep it away from direct sunlight and moisture to prevent fading and mold.

Use a storage box or bag that fits all pieces. Label the container to find the set easily next time.

* Choose a cool, dry area for storage
* Keep toys separated to avoid scratches
* Avoid heavy items on top of the set

### Extending Toy Lifespan

Handle the toys gently during play and cleanup. Rough use can break parts and reduce the set’s life.

Inspect the toy set regularly. Fix small issues early to avoid bigger damage later.

* Keep toys away from extreme temperatures

* Store the set properly between uses
