Sensory toys are beneficial for toddlers with ADHD. They provide calming tactile stimulation and improve focus. These toys are great for travel as they reduce anxiety and keep children engaged. Montessori sensory boards are especially recommended for children with sensory processing needs.

Look for toys that are compact, lightweight, and easy to clean. Choose toys with multiple interactive elements to develop motor skills and sensory exploration. Safety is key, so avoid small parts. Educational and Montessori-inspired toys are excellent choices for travel with 1 year olds.



### Are Busy Boards Good Gifts For Toddlers Aged 1 To 3?



Busy boards make excellent gifts for toddlers aged 1 to 3. They offer educational activities that promote fine motor skills and sensory learning. These boards are engaging and suitable for travel, making them thoughtful and practical birthday or holiday presents.
