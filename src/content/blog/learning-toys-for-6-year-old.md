### Safety Considerations

Safety is very important when selecting toys. Avoid toys with small parts that can be swallowed or sharp edges.

Check for non-toxic materials and sturdy construction. Look for toys that meet safety standards and labels.

* No small parts that can cause choking
* Use BPA-free or non-toxic plastics
* Strong and durable materials
* Clear safety labels and instructions

### Balancing Fun And Education

## Incorporating Learning Toys At Home

Learning toys help children develop skills while having fun. For 6-year-olds, these toys support growth in many areas.

Using learning toys at home creates chances for kids to explore and discover new things every day.

### Creating Learning Zones

Set up special areas in your home for learning and play. These zones help kids focus on activities and stay organized.

Include shelves or bins for toys, puzzles, books, and art supplies. Keep the space bright and inviting.

* Choose a quiet corner or small table
* Use labels for toy storage
* Add comfortable seating for reading or building
* Rotate toys to keep interest fresh

### Balancing Screen Time

Limit time spent on screens to protect your child’s eyes and encourage active learning. Use timers if needed.

Offer learning toys as fun alternatives that build skills without needing technology.

* Set daily screen time limits
* Encourage hands-on play after screen use
* Choose educational apps when screens are used
* Join your child during screen time to guide learning

### Encouraging Independent Play

Learning toys can help children play on their own. Independent play builds creativity and problem-solving skills.

Offer toys that match your child's interests and skill level. Praise efforts to boost confidence.

* Provide open-ended toys like building blocks or art sets
* Keep toys accessible so kids can choose freely
* Allow time for quiet play daily
* Give gentle guidance but let kids try tasks alone
