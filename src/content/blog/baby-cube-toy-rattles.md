## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Is The Baby Cube Toy Rattle Suitable For?



Most baby cube rattles are suitable for newborns to toddlers. They are designed to support sensory development. Always check the specific age recommendation on the packaging. Typically, these toys are safe for ages 3 months and up. They provide tactile, auditory, and visual stimulation for young infants.



### How Do Baby Cube Rattles Aid In Development?



Baby cube rattles stimulate sensory development through sound, texture, and color. They help improve hand-eye coordination and motor skills. Babies learn to grasp and shake the toy, enhancing their dexterity. The varied textures and colors also engage visual and tactile senses, promoting cognitive growth.



### Are Baby Cube Rattles Safe For Teething?



Yes, many baby cube rattles are made from BPA-free, non-toxic materials. They are safe for teething. Always ensure the product specifies it's suitable for teething. These toys provide relief for sore gums and are easy for babies to grip.



### How Do You Clean A Baby Cube Rattle?



Most baby cube rattles can be cleaned with a damp cloth and mild soap. Ensure the toy is completely dry before giving it back to your baby. Some are dishwasher safe, but always check the manufacturer's cleaning instructions to maintain the toy's integrity.



### Do Baby Cube Rattles Make Noise?



Yes, baby cube rattles often include features like crinkle sounds, rattles, or clacker rings. These sounds engage a baby's auditory senses. The noise helps in stimulating hearing and can be soothing for infants. It also encourages them to explore cause and effect by shaking the toy.
