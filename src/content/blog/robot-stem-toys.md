Yes, STEM robot toys are designed for all children, regardless of gender. They encourage creativity, problem-solving, and technical skills equally. The kits provide inclusive fun and learning opportunities, making STEM accessible and enjoyable for boys and girls alike.

Yes, programmable robot kits teach coding fundamentals through interactive play. Kids learn to write simple code to control robot movements and functions. This hands-on approach makes coding fun and practical, fostering early interest in computer science and technology.
