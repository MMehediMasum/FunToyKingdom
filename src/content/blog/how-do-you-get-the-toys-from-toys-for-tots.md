## About Toys For Tots

Toys for Tots is a well-known charity in the United States. It collects and distributes new toys to children in need. The program aims to bring joy and hope during the holiday season. It helps children from low-income families feel special and cared for.

The organization started in 1947. It is run by the U.S. Marine Corps Reserve. Volunteers gather toys from donors and local businesses. Then, they sort and distribute the toys to communities across the country. This effort helps thousands of children every year.

### Mission And Purpose

Toys for Tots wants to brighten children’s holidays. It focuses on children who might not receive gifts otherwise. The program supports their emotional and social growth. It believes every child deserves to feel happiness and hope.

### How Toys Are Collected

Donors give new, unwrapped toys at collection sites. These sites include schools, businesses, and community centers. Volunteers pick up and sort the toys carefully. They make sure each toy is safe and suitable for children.

### Who Receives The Toys

Families in need can receive toys through local programs. Community centers and social service agencies help distribute them. The goal is to reach children who may face hardship. Each child gets a gift to enjoy during the holidays.
