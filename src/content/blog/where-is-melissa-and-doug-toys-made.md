## Melissa And Doug Brand Background

Melissa and Doug is a well-known toy brand loved by many families. The brand focuses on creating toys that help children learn and grow. Their toys often use wood and bright colors to attract kids. Parents trust Melissa and Doug for safe and fun playthings. Understanding the brand's background helps explain why their toys stand out.

### Company History

Melissa and Doug started in 1988. The company was founded by Melissa and Doug Bernstein. They wanted to make toys that spark imagination. Their first products were wooden puzzles and toys. Over the years, the brand grew fast. Today, their toys reach children worldwide.

### Mission And Values

The brand aims to inspire creative play. They believe play is key to child development. Melissa and Doug focus on quality and safety. They use non-toxic materials in their toys. The company supports open-ended play without screens. Their goal is to help children explore and learn through play.

Melissa and Doug toys meet all safety laws in the USA and worldwide. The company follows rules set by the Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC).
