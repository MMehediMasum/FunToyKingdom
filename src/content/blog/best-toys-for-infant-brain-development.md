Musical toys and activity stations introduce cause and effect and stimulate hearing. Tummy time toys strengthen muscles and promote sensory exploration. Selecting age-appropriate, safe, and engaging toys encourages babies to discover the world around them. These carefully chosen items support babies’ growth from newborn to toddler stages. Parents find these toys helpful for creating fun and meaningful learning moments every day.

### Which Toys Support Tummy Time Benefits?

### What Makes Baby Einstein Toys Educational?
