## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Toys For 3-month-old Infants?



The best toys for 3-month-olds include soft rattles, sensory tummy time toys, and musical toys like Baby Einstein Take Along Tunes. These toys stimulate senses and promote motor skills development safely.



### How Do Sensory Toys Help 3-month-old Babies?



Sensory toys like Melissa & Doug Flip Fish enhance visual and tactile senses. They encourage exploration, improve focus, and support brain development during this crucial age.



### Are Teething Toys Safe For 3-month-olds?



Yes, teething toys such as silicone rings are safe for 3-month-olds. They soothe gums and support early biting reflexes while being made from non-toxic materials.



### Why Choose Soft Fabric Toys For Infants?



Soft fabric toys, like tag toys, are gentle and safe for infants. They offer texture variety, promote sensory exploration, and are easy for babies to grasp and cuddle.



### How Do Rattles Benefit Infants Aged 0-6 Months?



Rattles improve hand-eye coordination and auditory skills. For 3-month-olds, they encourage grasping and cause sensory stimulation, aiding early motor skill development.



### Can Tummy Time Toys Improve Infant Development?



Yes, tummy time toys with high-contrast patterns, like the URMYWO baby mirror, strengthen neck muscles and enhance visual tracking for 3-month-olds during playtime.
