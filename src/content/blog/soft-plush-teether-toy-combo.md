## Tips For Using Teether Toys Safely

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=teether-toys-help-babies-soothe-their-gums-during-teething.-using-them-safely-is-very-important-to-protect-your-child.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Teether toys help babies soothe their gums during teething. Using them safely is very important to protect your child.

This guide shares easy tips to keep your baby safe while enjoying soft plush teether toys.

### Cleaning And Maintenance

Clean teether toys often to remove germs and dirt. Use mild soap and warm water for washing.

Check the toy for loose parts or damage before each use. Damaged toys can hurt your baby.

* Wash toys after every use or at least once a day
* Air dry toys completely before giving them to your baby
* Inspect toys regularly for tears or broken pieces

### Avoiding Choking Hazards

Choose teether toys with no small parts that can come off easily. Soft plush teethers should be stitched well.

Do not give toys that are too small or have loose attachments. These can cause choking.

* Pick teethers larger than your baby's mouth
* Avoid buttons, beads, or ribbons on teethers
* Replace toys that show signs of wear or damage

### Supervision During Playtime

Always watch your baby when they use teether toys. Supervision helps prevent accidents.

Do not leave babies alone with teether toys, especially if they are new or have parts that could break.

* Stay close and observe your baby during teething
* Remove the toy if your baby bites too hard or chews unsafe parts
* Keep an eye for any signs of discomfort or allergies
