## Care And Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-good-care-of-your-handmade-fabric-toy-food-set-keeps-it-safe-and-clean.-proper-maintenance-helps-the-toys-last-longer-and-stay-nice.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking good care of your handmade fabric toy food set keeps it safe and clean. Proper maintenance helps the toys last longer and stay nice.

Use simple methods to clean, store, and fix small damages. This guide gives easy tips to follow.



### Washing And Cleaning

Wash the fabric toys gently to keep their shape and colors. Avoid harsh chemicals or strong scrubbing.

* Use cold water and mild soap for hand washing.
* Spot clean stains with a soft cloth.
* Do not machine wash or tumble dry to avoid damage.
* Air dry flat away from direct sunlight.
* Brush fabric gently to keep it soft.

### Storage Solutions

Keep the toy food set in a clean, dry place to avoid dust and moisture.

| Storage Tip | Why It Helps |
| --- | --- |
| Use a [breathable fabric bag](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=cotton+drawstring+toy+storage+bags&tag=gearcookware-20) | Keeps toys dust-free and lets air circulate |
| Store in a cool, dry drawer | Prevents mold and fabric damage |
| Avoid plastic bags | Can trap moisture and cause mildew |
| Keep away from sharp objects | Prevents tears and punctures |

### Repairing Minor Damages

Fix small tears or loose threads quickly to stop bigger damage. Simple sewing tools work well for repairs.

* Use a [needle and thread](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=mini+sewing+kit&tag=gearcookware-20) matching the fabric color.
* Sew small holes or tears with tight, even stitches.
* Trim loose threads carefully after sewing.
* If stuffing escapes, patch the hole before adding more filling.
* Check toys regularly for any signs of damage.
