## Model Airplane Brands

Miniature toy airplanes are popular for collectors and hobbyists. Many brands create detailed model airplanes. These brands offer different styles and sizes.

Choosing a trusted brand helps find quality models. Below are some well-known model airplane brands to explore.

### Revell

Revell is a famous brand for plastic model kits. They make airplanes from many historical periods. Their kits range from beginner to advanced levels.

Revell models are known for good detail and fit. Many hobby stores and online shops sell these kits.

### Airfix

Airfix is a British company with a long history. They focus on realistic scale model airplanes. Their kits often include classic warplanes and civilian aircraft.

Airfix kits are popular with both beginners and experts. They are available in hobby shops and online worldwide.

### Corgi

Corgi is known for die-cast model airplanes. They create detailed miniature planes with metal parts. Their models often include famous military and commercial aircraft.

Corgi models are collectible and display well. You can find them in specialty stores and online marketplaces.
