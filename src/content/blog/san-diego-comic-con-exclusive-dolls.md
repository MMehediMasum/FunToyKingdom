## Collecting Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=collecting-san-diego-comic-con-exclusive-dolls-can-be-fun-and-rewarding.-these-dolls-are-rare-and-often-grow-in-value-over-time.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Collecting San Diego Comic Con exclusive dolls can be fun and rewarding. These dolls are rare and often grow in value over time.

Knowing how to spot real exclusives, keep them in good shape, and store them smartly helps keep your collection safe and valuable.

### Spotting Authentic Exclusives

Many fake dolls look like the real San Diego Comic Con exclusives. Check for official logos and certificates that come with the doll.

Look closely at the details, like paint quality and packaging. Authentic dolls usually have high-quality finishes and clear print on the box.

* Check for the official Comic Con logo on packaging
* Verify serial numbers or certificates of authenticity
* Compare details with trusted photos online
* Avoid dolls with poor paint or blurry text

### Preserving Doll Condition

Keep your exclusive dolls in the best condition by avoiding direct sunlight and dust. Sunlight can fade colors fast.

Handle dolls with clean hands or gloves. Oils and dirt can damage the paint and materials over time.

* Store dolls away from sunlight and heat
* Use soft cloths to clean dust gently
* Wear gloves when handling dolls
* Keep dolls in their original packaging if possible

### Smart Storage Solutions

Good storage protects your dolls from damage and dust. Use shelves with glass doors or clear display cases to keep them safe.

Organize your collection by size or theme to find dolls easily. Keep the area clean and dry to avoid mold or mildew.

* Use display cases with UV protection
* Keep storage areas dry and clean
* Group dolls by series or size
* Label shelves or boxes for easy access
