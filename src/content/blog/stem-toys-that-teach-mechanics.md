## Key Mechanical Concepts

### Simple Machines Basics

Simple machines are tools that help us do work with less effort. They include levers, pulleys, wheels, and inclined planes.

These machines change the direction or size of a force. Kids learn how small forces can move heavy objects using simple machines.

* Levers lift or move loads
* Pulleys lift heavy things using ropes
* Inclined planes make lifting easier
* Wheels reduce friction for smooth movement

### Gears And Levers

Gears are wheels with teeth that fit together. They help change speed and direction of movement in machines.

Levers are bars that pivot on a point called the fulcrum. They help lift or move loads with less force.

* Gears can increase speed or force
* Levers have three parts: effort, load, and fulcrum
* Using different gear sizes changes how machines work
* Levers help balance or lift heavy objects

### Forces And Motion

Forces make things move, stop, or change direction. Motion is how objects change their position over time.

Kids learn how pushing and pulling affect objects. They see how friction and gravity change motion in real life.

* Push and pull are types of forces
* Friction slows down moving objects
* Gravity pulls objects toward the ground
* Balanced forces keep objects still
