## Safety Tips For Water Play

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=playing-with-the-outdoor-lobster-sprinkler-is-fun-for-kids.-water-play-cools-children-on-hot-days-and-helps-them-enjoy-outside-time.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Playing with the Outdoor Lobster Sprinkler is fun for kids. Water play cools children on hot days and helps them enjoy outside time.

Safety is important to keep playtime happy and injury-free. Use these tips to make water play safe for all kids.

### Supervision Essentials

Adults should always watch children while they use the sprinkler. Close supervision helps stop accidents before they happen.

Stay near the play area and pay full attention. Avoid distractions like phones or reading.

* Keep kids within arm’s reach
* Watch for signs of tiredness or distress
* Check that all children play safely together

### Avoiding Slips And Falls

Wet surfaces can be slippery. Teach kids to walk carefully around the sprinkler to prevent falls.

Place the sprinkler on flat grass or soft ground. Avoid hard surfaces like concrete or tiles.

* Use non-slip shoes or play barefoot on grass
* Clear the area of toys or objects that cause tripping
* Remind children not to run near the sprinkler

### Sun Protection

Playing outside means exposure to the sun. Protect children’s skin to avoid sunburns and heat issues.

Use sunscreen with at least SPF 30 before water play. Reapply sunscreen regularly, especially after water contact.

* Dress kids in hats and UV-protective clothing
* Provide shaded areas for breaks
* Encourage drinking water to stay hydrated

## Maintaining Your Lobster Sprinkler

Keeping your lobster sprinkler in top shape ensures fun and lasting use. Regular care helps prevent issues and prolongs its life.

### Cleaning Guidelines

Clean your lobster sprinkler to prevent blockages and maintain water flow. Regular cleaning keeps it looking new.

* Rinse with clean water after each use.
* Use mild soap and a soft brush for dirt.
* Check and clear nozzle holes to prevent clogs.

### Storage Tips

Proper storage protects your lobster sprinkler from damage. Store it correctly to ensure it is ready for the next use.

**Storage Steps:**

* Dry the sprinkler completely before storing.
* Keep in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
* Avoid folding or bending the sprinkler parts.
