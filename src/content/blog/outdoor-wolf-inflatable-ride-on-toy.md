## Setting Up The Inflatable Ride

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-up-your-outdoor-wolf-inflatable-ride-on-toy-is-simple-and-quick.-follow-the-right-steps-to-enjoy-safe-and-fun-playtime.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting up your Outdoor Wolf Inflatable Ride on Toy is simple and quick. Follow the right steps to enjoy safe and fun playtime.

Proper setup helps keep the inflatable in good shape and makes sure it works well every time.

### Inflation Process

Start by unfolding the inflatable on a flat surface. Make sure it is clean and dry before inflating.

Use an electric air pump designed for inflatables. Connect the pump to the inflation valve and turn it on.

* Do not overinflate the ride. Stop when firm but not too tight.
* Check for air leaks or damage while inflating.
* Close the valve tightly after inflation.

### Placement Tips

Place the inflatable ride on a flat, soft surface like grass. Avoid rough or sharp areas that could cause damage.

Keep the inflatable away from trees, fences, and other obstacles. Make sure there is enough space around it for safe play.

* Clear the area of stones, sticks, and debris.
* Use a ground tarp under the inflatable for extra protection.
* Check the weather; avoid strong winds or rain.

### Maintenance And Storage

Clean the inflatable after each use with mild soap and water. Let it dry completely before storing.

Fold the ride carefully. Store it in a dry, cool place away from sunlight and heat.

* Repair small holes or tears with a patch kit.
* Do not store the inflatable while wet.
* Check the inflatable regularly for wear and damage.
