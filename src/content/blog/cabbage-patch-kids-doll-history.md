## Origins Of Cabbage Patch Kids

Cabbage Patch Kids are famous dolls loved by many. They started as simple art and became popular toys.

The story of these dolls began in the 1970s. Their unique look and soft bodies made them special.

### Creation By Xavier Roberts

Xavier Roberts created the first Cabbage Patch Kids in 1976. He was an artist from Georgia, USA.

He made soft sculptured dolls called "Little People." These were the first step to the famous dolls.

### Early Art To Doll Transition

The Little People started as drawings and small dolls. Xavier wanted to make bigger, softer dolls.

He worked with a doll maker to create hand-stitched dolls. These had unique faces and soft bodies.

* Original dolls had cloth bodies
* Faces were hand-sculpted
* Each doll looked different

### Initial Popularity Surge

The dolls became very popular in the late 1970s. People liked that each doll had its own name and adoption papers.

Stores sold out quickly. The demand grew fast, making Cabbage Patch Kids a big hit.

* Unique doll adoption process
* Soft, cuddly design attracted kids
* Limited availability increased interest
