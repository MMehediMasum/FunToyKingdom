## Flight Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=flying-your-rc-quadcopter-waterproof-edition-can-be-fun-and-safe.-follow-simple-tips-to-keep-it-in-top-shape.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Flying your RC Quadcopter Waterproof Edition can be fun and safe. Follow simple tips to keep it in top shape. Proper care helps avoid damage and extends flight time.

Before, during, and after flights, check important things. This guide shares key points for smooth flights in wet conditions.

### Pre-flight Checks

Check your quadcopter carefully before each flight. Look for signs of damage or loose parts. Make sure the battery is fully charged and secure. Test the controls and sensors on dry ground first.

1. Inspect propellers for cracks or chips.
2. Verify waterproof seals are intact.
3. Confirm battery is fully charged.
4. Test remote control signal strength.
5. Check GPS and compass calibration.

### Handling Wet Conditions

Flying in wet conditions needs care. The Waterproof Edition protects against water, but avoid deep water or heavy rain. Fly slowly and keep the quadcopter close. Watch for slippery surfaces when landing.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Wet Condition** | **Recommended Action** |
| Light rain | Fly cautiously, avoid sudden moves |
| Wet grass | Land slowly, watch for slipping |
| Water puddles | Avoid landing or flying low |
| Strong wind with moisture | Delay flight for safety |

### Post-flight Maintenance

After flying, clean and dry your quadcopter well. Remove dirt and water from all parts. Check for damage or loose screws. Store the drone in a dry place to avoid rust and corrosion.

**Post-Flight Care Checklist:**

* Wipe down with a dry cloth
* Remove and dry battery compartment
* Check all screws and tighten if needed
* Inspect propellers and motors
* Store in a cool, dry area

## Common Issues

Understanding common problems helps in maintaining your quadcopter's performance. Let's discuss what to look for and how to address them.

### Water Damage Signs

Water damage can affect the quadcopter's functionality. Recognizing the signs early is crucial.

* Reduced flight time
* Unresponsive controls
* Corrosion on metal parts
* Foggy camera lens

### Troubleshooting

Fixing issues promptly can prevent further damage. Follow these steps to troubleshoot effectively.

1. Check for visible water entry points.
2. Dry the quadcopter with a soft cloth immediately.
3. Inspect battery connections for moisture.
