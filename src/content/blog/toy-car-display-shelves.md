## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Types Of Toy Car Display Shelves Are Available?



Toy car display shelves come in acrylic, wood, and glass designs. They vary by tiers, capacity, and mounting style. Options include freestanding, wall-mounted, and portable cases for various car scales and collections.



### How Many Cars Can These Display Shelves Hold?



Capacity ranges from 25 to 100 cars depending on the model. Smaller shelves hold about 25 cars, while larger racks accommodate up to 100 diecast cars. Choose based on your collection size and shelf space.



### Are The Shelves Suitable For 1:64 Scale Toy Cars?



Yes, most shelves are designed for 1:64 scale cars like Hot Wheels and Matchbox. They provide perfect spacing and protection for this popular diecast scale. Always check product details for scale compatibility.



### Can These Shelves Be Wall-mounted Or Freestanding?



Many models offer both wall-mount and freestanding options. This flexibility lets you customize placement based on room layout and display preference. Wall mounts save space, while freestanding shelves are portable.



### Do Display Cases Protect Toy Cars From Dust And Damage?



Yes, cases with clear acrylic or glass panels protect cars from dust, scratches, and sunlight. Removable shelves and shatter-resistant glass add convenience and durability for collectors.



### Are Portable Display Cases Good For Kids And Collectors?



Portable cases with handles are ideal for both kids and collectors. They offer easy transport, safe storage, and organized display for toy cars on the go. Look for durable and lightweight designs.
