## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Diecast Toy Model Cars Made Of?



Diecast toy model cars are made of metal, usually zinc alloy. They often have plastic parts for details. This combination makes them durable and detailed. The models replicate real cars at a smaller scale.



### How Does Diecast Scale Affect Model Size?



Diecast scale represents the size ratio between the model and the real car. For example, 1:24 means the model is 24 times smaller. Smaller numbers indicate larger models. Understanding scale helps in choosing the right size for your collection.



### Are Diecast Model Cars Suitable For Kids?



Yes, diecast model cars are suitable for kids over three years. They offer durability and detail, making them great for play and learning. However, small parts can be a choking hazard, so adult supervision is advised.



### How Do You Care For Diecast Model Cars?



To care for diecast model cars, keep them in a dust-free environment. Use a soft cloth for cleaning. Avoid direct sunlight to prevent fading. Regular maintenance will keep them looking new.



### Can You Assemble Your Own Diecast Model Car?



Yes, assembly kits like Maisto's offer the experience of building your own model. These kits come with detailed instructions. They provide a fun and educational activity for car enthusiasts and hobbyists.
