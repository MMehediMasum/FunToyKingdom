## Setting Up And Storage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-up-and-storing-the-outdoor-iguana-inflatable-ride-on-for-kids-is-simple.-proper-care-helps-keep-it-safe-and-long-lasting.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting up and storing the outdoor iguana inflatable ride-on for kids is simple. Proper care helps keep it safe and long-lasting.

This guide explains how to inflate, deflate, and store the inflatable ride-on correctly.

### Easy Inflation Process

Start by placing the inflatable on a flat surface free of sharp objects. Use an air pump to fill it with air.

Inflate slowly and check for firmness. Do not overinflate to avoid damage.

* Lay the inflatable flat on the ground
* Attach the pump nozzle firmly
* Inflate until the ride-on feels firm but not hard
* Close the valve tightly to keep air inside

### Safe Deflation And Packing

To deflate, open the valve and press gently to release air. Avoid folding while air is still inside.

Fold the ride-on neatly and avoid sharp bends to prevent damage.

* Open the air valve fully
* Press the inflatable to push out air slowly
* Fold the ride-on flat and smooth out wrinkles
* Roll it up tightly for easy packing

### Compact Storage Solutions

Store the folded inflatable in a dry, cool place. Keep it away from direct sunlight and sharp objects.

Use a storage bag or box to keep the ride-on clean and safe until next use.

* Choose a dry storage area with good air flow
* Keep the inflatable away from heat sources
* Use a protective bag or container
* Check the inflatable before each use for damage
