## Setup Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-up-an-outdoor-jellyfish-sprinkler-inflatable-is-easy-and-fun.-this-guide-helps-you-prepare-it-correctly-for-safe-play.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting up an outdoor jellyfish sprinkler inflatable is easy and fun. This guide helps you prepare it correctly for safe play.

Follow simple steps to pick the best spot, inflate it properly, and connect the water safely.

### Location Selection

Choose a flat and open area for the sprinkler. Avoid sharp objects like rocks or sticks that can damage it.

Make sure there is enough space around the inflatable. This keeps kids safe while playing and running.

* Pick a grassy or soft surface
* Clear the area of debris
* Keep away from walls or fences
* Check for good drainage to avoid puddles

### Inflation Techniques

Use an air pump to inflate the jellyfish sprinkler. Electric pumps work faster but manual pumps are fine too.

Do not overinflate. The inflatable should be firm but still soft to avoid tears or damage.

* Connect the pump nozzle tightly
* Inflate slowly and check firmness
* Stop if the material feels too tight
* Seal the valve securely after inflation

### Water Connection

Attach your garden hose to the sprinkler’s water inlet. Make sure the connection is tight to stop leaks.

Turn on the water slowly. Check the spray pattern and adjust the hose if needed.

* Use a hose with a standard connector
* Check for leaks at the connection point
* Adjust water pressure for a gentle spray
* Turn off water when not in use

## Safety Precautions

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-an-outdoor-jellyfish-sprinkler-inflatable-is-fun-for-kids.-safety-must-always-be-the-top-priority.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using an outdoor jellyfish sprinkler inflatable is fun for kids. Safety must always be the top priority.

Here are some essential safety tips to ensure everyone stays safe while playing.

### Supervision Guidelines

Children should never be left unattended while playing with the sprinkler. Always have an adult nearby.

1. Keep a watchful eye on all children playing.
2. Ensure the area is clear of any sharp objects.
3. Limit the number of children playing at once.

### Slip And Fall Prevention

Water can make surfaces slippery. Take measures to prevent falls and injuries.

* Set up the sprinkler on a flat, grassy area.
* Have children wear non-slip water shoes.
* Remind kids to walk, not run, around the sprinkler.

### Weather Considerations

Check the weather before setting up the sprinkler. Play should only occur in safe conditions.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Weather Condition | Action |
| Sunny | Safe to play |
| Windy | Secure the sprinkler firmly |
| Stormy | Do not use the sprinkler |
