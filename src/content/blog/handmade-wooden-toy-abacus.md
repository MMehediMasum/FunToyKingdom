## Care And Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=handmade-wooden-toy-abacuses-are-durable-and-charming.-proper-care-helps-keep-them-safe-and-beautiful.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Handmade wooden toy abacuses are durable and charming. Proper care helps keep them safe and beautiful.

Simple steps can protect the wood and make the toy last for years of fun and learning.

### Cleaning Wooden Toys

Clean wooden toys gently to avoid damage. Use a soft cloth with mild soap and water.

Never soak the wood or use harsh chemicals. Dry the toy well after cleaning to prevent cracks.

* Use a damp cloth for light cleaning
* Apply mild soap for dirt removal
* Dry with a soft towel immediately
* Avoid soaking or submerging in water
* Do not use bleach or strong cleaners

### Storage Solutions

Store the wooden abacus in a dry place. Avoid areas with direct sunlight or high humidity.

Keep the toy away from heat sources and moisture to stop warping or mold growth.

* Use a closed box or drawer for storage
* Keep away from windows and heaters
* Store in a cool, dry room
* Place soft padding to avoid scratches
* Ensure good air circulation around the toy

### Extending Toy Lifespan

Handle the wooden abacus with care. Avoid dropping or rough play to keep it intact.

Apply natural wood oil occasionally to maintain the wood’s shine and prevent drying.

* Encourage gentle use to prevent damage
* Check for loose beads or parts regularly
* Use natural wood oil once every few months
* Repair small cracks or chips early
* Keep the toy clean and dry at all times
