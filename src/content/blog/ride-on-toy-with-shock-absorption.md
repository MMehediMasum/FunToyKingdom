## Types Of Shock Absorption Systems

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=ride-on-toys-use-different-shock-absorption-systems.-these-systems-help-make-the-ride-smooth-and-safe.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Ride on toys use different shock absorption systems. These systems help make the ride smooth and safe. They reduce the impact from bumps and rough surfaces.

Knowing the types of shock absorbers can help you choose the right ride on toy. Each system works in a unique way to protect the rider.

### Spring-based Suspension

Spring-based suspension uses metal springs to absorb shocks. The springs compress when the toy hits a bump. This reduces the force felt by the rider.

This system is common in many ride on toys. It is simple and effective for light to medium impacts. Springs also help keep the wheels in contact with the ground.

### Rubber Padding

Rubber padding is placed between parts of the ride on toy. It cushions shocks by softening the contact points. Rubber absorbs vibrations and sudden jolts well.

This system is quiet and low maintenance. It works best for small bumps and rough surfaces. Rubber padding adds comfort without extra moving parts.

### Air Cushioning

Air cushioning uses air-filled parts like tires or chambers. Air compresses under pressure to absorb shocks. This makes the ride smoother on uneven ground.

Air systems are adjustable by changing the air pressure. They provide good shock absorption for different weights and terrains. This type is common in higher-end ride on toys.
