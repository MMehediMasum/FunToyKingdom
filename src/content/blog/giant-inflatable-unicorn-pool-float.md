## Setting Up Your Float

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-up-your-giant-inflatable-unicorn-pool-float-is-easy.-follow-simple-steps-to-get-it-ready-for-fun.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting up your giant inflatable unicorn pool float is easy. Follow simple steps to get it ready for fun.

Take care during setup to keep your float safe and last longer in the water.

### Inflation Tips And Tools

Use a pump to fill your float quickly. Avoid over-inflating to prevent damage.

Check the valves for leaks before inflating. A tight seal keeps air inside longer.

* Use an electric or manual air pump
* Inflate on a flat surface
* Stop inflating when the float feels firm
* Close the valve tightly after inflation

### Safe Placement In Pool

Place the unicorn float in calm water areas. Avoid strong currents or deep edges.

Keep the float away from pool walls and sharp objects to stop punctures.

* Choose a flat, calm water spot
* Keep distance from pool edges and drains
* Check water depth before use
* Supervise children using the float

### Storage And Maintenance

Dry your float completely before storing it. Moisture can cause mold and damage.

Fold the float gently and store it in a cool, dry place. Avoid direct sunlight when not in use.

* Rinse off chlorine or salt water after use
* Dry fully before folding
* Store away from heat and sharp items
* Check for leaks before next use

## Caring For Your Unicorn Float

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=a-giant-inflatable-unicorn-pool-float-brings-fun-to-your-pool-days.-to-keep-it-looking-great,-you-need-to-care-for-it-properly.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

A giant inflatable unicorn pool float brings fun to your pool days. To keep it looking great, you need to care for it properly. Good care helps the float last longer and stay safe to use.

Below are tips on cleaning, fixing, and storing your unicorn float. Follow them to enjoy your float for many seasons.

### Cleaning Methods

Clean your unicorn float after every use. Dirt, sunscreen, and pool chemicals can damage the material. Use gentle cleaning to keep the colors bright and the surface smooth.

* Rinse the float with fresh water to remove sand and chlorine.
* Use a soft cloth or sponge with mild soap for stains.
* Avoid harsh chemicals and abrasive brushes.
* Dry the float completely before storage to stop mold growth.

### Repairing Punctures

Small holes can cause the float to lose air. Fix punctures quickly to keep the float safe. Use a repair kit made for inflatable materials. Follow the instructions carefully to seal the hole well.

| Repair Step | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Find the Leak | Inflate the float and listen for air escaping or submerge to see bubbles. |
| Clean the Area | Wipe the spot with rubbing alcohol to remove dirt and oil. |
| Apply Patch | Place the patch over the hole and press firmly. Let it dry as directed. |
| Test Repair | Inflate the float again and check for leaks. |

### Proper Storage Techniques

Store your unicorn float properly to keep it safe from damage. Avoid folding it tightly to stop creases. Keep it away from sharp objects and direct sunlight.

* Make sure the float is dry before storing it.
* Fold the float gently without sharp bends.
* Store in a cool, dry place indoors.
* Keep away from pets and sharp tools.
