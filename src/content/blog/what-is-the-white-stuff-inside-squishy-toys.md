## Material Basics

Squishy toys are popular for their soft, squeezable feel. The secret lies in the material inside. Understanding the basics of these materials helps explain why squishies are so unique. This section breaks down what makes up the white stuff inside these toys.



### Common Components

The white stuff inside squishy toys is usually foam. Polyurethane foam is the most common type. It starts as a liquid and then expands into a soft sponge. This foam traps air bubbles, making the toy light and squishy. Sometimes, squishies have a layer of silicone or rubber on the outside. These layers add durability and a smooth finish. The combination of foam and outer layers creates a fun texture.

### Why Squishies Are Soft

Softness comes from the foam’s air pockets. These tiny bubbles act like cushions. When you press the toy, the bubbles compress and then bounce back. This process makes the toy return to its shape slowly. The slow rebound feels satisfying to squeeze. The foam’s flexibility and air content work together. They give squishies their signature soft, stretchy feel.
