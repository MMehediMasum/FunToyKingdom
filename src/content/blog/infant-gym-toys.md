## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Age Is Best For Infant Gym Toys?



Infant gym toys are ideal from newborn up to 12 months. They support sensory, motor, and cognitive development during this stage. Toys like rattles and play mats engage babies effectively. Always check manufacturer age recommendations for safety.



### How Do Infant Gym Toys Aid Development?



These toys enhance hand-eye coordination, sensory skills, and muscle strength. Soft rattles and activity mats encourage reaching and grasping. Musical elements boost auditory learning. Overall, they promote early physical and cognitive growth in infants.



### Are Infant Gym Toys Safe For Newborns?



Yes, infant gym toys are designed with safety in mind. Materials are usually non-toxic, soft, and BPA-free. Ensure toys have no small parts or choking hazards. Always supervise your baby during play to maintain safety.



### Which Features Matter In Infant Gym Toys?



Look for bright colors, varied textures, and sounds. Toys with hanging elements and teethers are beneficial. Soft, washable materials add convenience. Interactive features like lights and music enhance engagement and learning.



### Can Infant Gym Toys Help With Teething?



Yes, many infant gym toys include teethers. They soothe gums while stimulating sensory play. Soft, textured teethers are gentle and safe. Combining teething relief with play keeps babies entertained and comforted.



### How To Clean Infant Gym Toys Effectively?



Use mild soap and warm water for cleaning. Machine washable mats and soft toys simplify hygiene. Avoid harsh chemicals to protect baby’s skin. Regular cleaning prevents germs and keeps toys safe.
