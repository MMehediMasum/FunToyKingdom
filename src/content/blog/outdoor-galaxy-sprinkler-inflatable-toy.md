## Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-outdoor-galaxy-sprinkler-inflatable-toy-clean-and-well-maintained-helps-it-last-longer.-regular-care-prevents-damage-and-keeps-it-safe-for-play.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Outdoor Galaxy Sprinkler Inflatable Toy clean and well-maintained helps it last longer. Regular care prevents damage and keeps it safe for play.

Follow simple steps to clean, store, and fix common problems with your inflatable toy.

### Cleaning The Inflatable

Clean the inflatable after each use to remove dirt and debris. Use mild soap and water for the best results.

Do not use harsh chemicals or abrasive brushes as they can damage the material.

* Mix mild soap with warm water
* Use a soft cloth or sponge to wipe surfaces
* Rinse with clean water to remove soap
* Let it air dry completely before storage

### Storage Recommendations

Store the inflatable toy in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Proper storage prevents mold and material damage.

Always ensure the toy is dry before folding and storing to avoid mildew growth.

* Deflate the toy fully before folding
* Fold gently to avoid creases
* Place in a storage bag or box
* Keep away from sharp objects and heat sources

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the inflatable loses air or has small tears, check for holes and patch them quickly. Fixing small problems early saves time and money.

Check the air valve to ensure it is closed properly to prevent leaks.

* Locate the leak by listening for hissing sounds
* Use soapy water to find bubbles at the hole
* Dry the area before applying a patch
* Follow patch kit instructions carefully
* Replace or tighten the air valve if needed
