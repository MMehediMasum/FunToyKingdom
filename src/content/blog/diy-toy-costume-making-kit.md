## Troubleshooting Common Issues

Every DIY toy costume making kit project can encounter unexpected problems. Don't worry, we've got you covered with these troubleshooting tips.

### Fixing Material Mistakes

Using the wrong materials can ruin your costume. Here's how to fix common material mistakes:

* Incorrect fabric choice: Replace the fabric with a suitable alternative.
* Color mismatch: Use fabric dye or paint to match the original color.
* Material shrinkage: Use a fabric stabilizer to prevent further shrinkage.

### Handling Size Adjustments

Costumes can be too small or too large. Here's how to adjust the size:

| Problem | Solution |
| --- | --- |
| Too small | Add extra fabric or use a larger pattern. |
| Too large | Remove excess fabric or use a smaller pattern. |

### Maintaining Durability

A well-made costume can last for years. Here's how to maintain its durability:

1. Wash the costume by hand with mild soap.
2. Iron the costume while it's still damp.
3. Store the costume in a cool, dry place.
