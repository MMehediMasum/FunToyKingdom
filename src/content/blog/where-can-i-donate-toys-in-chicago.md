## How To Prepare Toys For Donation

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=preparing-toys-for-donation-helps-make-sure-they-bring-joy-and-stay-safe.-clean,-check,-and-sort-toys-before-giving-them-away.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Preparing toys for donation helps make sure they bring joy and stay safe. Clean, check, and sort toys before giving them away. This care shows respect for the new owners and helps the donation process run smoothly.

### Cleaning And Safety Checks

Clean toys with mild soap and warm water. Remove dirt, dust, and stains carefully. Check for broken parts, sharp edges, or loose pieces. Fix small problems or discard unsafe toys. Batteries should be removed from electronic toys.

### Packaging Tips

Use sturdy boxes or bags for packing toys. Group similar toys together for easy sorting. Wrap fragile items in soft cloth or bubble wrap. Label boxes clearly with the type of toys inside. This helps donation centers organize donations quickly.

### Age-appropriate Selection

Choose toys suitable for different age groups. Avoid toys with small parts for young children. Include a mix of puzzles, books, and stuffed animals. This variety meets the needs of many kids. Follow donation center guidelines on age recommendations.
