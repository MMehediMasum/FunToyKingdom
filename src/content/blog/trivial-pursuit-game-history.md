## Origins Of Trivial Pursuit

Trivial Pursuit is a popular board game known for testing general knowledge. It has a rich history that dates back to the late 1970s.

The game was created by two Canadians who wanted a fun way to challenge friends and family with trivia questions.

### Creation And Founders

Scott Abbott, a sports editor, and Chris Haney, a photo editor, invented Trivial Pursuit. They met while working at a newspaper in Montreal.

* Scott Abbott brought the idea of making a trivia game.
* Chris Haney worked on designing the game board.
* Both wanted a game that was simple but fun.
* They started the project in 1979.

### Initial Concept And Design

The first version of Trivial Pursuit had a circular board with six colored wedges. Each wedge represented a category of questions.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Color |
| Geography | Blue |
| Entertainment | Pink |
| History | Yellow |
| Arts and Literature | Brown |
| Science and Nature | Green |
| Sports and Leisure | Orange |

The goal was to answer questions from all six categories to win the game. Players collected colored wedges as they answered correctly.

## Gameplay And Mechanics

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=trivial-pursuit-is-a-board-game-where-players-answer-questions-to-earn-pieces.-the-goal-is-to-collect-all-the-colored-wedges-by-correctly-answering-questions-from-different-categories.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Trivial Pursuit is a board game where players answer questions to earn pieces. The goal is to collect all the colored wedges by correctly answering questions from different categories.

The game tests players' general knowledge and offers fun and challenge through its simple rules and strategic moves.

### Basic Rules And Structure

Players move around a circular board that has spaces in six colors. Each color stands for a category of questions. Players roll dice and move their pieces to different spaces on the board.

1. Roll the dice to move your piece.
2. Answer a question from the category of the space you land on.
3. Correct answers let you keep playing and collect wedges.
4. Reach the center by collecting all wedges.
5. Answer a final question to win the game.

### Unique Features That Stand Out

The game uses a wedge system. Each wedge is a small colored piece that fits into the player's game piece. Collecting all six wedges means the player has answered questions in all categories.

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Wedge Collection | Players collect wedges by landing on "category headquarters" spaces and answering correctly. |
| Category Variety | Questions come from six different categories to test broad knowledge. |
| Final Challenge | After collecting wedges, players must answer a final question to win. |
| Dice Movement | Players roll dice to move, adding chance to the game. |
