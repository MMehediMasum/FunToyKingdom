## Choosing The Right Floorball Gear

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=playing-floorball-outdoors-is-fun-and-healthy-for-kids.-to-enjoy-the-game,-they-need-the-right-gear.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Playing floorball outdoors is fun and healthy for kids. To enjoy the game, they need the right gear. Choosing the right equipment helps kids play safely and comfortably.

Good floorball gear fits well and matches the child's skill level. This guide explains how to pick sticks, balls, and safety items for kids.

### Selecting Kid-friendly Sticks

Kids need floorball sticks that are light and the right size. A stick that is too long or heavy makes playing hard and less fun.

Look for sticks made with child-friendly materials. They should be strong but not too stiff for small hands.

* Choose sticks with a length near the child’s height
* Pick light sticks to improve control and reduce fatigue
* Select sticks with a soft grip for better handling
* Check the stick’s blade shape for easier ball control

### Picking The Perfect Ball

Floorball balls come in different sizes and weights. For kids, use balls that are lighter and smaller for easier play.

Choose balls made from safe plastic with smooth holes. This helps the ball fly well and stay safe during outdoor games.

* Pick balls designed for outdoor use to resist wear
* Choose lighter balls for younger children
* Look for balls with smooth surface and good durability

### Safety Equipment Essentials

Safety gear protects kids from injuries while playing floorball. Always include basic safety items for outdoor play.

Make sure the safety equipment fits well and is comfortable. This helps kids keep their gear on during the whole game.

* Use protective goggles or sports glasses to shield eyes
* Wear knee and elbow pads to reduce injury risks
* Choose gloves that protect hands but allow good grip
* Consider mouthguards for extra teeth protection

## Setting Up Outdoor Floorball Play

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=floorball-is-a-fun-sport-for-kids-to-play-outside.-setting-up-a-safe-and-fun-play-area-helps-them-enjoy-the-game-more.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Floorball is a fun sport for kids to play outside. Setting up a safe and fun play area helps them enjoy the game more. This guide explains how to create the right space and equipment for outdoor floorball.

With easy steps, you can prepare a good playing field, simple goals, and clear boundaries. These tips work well for kids playing in backyards, parks, or playgrounds.



### Ideal Play Areas And Surfaces

Choose a flat area that is safe and free of obstacles. A smooth surface helps the ball roll well and keeps the kids from tripping. Grass, asphalt, or concrete can all work if they are even and clean.

* Use a backyard, driveway, or park with enough space
* Clear away sticks, rocks, or holes
* Make sure the surface is not slippery or too rough
* Avoid areas with heavy traffic or sharp objects nearby

### Creating Simple Goals

Goals do not need to be fancy. You can use cones, boxes, or even markers on the ground. Make sure the goals are wide enough for kids to score but small enough to keep the game challenging.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goal Type | Materials | Size (Width x Height) |
| Cone Goals | 2-3 cones | 3-4 feet x 2 feet |
| Box Goals | Cardboard or plastic box | 3 feet x 2 feet |
| Marker Line Goals | Chalk or tape | 4 feet wide line |

### Marking Boundaries And Zones

Use clear markings to show the play area and zones. This helps kids understand the game better. You can use tape, chalk, or rope to make lines on the ground. Keep the zones simple and easy to see.

* Mark the outer boundary where the play ends
* Draw a center line to divide teams
* Create a small goal zone near each goal
* Use bright colors for better visibility

## Basic Floorball Rules For Kids

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=floorball-is-a-fun-and-active-game-for-kids.-it-is-played-with-a-stick-and-a-ball,-similar-to-hockey.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Floorball is a fun and active game for kids. It is played with a stick and a ball, similar to hockey.

Learning the basic rules helps kids enjoy the game safely and fairly.

### Game Duration And Teams

Floorball games for kids usually last for a short time. This keeps the game exciting and fun.

1. Each game lasts 10 to 20 minutes.
2. Teams have 3 to 5 players each.
3. Players switch roles to keep everyone involved.

### Scoring And Fair Play

Scoring in floorball is simple and encourages teamwork. Fair play is important for everyone to have fun.

* Score by getting the ball into the opponent’s goal.
* Each goal counts as one point.
* Respect other players and follow the rules.

### Common Fouls To Avoid

Understanding common fouls helps keep the game safe. Avoiding these actions ensures everyone enjoys playing.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Foul | Description |
| High Sticking | Stick goes above the waist |
| Tripping | Causing another player to fall |
| Body Contact | Using body to push opponents |

* Practice quick passes in small groups
* Use both forehand and backhand passes
* Dribble while changing speed and direction
