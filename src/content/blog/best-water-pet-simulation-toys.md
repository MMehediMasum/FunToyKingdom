## Care And Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-water-pet-simulation-toys-clean-and-well-maintained-helps-them-last-longer.-proper-care-ensures-your-toy-stays-safe-and-fun-to-use.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your water pet simulation toys clean and well-maintained helps them last longer. Proper care ensures your toy stays safe and fun to use.

Follow simple steps to clean, store, and fix common problems with these toys. This guide makes caring for them easy.

### Cleaning Guidelines

Clean your water pet toys regularly to avoid dirt and bacteria buildup. Use gentle methods to protect the toy’s surface and electronics.

* Use a soft cloth with warm water and mild soap.
* Wipe the toy gently; avoid soaking it in water.
* Dry the toy completely with a clean towel after cleaning.
* Do not use harsh chemicals or abrasive scrubbers.
* Clean sensors and buttons carefully to keep them working well.

### Storage Recommendations

Store your water pet simulation toys in a safe place to protect them from damage and dust.

**Storage Tips:**

* Keep toys in a dry, cool area away from direct sunlight.
* Store them in a box or container to prevent dust buildup.
* Remove batteries if you won’t use the toy for a long time.
* Avoid placing heavy items on top of the toys.

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

Sometimes your water pet simulation toy might not work as expected. Try these fixes to solve common problems.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Issue** | **Cause** | **Solution** |
| Toy won’t turn on | Dead or missing batteries | Replace or insert batteries correctly |
| Buttons not responding | Dirt or moisture | Clean buttons gently and dry thoroughly |
| Water leaks inside toy | Cracks or loose seals | Stop use and contact manufacturer for repair advice |
| Toy moves slowly | Low battery power | Charge or replace batteries |
