## Monopoly’s Origins

Monopoly is a famous board game known worldwide. It has a long history that started over a century ago. The game shows how people buy and sell properties.

This article explains how Monopoly was created and how it became popular. We will look at its early versions and how many people began to enjoy it.

### Creation And Early Versions

Monopoly was first created by Elizabeth Magie in 1903. She called it The Landlord’s Game. The game was meant to show the problems of land monopolies.

* Elizabeth Magie designed the game to teach about economics.
* The Landlord’s Game had different rules from today’s Monopoly.
* Players could choose to play cooperatively or competitively.
* The game used a square board with properties to buy and rent.

Over time, the game changed as people made their own versions. Charles Darrow is often credited with the modern version. He sold it to Parker Brothers in 1935.

### Initial Popularity

After Parker Brothers published Monopoly, it quickly became popular. The game spread during the Great Depression when people wanted affordable fun.

### Acquisition History

Monopoly was first created by Charles Darrow in the 1930s. The Parker Brothers bought the rights to the game in 1935.
