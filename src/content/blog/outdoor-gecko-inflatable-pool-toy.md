Using the right pump makes inflating the pool toy fast and easy. Choose pumps that fit the valve and give good airflow.

Deflating your pool toy quickly saves time and space. Release air fully to fold and store it compactly.

* Press gently on the pool to push air out.

* Fold the pool toy carefully as you remove air to avoid creases.

## Safety Tips For Outdoor Use

Using an outdoor gecko inflatable pool toy can bring lots of fun. It is important to keep safety in mind while using it. Following safety tips helps prevent accidents and injuries.

This guide shares easy safety tips for outdoor use of your inflatable pool toy. Pay attention to supervision, sun protection, and the right surfaces.

### Supervision Guidelines

Always watch children closely when they play with the inflatable pool toy. Never leave kids unattended near water or inflatable toys. Adults should stay within arm’s reach.

Keep the play area clear of hazards. Make sure children know how to get help if needed. Check the toy for damage before use.

* Watch children at all times
* Stay close and ready to assist
* Remove sharp objects nearby
* Inspect the inflatable for leaks

### Avoiding Sun Damage

Strong sunlight can damage the inflatable pool toy and harm skin. Use shade or sunscreen to protect yourself and the toy. Avoid long exposure to direct sun.

Store the inflatable in a cool place after use. Let it dry before folding to prevent mold and material wear.

* Use sunscreen when playing outside
* Place the toy in shaded areas
* Limit playtime under strong sun
* Keep the inflatable dry and cool

### Safe Use On Various Surfaces

Choose the right surface for your inflatable pool toy. Soft, flat surfaces reduce the risk of punctures or slips. Avoid rough, sharp, or uneven ground.

Check the surface before inflating. Clear away sticks, stones, and other objects that can cause damage or injury.

* Use the toy on grass, sand, or smooth ground
* Do not place on concrete or rocky areas
* Clear the area from sharp objects
* Inflate fully for stability
