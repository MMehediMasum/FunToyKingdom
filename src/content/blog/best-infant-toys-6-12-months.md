## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Toys For Infants Aged 6 To 12 Months?



The best toys for 6 to 12 months include stacking rings, sensory rattles, teething toys, and push cars. These toys support motor skills, sensory development, and early learning in infants effectively.



### How Do Stacking Toys Benefit 6-12 Month Old Babies?



Stacking toys improve hand-eye coordination, fine motor skills, and problem-solving. They also promote cognitive development by teaching size, color, and spatial relationships.



### Are Teething Toys Safe For Infants 6 To 12 Months?



Yes, teething toys like silicone teethers are safe and BPA-free. They soothe gums and encourage healthy chewing habits during teething phases.



### Which Toys Encourage Sensory Development In Infants 6-12 Months?



Sensory toys like rattles, rain sticks, and Montessori sets engage sight, sound, and touch. They enhance sensory processing and curiosity in babies.



### What Features Should I Look For In Infant Push Car Toys?



Look for lightweight, easy-to-grip, durable, and safe push cars. These encourage walking, balance, and gross motor skills for infants aged 6-12 months.



### How Can Activity Cubes Help Babies Aged 6 To 12 Months?



Activity cubes offer multiple interactive elements that develop fine motor skills, cause-effect understanding, and early cognitive learning in infants.
