## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Materials For 6-month-old Teething Toys?



Safe teething toys are made from BPA-free silicone, food-grade materials, and soft plastics. These materials soothe sore gums gently and are non-toxic for babies. Examples include silicone teethers and gel-filled toys like the Nuby Ice Gel Teether Keys.



### How Do Montessori Sensory Toys Benefit 6-month-old Babies?



Montessori sensory toys enhance motor skills, hand-eye coordination, and sensory development. They encourage exploration and learning through touch and movement, supporting early brain growth. Toys like Baby Montessori Sensory Toys offer safe, engaging textures and shapes ideal for this age.



### Can Teething Toys Double As Learning Developmental Toys?



Yes, many teething toys also promote learning and development. They stimulate senses and encourage grasping and chewing habits. For example, Baby Einstein teether-rattles combine soothing gum relief with sensory play and cognitive engagement for infants.



### Are Silicone Teething Mittens Effective For 6-month-olds?



Silicone teething mittens provide a safe chewing surface and prevent dropping. They are easy to wear, soft, and BPA-free. These mittens, like the Food Grade Silicone Teething Mitten, help babies soothe gums while keeping toys clean and close.



### How Should I Clean And Maintain Baby Teething Toys?



Clean teething toys with warm soapy water or sterilize by boiling if safe. Avoid harsh chemicals. Regular cleaning prevents germs and keeps toys safe for babies. Most silicone and plastic teethers are dishwasher safe for easy maintenance.

Picking items free from harmful chemicals is very important for your baby’s health. Also, toys that are easy to hold and chew encourage your baby’s development. Remember, every baby is different, so watch how your child reacts to each toy.
