## Benefits Of Toys At 22 Months

Toys play a key role in a 22-month-old baby's growth. They help babies learn new skills while having fun. Choosing the right toys supports many areas of development.

At this age, babies explore the world by touching, moving, and talking. Toys can guide their learning and help them build important abilities.

### Boosting Motor Skills

Toys that encourage movement improve both fine and gross motor skills. Babies learn to control their hands and legs better. This helps with everyday tasks like eating and walking.

* Stacking blocks to practice hand-eye coordination
* Push toys that support walking and balance
* Shape sorters to develop finger dexterity
* Soft balls to catch and throw

### Encouraging Language Development

Toys that make sounds or have pictures help babies learn new words. Talking about the toys builds their vocabulary. It also teaches them to listen and respond.

Creative toys let babies use their imagination. They can try new ideas and make choices. This builds problem-solving skills and confidence.

* Crayons and large paper for drawing
* Building blocks for making shapes
* Dress-up clothes for pretend play

## Safety Tips For Baby Toys

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-safe-toys-is-very-important-for-a-22-month-old-baby.-parents-should-check-toys-carefully-before-giving-them-to-their-child.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing safe toys is very important for a 22-month-old baby. Parents should check toys carefully before giving them to their child.

Safety helps prevent accidents and keeps babies happy while they play.

### Choosing Non-toxic Materials

Babies often put toys in their mouths. Toys made from non-toxic materials keep them safe from harmful chemicals.

Look for labels that say the toy is BPA-free, lead-free, and made with safe paints.

### Avoiding Small Parts

Small parts can cause choking if a baby swallows them. Toys for 22-month-olds should not have loose or tiny pieces.

* Check toys for buttons, beads, or detachable pieces.
* Test by trying to pull off small parts with your hands.
* Choose toys that are large enough to prevent swallowing.

### Checking For Durability

Durable toys last longer and stay safe after rough play. Fragile toys can break and create sharp edges or small pieces.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Material** | **Durability Level** | **Safety Tip** |
| Hard Plastic | High | Inspect for cracks regularly |
| Wood | Medium | Smooth edges to prevent splinters |
| Soft Fabric | Low | Check seams and stuffing often |

## Tips For Choosing The Right Toy

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-the-right-toy-for-a-22-month-old-baby-can-be-exciting-and-challenging.-it's-important-to-select-toys-that-match-their-interests-and-developmental-stage-while-also-being-budget-friendly.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing the right toy for a 22-month-old baby can be exciting and challenging. It's important to select toys that match their interests and developmental stage while also being budget-friendly.

Here are some tips to help you make the best choice for your little one.

### Matching Interests

Observe what your child loves to do. Do they enjoy stacking blocks or playing with dolls? Picking toys that align with their interests can keep them engaged longer.

* For a child who loves music, consider a toy piano.
* For an animal lover, choose soft animal figurines.
* For a child who enjoys building, look for stacking blocks.

### Considering Developmental Stage

At 22 months, children are developing motor skills and problem-solving abilities. Toys should encourage these skills without being too complex.

* Push and pull toys help with walking and coordination.
* Simple puzzles develop problem-solving skills.
* Art supplies like crayons foster creativity.

### Budget-friendly Options

Toys don't have to be expensive to be enjoyable. Look for affordable options that still offer quality and fun.

* Consider second-hand toys in good condition.
* DIY toys, like homemade playdough, can be fun and cheap.
* Check for sales and discounts on popular toys.
