## Care And Maintenance Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=handmade-wooden-toy-vegetables-need-gentle-care-to-last-long.-proper-cleaning-and-storage-keep-them-safe-and-beautiful.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Handmade wooden toy vegetables need gentle care to last long. Proper cleaning and storage keep them safe and beautiful.

Simple repairs help fix small damages and keep the toys ready for play. Follow these easy tips to maintain your wooden toys.

### Cleaning Methods

Clean your wooden toy vegetables with mild soap and water. Avoid soaking them in water to prevent damage.

* Use a soft cloth or sponge to wipe the surface.
* Rinse quickly with clean water.
* Dry immediately with a towel.
* Let them air dry completely before storing.

### Storage Recommendations

Store wooden toys in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Avoid damp areas to prevent warping and mold.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Tip | Reason |
| Use a breathable basket | Allows air circulation |
| Keep away from heaters | Prevents drying and cracking |
| Store flat or upright | Keeps shape intact |
| Avoid plastic bags | Traps moisture and causes mold |

### Repairing Minor Damages

Small chips or cracks can be fixed at home with simple tools and materials. This keeps toys safe for children.

Try these repair steps:

1. Sand the damaged area gently with fine sandpaper.
2. Apply non-toxic wood glue to cracks or loose parts.
3. Clamp or hold the parts together until dry.
4. Use a child-safe paint to touch up any worn spots.
