## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Toys For Infants 0-3 Months?



The best toys for infants 0-3 months include high contrast black and white toys, soft sensory books, and rattles. These stimulate visual and tactile senses, aiding early development and tummy time activities.



### How Do Black And White Toys Benefit Newborns?



Black and white toys enhance newborns’ visual development by providing high contrast images. This helps babies focus and strengthens their eyesight during the first 3 months.



### Are Musical Toys Suitable For Infants Under 3 Months?



Yes, musical toys like the Baby Einstein Take Along Tunes engage infants with soothing sounds. They promote auditory development and calm babies effectively.



### What Makes Tummy Time Toys Important For Newborns?



Tummy time toys encourage infants to lift their heads and strengthen neck muscles. They aid motor skills and prevent flat head syndrome in babies 0-3 months.



### Can Rattles Help With Sensory Development In Infants?



Rattles stimulate babies’ hearing and grasping skills. They promote hand-eye coordination and sensory exploration, essential for infants aged 0-6 months.



### What Features Should I Look For In Newborn Toys?



Look for soft textures, bright colors, safe materials, and multi-sensory elements like sound and touch. These features support newborns’ growth and curiosity safely.
