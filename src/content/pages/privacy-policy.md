## Privacy Policy

At **FunToyKingdom**, your privacy matters to us. We are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use, store, and safeguard data. Because our website focuses on **toys for babies, toddlers, children, and collectors**, we take extra precautions regarding privacy—especially for parents, guardians, and child safety.

This Privacy Policy explains in detail how we handle your personal information when you use our website, interact with our services, or engage with third-party partners through affiliate links, advertisements, or sponsored content.

By accessing and using FunToyKingdom, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our site.

This Privacy Policy aims to:

* Inform you about the **types of information** we collect.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect both **personal information** (data that identifies you) and **non-personal information** (data that does not directly identify you).

### a) Personal Information You Provide Voluntarily

When you interact with FunToyKingdom, you may provide information such as:

* Name
* Email address (e.g., newsletter subscription, contact form)
* Phone number (if voluntarily submitted)
* Shipping or billing details (if applicable for purchases or giveaways)
* Any content you submit in forms, comments, or reviews

### b) Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain data, including:

* IP address
* Browser type and version
* Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
* Operating system
* Referring/exit pages
* Time and date of visits
* Pages viewed and time spent on each page

### c) Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third parties, such as:

* Affiliate partners (purchase details after you click affiliate links)
* Advertising networks (general browsing behavior)
* Social media platforms (if you interact with our content via social login or sharing features)

## 6. Child Privacy and COPPA Compliance

Because FunToyKingdom features content about **toys for children**, we comply with the **Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and similar child privacy regulations.

* Our content is intended for **adults—parents, guardians, and educators—not children under 13**.
* We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
* If we discover a child has provided us personal data, we will delete it immediately.
* Parents who believe their child has shared personal data with us should contact us at **support@funtoykingdom.com**.

## 9. Your Rights Under GDPR

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=if-you-are-a-resident-of-the-european-union-(eu)-or-european-economic-area-(eea),-you-have-specific-rights-under-the-**general-data-protection-regulation-(gdpr)**,-including:&w=424&h=324&c=7)

If you are a resident of the European Union (EU) or European Economic Area (EEA), you have specific rights under the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, including:

* Right to Access – Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
* Right to Rectification – Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
* Right to Erasure – Request deletion of your data (“right to be forgotten”).
* Right to Restrict Processing – Limit how we process your data.
* Right to Data Portability – Request your data in a portable format.
* Right to Object – Opt-out of certain processing activities, including marketing.
* Right to Withdraw Consent – Revoke consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at **support@funtoykingdom.com**.

---

## 10. Your Rights Under CCPA

If you are a **California resident**, you are protected under the **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)**. Your rights include:

* Right to Know – Request details about the personal data we collect and how we use it.
* Right to Delete – Request deletion of personal information.
* Right to Opt-Out – Prevent the sale of your personal data (we do not sell data, but we honor opt-out requests).
* Right to Non-Discrimination – We will not deny services or offer different pricing if you exercise privacy rights.

To exercise your CCPA rights, email us at **support@funtoykingdom.com**.
