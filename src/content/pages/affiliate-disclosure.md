## How FunToyKingdom Generates Revenue

Running a high-quality toy-focused website requires resources. To sustain our platform and continue providing valuable information, we may earn income through the following methods:

### 1. Affiliate Links

* Many of the products featured on our website, including **baby & toddler toys, collectible toys, educational toys, and creative & DIY toys**, may contain **affiliate links**.
* If you click on an affiliate link and purchase a product, we may receive a small commission at no extra cost to you.
* These commissions help us maintain our website, create new content, and continue offering free resources.
* Affiliate partnerships never influence our opinions—we recommend only what we believe adds value.

### 2. Sponsored Content

* Occasionally, we may collaborate with brands or companies that align with our values and mission.
* Sponsored posts, reviews, or product highlights will always be clearly labeled as “Sponsored” or “Paid Partnership.”
* Even when sponsored, our content reflects **honest opinions and genuine assessments**.

### 3. Advertisements

* FunToyKingdom may display advertisements through networks such as Google Ads or other third-party ad services.
* These ads help us generate revenue but do not represent personal endorsements.
* Ads may be targeted based on your browsing behavior; however, we do not control the exact ads displayed.

### 4. Product Reviews and Promotions

* We sometimes receive toys or related products for free from companies for testing or review.
* Receiving a product does not guarantee a positive review—we provide **fair, balanced, and unbiased feedback**.
* When we feature such items, we will disclose that the product was provided at no cost or in exchange for review.

## Product Reviews – Honesty First

We take toy reviews seriously because toys are directly used by children and families. Our review process includes:

* **Research:** Understanding the brand, safety certifications, and intended age group.
* **Testing:** Evaluating durability, educational value, creativity, and user experience.
* **Safety Checks:** Ensuring toys meet recognized safety standards (ASTM, CPSIA, EN71, etc.).
* **Balanced Opinions:** Highlighting both strengths and potential drawbacks.

Even if a toy is provided to us for free, our review will remain unbiased and honest. If we do not recommend a product, we will state so clearly.
